\section{The cubic-seven ground field}\label{app:kseven}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:kseven19}
A compact arithmetic hyperbolic Coxeter polytope with actual ground
field $K_7=\Q(2\cos(2\pi/7))$ has dimension at most19.
\end{theorem}
Theorem~\ref{thm:main} supplies the dimensions at least21 tail. We
exclude dimension20 here; arithmetic inheritance by arbitrary faces
is not assumed. Together with Theorem~\ref{thm:eight19}, this gives
nine specified fields with ceiling19. The all-field ceiling remains20;
no ceiling19 is asserted for $\Q(\sqrt2)$, $\Q(\sqrt5)$ or
$\Q(\sqrt2,\sqrt5)$.

\paragraph{The strict contradiction and proof boundary.}
For total charge $C$, polygon payments, simplicity and the symmetric
nonnegative dual $h$-vector give
\[
 35f_0\ge C\ge5f_2-190f_0
 =35f_0+\frac52\sum_{i=0}^{20}(i-10)^2h_i
 \ge35f_0+500,
\]
since $h_0=h_{20}=1$. This is impossible and proves
Theorem~\ref{thm:kseven19} using the existing higher-dimensional tail.
