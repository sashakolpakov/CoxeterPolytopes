These results supplement the completed compact arithmetic21 package.
They preserve the published general compact ceiling29 and give no new
compact example.

* [Universal compact triangle constraint](../results/fresh_compact29_three_triangle_star_constraint_20261018.md):
  full triangle contact domains at an E7³+E8 vertex and at least six
  nontriangles among nine designated two-faces.
* [Arithmetic21 transfer limitation](../results/fresh_compact21_triangle_transfer_scope_20261018.md):
  the analogous E7³ restriction leaves an explicit golden-field dual
  above40 because that forest has zero dual multiplier. New incidence
  columns remain open.
* [Original-wall cap obstruction](../results/fresh_exact66_old7_boundary_cap_obstruction_20261018.md):
  the two specified finite duties at the old7 boundary admit no compatible
  norm-two root in the fixed H9 space. Other incidence choices remain open.
* [D5/E8 triangle bundles](../results/review_compact21_d5_two_e8_triangle_coupling_20261018.md):
  at most one designated D5 triangle beside two E8 factors, forced E8
  simplices and a product seven-face, with no new charge saving.
* [D5 beside A1/E7/E8](../results/fresh_compact21_d5_a1_triangle_coupling_20261018.md):
  the complete six-root domain adds one possible third wall, but all six
  candidates are pairwise incompatible. The
  [root review](../results/review_compact21_d5_a1_triangle_coupling_20261018.md)
  records precise completeness and verification scope.
* [Seven-triangle supported forest](../results/fresh_compact21_supported_forest_triangle_coupling_20261018.md):
  among42 nonadjacent component-marked faces at A1+D5+E7+E8, at most seven
  are triangular, with a unique necessary equality subset. The
  [root source/geometric review](../results/review_compact21_supported_triangle_coupling_20261018.md)
  passes. Actual quadrilateral charges remain to control.
