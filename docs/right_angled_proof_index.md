# Right-angled results

The current submissions contain the complete arguments:

- [Sharp dimension bounds 8 and 4](../arxiv/sharp_dimension_bounds_8_and_4/main.tex):
  dimension at most eight for finite-volume right-angled polytopes, and at most
  four when every vertex is ideal. Both bounds are attained.
- [The second smallest ideal right-angled four-polytope](../arxiv/ideal_four_dimensional_volume_and_structure/main.tex):
  the second distinct volume is 8π²/3, attained uniquely by the reflected
  double of the 24-cell. The restricted chain theorem supplies the structure
  argument after the equality facet spectrum has been established.

The [submission guide](arxiv_submission_editions_20261028.md) lists the two
current PDFs. Their [dimension proof map](right_angled_sharp_dimensions_publication_proof_map_20261021.md)
and [volume proof map](ideal_ra4_volume_and_structure_proof_map.md) distinguish
written geometry, published inputs, exact checks and conditional Lean logic.

Compact RA4 [constraint results](compact_ra4_publication_proof_and_verification_map_20261024.md)
remain supporting work. The compact second-volume problem is not resolved
here. Earlier partial dimension arguments and construction attempts retain
their dated scientific records; they do not define the current bounds.
