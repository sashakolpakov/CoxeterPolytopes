## Quantified internal lemma interfaces

These labels are proposed proof boundaries, not declarations of axioms.

1. **`ActualFaceSupport`.** For each actual two-face of a finite-volume
   Coxeter n-polytope, its white Gram is positive definite of rank n−2;
   each white-plus-one-black edge principal is positive definite and each
   adjacent corner is positive semidefinite. A finite corner is a finite
   Coxeter forest of rank n. The ideal varying-factor statement must allow
   nonsimple cusps.
2. **`FactorFiberCharge`.** For every positive rank list summing to n−1,
   with `V=∏(r_i+1)`, a marked pair in factor i occurs in `V/(r_i+1)`
   actual corner fibers. If each factor cost is≤(r+1)c(r), then
   `C_actual≤V*Σc(r_i)`. Whole affine containment is proved before this
   multiplication, using actual-corner PSD and a positive kernel.
3. **`OldLocalRule58`.** There exists one intrinsic nonnegative current
   corner weight rule with triangle total≥2 and quadrilateral total≥1,
   whose finite costs and affine envelopes are those of the coupled
   packet. Every changed later output either retains an older chosen
   contribution or has an independent replacement. This is a universal
   arbitrary-support theorem, not216 saved-face absence.
4. **`OldFiniteEightObstructions`.** For every actual finite Coxeter forest
   of rank58, the old charge is≤377 unless it is exactly B13 or D13 plus
   three factors from{A15,D15}; those eight forests have cost378. The
   proof uses the 3298 representative cover, both increasing families,
   all 748 actual fibers and 1549 exact residual certificates, with all
   presence guards and unreachable ranks treated explicitly.
5. **`ConnectedInverseLower`.** Let B be a positive-definite symmetric
   Coxeter Gram of rank k, diagonal2, nonpositive off-diagonal entries and
   nonzero magnitudes≥1. If its graph is connected, every inverse diagonal
   is≥k/(k+1). Prove this by root deletion, inverse positivity and Schur
   induction. For a forest with a unit-or-larger nonnegative contact in
   each component of total rank M, the contact energy is≥M/(M+1).
6. **`AttachedForestRank`.** Let G be a nondegenerate Lorentz core with
   one negative direction and inverse diagonal r>1 at p. Suppose the new

   at most one negative direction. Then `G_Y−r*u*uᵀ` is PSD, so its
   contact energy is≤1/r. Lemma5 yields `M≤1/(r−1)`. Orthogonal indirect
   tails are included in Y; no bound just on first neighbors is enough.
