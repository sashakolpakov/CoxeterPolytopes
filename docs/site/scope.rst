Inputs and open scope
=====================

The papers separate published theorems, internal geometric proofs, and exact
computed propositions. The :download:`assumption register <../manuscript_assumptions.md>`
and :doc:`paper maps <papers>` are the detailed guides. An inherited input is
an explicit proof boundary, not a claim that its entire provenance was rerun.

Published and geometric inputs
------------------------------

Depending on the paper, named inputs include acute Coxeter geometry,
spherical/affine diagram classification, incidence and h-vector results,
arithmetic cyclic-integrality and admissibility criteria, and the stated
rational anisotropy obstruction. The sharp right-angled paper names the
Musin kissing bound and Kolpakov facet-minimum/equality results. The RA4
structure arguments use their stated three-dimensional recognition and
rigidity inputs.

The papers give the precise statements they use. In particular, a volume
minimum is not silently substituted for a facet minimum, and a numerical
minimum does not supply equality rigidity without an appropriate theorem.

Finite data and formal boundaries
---------------------------------

Finite57 retains named inherited finite tables and companion propositions.
Its selected replay does not regenerate every ancestor census. The two
pending loader replacements affect safe current replay; they do not justify
calling a partial run complete. The public-data manifest binds the bulk inputs
separately from the source tree.

Lean kernels assume the geometric and script interfaces specified in their
maps, then prove internal implications from them. Actual wall/face incidence,
coverage by classified diagrams, field-to-coordinate interpretation, and
some radical estimates remain written inputs. Neither a standard printed
axiom list nor a cached object discharges those hypotheses.

Preserved research is not an extra paper
----------------------------------------

The parked field-specific arithmetic refinements, predecessors, literal-host
construction investigations, and supporting finite-transfer notes remain
preserved. They are excluded from the current universal arithmetic 20 edition
unless explicitly named as an input there. Later reviewed 19/17 results are
separate written proof records; they do not relabel the compiled 23/20 PDFs.

What remains open
-----------------

* Lowering the nonsharp general finite-volume, compact, and compact arithmetic
  bounds further.
* Finding a new compact hyperbolic Coxeter example in dimension nine or higher;
  no such example is supplied by the local construction profiles here.
* Unrestricted minimality and the second volume for compact right-angled
  four-polytopes.
* Extending the restricted ideal RA4 chain theorem to arbitrary ideal
  right-angled four-polytopes, if such an extension holds.
* Completing finite57's bounded loader replay and integrating it into the
  selected plan.
* Formalizing the actual geometric and arithmetic interfaces beyond the
  selected compiled kernels and their explicit premises.

The sharp right-angled 8/4 bounds are already attained by published examples.
Those dimension questions are not presented as open searches.
