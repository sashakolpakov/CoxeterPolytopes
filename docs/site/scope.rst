.. _scope-chapter:

What is proved, assumed and still open
======================================

The repository contains six paper families, with complete written results
at their stated inputs and several different levels of verification.
“Internally reviewed” means that the written argument has a source-bound
review by another author and root acceptance. It does not mean external
refereeing, complete regeneration of every finite input or end-to-end
formalization of hyperbolic geometry.

The three current illustrated dimension copies have 50 pages for finite
volume 57, 286 for general compact 17 and 217 for compact arithmetic 15.
Removing their marked illustration additions recovers the reviewed base
sources exactly. Their diagrams explain existing dependencies; no new
bound, computation or Lean theorem follows from the illustration builds.
The unchanged right-angled reading copies have 5, 17 and 14 pages.
:doc:`papers` distinguishes these copies from the six frozen editions
and the preserved consolidated bases.

.. _scope-results:

The result and its quantified class
------------------------------------

.. list-table:: Current theorem boundaries
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 23 42 35

   * - Family
     - Written conclusion
     - Scope that must remain visible
   * - General finite volume
     - Every finite-sided finite-volume hyperbolic Coxeter polytope has dimension at most 57, at the declared geometric and finite-input interfaces.
     - Compact and noncompact cases are included. Arithmeticity, a fixed facet count and a special cusp type are not hypotheses.
   * - General compact
     - Every compact hyperbolic Coxeter polytope has dimension at most 17, at the declared inputs of its complete written proof.
     - No arithmetic hypothesis. Exact dimension 18 and the independent higher-dimensional tail are both needed.
   * - Compact arithmetic
     - Every compact arithmetic hyperbolic Coxeter polytope has dimension at most 15, at the stated literature and computed premises.
     - Uses the general compact-17 theorem, actual-field arguments, four computed outputs and a manual exact-16 exclusion.
   * - Sharp right-angled dimensions
     - Finite-volume right-angled polytopes have dimension at most 8; entirely ideal ones have dimension at most 4.
     - The first conclusion allows all vertex types. Published examples attain both bounds; the written improvements are internally sharp at their named inputs.
   * - Ideal right-angled dimension four
     - The published minimum is used, and the paper proves the stated second-volume equality result and restricted chain theorem.
     - The chain theorem assumes that every facet is isometric to the metric octahedron Oct or its triangular-face double D. Equality in the universal second-volume theorem derives this spectrum.
   * - Compact right-angled dimension four
     - Complete facet-capacity, excess and local structural constraints.
     - These results are partial progress toward unrestricted volume minimum and second-volume questions.

Necessary forest lists, algebraic survivors and local model profiles are
not realized polytopes. No new hyperbolic Coxeter example is supplied.
The current dimension arguments also do not establish that 57, 17 or 15
is sharp.

.. _scope-geometry:

Geometric inputs are not arithmetic inputs
-------------------------------------------

The general finite-volume and compact arguments use the physical Lorentz
form, acute Coxeter walls, native angles, actual wall and face incidence,
and the stated spherical and affine link classification. A field of
definition, integral cyclic products and positivity at nonphysical
embeddings enter only the arithmetic arguments.

The :doc:`finite_volume` proof uses one simultaneous nonnegative corner
rule. Its exact-dimension-58 contradiction, bounded 59–163 tail and
unbounded tail from dimension 164 have different proof homes. The
narrow-face assertion used in the last branch is the stated general
geometric assertion; the argument does not import arithmetic field
conditions from the surrounding reference. Finite vertices and affine
ideal links have different local domains and multiplicities, which the
proof retains.

The :doc:`compact_bounds` proof fixes one interior point and one original
farthest vertex in each argument. Every component projection belongs to
the same original vector and spends the same norm budget. Actual
auxiliary points must satisfy every original wall. A second locally
convenient vertex does not acquire the original maximizing property, and
overlapping optimized energy gains cannot be added without a joint
funding argument.

Neither proof reduces a higher-dimensional case by declaring an arbitrary
face to be Coxeter. Arithmeticity is likewise not inherited by an
arbitrary face. The compact-17 proof has independent actual-rank cases
through dimensions 19–29 in addition to exact dimension 18. The published
compact ceiling of 29 handles the remaining higher dimensions; it does
not replace those intermediate cases.

.. _scope-finite57:

Finite57's completed conjunction and inherited inputs
------------------------------------------------------

The current-rule application package has actual execution for all 64
selected shards and its final 259-unit conjunction. The covered 28,028
maps include 12,518 nonunit applications and 83 prefixes. This is a
completed application-and-evidence scope at explicit ordinary and
supplemental premises. It is not the historical 146-job manuscript replay
and does not regenerate every ancestor classification or numerical table.

The distinction is mathematical as well as administrative. A native-map
index requires an exhaustive labelled-injection domain and its justified
target cap. A selected application requires its actual map key, applicable
provider and protected-prefix coverage. The final reader verifies the
registered joins. These steps do not turn source hashing into a proof of
geometric completeness or silently supply a missing inherited census.

The two bounded loader replacements separately have all 25 distinct actual
PASS units and primary-catalogue integration. The other 111 historical
script-command matches retain inherited execution credit. The full
historical inventory has 136 scripts, nine Lean roots and one PDF job,
with an unchanged core inventory of eleven. No combined fresh full-run
PASS is claimed by either inventory or by a newer illustration build.

The :download:`finite57 reproduction guide <../finite_volume57_reproduce.md>`
states the current mathematical algorithm contract beside the exact
source/input bindings, actual receipts, remaining premises and failure
history. Its pinned replay predecessor under ``manuscripts/revisions/``
is preserved. It must not be silently replaced by the newer human reading
source when interpreting older receipts.

.. _scope-arithmetic:

Arithmetic 15: the actual field and the external cutoff
-------------------------------------------------------

Arithmetic reflection lattices admit the required quadratic-form model;
“first type” is not an extra restriction on the theorem's class. The
proof nevertheless has to identify the actual field used by its local
root lines. Physical unit normals need not all have coordinates in that
field, and disconnected components need not begin with identical raw
scales. Permitted rescalings, cyclic integrality and every required
nonphysical embedding remain part of the argument.

The four grouped computed premises are the quadratic whole-A17 output,
the two cubic finite-anchor A17 outputs, the shared quadratic B17/D17
output and the whole-B16 output. The shared B/D output has distinct
geometric domain inclusions and consumers; one file does not identify
those geometries. Surviving arithmetic pairs are algebraic candidates
that still require their geometric exclusions. Replaying the same worker
is not an independent second implementation, and the B/D17 conjunction's
survivor-and-counter check has a narrower scope than re-enumerating every
rejected trial.

The remaining whole-D16 exclusion is a handwritten dyadic argument. It
retains both signed forks, all relevant splitting types and the full
maximal-order strips. It is distinct from a rank-17 forest with a D16
component and a spectator. A prepared D16 census has no execution credit
and is not used to replace the manual proof.

One external statement is deliberately left literal. With
:math:`\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2`, the article assumes that the full integral
reflection arrangement for

.. math::

   L=\mathbb Z[\varphi]^{n+1},\qquad
   f=-\varphi t^2+\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i^2,\qquad n\geq8

has no compact chamber. The stronger infinite-covolume assertion is a
separate premise wherever used. The cited bibliographic record has been
checked, but the precise primary theorem has not been independently
authenticated from its full text. The paper's internal bridge to
compatible invariant lattices is written out; similarity or
commensurability alone does not supply this literal lattice conclusion.
The distinct form with coefficient :math:`-2\varphi` cannot be
substituted for it.

The :download:`arithmetic dependency map <../compact_arithmetic_consolidation_proof_map_20261025.md>`
and the :download:`final source review <../../results/review_arithmetic15_final_covers_and_composition_root_20261025.md>`
keep this authentication limit visible. General compact 17 does not use
this arithmetic lattice premise. Arithmetic 15 depends on that general
theorem as well as its own exact-17 and exact-16 exclusions.

.. _scope-right-angled:

Right-angled code, equality and recognition inputs
--------------------------------------------------

The :doc:`right_angled` argument first proves the projection and height
facts for actual walls. Its spherical-code and facet-count inputs then
have their stated dimensions and strictness. The ideal branch uses the
metric equality information it actually needs, rather than treating a
cardinality bound as a uniqueness theorem for every kissing configuration.

For ideal RA4, the published volume minimum, its equality statement, the
internally proved second-volume equality spectrum and the restricted
cutting/recognition theorem have separate roles. The unique second-volume
double does not imply that every ideal right-angled four-polytope is a
24-cell chain. The chain theorem assumes that every facet is isometric
to the regular ideal right-angled octahedron Oct or to its double D
across a triangular face. The triangular face describes D, rather than
imposing an additional adjacency hypothesis on the four-polytope.

For compact RA4, the local excess inequalities do not prove that the
regular 120-cell minimizes volume among all compact right-angled
four-polytopes. They do not classify a universal second-volume double.
The :doc:`volume_problems` chapter explains the exact universal constraints
and where the unrestricted problems remain open.

Named literature inputs include the relevant incidence and
:math:`h`-vector theorems, spherical/affine classifications, arithmetic
integrality and admissibility results, the rational anisotropy
obstruction, and the right-angled code, facet and recognition theorems.
Their precise scopes belong to the individual proof maps and sources.
Retrieving a citation or checking its bibliographic metadata is distinct
from verifying the proposition used by a proof.

.. _scope-formal:

What the Lean records establish
--------------------------------

Selected Lean modules prove conditional internal implications. Their
ordinary parameters retain geometric incidence, actual-wall feasibility,
complete covers, analytic estimates, field interpretations or computed
outputs as specified by their logic maps. Printed foundational axioms
do not list those parameters.

The selected publication gate has two modules, two separate actual axiom
audits and nine declarations in its
:download:`completion manifest <../../results/lean_publication_completion_20261021/manifest.json>`.
Later compact-17 scalar and composition modules have their own actual
compilation and separate audits for ten declarations in the
:download:`targeted completion <../../results/compact17_targeted_lean_completion_20261025/manifest.json>`.
These packages retain their individual scopes; they are not a count of
every formal result in the repository or a new audit of all modules.

The arithmetic dyadic kernels formalize the listed valuation/parity and
scalar deductions. They do not construct the actual number field,
maximal-order strips or physical roots from geometry. Likewise the
right-angled kernels do not identify an abstract code with every actual
polytope without the written projection interfaces.

A compile receipt, an actual axiom audit and a source review are separate
evidence. A cached object or a successful PDF build supplies neither a
fresh axiom audit nor an instantiation of a theorem's geometric
hypotheses. :doc:`formal_logic` exposes the module-level boundaries;
:doc:`reproducibility` explains the serial, separately monitored stages.

.. _scope-availability:

Public sources and unavailable bulk inputs
-------------------------------------------

The public source tree, final PDFs and site do not include every large
research input. The bulk manifest binds 936 scientific files in 33
archives; **none of those assets has been uploaded**. A further 37
third-party files are excluded from both Git and the bulk payload.
Their provenance and retrieval references are preserved separately.

The manifest and restoration helper establish a byte-identity interface,
not successful public availability or mathematical verification. Exact
upstream bytes may need separate retrieval, and no redistribution licence
is implied. A local file must not be exposed through a Sphinx download
as an alternative route around the bulk publication boundary.

Cleanup preserves evidence and scientific sources while removing owned
unused build products. Preserved local legacy objects do not become
source-to-object proofs merely through restoration. The documentation
therefore makes no complete clean-checkout or transitive-regeneration
claim. :doc:`sources` and :ref:`repro-input-availability` identify the
separate source, bulk and external-input routes.

.. _scope-history:

Read dated guides as dated records
-----------------------------------

The catalogue's compact-23 and arithmetic-20 editions remain at their
original identifiers ``compact_farthest_bounds`` and ``compact20``. They
are not renamed compact 17 and arithmetic 15 by a newer paper index.
Their sources, PDFs and successful verification records remain preserved.
The illustrated working editions have their own source and build
identities in the same paper families.

Earlier preparation notes can accurately describe a source as uncompiled,
a phase as pending or a job as deferred under the former fixed-GiB policy.
Later source-bound receipts record what subsequently ran; they do not
rewrite those earlier events. Use the current proof maps and completion
records for status, and the dated predecessor for its original contract.
Neither old prose nor a plan is new execution evidence.

Parked field-specific arithmetic refinements, stronger-bound frontiers,
literal-host construction studies and spectral searches remain preserved
supporting research. They do not acquire universal-bound or realized-example
credit from their presence in the repository. A cleared but unexecuted
worker remains unexecuted. These developments stay outside the complete
reading editions unless a proof map explicitly names a required input.

.. _scope-open:

Questions left open
--------------------

* Sharpen the general finite-volume, general compact and compact arithmetic
  bounds beyond 57, 17 and 15, or determine their actual maxima.
* Construct a new compact hyperbolic Coxeter example in dimension nine or
  higher. Necessary local diagrams and outer chamber relaxations supply
  no such example.
* Determine unrestricted volume minimality and the second volume for
  compact right-angled four-polytopes.
* Determine the scope of any ideal RA4 chain classification beyond the
  current metric Oct/D facet condition.
* Regenerate the inherited finite57 inputs if complete transitive
  reproduction is required, while retaining the completed bounded-loader
  and current-application scopes already achieved.
* Formalize and instantiate the geometric, literature, field and
  enumeration interfaces beyond the selected conditional Lean kernels.

The right-angled dimension 8 and entirely ideal dimension 4 bounds are
already attained by published examples. Those dimension questions do not
remain open searches in this project.
