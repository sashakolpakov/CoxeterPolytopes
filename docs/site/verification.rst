What has been verified
======================

There are four distinct layers: written mathematical proof, replay of finite
computed propositions, typesetting, and conditional formal proof. A successful
run at one layer does not complete the others.

-------------------------------

mathematical reviews at their stated input boundaries. The compact PDFs still
prove 23 and 20; the later written 19 and 17 proofs have their own source and
