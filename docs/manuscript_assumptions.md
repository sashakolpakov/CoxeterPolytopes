Updated20 October2026. The original18October source-preparation register
is retained below, followed by dated verification updates. Current
source-bound results are recorded by the per-module ledgers and the
[three-kernel verification receipt](../results/publication_logic_verification_20261020.json),
not by a source file's presence. The initial preparation and handoff

not appear in the output of Lean's axiom printer. Successful compilation
with only standard foundational axioms would certify each implication
with the hypotheses listed here; it would not certify that those
hypotheses have been instantiated for actual hyperbolic polytopes.

## Arithmetic reflection groups and the quadratic-form model

Clarification, 21 October 2026. Every finite-covolume arithmetic
hyperbolic reflection group is of first type, also called simplest type.
Thus the quadratic-form model used by the arithmetic arguments is not
an additional restriction on the class of arithmetic Coxeter polytopes.
After conjugacy and the usual commensurability identifications, the model
uses an admissible form over a totally real field: signature (n,1) at
the physical embedding and positive definite at every other embedding.

The published reflection-to-quadratic-form reduction is Vinberg,
*Discrete groups generated by reflections in Lobachevskii spaces*
(1967), Lemma7 ([original article](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/sm4099)).
It is explicitly restated in Agol–Belolipetsky–Storm–Whyte,
Lemma5.2 ([author-hosted paper](https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~kwhyte/ahfinite.pdf)),
following their discussion of Tits' classification. Bogachev–Kolpakov
also state this model reduction in the introduction to
[*Thin hyperbolic reflection groups*](https://doi.org/10.1112/blms.70108).

This is a published external input, not a newly proved internal result
or a Lean-verified classification. Existing kernels take the resulting
quadratic-form/arithmetic interfaces as premises. Source-bound notes
and historical receipts retaining the phrase first type keep their
original bytes; it denotes this automatic model, not a proper subclass
of the arithmetic reflection lattices under consideration.
