## The exact external result

Put phi = (1 + sqrt(5))/2. Bugaenko's 1984 paper considers the integral
automorphism group of

```text
L_n = Z[phi]^(n+1),     f_n = -phi*x_0^2 + x_1^2 + ... + x_n^2.
```

On printed page 6 he defines the subgroup generated by **all** hyperplane
reflections in that integral group. He states that its fundamental polyhedron
is bounded for n <= 7 and has infinite volume for n >= 8. The form, coefficient,
ring, ambient dimension and full reflection subgroup match the input used by
the arithmetic manuscript. This is a theorem about this precise lattice.

The [publisher's record](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/vmumm3408) identifies
V. O. Bugaenko, *Groups of automorphisms of unimodular hyperbolic quadratic
forms over Z[(sqrt(5)+1)/2]*, Vestnik Moskov. Univ. Ser. 1 Mat. Mekh.,
1984, no. 5, pp. 6–12. The

## What the arithmetic-15 proof needs

The [consolidated arithmetic source](../manuscripts/revisions/compact_arithmetic_dimension_bound_consolidated.tex),
Appendix I.1 (`app:arithgoldenlongstar`), states the external theorem in the full
range n >= 8. Its current applications are narrower: the isolated long-head
label-five cases for whole B17 and whole D17, both in **ambient dimension 17**.
The present proof therefore needs only the n = 17 consequence. Nearby B16/D16
wall-deletion diagrams do not change the ambient dimension of those cases.

The internal argument must connect an actual polytope to the literal lattice.
It identifies the golden field, retains every compatible invariant lattice
Lambda, and proves that each actual facet reflection preserves L_n. Consequently
its chamber is subdivided by the full integral reflection arrangement. If the
actual chamber were compact, an arrangement chamber inside it would be compact,
contradicting the published theorem at n = 17. Sharing a field or a
commensurability class would not by itself establish this containment.

No separately necessary stronger-volume premise was found in the current
consumers: infinite volume implies the no-compact-chamber assertion they use.
The whole-B16 and manual whole-D16 exclusions do not invoke this cutoff.
The general compact-17, finite-volume-57 and right-angled arguments do not use it.
