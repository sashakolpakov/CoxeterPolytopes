## Algorithms and formal logic

Every proof-relevant computation has separate obligations: a finite
input domain, the algorithm, termination, soundness, completeness and
the exact output from execution. These are mathematical propositions;
a list of filenames or a saved PASS cannot establish coverage.
The [computational proof contract](computational_proof_contract.md)
and each paper’s proof map record those obligations.

Finite57’s 25-unit loader replacement is reviewed but has only two actual
PASS records, with the next unit refused for RAM before launch. Its old
full selection remains an inventory until the replacement is completed
and integrated. Do not run its eager commands as though the entire plan
were memory-safe. The [partial ledger](../results/finite57_loader_replay_post_finishing_20261021.json)
is immutable; resume in a new ledger. There is no combined full-replay claim.

Selected Lean kernels prove internal implications with external theorem,
geometry and script premises explicit. Their assumption maps separate
those premises from derived conclusions; actual axiom audits are recorded
separately from compilation. The [assumption register](manuscript_assumptions.md)
and [verification guide](manuscript_verification.md) explain that boundary.
The regular 24-cell coordinate-height kernel and classical dimension
composition kernel now have bounded compilation and separate actual
five-/four-declaration axiom audits. The
[completion receipt](../results/lean_publication_completion_20261021/manifest.json)
records the sources and output; actual geometry and field/script bridges
remain explicit premises.
