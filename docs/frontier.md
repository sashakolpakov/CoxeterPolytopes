## Claims deliberately not made

- "Only one compact dimension-8 Coxeter polytope exists" is **not** asserted.
  Only one is currently known; uniqueness is established only in specified
  few-facet classes.
- A reflective modular form does not by itself certify a cofinite reflection
  chamber.
- Failure of Vinberg enumeration to terminate does not prove
  non-reflectivity.
- Exhaustion of a bounded lattice census does not classify all rank-10
  Lorentzian lattices.
- A self-adjoint \(\mathcal O_F\)-action on a divisor lattice does not by
  itself imply that the nef cone, its eigenspace section, or its wall set is
  reflection invariant.
- A finite quotient of a locally finite wall arrangement does not imply that
  a lifted chamber is finite-sided or compact; one must control its chamber
  stabilizer (equivalently, the reflection index in the even norm-2 case).
- Nonexistence of a reflective modular form does not imply that the associated
  hyperbolic reflection subgroup has infinite index; the converse bridge fails
  in published examples.
