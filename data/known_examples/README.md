# Known examples and their evidence

[index.json](index.json) records exact Gram matrices, roots and source locators.
An artifact's status distinguishes chamber geometry from completeness of a
root search. No entry is a new-polytope claim.

| Artifact | Recorded scope |
|---|---|
| [Bugaenko H8](bugaenko_compact_h8.json) | Exact eleven-wall reconstruction, endpoint criterion and compactness; see the [Vinberg transcript](bugaenko_vinberg_transcript.json). |
| [P6,10](p6_10_exact_certificate.json) | Exact known geometry and index-two lineage; the source audit does not import the disputed uniqueness reduction. |
| [K3 chambers](k3_high_rank_nef_chambers.json) | Five source-audited full-nef Gram systems in dimensions 14–18, with separate geometric/volume records. |
| [Enriques chambers](enriques_finite_automorphism_nef_chambers.json) | Seven known full-nef configurations in H9; recorded exact replay and endpoint tests. |

The **[detailed examples guide](https://sashakolpakov.github.io/CoxeterPolytopes/examples.html)**
explains the 164-edge/41-vertex Bugaenko certificate, the 2508-ray Enriques
transfer and independent audit, K3 provenance, and limitations of inherited
classifications. Other stocks in this directory retain their own statuses.
Third-party inputs are retrieved under [their provenance](../../repro/EXTERNAL_INPUTS.md).
