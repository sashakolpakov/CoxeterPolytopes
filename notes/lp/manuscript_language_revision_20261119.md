# Primary-source language check for the LP manuscript

Date: 19 November 2026. Reviewer: /root/language_reviewer.
Scope: primary-source reading and terminology audit only. No mathematical
worker, solver, finite enumeration, Lean module, TeX engine or tests launched.
No manuscript source edited by this reviewer.

## Sources actually consulted

1. Alexander Kolpakov, *On the optimality of the ideal right-angled 24-cell*,
   arXiv:1211.2944v2, PDF pages 1–3:
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1211.2944v2 .
   The introduction states concrete extremal and dimension conclusions.
   The preliminaries define half-spaces, supporting hyperplanes, facets,
   vertices, edges, vertex figures and the face vector before using them.
2. Alexander Kolpakov, *Deformation of finite-volume hyperbolic Coxeter
   polyhedra, limiting growth rates and Pisot numbers*, arXiv:1105.6267v5,
   PDF pages 1–4, fetched at https://arxiv.org/pdf/1105.6267 .
   The generating set is fixed explicitly. Definitions precede the named
   theorems. Geometric and algebraic statements refer to the particular
   group, polyhedron and generating set under consideration.
3. E. B. Vinberg, *Hyperbolic reflection groups*, Russian Mathematical
   Surveys 40:1 (1985), 31–75. Official metadata:
   https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/rm2140 . Primary indexed English-PDF passages
   at journal pages 31 and 39 were read, using the Math-Net PDF search
   results for the introduction and the definition of a convex polytope.
   Direct full-PDF requests redirected or timed out; this review does not
   claim that the full English PDF was fetched. The passages define the
   polytope through its bounding hyperplanes and use Gram matrices,
   compactness and finite volume as explicit mathematical properties.
4. V. V. Nikulin, *On arithmetic groups generated by reflections in
   Lobachevsky spaces*, English translation, Math. USSR-Izvestiya 16:3
   (1981), 573–601, journal pages 573–575:
   https://www.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=im&option_lang=eng&paperid=1742&what=fullteng .
   The ground field, Gram matrix, cyclic products and real embeddings are
   specified before the finiteness statements. Fixing dimension and fixing
   field degree are separate hypotheses.
5. V. V. Nikulin, *On the classification of arithmetic groups generated by
   reflections in Lobachevsky spaces*, English translation, Math.
   USSR-Izvestiya 18:1 (1982), 99–123, journal pages 99–102:
   https://www.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=im&option_lang=eng&paperid=1550&what=fullteng .
   Its nonexistence statement in dimensions above 15 also requires the
   ground-field degree to be sufficiently large. That statement must not
   be presented as an unconditional dimension bound 15.

6. E. B. Vinberg, *Absence of crystallographic groups of reflections in
   Lobachevsky spaces of large dimension*, Trudy Moskov. Mat. Obshch. 47
   (1984), 68–102. The original Russian PDF was actually fetched and
   journal pages 68–74 were read at
   https://www.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=mmo&option_lang=eng&paperid=444&what=fullt .
   Journal page 71 defines a planar angle as a pair consisting of a
   vertex and an incident two-dimensional face. It states weight
   inequalities on vertex sums and face sums; page 72 compares these
   sums. Pages 72–74 define graph and Coxeter-diagram terminology,
   including order, rank, Gram matrix and elliptic/parabolic diagrams.
   The English metadata was checked at https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/mmo444;
   no claim is made that the English translation was fetched.

The sources supply terminology and examples of precise mathematical
exposition. No prose is copied, and no theorem from these papers is added
to the LP proof merely because it was consulted for language.

## Manuscript-wide corrections needed

- A dimension bound is a numerical conclusion. The necessary conditions
  for a hypothetical polytope, or an exclusion criterion, are formulated
  as a linear feasibility problem. Replacing one noun in the original
  abstract is insufficient to repair that logical distinction.
- The variables e_j are squared norms of orthogonal projections, not
  projections themselves. The Euclidean space p-perp does not have
  spherical components: the connected components of the vertex Coxeter
  diagram span mutually orthogonal subspaces W_j of p-perp.
- Replace payment/budget/debit/ownership metaphors by lower and upper
  bounds, sums of weights, nonnegative variables and dual inequalities.
  If a conventional LP term is retained, define it through its formula.
- Replace slot by coordinate or projection variable. An inequality on a
  joint set of components uses the same e_j variables as the individual
  estimates; its coefficient sum must remain at most one.
- Use a class of configurations with specified hypotheses, and an
  exhaustive family when every required configuration is represented.
  The word complete should not replace an explanation of this coverage.
- Use a fixed vector x throughout the compact proof. State the convention
  once, then repeat original or actual only where it distinguishes a
  facet of the polytope from an auxiliary or formal vector.
- Define an exceptional wall configuration through the participating
  facets, their incidence relations and the stated parameter intervals.
  Squared time, owned rank and spatial contact are not self-explanatory.
- Replace computational output/survivor/census phrases by the actual
  finite algebraic classification: no solutions, one possible parameter
  pair, or two possible pairs, with the subsequent geometric conclusion
  kept separate.
- Give a precise local theorem or scope in the exclusion tables. Labels
  such as six-role short cap or genuine fork are internal shorthand,
  not mathematical explanations.
- Keep the distinction between retained local theorems and consequences
  of the LP. Arithmetic pruning remains a hypothesis of this recovery.
- Retain all rank restrictions, parameter ranges, signs, strictness,
  original-field assumptions, lattice inclusions and whole-diagram
  restrictions. Clear prose must not silently enlarge applicability.
- Keep the exact restricted optimum 377 distinct from a universal
  optimization over all possible weight functions.
- Replace repetitive execution or editorial promises in the main text
  by the mathematical statement being established. Verification records
  belong in the computational/reproduction discussion.
