## 1. One law on actual corner incidences

Fix an integer dimension \(n\ge58\). A corner \(\kappa=(v,F)\) is an
incidence of a vertex and a two-dimensional face. Let
\(\phi(\kappa)\in\mathbb R^{26}\) be the retained raw feature vector,
with \(\phi_0=1\). A native numbering is chosen once for each complete
vertex diagram and reused at all its corners. Different vertices may
have different numberings. At an ideal vertex the description also
retains each corner's actual residual node choices and complete
induced native maps in the product-link construction. Its varying
factor together with those residual diagrams is the complete corner
diagram; it is not replaced by the full undeleted parabolic diagram.
The transferred face statements retain all the permitted coherent
numberings; one does not choose a favourable new numbering independently
at each occurrence of a vertex.

Let \(\chi_B(\kappa)\) be the accepted guarded ideal feature: the
corner is ideal, its complete varying affine factor is native \(B[13]\),
its marks are \(\{10,11\}\) or \(\{10,12\}\), and the complete corner
diagram contains three distinct connected components, disjoint from
both marks, of types \(A_{15}\) or \(D_{15}\). Deleting the marks
first and counting the newly separated pieces is not this predicate.
It is zero otherwise. This is the guarded
feature of [GI], not the unguarded B13 indicator.

With \(b=[n=58]\), use the single law
\[
 w(\kappa)=\bigl(be+(1-b)c\bigr)\cdot\phi(\kappa)
                         +b\lambda\chi_B(\kappa),\qquad
 \lambda,\rho\ge0.                                      \tag{1}
\]
The two 26-vectors \(e,c\), the margin \(\rho\), and \(\lambda\)
are the 54 shared coordinates. The endpoint and tail are values
of this one dimension-dependent function, not different laws on
the vertex and face sides.

For actual incidences the following three vectors are conserved
between vertex sums, small-face sums and the remaining corners:
\[
             b\phi,\qquad (1-b)\phi,\qquad b\chi_B.       \tag{2}
\]
This is ordinary double counting of the same corners. It includes
all residual choices at ideal links; a face-only bonus would not
satisfy it. If columns are rescaled by a positive strictness weight,
all coordinates in (2) receive the same weight and their counting
mass is divided by it. A truncated moment model must retain the
corresponding weighted conservation coordinates; a branch label
alone is insufficient.

No realization theorem for arbitrary local Gram assignments is needed.
Only the necessary direction is used: every actual finite-volume
Coxeter polytope supplies the stated stars, vertex diagrams, complete
link data and conservation equations. Extra local configurations
which cannot satisfy omitted cusp walls merely enlarge the domain
on which a payment proof is being required.

## 4. The global implication uses only necessary incidence identities

Write \(r=n-1\). A vertex figure is a simplex at a finite vertex
and a product of simplices at an ideal vertex. Let \(h_j\) be its
\(h\)-vector, \(V_L=\sum_jh_j\) (also at a finite vertex, where
\(V_L=n\)), and
\[
 G_v=\sum_{j=0}^r h_j\min(j,r-j),\qquad
 E_v=rV_L/2,\qquad b_n(v)=5G_v/2-E_v.                    \tag{5}
\]
At a finite vertex \(h_j=1\), so
\[
 \beta_n=
 \begin{cases}
 n(n-6)/8,&n\ \text{even},\\
 (n-1)(n-5)/8,&n\ \text{odd}.
 \end{cases}
\]
At an ideal vertex \(b_n(v)=\kappa_n(v)\).

Use the retained incidence/index theorem in [Incidence], with its
published literature premises. Let \(I\) count vertex--two-face
incidences, \(f_2\) be the number of two-faces, and \(v_f\) the number
of finite vertices. Write \(s(F)\) for the number of sides of \(F\).
For a generic linear height,
\(I=\sum_vE_v\), \(2f_2=\sum_vE_v^0\), and \(E_v^0\ge G_v\),
where \(E_v^0\) counts uncut edges in the vertex figure.
The global minimum and maximum have their entire figures uncut.
The simplex/product-of-simplices \(h\)-polynomial has \(h_j\ge1\)
for every \(0\le j\le r\). Symmetry gives
\[
 E_v-G_v=\sum_{j>r/2}h_j(2j-r)
       \ge\sum_{j>r/2}(2j-r)\ge r.
\]
The two extrema therefore yield
\[
 D_5:=\sum_{\dim F=2}(5-s(F))
 \ge \beta_n v_f+\sum_{v\ {\rm ideal}}\kappa_n(v)+\Delta_n,
 \qquad \Delta_n\ge5(n-1)>0.                             \tag{6}
\]
At58 the sharper displayed sum is \(5\cdot29^2=4205\).
This is the accepted incidence argument at general rank; it has
no dependence on a face being Coxeter in its intrinsic metric.

On the other hand, Section2 pays every triangle and quadrilateral.
For \(s(F)\ge5\), nonnegative corners already pay \(5-s(F)\).
Using (2) to sum the same law over faces and vertices, then (3), gives
\[
 D_5\le\sum_v C(v)
 \le\beta_n v_f+\sum_{v\ {\rm ideal}}\kappa_n(v)
       -\rho\left(v_f+\sum_{v\ {\rm ideal}}V_L(v)\right).
                                                               \tag{7}
\]
Equations (6)--(7) contradict each other even when \(\rho=0\).
Consequently the listed complete premises and one feasible common
certificate imply the necessary-direction exclusion at every
\(n\ge58\). This conditional composition is the statement submitted
for review here; no manuscript or published bound is changed by it.

## 5. What is mathematical closure, and what remains evidence work

The finite58 networks and the local 54-coordinate program are
mathematically one coupled finite LP: they share exactly the same
\(e,\rho\), while network potentials are separate private variables.
The ideal frontier costs/averages and finite-tail \(\xi\) are auxiliary
certificate coordinates. The network gives an exact potential
formulation of its finite forest budgets; fixed frontier dominators
and finite-tail domination are sufficient certificate restrictions.
The two A13 rows are also sufficient restrictions. Derived coefficient
equalities, such as tail quarter coefficients forced by D14 and caps,
are linear consequences of these rows, not geometric axioms.

The D14 successor [Assembly] with A13 has hand counts 5,810 columns,
103,606 local rows and 935,736 local nonzeros. These counts exclude
the separate complete finite58 network, its aliases and potentials.
They are not a newly constructed or checked global matrix.
Fresh issued objects, full candidate documents, complete shard/page
evidence joins and actual verification of the successor remain absent.
Their absence does not invalidate the conditional mathematical
pullback; conversely that pullback is not a new execution receipt.

All mathematical selector substitutions, vertex implications and
incidence cancellation are now identified. Acceptance of this
assembly, with all the imported complete premises, is the remaining
proof-review boundary. No additional global
realization theorem or repeated scientific key census is demanded.
The original classification, source completeness and literature
theorems remain real hypotheses: this is not yet a classification-free
universal SDP.

Actual dimensions give \(t=1/n\). The fixed endpoint selector
\(b/58\le t\le(1-b)/59+b/58\), \(b\in\{0,1\}\), \(t\ge0\),
has the closed outer support \(\{1/58\}\cup[0,1/59]\).
The interval also contains nonreciprocal points, and \(t=0\) is a
closure point. This support condition alone does not assert that
complete local geometric data exist at every point in the support.
Its sole threshold-dependent bound is \(t\le1/N\).
Increasing the integer threshold \(N\ge58\) only shrinks this outer
support and its admitted actual dimensions. The dimension58 point
plus every actual tail dimension \(n\ge59\) already covers the
threshold; no reduction to a Coxeter face of dimension58 is used.
Lowering \(N\) requires newly justified local domains and vertex
budgets, not just a different right side.

For further hierarchy work, a valid necessary geometric or moment
constraint shrinks, or leaves unchanged, the primal counterexample
set and supplies additional dual certificate mechanisms. Adding a
missing payment row to the
fixed-feature coefficient search instead restricts the permitted
laws; it completes an obligation and need not improve the bound.
The feature conservation in (2), primal geometric refinements,
sufficient certificate families, and derived linear consequences
must remain distinct.
