# Hostile audit: reflection-maximal strong-Coble exact nef return

**Verdict (2026-08-10): PASS, with one ideal-boundary clarification.**
The audited theorem is
[`reflection_maximal_coble_exact_nef_return.md`](reflection_maximal_coble_exact_nef_return.md).
The proof is carrier-relative and uses no classification of reflective
lattices or surfaces.

## 1. Hidden negative curves

Let \(B=\sum B_i=-2K_S\), with the \(B_i\) disjoint smooth rational
curves of square \(-4\), and let \(C\notin\{B_i\}\) be an irreducible curve
with \(C^2<0\).  Distinct effective curves have nonnegative intersection,
so \(B.C\geq0\).  Adjunction gives

\[
 C^2=2p_a(C)-2+\frac{B.C}{2}.
\]

If \(p_a(C)\geq1\), the right-hand side is nonnegative.  Hence
\(p_a(C)=0\), and \(C\) is smooth rational because its normalization has
genus zero and total delta invariant zero.  Since \(B.C=-2K_S.C\) is an
even nonnegative integer, negativity leaves exactly

\[
 (C^2,B.C)=(-2,0)\quad\text{or}\quad(-1,2).
\]

This classifies every hidden negative irreducible curve; it does not assume
that the proposed facets generate the Mori cone.

## 2. The three integrality checks

On

\[
 M=\left\langle\operatorname {NS}(S),B_1/2,\ldots,B_b/2\right\rangle,
 \qquad q_M=2q_{\operatorname {NS}(S)},
\]

all three possible reflections are integral.

1. If \(C^2=-2\), then \(B.C=0\).  Nonnegativity of every \(B_i.C\)
   forces \(B_i.C=0\) term by term, so the ordinary \((-2)\)-reflection
   fixes every \(B_i/2\).
2. If \(C^2=-1\), then \(C^2_M=-2\) and

   \[
   s_C(x)=x+2(x.C)C,\qquad
   s_C(B_i/2)=B_i/2+(B_i.C)C.
   \]

3. The root \(h_i=B_i/2\) has \(h_i^2=-2\) in \(M\), and

   \[
   s_{h_i}(x)=x+(B_i.x)h_i.
   \]

   It preserves every other half-boundary because the \(B_i\) are
   pairwise disjoint.  This is the exact point at which disjointness is
   indispensable.

## 3. Saturation and the chamber argument

The integral orthogonal group \(O^+(M)\) is discrete.  For every integral
reflection \(s\in O^+(M)\), the group generated by \(W(P)\) and \(s\) is
therefore a discrete reflection overgroup.  Relative reflection maximality
gives \(W(P)=R(M)\).

An ample class \(A\) has \(A.D_j>0\) for every displayed effective facet
curve.  These strict inequalities also select the intended component of
the positive cone, so \(A\in\operatorname {int}P\).  If \(C^2<0\), the
preceding classification puts its reflection in \(W(P)\).  A reflection
mirror of \(W(P)\) cannot cross the interior of its fundamental Coxeter
chamber.  The sign at \(A\) is positive, hence \(x.C\geq0\) on the closure
of \(P\).

If \(C^2\geq0\), then \(A.C>0\) places the nonzero class \(C\) in the closed
future causal cone selected by \(A\).  Every representative \(x\) in the
closed cone over \(P\) is in the same causal cone, and Lorentzian reverse
Cauchy--Schwarz gives \(x.C\geq0\).  Thus the cone over \(P\) is nef.

For the reverse containment, a nef class of positive square satisfies all
the displayed effective-curve inequalities and therefore lies in \(P\).
A nonzero nef isotropic ray is obtained as the limit of
\(D+\varepsilon A\) as \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\).  Accordingly the equality
is between the **ideal closures** in the projectivized positive cone:

\[
 P=\mathbf P(\operatorname {Nef}S).
\]

This closure sentence is the only clarification needed in the source
proof; it does not change the theorem.

## 4. Exact scope of the PASS

The theorem removes hidden-negative-wall enumeration and complete-Mori
return **after** a strong-Coble carrier has been constructed and the
equation \(B=-2K_S\) has been proved.  It does not derive that equation from
reflection maximality.  In particular, the reflection-maximal
\((2,3,\infty)\) chamber on the blow-up of \(\mathbb F_4\) remains outside
the hypotheses and remains a valid counterexample to any maximality-to-log
implication.  The non-Coble square-minus-four rank problem is therefore
unchanged by this PASS.
