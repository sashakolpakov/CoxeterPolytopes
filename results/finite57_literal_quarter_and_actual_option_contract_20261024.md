The four batches are `options-0` through `options-3`. These repair a specific
remaining gap in the first driver: matching residual coefficients and an
attaining forest does not bind fixed costs, w1/w2, or every OTHER explicit

   checks the resulting value is at most that row's frozen maximum. It skips
