## A different construction objective: cover the new facet's inward rays

The [dotted-cap lemma and coverage criterion](fresh_h9_mixed_d4_global_caps_20261014.md), with a [separate exact review](fresh_h9_mixed_d4_global_caps_20261014_review.md), show why a universal no-cutter claim would be false. Every strict spacelike escape admits a coherent Coxeter wall dotted to every old wall that cuts it. An exact example is provided, but its new facet is the entire H8 plane and volume is infinite.

Attaching a compact perpendicular H8 source turns completion into a concrete hyperbolic-ball covering problem. Old walls truncate the inward rays over explicit balls in that source. A strict cover by ball interiors is sufficient for compactness, provided all Coxeter pairings and a common interior have been verified. If every old/new side interaction is dotted or parallel, the balls leave an uncovered base-boundary patch and volume remains infinite. Finite contacts are necessary.

Acuteness also requires the source to contain all projected old centers. Thus their hyperbolic convex hull must fit inside the union of the old truncating balls. A bounded numerical scout found no obstruction witness for the displayed cap; this is neither a covering certificate nor a feasible source placement. Solving exact source placement, finite contacts and coverage together remains open.
