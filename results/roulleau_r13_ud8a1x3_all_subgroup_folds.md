printed graph of `A1,...,A19` is consistent.  Its leading

reconstruct `A20,...,A39` integrally.  The complete Gram has rank 13,

the claimed weight-two Petersen graphs, and every cross pairing satisfies
`A_J.B_J' = 4-A_J.A_J'`.

The exact source certificate keeps these layers separate: the first-19

published induced-chamber argument inside `II_(1,17)`, not an inference from
the finite degree cutoff in the ancillary search transcript.
