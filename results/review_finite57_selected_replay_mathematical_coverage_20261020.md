20 October 2026. Source/metadata inspection only. No scientific checker,

## Verdict

The current selection is 111 script invocations, eight conditional Lean
roots and one optional PDF job: 120 full jobs, ten core jobs with PDF.
It is a **selected certificate replay**, not a transitive replay of every
finite proposition used by the manuscript. In particular it does not
recheck the 6,596 coarse rank58 Bellman tables or the 759-row first
increasing-family Bellman calculation. Their saved outputs and internal
proof status enter selected downstream checks through source pins.

This is a meaningful reproducibility boundary, but I found no new
mathematical contradiction or undeclared external axiom in the inspected
interfaces. The current manuscript names the finite computed propositions,
prints their mathematical domains/algorithms, and distinguishes those
inputs from hashes and geometry. The lemma map explicitly says the prior
finite-domain theorems and universal local atlas remain obligations.
Historical reports claim generation and separate replay of both omitted
calculations. This audit does not independently authenticate or repeat
those historical executions. Calling the 120-job selection a complete
fresh check of every required finite computation would overstate it.
