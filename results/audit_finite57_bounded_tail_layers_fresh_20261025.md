## 1. The common implication and what its data must mean

At ambient n, put d=n-1. The proof needs ONE simultaneous nonnegative
marked rule, not separately optimized costs at different vertices.
Every actual triangle must receive at least2 and every quadrilateral
at least1 under that rule. The same rule must give finite cost U_n and
actual cusp charge C(v). Then

    U_n <= beta_n,

contradict the shape inequality, including the all-finite case.
The parity formulas for beta_n and factor-fiber multiplicity were already
reviewed. Their geometric hypotheses do not include arithmeticity,
simple cusps, a facet-count bound or Coxeter heredity of arbitrary faces.

For a rank profile r, the ideal data must bound its actual rule by

    C(v)/V <= sum_i c(r_i),
    V=product_i(r_i+1),
    kappa(v)/V=(5G-dV)/(2V).

An affine component cost is therefore divided by its ORDER, not its rank.
The marked factor pair appears V/(r_i+1) times by the actual fiber theorem.
An unmarked graph count is not a replacement for that theorem.

The exact outputs U_n and positive profile margins may remain old
authenticated numerical premises. Their domain must still be explicit:
