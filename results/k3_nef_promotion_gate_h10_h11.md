# Exact K3 nef-promotion gate for `H10/16`, `H10/45`, and `H11/29`

## Outcome

None of the three new divisor/reflection carriers promotes to the displayed
polytope being a full nef object in its presently admissible rank-12 Picard
lattice.

- `H10/16`: the unique action-stable even saturation contains an additional
  ((-2))-root whose hyperplane cuts the strict interior.
- `H10/45`: every one of the ten action-compatible finite overextensions
  contains such a cutting ((-2))-root.  This exhausts the seven index-two and
  three index-four extensions allowed by the fixed discriminant form and
  eigen-glue.
- `H11/29`: twelve genuine facets have primitive ambient normal of square
  (-4).  Such a facet cannot be a K3 nef wall.

This is a no-promotion theorem, not a retraction of the positive results.
All three polytopes remain genuine finite-volume algebraic
divisor/reflection-subsystem chambers.  The gain here is an exact reusable
criterion which tells us, before building more geometry, when a divisor
carrier can possibly be upgraded to a full nef realization.

## The exact promotion criterion

Let (M\subset N=NS(X)) be a finite-index, (g)-stable inclusion of even
hyperbolic lattices.  Put

\[
F=\operatorname{Fix}(g)_{\mathbf R},
\]

and let (C\subset F) be the full-dimensional pointed fixed section of a
complete (M)-root chamber.
Assume that every displayed old facet is the restriction of a genuine
((-2))-root wall in (N), and that no root of (N) is perpendicular to all
of (F).  Then

> (C) is the intersection of (F) with the closure of an (N)-Weyl
> chamber if and only if no ambient ((-2))-root hyperplane meets the relative
> interior (C^\circ).

If (v_1,\ldots,v_k) are all extreme rays of the polyhedral cone (C), the
test is completely explicit:

\[
\text{for every }\delta\in N,\quad \delta^2=-2,
\qquad
\{(v_i,\delta)}_{i=1}^k
\]

must have one weak sign and must not vanish identically.  Mixed signs mean
that \(\delta^\perp\) cuts (C^\circ).  Identical zero means that the whole
fixed space is trapped on an ambient root wall, so there is no invariant
ample class.

Necessity is immediate from the ambient root arrangement.  For sufficiency,
choose a generic point of (C^°) and the ambient Weyl chamber containing it.
The no-cut condition keeps all of (C) in the closure of that chamber.  The
old facet roots, which remain roots of (N), prevent its fixed section from
extending beyond (C).

A particularly short failure certificate consists of strict timelike points
(x_+,x_-\in C^°) with

\[
(x_+,\delta)>0>(x_-,\delta).
\]

Their positive linear combination on \(\delta^\perp\) is a strict timelike
interior point of the unwanted ambient wall.

## `H10/16`: the unique saturation is cut

The unique admissible nontrivial saturation is

\[
N=U\oplus E_8(-1)\oplus A_1(-1)^2,
\qquad [N:M]=2.
\]

In the archived saturation basis, take

```text
delta = (-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,0,0,1,1,0,1,-1).
```

It is a primitive vector of square (-2).  In the original (M\otimes
\mathbf Q) coordinates it is

```text
(-1,-1,-1,-1/2,-1/2,0,1/2,1,1/2,0,1,-1),
```

so it is genuinely new: it does not lie in (M).  Its action orbit consists
of two orthogonal roots, and the two roots restrict to the same linear form
on the fixed space.

On the complete list of 76 extreme rays, the pairing distribution is

```text
-7:1, -5:3, -4:2, -3:4, -2:5, -1:8, 0:30,
 1:8,  2:5,  3:4,  4:2,  5:3,  7:1.
```

Thus the signs are mixed.  More strongly, in the archived eleven-vector
fixed basis there is the exact strict point

```text
x0 = (296,610,966,457,889,854,835,870,281,321,0),
x0^2 = 422572,
min old-wall pairing = 16,
(x0,delta) = 0.
```

The construction also records strict timelike points on both sides, with
pairings (+1) and (-1).  Therefore `H10/16` is strictly refined by the
ambient K3 Weyl arrangement in its unique rank-12 saturation.

## `H11/29`: twelve facets fail the K3-wall norm gate

Here the chamber has full hyperbolic dimension eleven in a rank-12 Picard
lattice.  After primitive normalization in

\[
N=U\oplus D_8(-1)\oplus A_1(-1)^2,
\]

the facet-normal split is

```text
17 normals of square -2,
12 normals of square -4 and divisibility 2.
```

The square-(-4) facets are numbered

```text
1, 2, 7, 8, 9, 10, 13, 19, 21, 25, 28, 29.
```

A codimension-one K3 nef facet must lie in \(\delta^\perp\) for an integral
root \(\delta^2=-2\).  If this hyperplane equalled (n^\perp) for one of the
primitive square-(-4) normals, then \(\delta=c n\) with (c\in\mathbf Q),
and comparison of norms would give (c^2=1/2), impossible in
\(\mathbf Q\).  This obstruction is preserved by every lattice marking.

Thus the mixed 29-facet divisor-reflection chamber is definitively not the
full nef cone of its existing rank-12 carrier.

## Positive interpretation and next search rule

The exact boundary is now useful for inverse design:

1. Normalize every proposed facet in the eventual primitive Picard lattice.
   Reject any full-rank candidate having a primitive facet normal that is not
   proportional to a ((-2))-root.
2. Enumerate or otherwise control ambient ((-2))-roots and run the one-sign
   test on all chamber extreme rays.
3. Only after those gates pass, spend effort constructing the K3 family,
   proving generic (NS), and moving the chamber to the ample cone.

The already certified ambient complete-nef sections remain positive landing
objects: `H10/12` for (U+E_8(-1)+A_1(-1)^2) and `H10/15` for
(U+D_8(-1)+A_1(-1)^2).  The present theorem says precisely why the coarser
`H10/16`, `H10/45`, and mixed `H11/29` carriers do not replace those full-nef
objects.

The scope is limited to the unique/admissible rank-12 carriers enumerated
above.  It is not a worldwide nonexistence theorem for every unrelated
higher-Picard embedding.
