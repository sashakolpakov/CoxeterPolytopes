finite-automorphism K3 inventory pass Vinberg's literal finite-covolume
criterion.  In every case:

1. the supplied walls are the complete irreducible `(-2)`-curve list (or,

## Why this is stronger than the structural argument

For these K3 surfaces, finite automorphism group already implies that the
Weyl group has finite index in the arithmetic orthogonal group; equivalently,
the nef chamber has finite hyperbolic volume.  That gives a correct
algebro-geometric proof, but it does not itself enumerate the Coxeter
edge-endpoint incidences.

The present certificate applies the diagram criterion directly.  Its exact
generic verifier enumerates every crystallographic finite special subset,
retaining all rank-`n-1` edges and rank-`n` finite vertices.  Independently,
it reaches each irreducible affine component as the first nonfinite extension
of an elliptic proper subset, scans parallel affine `A1` pairs separately,
and then forms the component's full zero-neighbourhood.  A cusp is admitted
only when that entire neighbourhood is parabolic of rank `n-1`.  Finally,
each elliptic edge is checked to have endpoint count exactly two.

Thus the 1,119 cusps in the table are not arbitrary affine subdiagrams.  They
are the distinct valid full parabolic neighbourhoods obtained after all
4,177 connected affine-component audits.

## The rank-11/22 source gap and its exact repair

Thirteen of the fourteen negative curve Gram matrices were already frozen in
the project's Roulleau denominator artifacts.  The row

```text
U + D4(-1)^2 + A1(-1),  rho=11,  H10/22
```

was the exception.  A literal long-segment-as-chain reading of the TikZ graph
at source lines 8206--8318 gives a basis determinant `128`, whereas the named
lattice has determinant `32`; inserting the printed coordinates of `A13`
then gives square `-14`, not `-2`.  That transcription is therefore not used
as input and no entry is guessed.

Instead, the missing chamber is reconstructed directly on the standard
lattice `U+D4(-1)^2+A1(-1)`.  For a norm-minus-two root

```text
r = (a,b; v,w,z),   m=-(a+b)>0,   d=a-b,
```

the exact level equation is

```text
d^2 + 2|v|^2 + 2|w|^2 + 4z^2 = m^2 + 4,
d == m (mod 2).
```

Both `D4` factors are enumerated in the dominant chamber
`x1 >= x2 >= x3 >= |x4|`, with the even-coordinate-sum root-lattice
condition.  The complete level transcript is:

| control `m` | height `m^2/2` | dominant candidates | accepted simple roots |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | `1/2` | 3 | 3 |
| 2 | `2` | 12 | 0 |
| 3 | `9/2` | 18 | 0 |
| 4 | `8` | 54 | 9 |

Together with the ten height-zero walls this gives exactly 22 primitive
norm-minus-two simple roots.  Their Gram matrix has rank 11 and inertia
`(1,10,11)`.  Its literal Vinberg audit has 1,343 elliptic edges, 185 finite
vertices, 25 cusps, 72 connected affine audits, and no failure.  Vinberg's
finite-volume stopping theorem therefore proves that no later norm-two
simple root can occur.

This identifies the published row without relying on its problematic
coordinate lane: Roulleau's nef cone and the reconstructed cone are
fundamental chambers of the same norm-two reflection group `W_2` in the same
Picard lattice, hence are Weyl-isometric.

## Classification consequence

The earlier high-rank synthesis had literal Vinberg replays only for the five
ranks 15--19.  Combining those five frozen audits with this fourteen-row
theorem gives literal endpoint and full parabolic-neighbourhood certificates
for **all 19** members of the finite-volume class `K3RootFV[11,20]` in
hyperbolic dimensions 10 through 18.

This closes the literal polyhedral part of the high-rank unmarked
surface-to-polytope classification transfer.  It does not by itself enumerate
finite presentations of oriented monodromy groups, and it does not turn any
of these published chambers into a newly discovered abstract polytope.
