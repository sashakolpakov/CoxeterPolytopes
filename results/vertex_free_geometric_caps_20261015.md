# Exact walls found on edges with no finite endpoint

Four exact walls extend the fixed geometric H9 chamber from25 to29 actual facets. The first two cut the old strict escape; the third and fourth successively cut replacement escapes. Each wall is reconstructed from ten independent finite-contact equations, has squared norm2, retains the common timelike center, and satisfies every old Coxeter pair constraint. The first two are mutually perpendicular. **The29-wall chamber still has infinite volume**, certified by a new strict spacelike escape. No finite-volume example or novelty claim follows from these extensions.

## The part of the edge frontier previously missing

The25-wall cone has81 finite vertices and513 hyperideal extreme rays. The [complete line census](fresh_h9_vertex_free_edges_20261015.json) checks all2246 pairs of exterior rays sharing at least eight active walls:1679 do not give a finite-angle clique,534 have a nonpositive support Gram, and33 give positive-definite rank-eight supports. Each remaining two-dimensional cone contains a future timelike point strict against all walls outside its support, proving that its hyperbolic one-face is a complete geodesic with no finite endpoint. These faces are absent from a queue that starts only at finite vertices.

The [new polar-vertex frontier](fresh_h9_polar_endpoint_frontier_20261015.json) separately records all122 open germs at the28 vertices created by the six preceding polar caps. It has5514 finite spherical endpoint profiles and288 symbolic dihedral tails. The older113 open germs are inherited. Isolated I2(10) support components permit no nonright attachment: their normalized inverse diagonal is3+sqrt5>2. The effective inverse omits only entries multiplied by identically zero contacts.

A [separate arithmetic review](fresh_h9_vertex_free_edges_20261015_review.md) reproduces both complete frontiers, the pair bookkeeping and all profile domains. Its corrected metadata uses global wall indices throughout.
