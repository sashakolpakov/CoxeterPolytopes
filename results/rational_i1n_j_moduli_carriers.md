# A non-isotrivial family of rational `P12`--`P16` nef-cone carriers

## Positive result

The rational-surface carriers of Vinberg's odd-unimodular chambers are not
isolated constructions.  For

\[
C_a:\quad Y^2Z=X^3+aXZ^2+Z^3,
\]

put

\[
Q_a=Z^4+(X+Z)C_a,
\qquad
S_a=C_a^2+Z^2Q_a.
\]

For every `12 <= n <= 16`, the same free infinitely-near cluster used in
the inverse-design theorem can be followed on `C_a`, the contact-11 branch
of `Q_a`, and, for `n=16`, the selected branch of `S_a`.  On a nonempty
Zariski-open set of parameters, the resulting smooth rational surface
`X_n(a)` has

\[
\mathbf P(\operatorname{Nef}(X_n(a))\cap\mathcal C^+)
=P_n\subset\mathbb H^n.
\]

Thus each classical chamber `P12,...,P16` has a non-isotrivial family of
complete divisor-cone carriers.  In particular,

> `a=1`, `n=16` gives a second explicit `H16/20` carrier, non-isomorphic to
> the archived `a=0` surface, with the same complete finite-volume nef
> chamber `P16`.

The independent [symbolic cusp-family theorem](rational_i1n_symbolic_cusp_family.md)
applies uniformly because the facet classes, affine null cycles, and complete
jet ideals vary polynomially in `a`.  It reconstructs all 28 ideal rays and
proves that every one is semiample throughout the carrier-open locus:
27 move primitively and the last has exact semiample index four.  Its two
exact rank/section charts have coprime gates, so there is no additional
cusp-specific exceptional parameter.

The abstract Coxeter polytopes are Vinberg's classical examples; this is a
positive algebro-geometric carrier theorem, not an abstract-polytope novelty
claim.  Worldwide priority for the carrier family remains open.

## Why the cluster is uniform in `a`

The discriminant factor of `C_a` is `4a^3+27`.  When it is nonzero, `C_a`
is smooth; `p=[0:1:0]` is a flex because `Z=0` restricts to `-X^3`.

In the chart `Y=1`, write `x=X/Y`, `z=Z/Y`.  Then

```text
C_a = z - x^3 - a*x*z^2 - z^3.
```

The identities

```text
Q_a = z^4 + (x+z) C_a,
S_a = C_a^2 + z^2 Q_a
```

hold coefficientwise for every `a`.  The tangent cone of `Q_a` at `p` is
`z(x+z)`, so `Q_a` is nodal there.  Since `Q_a|C_a=z^4` and `z` has order
three on the flex cubic, the total contact is 12.  The branch tangent to
`x+z=0` is transverse to `C_a`, contributing one; the other branch has
contact 11.  Hence that selected branch and `C_a` share the first eleven
blow-up centres and then separate.

Similarly, `S_a|Q_a=C_a^2`; on the selected branch this gives contact 22.
The same divisorial-valuation calculation as in the original construction
therefore gives the parameter-independent multiplicity sequences

```text
C_a : (1^11),
Q_a : (2,1^14),
S_a : (2^7,1^9).
```

The certificate reruns absolute factorization over the algebraic closure for
the two explicit fibres `a=0,1`.  In both fibres, `C_a`, `Q_a`, and `S_a`
have exactly one nonconstant absolute factor of exponent one.  Geometric
integrality and smoothness are open conditions, so the construction applies
on a nonempty open parameter set.

## Why these are genuinely different surfaces

The elliptic curve `C_a` has

\[
j(C_a)=\frac{6912a^3}{4a^3+27}.
\]

The complete Mori-cone generator list has exactly one extremal curve of
arithmetic genus one: the strict transform of `C_a`.  All exceptional-chain
and line generators have genus zero; the quartic and sextic generators have
genera two and three.  Any surface isomorphism must therefore carry `C_a`
to the unique genus-one extremal curve on the other surface and preserve its
`j`-invariant.

For the two exact fibres,

```text
a=0: j=0,
a=1: j=6912/31.
```

Hence `X_n(0)` and `X_n(1)` are non-isomorphic for each `12<=n<=16`.
Because `j(a)` is nonconstant, the open family contains infinitely many
pairwise non-isomorphic carriers.

## Algebraic meaning of the cusp orbits

The certificate also enumerates every primitive nef extreme ray.  In every
dimension `12` through `16`, two ideal vertices have direct semiample
interpretations:

```text
H-E_1                    rational pencil of lines through p,
3H-E_1-...-E_9           genus-one pencil <C_a,3L>.
```

Both pencils remain basepoint-free after the later blow-ups because they are
pulled back from morphisms already obtained after resolving their original
base cluster.  This family certificate proves those two cases directly from
the defining polynomials.  The separate exact affine-cycle and plane-jet
certificate strengthens the result to all 28 cusps.  It proves the
multi-component pencils and then supplies coprime second sections for five
formerly open one-component systems.  The `P16` genus-three class is rigid
in degrees one through three, but `|4F|` is an exact base-point-free pencil;
the semiample index is exactly four.  The symbolic certificate upgrades the
two explicit `a=0,1` replays to every parameter in the carrier-open family.
For example, `P16` has twelve ideal rays in total, with
arithmetic genera

```text
0,1,2,3,3,4,5,6,6,7,10,11.
```

This is a concrete algebro-geometric translation of the finite-volume
boundary: every cusp ray produces a rational, elliptic, or higher-genus
fibration.  Twenty-seven move in their primitive classes and one in its
fourth multiple.

## Claim boundary

What is new here is the non-isotrivial divisor-cone realization and the
second explicit `j=6912/31` `P16` carrier.  Nothing here changes the abstract
lineage: `P12` through `P16`, their lattices, their reflection groups, and
their hyperbolic volumes were already known.  No worldwide first-carrier
claim is made without a broader historical audit.
