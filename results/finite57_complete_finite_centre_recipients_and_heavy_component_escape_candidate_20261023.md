## 6. A closed all-heavy centre component is impossible

endpoint is heavy. No connected component is closed under all three
centre moves. This is an actual finite-volume obstruction, not the
assertion that a local right-angled cube is the whole face.

and heavy. Finite volume then makes F compact: an unbounded direction
would yield an ideal vertex of this finite-type face.

The facets of F have three well-defined colours given by their fixed
contact rows on the three W3 slots. At any vertex the three colours
are distinct. Old centres from different slots are orthogonal, and
their projections onto W3 lie in orthogonal blocks. Thus the induced
three-dimensional facet normals are pairwise orthogonal there. Every
dihedral angle and every two-dimensional face angle of F is right.
