# Arithmetic dimension19: every farthest vertex has whole diagram D19

arithmetic paper. No computation, Lean or TeX run, manuscript edit,
new example or dimension-bound promotion.

**Theorem.** Let P be a compact arithmetic first-type hyperbolic
Coxeter19-polytope over ANY actual admissible ground field. For EVERY
strict interior point z and EVERY vertex p farthest from z, the entire
finite diagram at p is D19. There is no spectator component.

**Proof.** Fix z and p as in the statement. The complete two-diagram
frontier applies to this dimension, this same farthest vertex and every
actual admissible first-type field. It leaves precisely whole A19 and
whole D19. The whole-A19 theorem has exactly these hypotheses, including
the absence of spectators, and excludes the former possibility. The
entire diagram must therefore be D19. Since z and the choice of a
farthest vertex were arbitrary, the asserted quantifiers follow.

This necessary condition does not construct a D19 polytope or exclude
one. In particular the accepted compact arithmetic first-type ceiling
remains19; no ceiling18 or stronger general compact theorem follows.
No assertion that every vertex is farthest for some interior point is
made. The six completed manuscript/PDF editions and their old formal
bindings remain unchanged. The new isolated-dotted-centre and
single-fork-helper lemmas are not inputs to this composition.
