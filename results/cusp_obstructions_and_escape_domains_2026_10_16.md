## H20: a completed cusp, a complete obstruction and an off-cusp cutter

completes the previously unbounded link at nu to affine G2 + affine A17.
Its two positive affine relations have Euclidean ranks 2+17=19 at the
same future null direction. The [independent review](fresh_h20_affine_a17_completion_20261016_review.md)
reconstructs all 685 new pair gates, all 140 actual facets, the four
proper cusps, and the complete exclusion of new finite vertices. The
old strict escape E1 survives.

Changing only the through-nu walls cannot cut E1. The
[complete contact reduction](fresh_h20_null_link_profiles_20261016.md)
and its [independent exact-tower census](fresh_h20_null_link_profiles_20261016_review.md)
cover 125 finite profiles and two continuous tails, without a finite
label cutoff. The [resulting dual theorem](fresh_h20_null_link_escape_gate_20261016.md)
has a [separate independent physical and dual review](fresh_h20_null_link_escape_gate_20261016_review.md).
All 2,032 domains are covered: 1,739 exact infeasibility certificates,
292 strict negative E1 bounds and one exact zero-line exclusion. The
unbounded height cancels by an exact defining equation, not a small
numerical residual. No old-center-preservation condition is imposed.
The theorem applies only to walls through this nu while retaining the
135-wall core; it does not exclude walls away from the cusp.

That remaining class already supplies a concrete cutter. The
[rational off-cusp cap](fresh_h20_off_cusp_cap_20261016.md) is

```
r = 8*x - (3/2)*e - (77/10)*f - (14/5)*omega17,
r^2 = 73/50,                 r.E1 = 961/1000 > 0.
```

Its [independent review](fresh_h20_off_cusp_cap_20261016_review.md)
checks 17 right and 123 dotted old contacts, all 141 actual facets and
preservation of all four proper cusps. No new finite vertex is created;
the previously certified lower bound of 170 survives. A different
strict escape E2 has square 427500061/5000000000>0 and new-wall pairing
-19/1000. Thus this is a coherent infinite-volume extension, not a new
finite-volume example. Mixed contacts involving both isolated support
directions are a next search target.

## H9: rejecting an uncompletable cluster before continuing

The [first three-cap cluster](fresh_nonarithmetic_escape49_20261016.md)
cuts the preceding escape but introduces a permanent obstruction. Five
walls suffice: an affine G2 positive-null circuit is orthogonal to a
retained pi/5 pair. Every future acute addition preserves that ideal
direction. Finite volume would require a bounded Euclidean link whose
reflection product has irrational trace 5+phi, contradicting its full
translation lattice. This uses the
[previously reviewed cusp argument](fresh_h9_fixed_core_cusp_obstruction_20261013.md).
The primary exact five-wall certificate and complete 52-wall cone pass
generation and replay. The latter has 178 finite, one ideal and 2,278
exterior extreme rays; it has not received a separate independent
52-wall reconstruction. The obstruction is stronger than that census
and rules out further acute completion retaining its five anchors.

The [independent retained-49 inventory](fresh_h9_persistent_cusp_screen_20261016.md)
checks every pair and every possible irreducible affine circuit. All
630 connected admissible subsets are positive definite. There are no
old affine circuits, including in the common-orthogonal domains of all
40 forbidden-angle edges. Therefore the old chamber itself is not
excluded by this particular cusp obstruction.

A [filtered replacement cluster](fresh_nonarithmetic_affine_filtered_20261016.md)
returns to that frozen 49-wall chamber and adds three different caps.
Its complete primary cone has 178 finite, zero ideal and 2,279 exterior
extreme rays, and an exact renewed strict escape. All 52 facets are
actual. The complete new affine gate enumerates 14 connected possible
standard-label subsets, each with nonzero exact determinant; combined
with the old inventory, the new chamber has no affine circuit.
These new local and complete-cone checks have saved producer replay;
they are not an independent second 52-wall implementation.

Each new cap has an actual pi/10 ridge but no finite vertex. The
[reviewed forbidden-label ridge lemma](fresh_h20_weighted_binary_20261014_dotted_completion_review.md)
therefore forces any finite-volume continuation to add a wall meeting
each cap. Purely dotted additions cannot suffice. The old 1,846-proposal
bank contains no numerical finite-angle partner for these three caps;
that is a bounded diagnostic, not a general exclusion. New ridge
contacts and compatible escape cutters remain to be found.

The [subsequent ridge gate](fresh_nonarithmetic_pi10_ridge_20261016.md)
also passes exact generation and replay. A literal basis expansion of
the renewed escape has positive coefficients on nine retained normals
and a negative coefficient only on wall 33. Hence every nonzero acute
added wall right to wall 33 has strictly negative escape pairing. This
is an all-real statement, not a bounded label search. It does not remove
the need to close the pi/10 ridges; it shows that such right-to-33 ridge
walls need a separate escape cutter. Its companion numerical pilot
found 477 acute branches, all noncutting, and is labeled as discovery
only. The new basis-expansion gate is a primary exact certificate.

[global rescaling diagnostic](fresh_sqrt5_marked_old_bonus_scaling_20261016.md)

model while preserving the old quadrilateral obligations. The latter
still has minimum approximately 42.875.

The blocking faces explicitly identify alternative corner payments,
including a previously omitted same-I2(5)-hub family. A fresh model may
move payment among those corners rather than only increasing old
bonuses. These five diagnostic packages pass exact generation and saved
replay; they are primary results, not separately independently reviewed
new dimension proofs. Any successful future rule still needs complete
coverage of every newly affected face.
