# A K3-Realizable Rank-10 Form of Discriminant 11

Status: exact field-form and K3-lattice construction.  The fixed
real-multiplication eigenspace misses the nef cone, while an exact integral
conjugate has a nonempty ample section in Shimada chamber 68.  Wall
completeness and abstract Coxeter reflectivity remain open.

## Field form

Let \(F=\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\) and define the rank-10
\(\mathbb Z[\phi]\)-lattice

\[
H_{11}=
\begin{pmatrix}
-2\phi&1\\
1&2
\end{pmatrix}
\oplus E_8.
\]

Exact inertia calculation gives signature \((9,1)\) at the identity embedding
and \((10,0)\) at the conjugate embedding.  Its determinant is
\(-1-4\phi\).  Thus it has the real-place signatures required of a compact
arithmetic hyperbolic 9-lattice of simplest type.

The coupling \(1\) is essential.  The orthogonal extension family fails the
K3 discriminant-length gate, whereas this single off-diagonal entry glues away
the excess 2-primary discriminant.

## Trace Néron--Severi lattice

For the totally negative codifferent scalar

\[
\alpha=-\phi/\sqrt5,
\]

the trace pairing is even integral and has signature \((1,19)\) and
determinant \(-11\).  A determinant-\(-1\) basis change verifies

\[
N_{11}\simeq
U\oplus T_{11}(-1)\oplus E_8(-1)^{\oplus2},
\qquad
T_{11}=\begin{pmatrix}18&5\\5&2\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The positive binary lattice is integrally equivalent to its reduced form

\[
T_{11}^{\mathrm{red}}=
\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&6\end{pmatrix},
\]

of determinant 11.  Consequently \(A_{N_{11}}\simeq\mathbb Z/11\mathbb Z\).

Multiplication by \(\phi\) on the underlying rank-20 lattice is supplied as an
exact integral matrix.  The certificate verifies

\[
M_\phi^2=M_\phi+I,
\qquad M_\phi^{\mathsf T}B=BM_\phi,
\]

so the required self-adjoint \(\mathcal O_F\)-marking is genuinely present.

## Singular K3 realization

The script gives an explicit primitive embedding

\[
N_{11}\hookrightarrow
\Lambda_{K3}=U^{\oplus3}\oplus E_8(-1)^{\oplus2}
\]

and verifies that its orthogonal complement has Gram matrix \(T_{11}\).  The
18 Néron--Severi columns together with two additional columns extend to a
determinant-\(-1\) integral basis, so primitivity is certified directly.

By the Shioda--Inose classification, the oriented positive even binary lattice
\(T_{11}\) determines a singular K3 surface.  Its Néron--Severi lattice is
\(N_{11}\).  More explicitly, the reduced form has CM discriminant \(-11\),
whose singular modulus is \(j=-32768\).  The standard Inose pencil gives the
surface over \(\mathbb Q\)

\[
y^2=x^3-\frac{64}{3}t^4x
+t^5\left(t^2-\frac{1078}{27}t+1\right).
\]

The exact Weierstrass discriminant is

\[
\Delta(t)=-16t^{10}(t-1)^2(27t^2-2102t+27).
\]

Thus the fibration has two \(\mathrm{II}^*\) fibers, one \(I_2\) fiber, and two
\(I_1\) fibers; their Euler numbers sum to 24.  Shioda--Tate gives
Mordell--Weil rank 1.  The equation and factorization are independently
generated by `scripts/discriminant11_inose_model.py`.

## Published nef-cone data for the same surface

Shimada's discriminant-11 singular K3 has the same reduced transcendental
lattice \(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}2&1\\1&6\end{smallmatrix}\right)\).
Consequently his automorphism and nef-cone computation applies to this exact
surface.  An exact basis translation imports the 347 published
\((-2)\)-curve representatives into our real-multiplication trace basis.

Only 17 representatives survive positivity at both real places, and all 17
are integral field-reflection roots: eight have field norm \(2\), while nine
have norm \(4-2\phi\).  Their field span has rank 9.  Applying each of the 767
published automorphism generators once gives 2,567 distinct effective
\((-2)\)-classes but no additional surviving field root.  None of the 767
displayed generators individually centralizes multiplication by \(\phi\).

More strongly, two of Shimada's oriented effective \((-2)\)-classes have
timelike projections in opposite components of the identity-eigenspace
positive cone.  Reverse Lorentzian Cauchy--Schwarz then shows that every
positive eigenvector violates at least one of their nef inequalities.  Hence
the eigen-nef section for this fixed marking is empty.  The two exact vectors,
pairings, source indices, and regression test are given in
`results/shimada_disc11_rm_filter.md`.

An exact integral conjugation now moves the identity eigenspace through the
interior of Shimada chamber 68.  The 28 defining inequalities are strictly
positive on an archived positive witness.  Its 17 local curve walls all pass
the field-reflection filter, while the 347 published curve representatives
give 18 survivors of full field span rank 10.  See
`results/shimada_disc11_conjugate_marking.md`.

## Remaining Coxeter problem

An exact norm-two Vinberg run exhausts every height with control coordinate
\(0<x\leq20\), and a resumable exact continuation exhausts the next
admissible height \(x=6+9\phi\), producing 97 walls in total.  Its present
cone has an exact spacelike extreme ray and is therefore not finite-volume.
All 97 walls have also been transported to distinct paired rational-curve
facets of the nonempty K3 eigen-nef section.  These bounded results and their
precise stopping gap are recorded in `results/h11_vinberg_prefix.md` and
`results/h11_k3_weyl_frame.md`.

This result constructs a rigid algebraic-geometric carrier, excludes its fixed
marking, and supplies a nonempty conjugate marking.  It does not yet decide:

1. the complete restricted-wall set of the nonempty section;
2. whether its complete section has finite volume or is compact;
3. whether all complete facets are \(\mathcal O_F\)-reflection walls at Coxeter
   angles; or
4. whether the abstract field lattice is reflective independently of K3
   geometry.

The status is therefore
`conjugate_marking_eigen_ample_nonempty_wall_completeness_undecided`, not
`compact_reflective`.

The first wall reduction is nevertheless finite.  If
\(h(\delta,\delta)=a+b\phi\) is the field norm of a trace-lattice
\((-2)\)-class and its wall meets the identity eigen-positive cone, then

\[
a+b=2,
\qquad h(\delta,\delta)>0
\quad\hbox{at both real embeddings}.
\]

These exact conditions force its norm into the five-element set

\[
\{5-3\phi,\;4-2\phi,\;3-\phi,\;2,\;1+\phi\}.
\]

For each enumerated vector one must additionally test
\(2h(M,\delta)/h(\delta,\delta)\subseteq\mathbb Z[\phi]\).  The shell and
reflection-integrality tests are implemented in
`src/coxeter_polytopes/geometric/k3.py` and tested independently.  For a new
or Weyl-conjugate marking, the opposite-timecone effective-wall test must run
before any five-shell enumeration.  Only a marking passing this nonemptiness
gate should receive a justified height-bounded shell search.
