# K3 Embedding Obstruction for the Natural Rank-10 Seed

## Seed and trace form

Consider the proposed dimension-9 extension

\[
L_9^{(0)}=\langle-2\phi\rangle\oplus E_8\oplus A_1
\]

over \(\mathbb Z[\phi]\), and again take
\(\alpha=-\phi/\sqrt5\).  Exact restriction of scalars gives

\[
N_9=\bigl(L_9^{(0)},
\operatorname{Tr}_{F/\mathbb Q}(\alpha(\ ,\ ))\bigr)
\simeq
\langle2\rangle\oplus\langle-2\rangle^{\oplus3}
\oplus E_8(-1)^{\oplus2}.
\]

The supplied determinant-one change of basis verifies that \(N_9\) is even,
has signature \((1,19)\), and has determinant \(-16\).

## Obstruction

Its discriminant group is

\[
A_{N_9}=N_9^*/N_9\simeq(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^4.
\]

Suppose \(N_9\) embedded primitively in the even unimodular K3 lattice
\(\Lambda_{K3}\), of rank 22.  The orthogonal complement \(T=N_9^\perp\)
would have rank 2, and unimodular gluing would give an isomorphism
\(A_T\simeq A_{N_9}\) (with opposite discriminant quadratic form).

For any rank-2 integral lattice, \(A_T\) is a quotient of \(\mathbb Z^2\), so
it can be generated by at most two elements.  The group
\((\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^4\) needs four.  This is impossible.

Therefore \(N_9\) cannot be the Néron--Severi lattice of a complex
Picard-rank-20 K3 surface, and the natural \(E_8\oplus A_1\) extension is
excluded from the proposed K3 eigen-nef construction before any Vinberg wall
enumeration.

This is a scoped obstruction to this trace realization, not a proof that
\(L_9^{(0)}\) is nonreflective and not a non-existence theorem for compact
Coxeter 9-polytopes.
