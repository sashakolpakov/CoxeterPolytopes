# Characteristic-5 K3: the published D0--D2 groups give seven H13 no-go casts

## Result

The three period-preserving groups explicitly archived for the characteristic-5
supersingular K3 surface yield exactly seven inequivalent subgroup-fixed slices
above H^9.  All seven have dimension H^13, and every one fails the Coxeter-angle
gate by a pair of independently certified genuine facets.

This is a rigorous bounded negative result, not a new hyperbolic example and not
a theorem about the full lattice chamber stabilizers.

| chamber | walls | archived group | rank-14 involutions | classes | forbidden ratios |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| D0 | 16,302 | 756,000 | 525 | 1 | `5/16` |
| D1 | 7,832 | 20,160 | 315 | 2 | `5/8`, `5/12` |
| D2 | 2,534 | 1,152 | 99 | 4 | `5/8`, `5/12`, `5/12`, `5/8` |

No Vinberg compactness or finite-volume test was run: a failed Coxeter-angle
gate cannot define a Coxeter polyhedron, so volume language would be invalid.

## Exhaustive high-rank reduction

For every archived product `SAutXD[i][s] * TAutXD[i][t]`, the program reduces
`g-I` modulo 1009.  Since

`rank_Q(g-I) >= rank_F_1009(g-I)`, 

the modular computation gives an exact upper bound on the rational fixed rank.
Only the identity and trace-6 involutions survive above rank 10.  The latter
have fixed rank exactly 14 because an involution on a 22-dimensional space with
trace 6 has a 14-dimensional `+1` eigenspace.

The complete survivor counts are:

- D0: identity plus 525 rank-14 involutions;
- D1: identity plus 315 rank-14 involutions;
- D2: identity plus 99 rank-14 involutions.

The same exact modular calculation on every unordered pair of distinct
rank-14 involutions gives:

| chamber | pair fixed-rank upper-bound histogram | maximum |
|---|---|---:|
| D0 | `6:69300, 7:31500, 8:31500, 10:5250` | 10 |
| D1 | `6:18480, 7:17640, 8:8400, 10:4935` | 10 |
| D2 | `6:864, 7:1800, 8:1440, 10:747` | 10 |

Consequently, a subgroup with fixed rank greater than 10 cannot contain two
distinct nonidentity elements.  It must be generated by one of the rank-14
involutions.  Exact centralizer counts and wall-block fixed-point signatures
split these into `1 + 2 + 4 = 7` conjugacy classes.

## Genuine-facet obstruction

The wall rows are dual covectors.  For each involution, its inverse-transpose
action is derived on every archived wall, and the sum over each one- or
two-element orbit is the restricted wall normal `n`.  Pairings use the integral
dual form `H = 5 GramSX^-1`.

Let `w` be the chamber's invariant interior Weyl covector, put

```text
q = n H n^T,       p = w H n^T,
W = (-q) w + p n.
```

For each of the fourteen walls used in the obstruction, exact integer
arithmetic verifies

```text
W H n^T = 0,
W H W^T > 0,
W H n_j^T > 0 for every other restricted inequality n_j.
```

Thus `W` is a timelike point in the relative interior of that wall: the wall is
a genuine facet, not a redundant candidate.  The two certified facets in each
cast have normalized squared product `5/16`, `5/12`, or `5/8`.  A rational
value `cos(pi/m)^2` can only be `0`, `1/4`, `1/2`, `3/4`, or `1`; hence all
three observed values are forbidden.

## Exact scope and why this lane now stops

The source distinguishes

`Aut(D) = {g in O^+(S_X) : Dg = D}`

from the period-preserving group

`Aut_X(D) = Aut(D) intersect G_X`.

Only factorizations named `SAutXD[i]`, `TAutXD[i]` are published.  Therefore
this computation exhausts the three archived `Aut_X(D_i)` groups; it does not
pretend that a geometric automorphism condition is part of the desired general
construction, and it does not claim to exhaust the unreported `Aut(D_i)`.

The paper also reports 12 chamber classes at level 2, 328 at level 3, and more
than 6000 found at level 4 before that enumeration was abandoned.  Full wall
and lattice-stabilizer matrices for those chambers are absent.  That is the
precise data obstruction, so this reservoir is parked rather than overclaimed.
