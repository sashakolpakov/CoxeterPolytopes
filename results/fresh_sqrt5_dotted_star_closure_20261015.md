# Fixed-distance dotted attachment closure

The [checker](../scripts/fresh_sqrt5_dotted_star_closure_20261015.py) closes
the attachment queue of all 27 necessary arithmetic completions in the
[missing-entry rank calculation](fresh_sqrt5_dotted_completion_20261015.md).
The [certificate](fresh_sqrt5_dotted_star_closure_20261015.json) has 30 marked
states: 26 sealed states and four states with one accepted next contact each.
All four children occur in the same closed queue. No rank or length cutoff
is used.

Across the complete finite-corner/no-short-path contact domains, 307 trials
force a second negative direction, 64 fail the finite-edge field condition,
60 fail the dotted-edge field condition, thirteen contain an exact affine
principal diagram, and six create a persistent H4 payment. The remaining
four are exactly the saved child maps.

The squared normalized dotted inner product stays fixed along each branch.
The checker reconstructs a ground-field Gram matrix for every proposed
extension, checks physical and conjugate inertia, and checks integral cyclic
products for every survivor. Finite paths joining the dotted endpoints force
the corresponding scaled dotted entry to lie in the ground field. When
there is no finite path, scaling an entire finite component gives an exact
matrix in that field.

Sealing is conditional on remaining unpaid by the persistent baseline rule.
In such a completion every external white root is orthogonal to a sealed
core, so its corner components remain entire. The four nonsealed starts
also need payment in the case of no further touching root. An additional
root that does touch must follow a saved child. Thus paying all thirty
displayed zero-contact cases would pay this specified seed family under
arbitrary attachments.

This package does not classify other dotted distances or all possible
nonsingular intermediate cores. It does not claim that the principal
matrices realize compact faces. Generation and saved replay pass;
independent review and the new face-payment constraints remain separate
obligations. No dimension bound follows from this closure alone.
