# Mirror lattices of the two characteristic-three H11 examples

## Cyclic 206-wall chamber

All 206 primitive mirrors act integrally on the saturated fixed divisor
lattice.  Their exact square/divisibility types are

| type | count |
|---|---:|
| `(-2,1)` | 12 |
| `(-2,2)` | 164 |
| `(-4,2)` | 30 |

Every reflection matrix is integral, involutive and preserves the fixed
Gram.  Hence the 206-wall Coxeter group is literally a reflection subgroup
of the integral orthogonal group of the fixed lattice.  The latter is the
even 2-elementary lattice with invariants `(1,11,a=10,delta=1)`, hence
`U + E8(-2) + A1(-2)^2` by Nikulin's classification.  Explicit coordinates
in a chosen standard target basis and the bridge to Alexeev's full 14-wall
group are given in the
[`206-wall lineage report`](ss_fermat_char3_c2_h11_lineage.md).

## Noncyclic 328-wall chamber

The primitive mirrors in the saturated fixed divisor lattice have types

| type | integral reflections | nonintegral reflections |
|---|---:|---:|
| `(-2,1)` | 16 | 0 |
| `(-4,2)` | 24 | 0 |
| `(-6,3)` | 96 | 0 |
| `(-8,4)` | 12 | 0 |
| `(-24,12)` | 144 | 0 |
| `(-24,6)` | 0 | 36 |

Thus the polytope is valid, but its reflection group is not contained in the
orthogonal group of the saturated fixed divisor lattice: precisely the 36
`(-24,6)` reflections have denominator two.

There is nevertheless a canonical minimal rational reflection-stable
overlattice.  Starting with `L_fixed`, adjoin every `s(L_fixed)` for the 328
facet reflections.  The one-step closure is already stable.  It contains
`L_fixed` with index four, is even integral of signature `(1,11)`, has
determinant 48 and Smith diagonal

`1^8,2^3,6`.

Minimality is literal: every reflection-stable lattice containing `L_fixed`
must contain the one-step closure, while the displayed closure is itself
stable.  In its primitive coordinates all reflections are integral and the
mirror types collapse to

| type | count |
|---|---:|
| `(-2,1)` | 28 |
| `(-4,2)` | 24 |
| `(-6,3)` | 276 |

Since the Coxeter group has finite covolume and preserves this integral
Lorentzian lattice, it is arithmetic over `Q`.  A subsequent explicit
unimodular isometry identifies this determinant-48 stable lattice with
Scharlau--Kirschmer List11/Gram11 entry 20 and places the 328-wall group
inside its known 22-wall full integral reflection group; see the
[`328-wall lineage report`](ss_fermat_char3_v4_h11_lineage.md).
