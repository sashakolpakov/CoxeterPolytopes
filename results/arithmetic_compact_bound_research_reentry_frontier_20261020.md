# Arithmetic compact bounds: mathematical re-entry frontier

20 October 2026. Read-only research triage under the resumed mathematical
checkpoint. No scientific job, enumeration, Lean, TeX or manuscript edit.
No new dimension bound or example is claimed. The parked nine-field 19
proof is preserved and is not the proposed research programme.

## Verdict

The useful new route is to combine the **same-vector farthest-component
budget with arithmetic restrictions on its actual opposite walls**. There
is no small deduction of a uniform ceiling 15–18 from the accepted results.
The main obstruction is already present at a whole A20 farthest vertex,
before any spectator bookkeeping. More distance-two refunds in the parked
eight/nine-field calculation do not address this obstruction uniformly.

A precise sufficiently strong target for ceiling 18 is:

> In a compact arithmetic first-type chamber, every ordinary whole finite
> component of rank r at a farthest vertex has actual projection energy
> greater than r/18, with arbitrary finite spectators; retain the already
> proved alternatives of one isolated short A1 or one isolated rank-two 7
> defect of energy greater than 9/100.

Then n>=19 is impossible: discarding the possible short A1 leaves energy
`>(n-1)/18>=1`; the rank-two defect gives
`>9/100+(n-2)/18>=931/900>1`. The existing ordinary rates already exceed
r/18. The genuinely new ranges would be A10–A20, B15–B20 and D14–D20.
In particular a rank-18-or-larger component would have to be **excluded**,
not merely assigned a slightly better energy estimate. This is a useful
uniform target, not an established lemma or an easy perturbation of 1/14.

## What the current dimension-20 frontier actually is

Use the accepted compact farthest normalization: one actual vector x with
norm squared below one, additive whole-component energies, ordinary rate
rank/14, and the mutually incompatible small defects. A dominant component
is A_r with r>=7 or B_r/D_r with r>=9. In dimension 20 it exists and is
unique: without one the ordinary budget already exceeds one; two each
cost more than 1/2. A dominant A leaves spectator rank at most 7, a dominant
B/D at most 5.

Applying the accepted **generic prefix table**, not a new computation,
gives the following necessary remaining alternatives:

This is a necessary energy frontier, not a catalogue of realizable
polytopes or a complete new contact classification. The B15/B16/B17

their 5/4/3 spectator ranks contribute more than 4/14,3/14,2/14, so all

## A concrete obstruction to a purely local arithmetic shortcut

Consider the forced **whole A_r plus isolated endpoint-5 wall** model,
r>=4, in norm-two normalization. Let B_r be the A_r Cartan Gram and append
a root c of square two with sole product `c.alpha_1=-phi`, where
`phi^2=phi+1`. The full Gram is

```text
G_r = [[B_r, -phi*e1], [-phi*e1^t, 2]].
```

Its Schur complement is
`2-phi^2*r/(r+1)=(r+2-r*phi)/(r+1)`, negative at the physical embedding
for r>=4. At the other golden embedding, `phi'^2=2-phi<1`, so the Schur
complement is positive. Therefore G_r is physically Lorentzian and
conjugately positive definite. Every root reflection is integral on its
O_phi root lattice: with diagonal two its matrix coefficients use the
integral Gram entries directly. The actual cyclic field is Q(phi).

Deleting alpha_1 gives A_(r-1)+A1, and deleting alpha_2 gives
A_(r-2)+I2(5), both positive definite. Thus the common-opposite edge-1/2
replacement tests also pass. This does **not** produce a compact chamber,
an arithmetic reflection lattice of finite covolume, or a farthest-vector
realization. It shows exactly why native labels, conjugate positivity and
reflection integrality alone do not eliminate this important A-end profile.

The full determinant is `r+2-r*phi`, of rational norm
`5-(r-1)^2`. At r=18,19,20 these norms are -284,-319,-356. The model is not
identified with a previously closed literal host by these numbers. A
similarity, exact lattice containment and full-chamber implication would
all need proofs. In particular the published golden diagonal cutoff and
the accepted whole-D isolated-five lattice theorem cannot simply be
applied to this A-root lattice.

## Concrete next subproblem and admission rule

The highest-value next **paper** subproblem is an arithmetic version of
the large-A opposite-wall analysis, beginning with the family G_r above:

> Does every invariant O_phi lattice compatible with the A_r Weyl group
> and its isolated endpoint-5 reflector, for all r>=18, force a full
> integral reflection arrangement that cannot have a compact chamber?
> Prove the lattice/group bridge or identify precisely why it fails.

This concerns a profile forced by the current farthest-wall inventories,
not another guessed construction host. It could remove one stubborn
family simultaneously in the relevant ranks. It still would not prove
the component-rate target or any ambient ceiling: adjacent-contact L,
central H/C and distinct-opposite branches, plus arbitrary spectators,
would remain. The first deliverable should be an exact quadratic/lattice
identification or obstruction, before authorizing a root/Pell/edge search
or a long residue classification. If it does not connect to a uniform
large-A energy improvement, stop rather than manufacture another local
19-to-19 refinement.

For broader uniform coverage, the existing arithmetic Lanner-field input
can first lock the finite retained A profiles to their actual fields
(endpoint-5 to golden, relevant 4-cycle profiles to silver). Their
opposite-wall coexistence must then use exact cyclic integrality and
conjugate signatures. Mixed-field incompatibility is useful, but the
same-field low-pressure alternatives must still be eliminated. Current
common-pair forcing proofs have specified energy thresholds; their
coverage may not be extrapolated to a proposed r/18 threshold.

A new compact 9 example remains valuable, but the closed literal golden,
silver, cubic, quadratic13 and biquadratic hosts must not be reopened.
The finite golden-family cutoff is not permission for a host-by-host
search. This triage identifies no verified new construction lead superior
to the structural arithmetic/farthest question above.
