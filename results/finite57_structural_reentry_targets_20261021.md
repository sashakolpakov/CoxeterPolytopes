## Two exact limits on counting-only improvements

**Local shape sharpness.** For EVERY product of simplices
`L=product Delta^(r_i)`, the uncut-edge lower bound `E0>=G(L)` is
attainable by a linear section. On factor i prescribe its ordered vertex
heights0,1,...,r_i; this is an affine function because its vertices are
affinely independent. On the product add these functions. A product
vertex of height j has exactly j downward edges, so its index is j.
Cut at the midpoint of the total height range, with a small generic
perturbation. Below the cut the contribution is j; above it is d-j.
Their sum is exactly `sum h_j min(j,d-j)=G`. At the middle index either
choice has the same contribution. A sufficiently small perturbation
retains all other index/side assignments.

Consequently no positive per-link improvement to kappa follows from
the product shape and an arbitrary section alone. A stronger global
inequality would need coupling between the sections at different actual
vertices. The extrema supply a positive constant, not a per-vertex refund
for an unrestricted number of costly vertices or negative-margin cusps.

**Changing5 by linear interpolation does not help.** For t>=5, the
automatic rule `w_t=(t/5)w+(t-5)/5` pays every face at least t-s if
w pays triangles2 and quadrilaterals1. At a vertex with E link edges,
`C_t=(t/5)C+(t-5)E/5`, while `kappa_t=tG/2-E`. Hence exactly

`kappa_t-C_t=(t/5)*(kappa_5-C)`.

This entire change of face functional preserves the sign of every
obstruction. A useful pentagon or other-face argument must add new
geometry, not merely this interpolation.

## A uniform triangle lemma already follows analytically

**Lemma.** In any actual triangular two-face, a varying affine A cycle
of order m>=10 cannot have marked cyclic distance at least5. Arbitrary
supporting roots and other cusp factors are allowed.

Let a,b be the marks and c the third side. Both `(F-a)+c` and
`(F-b)+c` are positive semidefinite principal diagrams. Deleting a
cycle node gives A_(m-1), whose norm-two inverse diagonal is
`k(m-k)/m`. For every3<=k<=m-3 this exceeds2, since its minimum is
`3(m-3)/m>=21/10`. Nonnegative inverse entries and native contact
magnitudes>=1 therefore allow c to contact only nodes within two
steps of the deleted mark. The radius-two neighborhoods of a,b are
disjoint. Each mark is forbidden by the other deletion as well:
its inverse diagonal is at least `5(m-5)/m>=5/2`.
Thus c is orthogonal to F. The positive affine kernel represents
the actual ideal corner, whereas the third side has strictly positive
height there. Contradiction.

This generalizes the manuscript's order10/11 triangle exclusion to
all orders and pairs, without a core census. It is not yet a payment
reduction: quadrilaterals still use those distance-five entries. Nor
does an A-only reduction improve an all-family B/C/D envelope.

## A material single target: a uniform7/4 quadrilateral transfer

The promising geometric target is ONE all-rank quadrilateral theorem
with the following intended budget, rather than separate midrank
coupon patches:

* finite same-component payments need only distance<=7, the existing
  deep deletions and essential protected leaf roles; retain explicitly
  justified cross/context payments within a global finite budget<=6n;
* affine factors of rank r have total charge at most
  `(r+1)*min(r/2,4)`, including all current small exceptional factors.

The second envelope is the count of an ideal distance-four rule:
for sufficiently large order m its classical pair counts are4m in A,
4m-9 in B,4m-10 in C,4m-8 in D. Small factors of order<=9 have at
most all pairs, giving r/2. A geometric theorem is required; the pair
counts do not prove face coverage, and existing protected distance5--7
triangle roles cannot simply be erased. The lemma above removes the
whole affine-A distance-five triangle obstruction. The finite-distance7
triangle theorem is already an accepted input, so the main genuinely
new obligation is uniform quadrilateral routing and its compensations.

The existing affine point-potential framework supplies an exact route.
After deleting a mark, native contacts have principal cost<=2, so the
allowed end-port profiles are controlled by inverse diagonals. If an
adjacent black contact or a common spectator contact occurs, a distinct
short unit already pays. Otherwise normalized opposite-root potentials
p,q and disjoint spectator rows x/h,y/k obey the exact adjacent-root
distance identity

`||p-q||^2+||x/h-y/k||_(G_Z^-1)^2=2/h^2+2/k^2`.

The remaining task is an all-rank inequality for these end profiles,
with their SEPARATE edge-positive budgets and native heights retained.
Closed cycles of changed corners should be paid as one face inequality;
an isolated half cannot be spent recursively. The current cycle10/11
proofs are concrete models, but do not establish this general transfer.

There is also a clean analytic reduction on the ideal side IF this new
plateau envelope is proved. Use the accepted concentration lemma first
on ranks<=7, and then on any two ranks>8. The first operation preserves
their linear cost; the second preserves their constant cost4. Both
decrease normalized curvature. Thus every worst profile reduces to

`(ell, 8,...,8, b)`, `0<=ell<=7`, `b>=8`,

with zero omitted and at most one rank>8. This is a one-parameter family
at each total rank, rather than an unrestricted partition census. A
proof of nonnegative margins for total ranks53--56 remains required;
it has NOT been evaluated or asserted here.

Why this target is material: if these finite and ideal inequalities
hold in ambient dimensions54--57, the shape comparison excludes all
four dimensions. The finite coefficient is at least6n there: equality
at n54, and strictly larger at55--57. Positive extrema slack then
contradicts the payments. Together with the already proved higher tail,
this would give a ceiling53. This is a sufficient research target,
not an achieved theorem or authorization for new batches.
