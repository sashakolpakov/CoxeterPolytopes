Let G be the unit-normal Gram matrix of a hypothetical finite-volume completion. Its cycle field k is generated by cyclic products in 2G. Quasi-arithmeticity requires the Gram field K to be totally real and every embedding of K that is nontrivial on k to send G to a positive semidefinite matrix. This is the necessary part of Vinberg’s criterion stated in [Guo–Ma–Zang–Zheng, Theorem-Definition 3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.14715v1#S3.SS1).

For these norm-two roots, the first pair contributes the cycle (−2−2√2)² = 12+8√2, so √2 belongs to k. If K were totally real, the conjugation √2 ↦ −√2 would extend to a real embedding of K nontrivial on k. The unit-normal principal block on walls 22 and 23 is the rational matrix [[1,−3],[−3,1]]. Every embedding fixes it, and its determinant is −8. Hence the conjugate Gram matrix cannot be positive semidefinite. If K is not totally real, the other necessary condition already fails. Thus no such completion is quasi-arithmetic, and in particular none is arithmetic.
