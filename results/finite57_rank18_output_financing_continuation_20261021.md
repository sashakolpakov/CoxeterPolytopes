## 3. Actual-face injection and simultaneous allocation

Partition the selected actual family F into F17 and F18 according as
its recipient's inert deletion components all have rank<=17 or include
rank18. Apply the accepted rank17 allocation to F17, restricted to that
selected family. This retains its old hard-source allocations and its
once-per-recipient finite-charge credits, with their proved curvature
bounds. The following operation treats ALL of F18 simultaneously.

At a finite recipient q let a(q) be its number of whole A17 components
and n(q) the number of selected F18 faces mapped to it. A selected face
maps injectively to (q,its marked whole A17). Each such component has
exactly one unordered endpoint pair; releasing those two facets and
intersecting the OTHER55 facets at the simple finite vertex determines
a unique actual two-face. Thus n(q)<=a(q). This is injection into the
marked-component pair, not an assertion that different faces have
different opposite vertices.

At a recipient of F18 the starting finite charge, after deleting every
A17 endpoint half, is C0(q)-a(q)/2. Reassign each selected face's restored
ideal half to this SAME actual opposite finite vertex. Its new effective
finite charge is exactly

    C0(q)-a(q)/2+n(q)/2 <= C0(q) <= 1455/4-5m/2.    (7)

This is a global accounting operation, not a statement that the ideal
corner itself has zero added weight: the selected face payment is first
repaired at its ideal corner. Its half is then charged to q in the global
sum. Explicitly, summing over F18 and their recipients,

    sum_q(C0(q)-a(q)/2) + |F18|/2
      = sum_q(C0(q)-a(q)/2+n(q)/2).                  (8)

The ideal effective total receives no new debt. No curvature envelope,
old D16 saving or unproved compatibility of such a saving is used.

The two parts of F cannot spend the same deleted finite half. If q has
a whole rank18 component, it remains in the inert output after choosing
ANY whole A17 as the marked component. Thus EVERY selected face with
that recipient belongs to F18; a recipient of F17 has no rank18 component.
The recipient sets are disjoint. A single ideal source may support both

a second refund to an already financed face.
