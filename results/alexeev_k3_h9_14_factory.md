# Three finite-volume `H9/14` polytopes from K3 divisor lattices

## Outcome

An exact search inside two published Alexeev 2-elementary K3 divisor-reflection
chambers produces **three** ambient section/cone orbits, each giving a
14-facet Coxeter polytope in `H9`.  All three have finite volume and are
noncompact.

Five raw subgroup rows occur in the inventory, but two pairs are duplicate
markings.  Exact conjugacy inside the complete order-1,152 weighted-diagram

```text

A18_4_0_C07  ~  A18_4_0_C35  ( 72 conjugators).
```

Thus the batch contains three section/cone orbits and three abstract
edge-labelled diagrams, not five distinct examples.

## Exact finite-volume gate

For every marked row, GMP rational double description enumerates all extreme
rays.  The exact pairing ranges with the certified strict timelike interior
are

```text
A14 C01   21..226
A18 C01   22..136
A18 C07   26..276
A18 C14   22..129
A18 C35   26..269.
```

Hence every extreme ray lies on the chosen sheet of the Lorentz cone.  On
that sheet every ray is positive or isotropic and none is hyperideal.  This
proves finite volume; the isotropic rays prove noncompactness.  An independent
literal Vinberg replay verifies that every elliptic rank-eight edge has
exactly two finite/ideal endpoints and that every affine component has a full
certified parabolic cusp neighbourhood.  Both bad-audit counts vanish in all
five marked replays.

## Algebro-geometric content and its boundary

The ambient Lorentzian lattices are K3-admissible Neron--Severi lattices.
Period surjectivity therefore supplies projective K3 surfaces with the
relevant Picard lattices, and the folded normals are algebraic divisor
classes.  The construction has the exact form

```text
P = projectivization(C_mixed cap Fix(G)_R),
```

followed by exact redundancy, Coxeter, integrality, time-orientation, and
Vinberg gates.  This is a genuine **weak full-Picard divisor carrier** for
each polytope.

The source-wall audit separates two geometric strengths.

- `T1` has six retained facets descending from square-minus-two source walls
  and eight descending from square-minus-four walls.
- Every retained facet of `T2` and `T3` descends from a square-minus-two
  source wall, although their ambient `(18,4,0)` chamber contains 24
  square-minus-four walls as well.  After folding, the primitive facet-normal
  squares are `4*(-2)+10*(-4)` for `T2` and `9*(-2)+5*(-6)` for `T3`;
  source-root provenance must not be confused with the norm of an orbit sum.

The second fact makes `T2` and `T3` especially promising promotion targets,
but it does not by itself prove that the source roots form the required
effective curve orbits or that the section is the complete nef cone.  No surface
automorphism, quotient-nef equality, effectivity, or full-nef theorem is
claimed here.

A subsequent exact promotion closes precisely those missing gates for `T2`
and `T3`: both marked actions are induced by genuine K3 automorphisms, and
exact redundancy of every restricted long wall identifies each section with
the full invariant nef cone, hence with the nef cone of the normal quotient.
See the
[`C01/C07 K3 nef promotion`](a18_c01_c07_k3_nef_promotions.md).  This does
not strengthen `T1` or turn the folded primitive `(-4)`/`(-6)` normals into
irreducible K3 curves.

## A reusable surface-to-polytope criterion

This batch freezes the following conditional extraction principle.  Let `N`
be a K3-admissible Lorentzian lattice, `C` a finite-sided integral
divisor-reflection chamber in its positive cone, and `G` a finite group of
weighted-chamber symmetries.  Put `F=N^G`.  If

1. `F` is saturated of signature `(1,n)`;
2. exact conic reduction of `C cap F_R` leaves a pointed set of strict facets;
3. their normalized Gram products are Coxeter products and their primitive
   reflections preserve `F`;
4. every extreme ray is on the chosen time sheet and is nonnegative; and
5. Vinberg's elliptic-edge and full parabolic-neighbourhood conditions hold,

then the projectivized section is a finite-volume hyperbolic Coxeter
polytope, and period theory gives it a weak full-Picard algebraic-surface
carrier.  It is compact exactly when the exact ray census has no isotropic
rays.

This is the rigorous bridge behind the proposed reverse strategy.  Surface
classification can replace unconstrained diagram fishing once one also
proves a universality statement saying that every polytope in a stated
arithmetic class admits such an integral carrier.  Without that last bridge,
failure of the surface tests is a carrier obstruction, not abstract-polytope
nonexistence.

## Lineage

`T3` is exactly the four-arm theta diagram whose four paths have labels
`3,6,3,3`.  Its face vector and six cusps agree with Bredon's `P_star` in
[On ADEG-polyhedra in hyperbolic spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05153),
and with the `List9` row `II_(9,1)(3^4)` in the
[Scharlau--Kirschmer database](https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Markus.Kirschmer/lorentz/index.html).

`T2` is the four-arm theta diagram with arm labels `3,4,4,3`.  Its scaled
dual lies in the known class-one `II_(9,1)(2^2)` arithmetic lattice, but that
lattice's recorded full reflection chamber is a 10-facet simplex, not this
14-facet chamber.  Thus the arithmetic class is known while the literal
subchamber is apparently new after the bounded search.

For `T1` and `T2`, no match was found in the Scharlau--Kirschmer list, the
project ledger, Bredon's census, or all 1,214 ordinary and 2,959 doubling
graphs at pinned CoxIter commit
[`b800be48`](https://github.com/rgugliel/CoxIter/commit/b800be48d8240fbabd2d1c9542e61054584ad7fe).
This is bounded evidence only.  Existing classification results do not cover
all noncompact, nonsimple `d+5`-facet polytopes, and the CoxIter bundle is not
a literature-complete database.
