# H9 coupled walls, complete frontier, and H20 cusp sections

No new compact or finite-volume Coxeter polytope is established. The exact
27-wall H9 chamber has a new exact **29-wall extension** that cuts the old
common-bank escape and creates an actual finite vertex. A different strict
escape still proves infinite volume. This checkpoint also replaces the
older numerical finite-corner and edge lists with a complete exact census,
excludes two specified candidate dictionaries, and closes a different
continuous H20 section mechanism.

## Coupled additions on a two-dimensional face

The [exact two-cap certificate](fresh_h9_makarov_joint_completion_20261013_coupled_exact.json)
retains all 27 old walls. Both new walls are right to old support
`[0,2,3,5,7,8,9]`, strictly dotted to the twenty other old walls, and right
to each other. All **55 new pairings** are exact: fifteen right and forty
dotted. The original 351 pairs are inherited, giving 406 verified pairs.
The common center remains strict and every inequality defines an actual
facet.

The seven-wall support and the two new normals form a positive-definite
nine-wall diagram. The certificate constructs its future timelike vertex
and checks all 29 inequalities. Both new walls strictly cut the escape
that excluded the old 25,101-recipe bank. Their projective normals lie
in `Q(sqrt(5))`; they arise from a three-dimensional Lorentzian residual
space, allowing more freedom than a prescribed eight-wall edge profile.
The [root proof review and exact saved replay](fresh_h9_makarov_joint_completion_20261013_coupled_exact_review.md)
pass.

The [separate Fraction/interval audit](fresh_h9_joint_frontier_20261013_coupled_independent.md)
also passes and completes the new geometry census: **66 finite vertices
and 455 geodesic edges**, consisting of 144 finite edges, 306 edges with
one finite endpoint, and five with none. The new vertex is isolated in
the finite-edge graph and has nine open outgoing directions. Exact
spacelike continuations certify all nine. The audit independently checks
both old-gap cuts, the new strict escape, and the Cartan cycle below.

The first new wall and old wall 1 also have a nonintegral Cartan cycle.
Its field trace is `1349500093/240130713`. Thus any finite-volume completion
retaining this pair must be nonarithmetic, by the necessary integrality
condition in Vinberg's criterion; see
[Theorem 2.8 of Bogachev–Kolpakov](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2002.11445).
This conditional conclusion does not supply finite volume: the new integer
escape in the certificate is strict on all 29 walls.

## The entire H9 edge frontier

The [finite-corner census](fresh_h9_joint_frontier_20261013.md) proves that
the chamber has exactly **65 finite vertices**, with 144 finite edges and
297 edges having one finite endpoint. Its separate arithmetic checker
reconstructs every vertex and open-end witness. The
[complete edge extension](fresh_h9_joint_frontier_20261013_all_edges.md)
exhausts all 1,105 finite-pair eight-cliques and finds **446 actual geodesic
edges**. The five additional edges are full geodesic lines with no finite
or ideal vertex at either end; all ten remote ends have exact feasible
spacelike continuations. The two independent replays pass.

These five lines matter algorithmically: traversing edges from the finite
vertices never reaches them. The finite-vertex graph is connected, but it
does not describe the whole boundary of this infinite-volume chamber.
Construction must check additional positive-definite supports, not infer
global closure from a completed walk on one component.

The census supplies 10,057 spherical endpoint profiles and 418 symbolic
dihedral tails on the 297 finite-corner open edges, plus 143 profiles and
14 tails on the five remote lines. These are complete local spherical
lists. They neither assert the existence of a globally compatible cap nor
include new affine endpoint profiles.

common spacelike escape, 553 pair positively with a retained cap, and 396
have a forbidden finite-angle pairing with a retained cap. Thus no selection
can complete the chamber while all 27 existing facets remain. The
[scoped proof review and saved replay](fresh_h9_makarov_joint_completion_20261013_old_bank_gate_review.md)
pass. This does not exclude new normals or facet replacements.

A separate [polar-truncation experiment](fresh_h9_makarov_polar_closure_20261013.md)
allows **every old wall to be deleted**. Its 1,564 exact polar recipes are
defined by nine orthogonality equations each. Twenty-one strict spacelike
witnesses reduce finite volume to a necessary compatibility-and-covering
problem. With every uncertain pair and every unbounded angle-label tail
conservatively allowed, exhaustive branching still fails in 35 nodes,
using 31,308 exact pair conflicts. No subset of these polars, together
with any subset of the old walls, has finite volume. The theorem concerns
this literal bank; the numerical exterior-corner census is not asserted
complete.

The [polar proof review and separate covering replay](fresh_h9_makarov_polar_closure_20261013_review.md)
also pass. The separate checker verifies the saved interval decisions and
repeats the exhaustive cover; it does not independently reconstruct the
polar normals or their pairing intervals.

The [construction report](fresh_h9_makarov_joint_completion_20261013.md)

sixteen seven-wall supports found 24 numerical cutters of another gap.
Nevertheless the combined 117,228-entry relaxation still has a numerical

nor a complete search over those continuous families. Further progress
requires global compatibility and enclosure, not merely another local gap
cut or an enlarged list of candidate walls.

## A different H20 construction, and its obstruction

The [cusp-section construction](fresh_h20_cusp_sections_20261013.md)
uses equal-length orthogonal twin walls in the complete 18-cusp orbit
census of the Borcherds H21 chamber. It examines 118 oriented pencils,
or **59 distinct normal planes**, including the newly available `D4^5`
cusp. Each genuine interior section has finite volume and a complete
rank-19 Euclidean Coxeter cusp link. Nevertheless an actual ridge always
has angle strictly between pi/3 and pi/2. Exact face points establish
incidence for every real parameter, so the failure is geometric, not
merely a forbidden entry in a projected Gram matrix.

The [independent Fraction review and replay](fresh_h20_cusp_sections_20261013_review.md)
pass. Changing coefficients within these planes cannot repair the angle.
Other local section directions and other nonarithmetic H20 constructions
remain open.

A further [mixed-factor attempt](fresh_h20_cusp_sections_20261013_absorption.md)
tries to absorb both bounds of the `A1` interval in cusp 0's
`D5 x A7 x A7 x A1` link. An exact width comparison rules out the apparent
`A7 x A1` to `C7` construction. An all-real signed-support argument then
excludes every such absorption using the `D5` factor, while the remaining
pure-`A1` plane has an actual obtuse ridge at every interior parameter.
This excludes all interior sections at that cusp for which **both A1
inequalities are redundant in the local link**. It leaves sections
retaining one or both bounds open. The producer replay and root source/
proof review pass. A [separate proof review and Fraction replay](fresh_h20_cusp_sections_20261013_absorption_review.md)
also verifies the signed supports, fourteen D5 norm forms, sixteen A7
width calculations, and the two actual ridge face points with all 210
wall signs.

The [subdivision observation](fresh_h20_cusp_sections_20261013_subdivision.md)
also rules out a finite face-to-face Coxeter subdivision of these recorded
fork-leaf and pure-`A1` sections at rational parameters: their actual bad
ridge has an irrational angle divided by pi. Irrational parameters and
other gluing constructions are outside that observation.

## Literature and scope

The [dimension and algorithm recheck](literature_frontier_recheck_20261013.md)
continues to govern the search: noncompact arithmetic examples cannot occur
in H20 or above H21. Compact H9 and unrestricted nonarithmetic H20/H22
remain different targets. Allcock's published lattice algorithm and the
broader diagram-based geometric construction must not be conflated.

Exact certificates, independent arithmetic replays, scoped code reviews,
and numerical scouts have different evidentiary roles in the linked files.
None of these intermediate constructions constitutes a new finite-volume
example or a new dimension.
