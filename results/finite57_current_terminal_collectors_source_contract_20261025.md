## Mathematical statement checked by a receipt

Fix one of the named physical corner models, its white wall set, and its actual
black corner pairs. Assume the geometric theorem cited for that model: the
marked components are whole finite/affine factors, the listed polygon corners
are distinct actual corners, and every explicitly used unmarked witness has
its stated whole-component seal. Assume also the source-specific completeness
statement giving all variants or all forced outputs of that geometric theorem.

The adapter reconstructs the native diagram from the physical Gram. It evaluates
the single **current** literal rule, rather than identifying an old lower bound
with that rule. For an ordinary receipt it proves that the sum of these current
corner lower bounds is at least one. For the original 216 receiver records it
also proves, corner by corner, that the latest B13 collector's `new_lower` is
no greater than the recomputed current value. These inequalities finance the
receiving quadrilateral. They do not erase earlier transfer debts or establish
an additional global incidence/budget inequality.

For a universal finite-output profile the assertion is instead the explicit
`required <= current` inequality. The universal profile theorem, arbitrary
spectator argument and relation of this finite representative to that theorem
remain named premises. A fixed finite model is never claimed to exhaust an
arbitrary native-root support domain by itself.

## Exact arithmetic and classification

The engine reads a rational radical expression through a restricted AST. Every
accepted scalar has at most 128 characters and 40 AST nodes; integer literals,
radicands and monomial counts have explicit caps. It implements exact addition,
multiplication and division by a nonzero rational. Square-root monomials are
reduced to squarefree radicands. A sign is accepted only when a rational interval
bound is strictly negative; an unresolved sign refuses the input.

For each nonzero Gram product it computes `4*g_ij^2/(g_ii*g_jj)`. Thus positive
root gauges do not change a native label. Only exact Coxeter labels 3, 4, 5, 6
and the parallel two-root link are accepted. This is a representation bridge,
not a theorem that every possible native angle lies in that finite alphabet.
The supplied local finite/affine classification theorem is the external scope
boundary; unsupported graphs fail.

For each marked whole factor the engine searches all full labelled graph
isomorphisms. Its recursive assignment checks every already assigned edge and
nonedge and every native label, as well as exact degrees. Every isomorphism has
one branch because its image of each successive source node is unique. A branch
that fails an edge, nonedge or degree constraint cannot be a full isomorphism.
Consequently the complete returned marked-image set is exhaustive for that
physical factor. All its images must have the same current payment.

Finite A/B/D, E6--E8, F4, H3/H4 and the supported rank-two factors are recognized.
The affine cases are A/B/C/D, affine E6/E7/E8 (orders 7/8/9, arms 2,2,2;
1,3,3; 1,2,5), affine F4 (path labels 3,3,4,3), affine G2 (3,6), and parallel
A1. Small affine orders at most nine have the literal unit value. This does not
supply a new classification theorem.

The graph search and JSON walks share one fail-closed one-million-visit budget.
Every recursion either fixes one new node or descends into a finite bounded
input. Thus the exact algorithm halts; exhaustion is refusal, never a truncated
canonical-image list. Order is capped at 64. Named receipts are capped at 4 MiB.
