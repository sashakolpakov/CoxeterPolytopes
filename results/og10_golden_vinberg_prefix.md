\[

## Geometric interpretation and scope

For every field root `r` in this prefix, the trace-lattice classes `r` and
`phi*r` have Beauville squares `-2` and `-4`, respectively.  They are
primitive of ambient divisibility one, so Mongardi--Onorati's OG10 wall
classification makes both numerical wall divisors; self-adjointness makes
their restrictions the same mirror on the golden eigenspace.  The prefix is
therefore generated by the designed divisor lattice rather than by an
unrelated abstract search.  More strongly, every field reflection in the
prefix is the restriction of a product of two orthogonal square-`-2`
prime-exceptional monodromy reflections.

The later complete-wall and chamber-selection theorem resolves two caveats
of the original computation: there are no primitive divisibility-three
classes in this `N`, and a marking can be selected whose eigen-movable cone
is the completed norm-two chamber.  It also proves that a full Kähler search
must add the field norms `4` and `6-2phi`.

Three conclusions remain deliberately undrawn:

1. later norm-two roots have not been exhausted, so the completed movable
   chamber may still cut the displayed ray;
2. the two extra square-`-4` Kähler families have not been enumerated against
   this ray; and
3. survival of the ray through bound 8 does not prove infinitely many simple
   roots or infinite volume of the completed chamber.

The calculation is therefore strong negative evidence for rapid termination,
but its formal status is `bounded prefix, globally undecided`.
