## Exact native fields and common future-side geometry

The fixed arithmetic ground field is K=Q(sqrt5), with norm2 roots.
For the two added families the dotted squared magnitudes are

    tau125 = 13 + 5 sqrt5 = tau23 tau295,
    tau205 = 6 + 2 sqrt5 = (1 + sqrt5)^2.

Thus tau125 introduces a product squareclass relative to older families;
it cannot be decoded by blindly reusing coefficients from another pair's
field. The decoder always starts from the native contact encodings. It
chooses the at most two nonproportional nonsquare K classes occurring in
the actual wall pair, using exact positive-K square roots to identify
proportional parameters. Two distinct nonzero squareclasses are independent
here; harmless independent padding supplies a four-term basis when fewer
are needed. Every projection, sign and membership calculation uses that
immutable pair's field. Cached keys are native covectors, not field
coefficient arrays.

Let V be the full positive-definite incident Gram matrix at an actual
finite vertex. An external wall with native contact covector p has
projection square p^T V^-1 p and positive timelike height squared

    h_p = p^T V^-1 p - 2 > 0.

All such heights are checked to lie in K. Two distinct external walls
on the common outward future side consequently have forced product

    g(p,q) = p^T V^-1 q - sqrt(h_p h_q).

The saved omitted-wall native product is recovered exactly for every
new literal star; all old and new individual height values are also
rechecked in their complete common frame. Coincident individual
covectors denote the same actual wall and are skipped before testing
incompatibility.

The inherited exact pair gate first determines the physical sign, then
uses acuteness, the norm2 finite-angle gap, membership of g^2 in the fixed
K and algebraic integrality of that two-cycle. It also checks the finite
angle alphabet when applicable. Membership is calculated in the actual
biquadratic field, including the separate case where sqrt(h_p h_q) lies
outside it. Unknown pairs remain compatible. Distinct full stars sharing
an occupied quadrilateral port pair are excluded by the inherited
