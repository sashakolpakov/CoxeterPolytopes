# A semiampleness-to-ABSW transfer theorem for rational weak-log carriers

**Status (2026-08-10): proved, carrier-relative, and strictly weaker on the
geometric input than the complete-Mori weak-incidence theorem.**  This note
composes three pinned project-native results:

1. an exact finite local-algebra certificate that a multiple of a divisor is
   base-point-free;
2. the reflection-maximal weak-log exact-return theorem; and
3. the klt boundary contraction and orbifold second-Chern inequality.

The latter two inputs have separate prior hostile audits.  The independent
hostile audit accompanying this composition reconstructs the first input and
the logical composition as well.  The result is a checkable implication from
one successful geometric carrier packet to an exact theorem about its
original Coxeter chamber.  It does not assert that every raw maximal
arithmetic chamber has such a packet.  That totality assertion is precisely
property 4 of the desired polytope-to-surface functor.

## 1. Input certificate

Let \(P\subset\mathbb H^n\) be a finite-sided finite-volume Coxeter chamber.
A **complete weak-log carrier certificate** for \(P\) consists of the
following data over \(\mathbf C\).

1. A smooth projective rational surface \(S\), its marked lattice
   \(L=\operatorname {NS}(S)\), and a possibly empty disjoint union

   \[
                         B=B_1+\cdots+B_b
   \]

   of smooth rational curves with \(B_i^2=-4\).
2. Distinct effective irreducible negative curves \(C_1,\ldots,C_f\) whose
   oriented intersection halfspaces reproduce the exact labelled chamber

   \[
      P=\{[x]\in\mathbf P(\overline{\mathcal C_S^+}):x.C_j\geq0\}.
      \tag{1.1}
   \]

   Here (1.1) is initially the *proposed* chamber; equality with the nef
   cone is a conclusion below.
3. The adapted integral host

   \[
     M=\left\langle L,B_1/2,\ldots,B_b/2\right\rangle,
     \qquad q_M=2q_L,                                      \tag{1.2}
   \]

   together with an exact check that every displayed facet reflection
   preserves \(M\).
4. Relative reflection maximality: the displayed facet group \(W(P)\) has
   no proper discrete reflection overgroup inside \(O^+(M)\).  Absolute
   reflection maximality, or maximality among arithmetic reflection
   overgroups in the real hyperbolic group, is also sufficient.
5. Put

   \[
                         D=-\left(K_S+B/2\right).             \tag{1.3}
   \]

   The packet supplies a positive integer \(m\) for which \(mD\) is
   Cartier, a certified finite affine cover of \(S\), local frames for
   \(\mathcal O_S(mD)\), compatible returned global sections, and on every
   localized chart an exact identity

   \[
       g_\alpha^{N_\alpha}
       =\sum_jq_{\alpha j}n_{\alpha j}
        +\sum_\ell r_{\alpha\ell}I_{\alpha\ell}.             \tag{1.4}
   \]

The cover, line-bundle transitions, section transitions, and (1.4) are
finite algebraic certificates.  In particular, item 5 does not infer
nefness by testing \(D\) only against the proposed walls.

## 2. Exact return and the dimension bound

### Theorem 2.1 (complete carrier transfer)

Every chamber carrying the certificate of Section 1 satisfies

\[
       \boxed{P=\mathbf P(\operatorname {Nef}S)},\qquad
       \boxed{n\leq25},\qquad
       \boxed{k(P)=\mathbf Q}.                              \tag{2.1}
\]

If \(B=0\), the sharper conclusion is \(n\leq9\).  Up to ambient
hyperbolic isometry, only finitely many relatively reflection-maximal
chambers admit certificates of this kind.

### Proof

Identity (1.4) puts \(1\) in the ideal of the returned local section
functions after localization.  The sections therefore generate
\(\mathcal O_S(mD)\) on every chart and hence globally.  Thus \(mD\) is
base-point-free and \(D\) is nef.

Nefness of \(D\), adjunction, and disjointness of \(B\) classify every
irreducible negative curve on \(S\): it is a boundary \((-4)\)-curve, a
\((-2)\)-curve orthogonal to \(D\) and \(B\), or a \((-1)\)-curve satisfying

\[
                         D.C+B.C/2=1.                         \tag{2.2}
\]

Every one of these reflections preserves \(M\).  Relative maximality gives
\(W(P)=R(M)\), the group generated by all integral hyperbolic reflections
of \(M\).  An ample class pairs positively with every displayed effective
facet and fixes the orientation of every hidden mirror; nonnegative-square
curves lie in the future causal cone.  The weak-log exact-return theorem
therefore proves the first equality in (2.1), including all ideal rays.
This argument quantifies over hidden curves but does not require them to be
listed or to generate the Mori cone.

It remains to bound the rank.  Suppose first that \(b>0\).  Choose an ample
Cartier divisor \(A\) and put

\[
 H=A+\sum_i\frac{A.B_i}{4}B_i.
\]

Then \(H\) is nef and big, \(H.B_i=0\), and \(H.C>0\) for every other
irreducible curve \(C\).  For a Cartier multiple \(L_0\) of \(H\), the snc
pair \((S,B/2)\) is klt and

\[
              aL_0-(K_S+B/2)=aL_0+D
\]

is nef and big for \(a>0\).  The klt surface base-point-free theorem
therefore contracts exactly the disjoint curves \(B_i\).  It gives a
projective normal surface \(f:S\to Y\) with \(b\) singularities of type
\(\frac14(1,1)\), and

\[
 K_S=f^*K_Y-B/2,\qquad f^*(-K_Y)=D,\qquad
 \rho(Y)=\rho(S)-b.                                        \tag{2.3}
\]

Thus \(-K_Y\) is nef.  The projective surface \(Y\) has an ample, hence nef
and big, class, and its quotient singularities are klt.  These are exactly
the hypotheses of Iwai--Jinnouchi--Zhang, Theorem 1.3
(source label thm-nef-anti-canonical), whose dimension-two conclusion is
\(\widehat c_2(Y)\geq0\).  Orbifold Gauss--Bonnet then gives

\[
                  0\leq\widehat c_2(Y)
                   =\rho(S)+2-\frac{7b}{4}.                  \tag{2.4}
\]

Independently, nefness of \(D\) gives

\[
                  0\leq D^2=10-\rho(S)+b.                   \tag{2.5}
\]

Hence

\[
  7b\leq4\rho+8,\qquad b\geq\rho-10,
  \qquad 3\rho\leq78,\qquad \rho\leq26.                     \tag{2.6}
\]

Since the hyperbolic projective dimension is \(n=\rho-1\), this proves
\(n\leq25\).  If \(b=0\), take \(Y=S\) and \(f=\operatorname{id}\).
Then \(D=-K_S\) is nef, and (2.5) directly gives \(\rho\leq10\) and
\(n\leq9\); no orbifold correction is needed.

Every facet normal of the returned full nef chamber is a rational numerical
curve class.  Hence all scaling-invariant cyclic Cartan products are
rational, and the minimal Vinberg field is \(\mathbf Q\).

Finally, bounded rank leaves only finitely many rational-surface
Néron--Severi lattice types: in rank two the possibilities are \(U\) and
\(I_{1,1}\), while in rank at least three the lattice is
\(I_{1,\rho-1}\).  For each \(L\), the adapted host lies in the finite
overlattice interval

\[
                       L(2)\subseteq M\subseteq\tfrac12L.    \tag{2.7}
\]

Relative maximality identifies the facet group with \(R(M)\).  The complete
locally finite mirror arrangement of \(R(M)\) has one chamber orbit: a
generic compact path between two regular chambers crosses finitely many
mirrors, and the successive wall reflections carry one chamber to the
other.  Thus each of the finitely many hosts contributes at most one
unmarked chamber, proving the finiteness assertion.  \(\square\)

### Why this is stronger than the old carrier hypothesis

The complete-Mori weak-incidence theorem derived nefness of \(D\) by
testing it on an independently known complete list of Mori generators.
Theorem 2.1 instead accepts a finite base-point-free certificate and then
uses reflection maximality to prove cone completeness.  It therefore does
not assume the conclusion it is meant to return and does not require a
prior hidden-curve census.

## 3. Nonzero calibration

On \(S=\operatorname {Bl}_p\mathbb F_4\), with \(p\) the torus-fixed point
on the positive section away from the negative section \(B\),

\[
                         D=\frac32B+6F-E,
                 \qquad 2D=3B+12F-2E.                       \tag{3.1}
\]

The finite semiampleness archive gives the complete five-chart toric cover
and four global character sections generating \(2D\).  The independent
weak-log archive verifies the \((2,3,\infty)\) chamber, adapted-host
integrality, and absolute reflection maximality by the sharp area argument.
Theorem 2.1 therefore returns its complete nef triangle without a hidden
Mori-wall enumeration.  Here \(D^2=8\), \(n=2\), and \(D\neq0\), so this is
not merely the Coble equality case.

## 4. Exact remaining property-4 debt

The theorem is a verifier and transfer result, not an exhaustive raw
compiler.  It proves

    raw P + one complete geometric certificate
        -> D semiample and nef
        -> exact same-P full-nef return
        -> n<=25, k=Q, and carrier-relative finiteness.

The still-open universal arrow is

    every raw maximal arithmetic P
        -> at least one such rational certificate
           or one exact minimalizable full-trace eigen-nef certificate.

In the rational branch this requires a terminating, exhaustive construction
of the blowdown/proximity frame, the incidence open, every irreducible
physical facet, the complete affine cover, and global sections.  The generic
localized-unit checker and the full toric calibration are implemented; the
arbitrary PB-Atlas B1/B2a-to-cover adapter is currently a precise contract,
not an implementation.  A failure to find this rational packet is not a
polytope nonexistence proof until all non-rational/eigen-nef carrier species
are also exhausted.

The accompanying composition producer is a dependency-pin, numerical
elimination, and \(\mathbb F_4\) calibration replay.  It does **not** ingest
and validate an arbitrary complete carrier packet: for such a packet, the
cover, transitions, sections, unit identities, facet geometry, and
maximality witnesses remain the certificate inputs that must be checked.
The general implication is proved by the argument above and its pinned
dependencies, not by hard-coded Boolean gates in that producer.
