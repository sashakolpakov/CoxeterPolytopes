## Algebro-geometric meaning

The source Lorentzian lattice is an actual K3-admissible Picard lattice, so
period theory supplies projective K3 surfaces with this full
Neron--Severi lattice.  All source mirrors and all folded mirrors are therefore
algebraic divisor classes, and every retained reflection is integral on the
saturated rank-ten divisor sublattice.  In this precise weak-carrier sense,

```text
P_A14_C01 = projectivization(C_mixed cap F_R),
```

where `F` is the saturated fixed sublattice of the diagram involution and
`C_mixed` is Alexeev's imported mixed divisor-reflection chamber.  The finite
conic, Coxeter, integrality, and Vinberg gates then extract the polytope.

This is **not** a nef-section theorem.  The source wall stock consists of
fourteen square-minus-two and ten square-minus-four mirrors.  Eight of the
fourteen retained facets (orbit indices `4,5,7,8,10,11,12,14`) descend from
square-minus-four source walls.  Those walls are divisor reflections, not
walls of irreducible rational curves, and no effectivity or full-nef equality
is asserted.

No surface automorphism is claimed.  A geometric promotion would require both
a cohomological action/period lift and a separate explanation of the
square-minus-four mirrors; the present result does not promote the mixed
section to an automorphism-fixed, quotient-nef, or full-nef cone.
