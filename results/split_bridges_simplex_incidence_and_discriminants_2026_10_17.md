## A structural change in the compact arithmetic method

Completing the [selected non-ADE triangle domain](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_all_selected_nonade_triangles_20261017.md)
produced173434 literal occurrences. The preceding independent-triangle payment
scheme cannot meet the dimension13 target12: an [exact triangle-only dual](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_active_triangle_dual_20261017.md)
forces a budget above15.23. An [orthogonal-padding certificate](fresh_sqrt5_rank14_triangle_padding_barrier_20261017.md)
also obstructs that scheme's dimension14 target14. These are limitations of
the weighting method, not dimension exclusions.

Two global constraints change the model. First, [maximal simplex incidence](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_triangle_simplex_flags_20261017.md)
partitions the actual triangular faces into their unique maximal simplex
extensions. The selected obstructing stars comprise409 triangles,743
tetrahedra and2497 four-dimensional simplices. Charging the triangles of each
simplex together permits compensation between faces forced to occur together.
The [partial aggregate model](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_simplex_aggregate_lp_20261017.md)
still has an exact obstruction above12.55.

Second, [arithmetic discriminants](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_triangle_discriminant_gate_20261017.md)
restrict which full-rank local stars can lie in the same ambient quadratic
form. Even-rank connected components determine a fixed determinant squareclass;
stars with an odd component remain flexible in this relaxation. Discriminants
alone still leave an [exact flexible-star obstruction](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_flexible_triangle_barrier_20261017.md)
above12.

Combining both constraints gives [exact feasible weights in all58 retained cases](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_discriminant_simplex_lp_20261017.md):
57 witnessed discriminant classes and an arbitrary unrepresented class. All605
represented whole forests retain their complete78-pair vertex budgets. The
worst saved maximum is237907426410/19999999999, approximately11.8953713211,
strictly below12.

The initially missing provenance was then checked: [all46032 known records](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_triangle_all_representatives_20261017.md)
are exactly isomorphic to the same1408 selected full stars, with no new star,
forest or discriminant class. The [direct payment binder](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_known_representative_payments_20261017.md)
reconstructs their complete simplex charges and verifies the applicable saved
class rules. Thus this particular provenance gap is closed.

The [complete saved selected-triangle separation](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_full_triangle_simplex_separation_20261017.md)
then expands all 197,957 ADE/non-ADE occurrences into 12,420 distinct full
stars and 158 fixed discriminant classes. Of the 7,084 stars whose corner
forests are represented, 4,277 fail at least one applicable saved rule. Another
5,336 stars need 1,362 absent forests. This makes the partial rule's remaining
failure explicit; the earlier feasibility certificate remains valid on its
declared smaller domain.

An [exact A13 triangle witness](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_a13_triangle_failure_20261017.md)
has corner forests 2A1+A11, A10+H3 and A13. Its full-star determinant is
-8-12sqrt(5), a new fixed class, and the saved generic rule pays zero against
the required two. Exactly three spherical deletions show that this triangle
does not extend to a larger compact simplex within its star. The certificate
establishes an actual compact local arithmetic triangle, not a compact ambient
polytope. Further triangle configurations, absent vertex budgets and
quadrilateral coverage are still needed before any dimension argument.

For that next stage, the [local realization lemma](fresh_singular_quad_local_realization_20261017.md)
and [1399 exact dotted cores](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_dotted_b1_quad_cores_20261017.md)
certify actual compact local quadrilateral faces over Q(sqrt5). Adding every
permitted orthogonal finite spectator gives [119698 contexts](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_dotted_b1_spectators_20261017.md),
82263 distinct inequalities and63618 marked corner orbits, touching6716
whole forests. These are ready constraints, not a complete dimension proof.

A [canonical quadrilateral aggregation theorem](fresh_quad_product_aggregation_20261017.md)
now groups quadrilaterals by their complete set of n+2 incident walls. They
correspond exactly to the K2,2 subgraphs of the positive-definite two-root
deletion graph, so every face and corner is counted once. For dotted stars
the aggregate is a unique maximal compact prism. Across the 1,399 marked
input records the certificate counts 2,532 quadrilaterals and 10,128 corners;
these totals do not quotient isomorphic stars in different records. An exact
all-finite local example contains two compact triangular prisms sharing one
quadrilateral, giving five distinct quadrilaterals rather than six. This
explains why summing overlapping maximal products would be invalid. The new
grouping supplies valid aggregate constraints; it supplies no feasible weights
or improved dimension bound by itself.
