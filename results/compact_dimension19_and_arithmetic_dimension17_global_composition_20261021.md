## 2. Common compact geometric setup

A compact finite-sided hyperbolic Coxeter polytope has finitely many
actual vertices. Choose a strict interior point z and an actual vertex p
at maximum distance from z. Compact acute simplicity gives a rank-n
finite spherical Coxeter forest at p. In the standard normalization,

    z=E(p-x), E>0, ||x||²<1.

Every theorem in the table concerns this ACTUAL farthest vertex and
the original x. Component statements are applied only to whole diagrams
or to exactly the typed spectator configuration in their theorem.
Actual positive finite projections and negative cores preserve every
unselected wall inequality by acuteness. No arbitrary Coxeter face,
formal Gram completion, change of the farthest vertex or sum of
independently minimized overlapping pressures is used.
