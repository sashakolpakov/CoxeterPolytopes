# K3 action carriers for the five parked Alexeev H9 sections

## Theorem

Four of the five parked finite-volume noncompact `H9` sections are now
carried by genuine symplectic Nikulin involutions, with the **original
archived lattice involution** preserved exactly:

| section | original algebraic coinvariant | final Picard lattice | full algebraic coinvariant | K3 action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `A14_8_0_C01` | `D4(-2)` | rank 18, determinant `-16`, Smith `1^16 4^2` | `E8(-2)` | symplectic involution |
| `A14_8_1_C02` | `D4(-2)` | same | `E8(-2)` | symplectic involution |
| `A14_10_1_C01` | `D4(-2)` | same | `E8(-2)` | symplectic involution |
| `A14_10_1_C02` | `D4(-2)` | same | `E8(-2)` | symplectic involution |

In each row an exact integral embedding of the original rank-14 ambient
divisor lattice into the rank-18 Picard lattice intertwines the two
involutions.  Consequently the original rank-ten fixed real space and its
certified Coxeter polytope survive unchanged.  This is a genuine
**invariant-divisor/action carrier**, not merely an abstract K3 lattice with
the same numerical invariants.

This does **not** identify the imported Coxeter cone with the full invariant
nef cone.  It makes no effectivity assertion for all displayed walls and no
nef assertion for a quotient surface.

The fifth row, `A16_14_1_C01`, has a sharp obstruction to this particular
construction: half of its rank-six coinvariant is an odd positive lattice of
determinant 16, with an explicit norm-three vector.  It therefore cannot
embed in the even `E8` lattice, so its coinvariant cannot be completed to
`E8(-2)` by the same Nikulin route.  This is **not** a no-surface-action
theorem.

## Exact construction

Let `M` be one of the first four archived ambient lattices and let `g` be its
diagram involution.  Exact fixed/coinvariant decomposition gives

\[
M^-=D_4(-2).
\]

The certificate supplies a unimodular change of basis for every row and a
primitive coordinate embedding

\[
D_4(-2)\hookrightarrow E_8(-2).
\]

It then forms the pushout generated by `M` and the whole `E8(-2)` inside
`M^+_Q + E8(-2)_Q`.  The involution is `+1` on the fixed factor and `-1` on
`E8(-2)`.  Its induced action on the pushout discriminant group is already
the identity.

An explicit even isotropic discriminant subgroup completes this pushout to a
rank-18 lattice `NS`:

| section | pushout-to-`NS` index | `det(NS)` | `A_NS` |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `A14_8_0_C01` | 8 | `-16` | `(Z/4)^2` |
| `A14_8_1_C02` | 8 | `-16` | `(Z/4)^2` |
| `A14_10_1_C01` | 16 | `-16` | `(Z/4)^2` |
| `A14_10_1_C02` | 16 | `-16` | `(Z/4)^2` |

The displayed subgroups are replayed element by element: they are closed,
their bilinear pairings are integral, and every quadratic value is even.
On the resulting Picard lattice the action is integral, has order two,
preserves the Gram matrix, and acts trivially on `A_NS`.

Two exact signature-`(2,2)`, determinant-16 transcendental forms occur.  One
is `U + U(4)`; the other is the explicitly archived lattice `T16a`.  For each
row the certificate searches all 96 automorphisms of `(Z/4)^2` and freezes a
quadratic anti-isometry

\[
(A_{NS},q_{NS})\simeq (A_T,-q_T).
\]

Graph gluing produces an even unimodular lattice of signature `(3,19)` and
an integral involution.  Its characteristic polynomial is

\[
(x-1)^{14}(x+1)^8,
\]

and its rank-eight coinvariant has determinant 256.  More strongly,
dividing the negative coinvariant Gram matrix by two gives a positive even
unimodular rank-eight lattice.  Hence the coinvariant is exactly
`E8(-2)`, with minimum norm `-4`; in particular it contains no `(-2)` root.

An explicit irrational period is positive, isotropic, fixed by the action,
and has no nonzero rational orthogonal vector in `T`.  Thus the displayed
rank-18 lattice is the exact Picard lattice.  The rootless algebraic
coinvariant is the invariant-Kähler gate: the Hodge isometry preserves a K3
Weyl chamber, so global Torelli gives a genuine symplectic Nikulin
involution.

## What this says about the converse map

Write `Phi_C` schematically for the passage

\[
(X,g,\text{marked divisor-wall data})
\longmapsto
(\text{Coxeter section in }NS(X)^g_{\mathbb R}).
\]

The present result separates four levels which should not be conflated.

1. **Weak lattice carrier.**  All five sections originally had an abstract
   hyperbolic divisor lattice and integral fixed-section data.
2. **Genuine invariant-divisor/action carrier.**  Four sections are now in
   the image of `Phi_C` with their original involution: a K3 surface, a
   genuine Nikulin automorphism, and the exact archived action are all
   present.
3. **Effective-wall carrier.**  Not proved here.  The construction does not
   place every imported wall on the effective side of the chosen K3 Weyl
   chamber.
4. **Invariant-nef or full-nef carrier.**  Not proved here.  Rootlessness of
   `E8(-2)` guarantees some invariant Kähler chamber; it does not identify
   that chamber with the archived mixed `(-2)/(-4)` Coxeter cone.

This also gives a reusable sufficient inverse criterion:

> A marked lattice involution with rootless coinvariant `D4(-2)` admits the
> Nikulin completion whenever its pushout with `E8(-2)` has an even stable
> overclosure whose discriminant form has a signature-`(2,2)` complement and
> on which the involution acts trivially on the discriminant.

All three discrete gates are exact and finite: the `D4` embedding, the
isotropic subgroup, and the discriminant anti-isometry.  The four rows pass
all of them.

The carrier is **not forced by the surface-free Coxeter diagram alone**.
The construction uses extra marking data: the ambient divisor lattice, its
integral involution, and the coinvariant embedding.  Thus this is a precise
partial surjectivity result for a marked `Phi_C`, not a theorem that every
abstract Coxeter polytope canonically determines a surface.

## A weaker genuine carrier for all five

There is also an independent alternative after forgetting the original
ambient action.  The unique maximal reflection-stable overclosure of each
rank-ten fixed lattice is one of

\[
(10,4,0),\qquad (10,6,0),\qquad (10,8,0).
\]

These are classical invariant lattices of nonsymplectic K3 involutions.  On
a very general member of the corresponding ten-dimensional moduli space,
the Picard lattice equals that invariant lattice and the involution fixes it
pointwise.  Every displayed Coxeter reflection extends integrally to the
overclosure.  Therefore all five sections have a genuine
**pointwise-invariant divisor carrier**.

This alternative is intentionally weaker:

- it does not realize the original ambient involution;
- it does not make the imported walls effective;
- it does not identify the imported cone with the nef cone.

## The fifth-row boundary

For `A16_14_1_C01`, the original algebraic coinvariant `K` has rank 6 and
determinant 1,024.  The lattice

\[
L=-K/2
\]

is integral, positive definite, and has determinant 16, but it is odd: the
first certified basis vector has norm 3.  If `K` embedded isometrically in
`E8(-2)`, division by two would embed `L` in the even lattice `E8`, an
impossibility.  This cleanly marks the boundary of the universal Nikulin
completion criterion.  Higher-order lifts, a different Picard enlargement,
or the pointwise-invariant carrier remain open or available as stated above.
