# Generic Hessian quartic: a complete 84-wall stock and its `H10/27` fold

## Result

The minimal resolution (X) of the Hessian quartic of a general cubic
surface supplies a new complete algebraic fishing denominator in this
project.  Its Picard lattice has rank (16).  Dolgachev and Keum embed it in
(II_{1,25}) and define the induced Conway chamber (P(S_X)').  Their
Section 8 proves that this chamber has finitely many faces and finite volume;
Lemma 8.3 and Remark 8.4 classify and count every wall:

| source case | walls |
|---|---:|
| `(0)` | 20 |
| `(1a)`, `(1b)` | 12 + 12 |
| `(2)` | 10 |
| `(3a)`, `(3b)` | 15 + 15 |
| **total** | **84** |

The twenty nodal and line curves form the Desargues `(10_3)` configuration
and generate `Pic(X)`.  The exact ingestion reconstructs all 84 wall normals,
their rank-16 Gram matrix, and the chamber group

\[
  \operatorname{Aut}(P(S_X)') \cong C_2\times S_5
\]

of order 240.  GAP has 57 subgroup conjugacy classes in this group.

Exactly one proper class can have fixed divisor rank at least 11: a `C2`
generated by an `S5` transposition.  Indeed this is the only nonidentity
element class with fixed rank at least 11, and a subgroup containing two
distinct transpositions also contains their product, a 3-cycle or double
transposition, whose fixed rank is at most 10.  Thus all 57 subgroup classes
are closed above `H9` without an unbounded subgroup search.

## The fixed section

The transposition has 48 wall orbits: 12 fixed walls and 36 pairs.  Their
orbit-sum Gram has rank 11 and inertia `(1,10,37)`.  There are

- 27 spacelike restrictions, with norm histogram `8*(-2) + 13*(-4) +
  6*(-36)`;
- 21 isotropic inactive restrictions;
- no timelike restrictions.

All 27 spacelike inequalities are genuine facets.  For every orbit root
(r), the orthogonal projection of the invariant ample Conway class
(omega'=sum N+\sum T) to (r^perp) is timelike, vanishes only on the
target inequality, and pairs strictly positively with all other 47
restrictions, including the isotropic ones.

Every one of the 351 facet pairs is Coxeter.  The relation census is

| relation | pairs |
|---|---:|
| right angle | 235 |
| order 3 | 15 |
| order 4 | 14 |
| parallel | 48 |
| ultraparallel | 39 |

The literal Vinberg audit gives:

| datum | value |
|---|---:|
| hyperbolic dimension | 10 |
| facets | 27 |
| finite vertices | 895 |
| cusps | 67 |
| elliptic rank-9 edges | 6,221 |
| connected affine components | 198 |
| bad edges / bad parabolic neighborhoods | 0 / 0 |
| endpoint types `(finite,ideal)` | `(0,2):432`, `(1,1):2628`, `(2,0):3161` |

Hence the section is a finite-volume noncompact Coxeter polytope in
`H10`.

## Integral carrier and exact lineage

The transposition is an integral isometry of `Pic(X)`.  Its saturated fixed
lattice has rank 11, determinant 256, and Smith diagonal

`1^4 2^6 4`.

After primitive reduction, the 27 mirror types are
`8*(-2,div1) + 10*(-4,div2) + 9*(-4,div4)`, and every reflection is integral
on this fixed lattice.

This is **not a new abstract hyperbolic polytope**.  The certificate gives an
explicit 27-facet permutation under which its normalized Coxeter relation
matrix agrees entrywise with the existing `D16-D4-C7-H10-27` chamber (and
hence with the Alexeev `(15,7,1)` realization).  The familiar chamber lies at
index 2016 in its known 14-wall maximal reflection group.  The Hessian fixed
divisor lattice is nevertheless a different integral carrier: determinant
256 here versus 512 in the archived Shimada realization.

The bounded conclusion is therefore:

> a new maintained-ledger algebraic/divisor-stock realization candidate of
> a known `H10/27` Coxeter polytope; no new abstract polytope, arithmetic
> class, maximal reflection group, or worldwide priority claim.

No surface-automorphism claim is made.  On a general Hessian quartic, the
`S5` transposition is a chamber/Picard-lattice symmetry, while the source's
discriminant condition leaves only the identity and Enriques involution in
the relevant general-surface automorphism image.
