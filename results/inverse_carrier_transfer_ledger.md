# Inverse-carrier transfer ledger

**Frozen scope date:** 2026-08-12.

This ledger records exactly how far each algebro-geometric construction can
be run in the reverse direction.  Its purpose is to prevent three recurrent
logical substitutions:

1. a Lorentzian divisor lattice is not yet an effective wall system;
2. an effective finite wall system is not yet a complete nef/Mori chamber;
3. a rational-polyhedral divisor subsystem is not yet the complete nef cone
   and does not supply Coxeter angles.  Once complete rational-polyhedral nef
   equality is proved in Picard rank at least three, finite hyperbolic volume
   is automatic by the convex--Hodge theorem below.

The primary association starts from the arithmetic polytope.  Its proved
levels—universal weak rational-surface eigencarrier, exact weak K3 embedding,
exact full-nef K3 root-completeness, and the first fixed-decoration
Looijenga period-character criterion—are assembled in the
[`arithmetic Coxeter-to-surface association theorem`](arithmetic_coxeter_to_surface_association_criterion.md).
The table below records the component theorems and the still-open middle
carrier classes separately.

descent, and `H2` branches.  The independent geometric ceiling `n<=995`
leaves finitely many native pairs.  For each pair, the connected `A=62`
frame, cycle invariant theory, Burnside, and a full-order conductor sandwich
give finitely many projective native hosts.  Maximality then gives the full
host reflection group and one mirror-chamber orbit.  This is a proved
AG-mediated hybrid theorem, not an AG-only theorem; it does not invoke
Nikulin's fixed-data reflective-lattice finiteness conclusion and does not
solve surface Property 4 or direct AG-DEG.  The historical
`ABSW-NATIVE-PAIR` shortcut below remains a valid comparison but is no longer
a dependency of the global theorem.

The governing completion standard is now stronger than any single ledger
row: for a non-singleton surface-free arithmetic class, the compiler must be
total and exhaustive, the AG realization criterion must be necessary and
sufficient, and classification/boundedness/emptiness must be transferred back
to a genuinely new theorem on the whole polytope class.  Per-polytope
recognitions and one-species obstructions below are calibration, even when
their embedding or wall enumeration is exact.

Every inverse claim is henceforth evaluated as
`(C, Car, Return, AG, Pushback)`: an independently defined surface-free class;
a finite jointly exhaustive carrier stack with a realized branch for every
member; an intrinsic iff
recovering the exact same labelled chamber; genuinely algebraic-geometric
classification, boundedness, or nonemptiness information; and a new theorem
transported back to every polytope in the class.  Its advertised status is the
weakest of these five entries.  A uniform divisor-lattice association with an
empty `Pushback` column is still only a carrier theorem.

For a surface-free arithmetic Coxeter specification $D$ equipped with explicit
bounds on dotted entries, determinant/discriminant, and glue, the IC0 compiler
must prove that $A(D)$ is a finite exhaustive set, modulo labelled
orientation-preserving integral isometry, of admissible normalization,
saturated-overlattice, glue, carrier, and embedding/action packages.  Bounded
dimension and a finite norm alphabet alone do not prove this.  For
$a\in A(D)$, let
$\mathcal{M}^{\mathrm{alg}}_a$ use only intrinsic algebraic-geometric conditions:
exact Picard/action data, effective extremal divisors, ample orientation, and
complete nef, invariant-nef, or Mori equality.  Let $\operatorname{Ext}_a$
be exact marked cone extraction and let $V$ be the independent Coxeter and
literal Vinberg predicate.  The target theorem, after `U/R/C/V`, is

\[
 \operatorname{Pol}^{\mathrm{fv,mark}}(D)\ne\varnothing
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \exists a\in A(D),\ x\in \mathcal{M}^{\mathrm{alg}}_a:\quad
 \operatorname{Ext}_a(x)\text{ has datum }D
 \text{ and }V(\operatorname{Ext}_a(x)),
 \tag{0.1}
\]

Only after proving `U`, exact marked recovery `R`, cone completeness `C`, and
the terminal predicate `V` automatic on every stratum retained by intrinsic
AG conditions may (0.1) be shortened to nonemptiness of the coproduct of the
moduli spaces.  Equation (0.1) is the desired equivalence of existence problems
for marked carrier data; forgetting markings and periods is generally
many-to-one.  If $D$ already includes an exact Gram matrix, it classifies
carriers of that realization and does not by itself decide existence from a
bare Coxeter diagram.

Every row is audited against four obligations: `U`, forward universality from
every actual realization to some $a,x$; `R`, exact labelled integral marked
recovery; `C`, complete cone equality with no hidden walls; and `V`, the
independent terminal hyperbolic gate.  Matching only signatures, Smith forms,
discriminant forms, action digests, or unordered root sets does not establish
`R`.

## Status vocabulary

- **Weak:** the hyperbolic lattice or displayed divisor classes occur on a
  variety.  No cone equality is asserted.
- **Strong:** the displayed cone is proved to be a complete nef, Mori,
  movable, or invariant-nef chamber.
- **Coxeter-certified:** exact angles, inertia, orientation, and
  irredundancy have been checked.
- **Volume-certified:** Vinberg's complete edge-endpoint and parabolic
  neighborhood conditions, or an equivalent exact cone theorem, have been
  checked.
- **Universal for a class:** every polytope in a class defined without
  mentioning a variety is proved to admit one of the listed carriers.  Only
  this property lets carrier emptiness imply abstract-polytope
  nonexistence.

`Frozen` below means internally theorem-checked and hostile-audited in this
project.  It is not a claim of external publication or worldwide priority.

## Exact content of each row

### SURF-NV — the general algebraic finite-volume bridge

Let `X` be a smooth projective surface over an algebraically closed field of
arbitrary characteristic, with Picard rank `rho>=3`.  Hodge index puts
signature `(1,rho-1)` on `N^1(X)_R`, and every nef class has
nonnegative square.  If the complete nef cone is polyhedral, its affine Klein
slice is therefore a finite Euclidean polytope contained in the closed Klein
ball.  Its vertices are finite or ideal, never hyperideal, and triangulation
into finite/ideal simplices proves finite hyperbolic volume.  Strict convexity
of the light cone shows that compactness is equivalent to absence of an
isotropic extremal nef ray.  Hyperbolic dimension one is the sharp exception:
the two fibre rays of `P1 x P1` bound an infinite-length ideal interval.

This closes only volume.  Exact full-nef equality, integral reflections,
irredundancy, and Coxeter angles remain independent gates; a weak divisor
subsystem may still have hyperideal ends.  An isotropic nef ray is an ideal
vertex, but it gives a fibration only under an additional semiampleness
theorem.  See the
[`projective-surface nef-volume theorem`](projective_surface_nef_hyperbolic_volume_theorem.md).

### AUT-DL — canonical determinant-line AG arrangement

For every compact arithmetic `P` presented by an even primitive totally-real
wall lattice, the determinant line has the forced real sign pattern and
produces only finitely many reflection-stable integral `(2,n)` gluings.  The
resulting compact orthogonal Shimura stack is constructed from `P`; its
canonical real slice pulls each facet Heegner divisor back to the
corresponding mirror.  Divisor intersections recover elliptic candidate
spans, oriented halfspace feasibility recovers actual faces, and local
dihedral inertia of order `2m_ij` recovers finite labels, while the marked
real-slice Gram retains parallel/ultraparallel data.  A fixed special-
divisor shell has a finite hidden-slice enumeration.

Bruinier's cusp-form pairing is necessary and sufficient for the selected
facet principal part to define a Whittaker/Borcherds divisor.  The direct
determinant factor now supplies more: its safe columns span the obstruction
dual, and a diagonal sign-change argument shows that their nonnegative cone
is the whole real dual.  Thus a deep level gives every compact marked input an
exact-facet complex carrier; with a real q-structure the correction is
nonnegative, and an integral q-structure gives an effective interior divisor.
McGraw supplies that arithmetic structure over `Q`; the general Hilbert case
currently relies on Bruinier's stated but unwritten adaptation.

Exact **global** reflective support is nevertheless nonautomatic.  The
rational finite-volume lattice `S=U+E8(-1)+D4(-1)` has determinant extension
`S+<8>` of signature `(2,13)`; Wang's complete classification proves that it
is not `2`-reflective.  Conversely, the determinant extension of
`II_(1,9)=U+E8(-1)` is isometric to the complete `2`-reflective lattice
`2U+E7(-1)`, giving a positive exact-slice realization of the known `E10`
`H9` simplex.  These controls are noncompact.  In the compact branch, an
unbounded effective compensation cannot by itself restrict `P`: for any
projective carrier, ample `lambda`, and target divisor `T`, Serre vanishing
gives `T+C_k~k*lambda` for every sufficiently large `k`.  Thus the needed
input is exact reflective support or a facet-count-independent bounded slope,
not bare compensated-divisor existence.  See the
[strict audit](borcherds_compact_inverse_leverage_audit.md).

The sheet-separating level is explicitly computable for each fixed marked
polytope.  At that level orbit-square scalarization bounds the largest scaled
trace index polynomially over `Q`; for higher-degree totally real fields the
bound includes a finite coefficient-rank detection term `R_det` with no
proved formula.  For a chosen finite support, Cramer--Hadamard minors and
Bruinier's Eisenstein/degree identity then bound conic denominators, weight,
and degree from the integral obstruction-column height and arithmetic volume.
This does not say that those controlling data are class-uniform, nor that a
general complete-intersection surface has the Coxeter chamber as its nef
cone.  An actual dimension bound still needs class-uniform
height/level/matrix/volume control, or an exhaustive alternative, on a
non-singleton compact class plus a uniform high-dimensional obstruction.  The qualitative
sheet/degree step is now closed on a neat compact exact-support cover:
`facets(P)*q_min <= w*lambda^n`.  Thus a facet bound needs the same remaining
upper bound on level, weight, and top degree, not another incidence lemma.
See the [sheet/degree theorem](facet_sheet_automorphic_degree_injection.md)
and the
[`determinant-line transfer theorem`](polytope_to_automorphic_divisor_transfer_theorem.md),
the [`orbit-square theorem`](orbit_square_quantitative_safe_height.md), and
the [`explicit level theorem`](explicit_sheet_separating_level_bound.md), and
the [`weight/degree theorem`](borcherds_weight_degree_quantitative_bound.md).

The first compact one-datum calibration is `AUT-DL-B8`.  Starting only from
Bugaenko's archived wall lattice, its determinant module has 64 elements and
only the zero isotropic class, so the even glue list is a singleton.  All
eleven facets have the same numerical Heegner type, but the complete shell
meeting the closed real chamber contains 320 mirrors: eleven facets, 309
lower-face contacts, and no interior cut.  The last zero is the exact
open-chamber gate; the middle 309 prove that a numerical Heegner shell must
not be confused with a facet-only divisor.  All eleven facets also collapse
to the one standard full-group column `(phi/sqrt(5),0)`.  The certified
diagonal estimate `|E|<0.742149649308345<1` makes its Poincare series
nonzero, so the pure norm-two Whittaker principal part is obstructed.
The complete globally minimal compensator shell has norm `1/80`, one
three-component unit orbit, and a nonempty divisor wholly disjoint from the
real slice.  Its target-plus-shell `2x2` Poincare matrix is invertible, so the
complete first support has no target-preserving kernel either.  Nevertheless,
the safe-span/positive-cone theorem proves that a larger finite correction
exists (positive conditional on the Hilbert q-structure), and exact
rank-enumeration supplies a finite trace-height stopping point.  Its actual
least support, numerical `R_det`, and resulting obstruction matrix remain
unknown; once known, the compression theorem bounds conic denominators,
weight, and degree.  The sheet-separating level is computable per chamber.
See the
[`compact Bugaenko calibration`](bugaenko_h8_determinant_line_ag.md) and

### LP-D — the first exact middle `P -> surface` theorem

all extracted from the walls.  With `Lambda=D^perp` and intended internal-root

```text
{phi in Hom(Lambda,C*) :
 phi|R0=1 and phi(beta)!=1 for every forbidden deformation root beta}.
```

Equivalently it is the set of characters of `Lambda/R0` nontrivial on every
forbidden-root image.  The torsion-character check is exact; for primitive

### K3-R — the first complete inverse theorem

Define the class using no surface: $N$ is even of signature $(1,n)$,
primitively K3-embeddable, and $C$ is a finite-sided component of the
complement of **all** primitive square-minus-two root hyperplanes.  Then

\[
 (N,C)\text{ exists}
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \exists(X,\eta)\text{ with }\eta(\operatorname{Nef}X)=C.
\]

For this class,

\[
 \operatorname{vol}_{\rm hyp}C<\infty
 \Longleftrightarrow [O^+(N):W_2(N)]<\infty
 \Longleftrightarrow |\operatorname{Aut}X|<\infty
 \Longleftrightarrow \operatorname{Cox}(X)\text{ is finitely generated}.
\]

Compactness is equivalent to rational anisotropy of $N$ and to absence of a
genus-one fibration.  Meyer therefore excludes compact full-nef K3 Coxeter
polytopes in every hyperbolic dimension $n\ge4$.  This is a genuine
surface-to-polytope obstruction, but only for this full-nef root-complete
class.

Let `M_Enr^fin` be the moduli problem of numerically marked complex Enriques
surfaces with finite automorphism group, and let `Phi_Enr` retain only the
full nef chamber in `H9(Num(Y))`.  The complete Kondō--Martin classification
of smooth rational curves and Shimada's exact `L10=U+E8(-1)` vectors give

```text
I=12A, II=12B, III=20B, IV=20F,
V=20A, VI=20E, VII=20C=20D.
```

Exact double description enumerates 2,214 finite and 294 ideal vertices,
with no hyperideal ray.  Independently, all 13,182 elliptic rank-eight edges
have exactly two finite/ideal endpoints and all 584 full affine
zero-neighborhood audits have parabolic rank eight.  The seven normalized
Gram types are pairwise separated by exact intersection histograms.  Hence
`Phi_Enr` has exactly seven images: two 12-facet and five 20-facet
finite-volume noncompact Coxeter polytopes.  If `C_Enr` is defined without
surfaces as those seven primitive normalized integral Gram types, then

```text
P in C_Enr  <=>  P has a full-nef finite-automorphism complex-Enriques carrier.
```

The finite-automorphism hypothesis can now be recovered rather than assumed.
Kondō proves that the complete nef chamber is a fundamental domain for the
nodal reflection group, and complex Torelli compares its finite index with
finiteness of `Aut(Y)`.  Since `O^+(L10)` is an arithmetic lattice, covering
volume gives

```text
finite hyperbolic volume of the full nef chamber
  <=> finite nodal-reflection index
  <=> finite Aut(Y)
  <=> rational-polyhedral Eff/Nef
  <=> finitely generated Cox(Y)
  <=> one of the seven exact Gram images.
```

Thus the seven types exhaust all full-nef finite-volume complex-Enriques
outputs, not merely a subclass declared finite-Aut at input.  This is the
first strong algebra-to-covolume classification in the project.  It is
deliberately not advertised as an infinite-class universality theorem for
abstract polytopes, a new abstract-polytope priority claim, or a global
compact `H9/14` obstruction.  See the
[`full-nef finite-volume classification`](enriques_full_nef_finite_volume_classification.md)
and the underlying
[`seven-image transfer theorem`](enriques_finite_automorphism_nef_transfer_theorem.md).
Its no-import raw-data replay is the
[`hostile Enriques audit`](enriques_finite_aut_nef_chambers_hostile_audit.md).

## What can already be inferred from surface algebra

The following implications are valid now.

1. For every smooth projective surface over an algebraically closed field of
   arbitrary characteristic and Picard rank at least three, complete rational-
   polyhedral nef equality implies finite hyperbolic volume, and absence of
   isotropic extremal nef rays is equivalent to compactness.
2. In K3-R, finite generation of the Cox ring is equivalent to finite
   hyperbolic volume of the complete root chamber.
3. In K3-R, a genus-one fibration is equivalent to an ideal direction; in
   dimension at least four it forces noncompactness of every finite-volume
   member.
4. In TR-E, finite complete-chamber stabilizer is equivalent to compact
   finite volume; an infinite-order stabilizer product is a terminal
   infinite-volume certificate.
5. In K3-G, quotient-surface nef-cone classification transfers exactly to
   the corresponding marked equivariant data, but not yet to all abstract
   polytopes sharing its Coxeter diagram.
6. In RS-M, adjunction and restriction to the anticanonical cubic classify
   the existence of strong rational carriers even though they do not decide
   existence of the underlying abstract chambers.
7. In ENR-F, the algebraic classification of complete nodal graphs transfers
   to exactly seven full-nef `H9` chambers.  It proves both existence and
   exhaustion among **all** full-nef finite-volume complex-Enriques chambers.
   Finite volume is equivalent to finite generation of the Cox ring.  There
   is no compact or 14-facet member, but this says nothing by itself about
   arbitrary `H9/14` polytopes.
8. In a compact exact full-trace K3 return, a spanning narrow star of literal
   in-trace physical `(-2)` curves bounds the complete native order-module
   host; when `NS` is the trace lattice, exact full-Picard eigen-nef return
   makes this condition automatic only if every eigenslice facet is exposed
   by an actual K3 root wall represented by that same in-trace vector.
9. A finite list of complete oriented native hosts, exact native chamber
   return and full same-host reflection maximality give at most one unmarked
   normalized chamber per host.  Native `k`-reflections, not ordinary
   rank-one trace-lattice reflections, are the relevant mirrors when
   `[k:Q]>1`.
10. At a K3 cusp, the affine physical packet alone does not bound the
    primitive isotropic divisibility.  A full bounded spanning facet-root
    packet repairs the failure by `R subset NS subset R*` after literal
    finite-volume full-Picard/full-nef return.  Otherwise the compiler must
    retain the separate physical in-trace/exact-native-return eigenslice gate
    or route the input to a finite rational-host lane.
11. For one pinned exact compact nonrational realization, the full-Picard K3
    eigen-nef existence question is a finite `PHYS -> EMB -> ROOT` iff/`EMPTY`
    decision.  An accepted package has `NS=M_Z` and an ample interior point,
    but its chamber is generally a proper eigenslice; an empty list is not
    universal Property 4 or abstract-polytope nonexistence.

The following implications are **not** valid.

- Hodge signature alone $\Rightarrow$ a Coxeter chamber.
- Primitive Picard embedding $\Rightarrow$ effective or complete walls.
- Rational-polyhedral complete nef cone $\Rightarrow$ Coxeter angles.
- Rational-polyhedral divisor subsystem $\Rightarrow$ complete nef equality
  or finite volume.
- Failure of one K3/rational/hyperkähler carrier $\Rightarrow$ abstract
  polytope nonexistence.
- A nonempty or reflection-maximal raw arithmetic packet
  $\Rightarrow\operatorname {K3Comp}(P)\ne\varnothing$.
- Full-Picard eigen-nef return $\Rightarrow$ literal
  full-Picard/full-nef `K3-HOST-FV13` scope.
- A finite list of observed cusps or vertices $\Rightarrow$ Vinberg finite
  volume.

## Next routes beyond the seven-image calibration

The direct Enriques generalization has reached a **variable lift-package
theorem**.  Fix `Lambda=U+L10+L10` with the Enriques involution, invariant
lattice `S=L10(2)`, and anti-invariant lattice `T=U+L10(2)`.  A downstairs
root `r_i` lifts precisely after choosing `delta_i in T` of square `-4` with
`(pullback(r_i)+delta_i)/2 in Lambda`.  If the saturated delta span is
negative definite and rootless, the exact-Picard period locus is nonempty.
A root-regular invariant positive class orients Riemann--Roch, and a finite
bounded-degree root exclusion is necessary and sufficient for every lift to
be irreducible.  Exact containment of the whole inequality cone in the
chosen closed positive sheet lets Namikawa's Proposition 6.9 rule out hidden
nodal walls, while a separate literal Vinberg audit proves finite volume.
Conversely, every full-nef finite-volume Enriques carrier supplies exactly
such a package.  Therefore `L1`--`L4`, together with the surface-free Coxeter
gate, are already an if-and-only-if theorem, not a proposed future bridge.
The theorem and its exact cover/glue obstruction are in the
[`variable Enriques lift-package report`](enriques_variable_lift_package_theorem.md).

The naive package is provably wrong: choosing
`b_i=(0,r_i,0)` for all walls gives anti-Gram `2 Gram(R)`.  For every spanning
`L10` system this has signature `(1,9)`, so its complement in `T` has only one
positive direction and cannot contain a K3 period plane.  The exact replay
checks this for all seven Kondō--Martin systems.  Thus individual wall lifts
are genuinely insufficient; nontrivial two-sheet intersection data are the
missing algebraic datum.

For Type I, that missing datum is no longer an unbounded fishing problem.
The complete finite alphabet has 27,648 assignments and 18 switching orbits.
Only the known rank-nine package and one rank-ten residue are negative
semidefinite; compatible half-sum glue forces a square-minus-two root in the
saturation of the latter.  The geometric `E8(-2)+<-4>` anti-Gram is therefore
unique up to sheet swaps, and its explicit rank-19 package passes independent
`L1`--`L4` and hostile audits.  This closes the anti-Gram projection stage,
but not yet the downstairs-only reconstruction of the surviving primitive
embedding, glue, period orientation, and irreducibility certificate.  See the
[`Type-I compiler`](enriques_type_i_downstairs_anti_gram_census.md), the
[`rank-ten glue obstruction`](enriques_type_i_rank_ten_residue_obstruction.md),
and the [`explicit positive package`](enriques_type_i_explicit_lift_package.md).

Type II closes the same projection stage by a different mechanism.  Its
32,768 binary anti-Grams form sixteen switching orbits, of which exact
signature leaves two of rank nine.  The complete half-sum congruence lattice,
not a binary-vector shortcut, is respectively `D9(-2)` and `<-2>^9`.  The
second has eighteen roots and is excluded; the first is rootless and is
realized by the known very-general Type-II surface.  A hostile replay uses a
different switching tree and independent Sturm, HNF, and LDL routines and
checks all 2,048 sheet switches against all 36 signed root presentations.
Binary presentations vary with the switching gauge, so only full-lattice
rootlessness is a valid invariant.  This freezes anti-Gram uniqueness but not
the downstream primitive embedding, period, effectivity, or complete-wall
reconstruction.  See the
[`Type-II compiler`](enriques_type_ii_downstairs_anti_gram_census.md) and its
[`hostile audit`](enriques_type_ii_downstairs_anti_gram_census_hostile_audit.md).

The remaining end-to-end projection/compiler layers remain open.  For
`k=r_i.r_j`, nonnegative lift intersections force
`delta_i.delta_j` into the finite set
`{-2k,-2k+4,...,2k}`.  The next completion is to enumerate the resulting
negative-definite saturated lattices and every primitive embedding/glue orbit
in `T`, including all non-stable-range discriminant cases, and thereby remove
the auxiliary deltas from the input.  This is an implementation and
elimination problem; existence with the deltas retained is already decided
by the proved finite checks.  The full-nef classification guarantees
that this can recover criteria and alternative realizations of the seven
types but cannot produce an eighth finite-volume full-nef Enriques chamber;
new positive examples require proper invariant/quotient sections.

The parallel finite target is the **finite-action K3 quotient class with
bounded Picard rank and prescribed root norms**.  The completion path is:

1. define the class using only $(N,G,F,Q)$, integral wall norms and
   divisibilities, exact Coxeter data, and ideal-vertex/cusp incidence data;
2. enumerate primitive K3 embeddings and all cohomological extensions of
   $G$ through their discriminant glue;
3. decide every character-period stratum and discard those with a
   coinvariant root or empty exact-Picard locus;
4. prove complete-root separation for each survivor and enumerate its true
   quotient-nef facets;
5. apply geometric Coxeter, optional integral-reflection, and full Vinberg
   gates; and
6. prove an intrinsic universality lemma identifying every polytope in the
   chosen surface-free class with one of these finitely many lattice actions.

Steps 1--5 classify marked carriers.  Step 6 is what converts that
classification into a theorem about abstract polytopes.  If step 6 fails,
the output remains a useful exact carrier census but cannot be reported as a
general polytope no-go.

The alternate positive route is RD: classify K3-admissible finite-volume
seeds and their doubling-wall graphs, then search the resulting finite trees
for new diagrams while the same moduli carrier is retained automatically.
