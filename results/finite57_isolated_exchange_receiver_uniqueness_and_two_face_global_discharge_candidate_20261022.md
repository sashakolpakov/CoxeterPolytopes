## 5. The exact remaining global condition

The old obstruction remains valid: with only one isolated exchange, the optimal six-and-a-half-unit net credit still leaves a heavy deficit of3/2. For a remaining collection of heavy sources, a sufficient aggregate condition is

    5 H_2 >= 8 H_0 + (3/2) H_1,                    (10)

provided all OTHER finite/ideal obligations have their own compatible caps. In particular, forcing two distinct isolated exchanges at every remaining heavy source would suffice for this entire source class. Neither (10) nor that stronger face-multiplicity statement has been proved here from finite volume.

An isolated long-edge completion does not itself force an actual quadrilateral; its relevant two-face may have more sides. Same-active-D16 and inert-D17 completions also remain possible, and other costly whole forests require their own global treatment. The missing step is therefore a quantitative actual-face coverage/routing theorem, or external credit paying exactly the residual terms in(9), together with those other obligations. It cannot be replaced by a formal local Gram completion, by assuming all isolated exchanges occur, or by counting the two D17 factors as two faces without establishing their actual continuations.
