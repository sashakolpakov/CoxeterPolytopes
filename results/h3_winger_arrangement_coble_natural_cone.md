# The exact H3 arrangement/Coble audit: positive surfaces, negative natural cones

## Frozen verdict

The icosahedral reflection arrangement gives two rigorous positive
algebro-geometric outputs over `K=Q(sqrt(5))`, but **no new complete
finite-volume Coxeter chamber** in the wall stocks audited here.

1. Its ten triple points determine the classical Winger sextic.  Exact Hilbert
   ranks are

   \[
      h^0(-K)=0,\qquad h^0(-2K)=1.
   \]

   The unique sextic is absolutely irreducible, rational, and has exactly the
   ten prescribed ordinary nodes.  Blowing them up is therefore a one-boundary
   strong Coble surface in hyperbolic dimension ten.  This surface is known;
   the contribution here is an exact arrangement/Hilbert/cone replay.

2. Reduced unions of six H3 mirrors give an automatic multi-boundary
   strong-Coble factory after the forced first-near resolution of triple
   concurrences.  There are `3,595` admissible marked six-mirror subsets in
   `76` projective-arrangement orbits.  In particular, `1,890` subsets in `39`
   orbits have two triple concurrences and hence

   \[
      b=8,\qquad N=17,qquad \dim\mathbb H=17.
   \]

   These are genuine characteristic-zero strong Coble surfaces, not claimed
   to be pairwise nonisomorphic.  They do not furnish the known complete
   `H17/48` chamber: that finite-automorphism Type-MII surface is the separate
   characteristic-three `P1(F3) x P1(F3)` construction.

For the ten-node Winger blowup, exact cddlib gives:

| declared effective walls | facets | finite | ideal | hyperideal | verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| ten exceptionals + fifteen H3 mirrors | 25 | 1 | 10 | 283 | infinite volume |
| previous walls + primitive boundary `b=-K` | 26 | 1 | 10 | 162 | infinite volume |
| `b`, ten exceptionals, all 45 pair-lines | 56 | 121 | 220 | 1,762 | infinite volume |

Even the next universal enlargement by all `252` conics through five of the
ten centers is rigorously infinite-volume.  For every seven-subset `S`,

\[
                 x_S=5H-2\sum_{i\in S}E_i,
                 \qquad x_S^2=-3,
\]

pairs nonnegatively with `b`, every exceptional, every pair-line, and every
five-point conic.  Its zero walls have rank ten, so it is an extreme
hyperideal ray.  This is a literal witness, not a heuristic ray count.

These cone conclusions are **strictly scoped to the displayed finite wall
sets**.  Cubics and higher-degree negative curves can cut the witnesses.  We
therefore do not infer full-nef infinite volume from a truncated cdd
calculation.  The separate finite-automorphism classification supplies the
algebro-geometric reason that the characteristic-zero `b=8` members cannot be
the complete Type-MII chamber.

## 1. Exact H3 intersection lattice

The fifteen projective mirrors are reconstructed from the coordinate normals
and cyclic permutations of

\[
                   (1,\ \pm\phi,\ \pm\phi^{-1}),
           \qquad \phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2,
\]

modulo projective sign.  Exact arithmetic in `Q(sqrt(5))` gives

\[
        6\text{ fivefold points},\quad
        10\text{ triple points},\quad
        15\text{ double points}.
\]

The completeness identity is

\[
 6\binom52+10\binom32+15\binom22=105=\binom{15}{2}.
\]

The projective arrangement group generated by the reflections has order 60.
Each mirror contains exactly two triple-orbit points, and the resulting
`10`-vertex, `15`-edge incidence graph is the Petersen graph.

## 2. Exact Winger/strong-Coble gate

At the ten triple points, the cubic evaluation matrix is `10 x 10` of rank
ten.  The sextic double-point matrix is `30 x 28` of rank 27.  With
`a=sqrt(5)`, the integral-normalized kernel generator is

\[
\begin{aligned}
F={}&2(x^6+y^6+z^6)\\
 &+(33+27a)(x^4y^2+x^2z^4+y^4z^2)\\
 &+(33-27a)(x^4z^2+x^2y^4+y^2z^4)
 -204x^2y^2z^2.
\end{aligned}
\]

There are no collinear triples among the ten centers.  Every local tangent
discriminant is nonzero.  Independently, Singular over
`Q(a)/(a^2-5)` certifies:

- one nonunit factor over `K`;
- projective Jacobian degree ten;
- radical degree ten;
- ten minimal associated primes.

Thus the singular scheme is exactly the ten reduced nodes.  The normalization
has genus `10-10=0`.  Absolute irreducibility follows from
`K`-irreducibility: geometric components would form an equal-degree transitive
Galois orbit, hence number `r=2,3,6`; the nodal genus formula forces
`sum g_i=r-1`, excluding all three possibilities.

The strict transform has class

\[
                         B=6H-2\sum_{i=1}^{10}E_i=-2K,
                         \qquad B^2=-4.
\]

The two Hilbert ranks prove the strong-Coble assertion directly.

## 3. Why a triple concurrence forces three near centers

Let six selected mirrors have `t` ordinary triple concurrence points and no
selected concurrence of multiplicity at least four.  Their remaining
intersection points are `15-3t` ordinary doubles.

At a triple point, the first exceptional must occur with coefficient one in
the reduced bi-anticanonical boundary.  It meets the three strict mirror
branches, so those three intersections must also be blown up.  The cluster is
therefore one proper plus three first-near centers.  Consequently

\[
 N=(15-3t)+4t=15+t,
 \qquad b=6+t,
 \qquad N=9+b.
\]

Every strict mirror and every first exceptional has square `-4`; all are
smooth, rational, and pairwise disjoint.  Their reduced sum is `-2K`.  Its
negative intersection with each component makes it the unique member of
`|-2K|`, and reducedness rules out halving it in `|-K|`.  This proves the
factory, including the first-near conditions, without a speculative
polynomial search.

The exact six-line census is:

| triple concurrences `t` | boundaries `b` | dimension `15+t` | marked subsets |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 6 | 15 | 20 |
| 1 | 7 | 16 | 260 |
| 2 | 8 | 17 | 1,890 |
| 3 | 9 | 18 | 1,300 |
| 4 | 10 | 19 | 125 |

Subsets with a selected concurrence of multiplicity at least four are rejected
because the first exceptional would have coefficient greater than one; they
do not produce a reduced strong-Coble boundary by this resolution rule.

## 4. Exact cone witnesses

Write the Petersen edges as `uv` and use the odd-unimodular basis
`H,E_1,...,E_10`.  The natural curves are

\[
              E_i,\qquad L_{uv}=H-E_u-E_v,
              \qquad b=3H-\sum E_i.
\]

For every independent triple `I` of the Petersen graph,

\[
                         H-\sum_{i\in I}E_i
\]

has square `-2`, satisfies all 26 inequalities, and has ten independent active
walls.  There are exactly thirty such witnesses.  This proves the natural
boundary-compatible carrier has hyperideal extreme rays even without cdd.

The seven-subset witnesses above do the same after every pair-line and
five-point conic has been adjoined.  Claims about the full nef cone require an
exhaustion of higher-degree negative curves and are deliberately left open.

## 5. Lineage

The one-boundary surface is classical.  Dolgachev identifies the unique
`A5`-invariant rational sextic with the ten-node orbit and explicitly notes
that blowing up its nodes gives a Coble surface.  Zi treats the ten-nodal
Winger member and its genus-zero normalization in the modern Winger-pencil
account:

- Igor Dolgachev, [*Quartic surfaces with icosahedral symmetry*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.02567), especially the Winger-sextic discussion and Remark 4.
- Yunpeng Zi, [*Geometry of the Winger Pencil*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.07194).
- Shigeyuki Kondō, [*Coble surfaces with finite automorphism group*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.00591), for the exhaustive finite-automorphism boundary types and the characteristic-three Type-MII endpoint.

No new Winger surface, new complete hyperbolic polytope, or sharper universal
dimension bound is claimed.  The project-new content is the exact H3
arrangement compiler, the complete six-mirror marked census, and the literal
hyperideal witnesses delimiting the natural wall search.
