Within the cone generated by the32 spacelike extreme rays of each
original raw23 cone, every nonzero Lorentz projection of a Coxeter-
compatible norm-two normal must lie on one of two specified extreme-ray directions.
All other face interiors are excluded, over any extension field and
with arbitrary passive components. Directions involving timelike rays
remain open; this is not an arbitrary-completion or dimension theorem.

## A sum of contacts gives an exact norm obstruction

Let F be any listed face, with basis vectors x_i and positive-definite
Gram H. The known scaled roots r_j have norm2s_j, where each s_j is
either1 or(7+√5)/2. A physical norm-two proposed normal n has Lorentz
projection x and positive-definite passive part z, so

    n=x+z,  x²=2−z² <=2.

All known-wall products depend only on x. For a known contact form
nonzero on F, nonnegativity on every ray makes it strictly positive
throughout the actual relative interior. Coxeter compatibility then
requires its physical nonzero squared contact to be at least1,
including every nonright finite angle, parallel pair or dotted pair.
Consequently its raw contact satisfies

    −x·r_j >=sqrt(s_j) >= l_j,
    l_j=1 if s_j=1, and l_j=2 otherwise.

The source checks l_j²<=s_j exactly. There is no extension-field
restriction in this inequality.

Sum all known contact forms nonzero on F. Let p_i be that sum on
basis vector x_i, and let w be its Riesz vector in span(F). Its exact
coordinates solve Hc=p, and its squared norm is q=pᵀc. Set
L=Σl_j over the same forms. A proposed normal in the relative interior
would satisfy x·w>=L, hence positive-definite Cauchy gives

    L² <= x²*w² <=2q.

The certificate stores H, p, c, q, L and the exact margin L²−2q.
A strictly positive margin excludes that entire face interior for
all real coefficients and all passive lifts. No assumption about
contacts with unknown passive walls is made.

All643 face interiors of dimensions2 through8 have positive margin
in each cone. Thirty of the32 ray interiors also have positive margin.
The three cases therefore exclude1,929 higher-dimensional interiors
and90 ray interiors. The only unexcluded directions under this sum
certificate are original ray IDs6 and31 in each case.

Each nonzero vector in a pointed polyhedral cone belongs to the
relative interior of a unique face. Since the entire face lattice is
covered, this is a complete reduction of the spacelike-generated cone
to those six ray directions. Zero projection, including a purely passive
normal orthogonal to the known Lorentz span, is not excluded. The result
does not yet claim that all six directions give compatible normals or
exclude the cone's timelike-generator regions.
