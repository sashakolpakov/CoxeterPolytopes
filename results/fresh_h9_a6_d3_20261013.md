The new lattice

\[
L=\langle-\beta\rangle\oplus A_6\oplus D_3,
\quad \beta=(T-1)(T+2),\quad T=2\cos(\pi/8),

## Why this was a different host

The field is \(K=\mathbf Q(T)\), \(T^4-4T^2+2=0\), with \(\mathcal O_K=\mathbf Z[T]\). The coefficient \(\beta\) is positive at the chosen embedding and negative at the other three, so the form is Lorentzian only at the chosen embedding. The spatial lattice uses the ordinary A6 Cartan basis and
\(D_3=\{(x,y,z)\in\mathcal O_K^3:x+y+z\in2\mathcal O_K\}\).

Its determinant is \(-28\beta\). Exact square-root tests reject the determinant ratios to the recent closed unit/ramified unimodular hosts, A9, D9, tripled D9, and tripled E8 plus one. Since rank ten is even, these ratios also exclude scalar similarity over K. This comparison establishes a new rational-space test relative to those named repository hosts; it is not a worldwide novelty claim or a classification of all lattices.

The trace argument below made this block tractable without assuming that displayed factors already give the full zero-height chamber. The initially considered unramified coefficient \(T-1\) was not searched: its D3 factor risked repeating the old D9 mechanism. The ramified coefficient \(\beta\) was used throughout the recorded computations.

## Full initial chamber and safe primitive norm pool

The rational even lattice \(A_6\oplus D_3\) has minimum two. For a spatial vector \(v=a+bT+cT^2+dT^3\), with coefficients in that rational lattice,

\[
\operatorname{Tr}(v^2)
=4|a+2c|^2+8|c|^2+8|b+3d|^2+8|d|^2.
\]

If \(v^2=2\), the trace is eight, so \(b=c=d=0\). Thus all norm-two vectors are rational. They span and generate the rational root lattice, so every integral spatial isometry is rational and belongs to \(\operatorname{Aut}(A_6)\times\operatorname{Aut}(D_3)\); the two irreducible root components have different ranks. The reflection subgroup of the second factor is B3. In the first factor, an outer element \(-w\), \(w\in S_7\), cannot be a reflection: an involution in \(S_7\) has at most three negative eigenvalues on A6, whereas \(-w\) would require five. Consequently the complete zero-height chamber is **A6+B3, with 30 mirrors**.

The dual exponent is 28, checked on the actual lattice Gram inverse. A primitive reflective root therefore has norm dividing 56. The [previous certified field structure](fresh_h9_glued_host_20261013_structure.json) proves that the ring is principal and every totally positive unit is a square. At two there are thirteen valuations, represented by the saved dyadic norms with absolute norms \(2^j\), \(0\le j\le12\). At seven,

\[
p_+=T^2+1=3+\sqrt2,\qquad p_-=5-T^2=3-\sqrt2,
\qquad p_+p_-=7.
\]

Both generators are totally positive and have norm 49. Modulo seven the defining polynomial factors into the two irreducible quadratics \((T^2+1)(T^2+2)\). The full safe pool therefore has **52 classes**: thirteen dyadic representatives times \(1,p_+,p_-,7\). This is a complete candidate pool, not an assertion that every class occurs primitively. Every returned root is checked for actual lattice membership, divisibility by the dyadic and seven-prime generators, and all reflection coefficients.

For the first three edges, every prime-seven profile has zero incident pairings. Reflection integrality at that odd prime then forces all residual coordinates divisible by it, contradicting primitivity. Those 39 profiles are explicitly rejected in each census. The A5+B3 edge retains the seven-prime classes and checks them through the complete norm-orbit computation.

## Global chamber obstruction

Only the last first-edge certificate is needed for the global conclusion. Its complete endpoint has type A6+B3, just like the initial corner. Let the initial simple normals be indexed 0 through 8 and the new A5-edge wall be 12. The exact transport maps

\[
(0,1,2,3,4,5)\longmapsto(1,2,3,4,5,12)
\]

and fixes the D3 coordinates pointwise. It maps the initial timelike point to a positive multiple of the endpoint. The certificate checks the form identity, the actual lattice matrix and its integral inverse, every oriented simple-normal image, both full 30-mirror closures, and the entire certified path's side conditions.

Its trace is strictly greater than ten. Thus the transport has infinite order. Because the complete first-edge census connects the two full oriented corner sectors in one chamber, their transport preserves the whole chamber, not merely the finite prefix. The arithmetic isometry group is discrete; infinitely many translates of a small interior ball force infinite chamber volume.

The exact finite-prefix escape has norm

\[

\]
