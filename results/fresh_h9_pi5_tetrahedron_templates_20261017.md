# Complete compact tetrahedron templates for the new pi/5 wedge

The [complete H9 endpoint family](fresh_nonarithmetic_h3_edge730_domain_20261017.md)
contains a root r_m for every m≥8. Its finite contact with old wall51 gives
a positive support H4+I2(m), and hence a different three-dimensional face.
The original H4+A2 three-face is not this new face: wall51 has nonright
contacts with the A2 roots5,6 and misses that original face. The new face
retains walls7,30, which meet at pi/5.

This package supplies **every compact Coxeter tetrahedron template containing
that fixed pi/5 pair**, with every literal placement of its two new walls.
It is a construction input, not a claim that one template passes the ambient
55-wall constraints. Larger compact polyhedra remain possible if all these
tetrahedron templates fail.

## Exhaustive angle reduction

Normalize the four facet normals to square two. Every principal three-root
Gram of a compact tetrahedron is positive definite. Suppose a pair has
label m≥6. If either endpoint has a nonright contact with a third root, the
three-root determinant is nonpositive: already

    cos²(pi/m) + cos²(pi/3) ≥ 1,

and the remaining nonpositive off-diagonal entry can only decrease that
determinant. Consequently both endpoints of the large-label pair must be
orthogonal to both remaining roots. The full Gram would then be a direct
sum of two positive-definite pairs, contradicting its Lorentzian signature.
Thus all labels are2,3,4,5, with no numerical cutoff assumption.

Fix label5 between roots0,1, corresponding to old7,30. Enumerating the other
five pair labels requires exactly4^5=1,024 cases. The
[checker](../scripts/fresh_h9_pi5_tetrahedron_templates_20261017.py) uses its
own exact Q(sqrt2,sqrt5) arithmetic and verifies all vertex determinants and
the full signature. The [certificate](fresh_h9_pi5_tetrahedron_templates_20261017.json)
records all18 accepted literal Grams in7 marked isomorphism classes. Of the
other cases,994 fail a spherical vertex test and12 have nonhyperbolic full
Gram. The
only identifications exchange the two old roots and/or the two new roots;
the explicit maps recover every placement for the ambient search.

## Actual compactness and embedding freedom

For every accepted Gram G, all entries of adj(G) are positive and det(G)<0.
Its columns v_i satisfy G v_i=det(G)e_i and

    (v_i,v_j)=det(G) adj(G)_ij <0.

They are therefore four timelike rays in one future cone, with exactly the
required zero facet products. The whole inequality cone is generated by
these rays, since G is invertible. Their sum is a strict timelike center.
This verifies an actual compact tetrahedron, beyond necessary Gram signatures.

With the old I2(5) pair fixed, its orthogonal complement inside the Lorentzian
four-space has signature(1,1). Its O(1,1) stabilizer supplies all realizations
of a template: a continuous boost parameter and the discrete components.
Thus the catalogue feeds a complete continuous embedding test for each fixed
r_m. Prescribed products are identities; all other old-wall Coxeter gates,
common interior and actual ambient face incidence must still be checked.
