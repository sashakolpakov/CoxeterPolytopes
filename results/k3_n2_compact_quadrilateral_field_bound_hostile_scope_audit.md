# Hostile audit: the all-degree compact K3 quadrilateral field bound

**Verdict (2026-08-11): PASS, all-degree compact-quadrilateral scope.**
The theorem retains the exact full-trace K3 carrier, the same compact
eigen-nef chamber, complete reflection saturation, and declared-host
reflection maximality.  It adds the literal ground-field premise that
\(k\) is generated by the cyclic Cartan packet.  The proof needs neither a
quadratic-field assumption nor an imposed mixed height.  Maximality is
retained but unused.

## 1. Degree and signed-place audit

The field module has rank three.  Exact full trace gives

\[
                         3d=\rho(X)\le20,
\]

so \(d\le6\) for a complex projective K3.  At the distinguished place, the
two opposite pairs are ultraparallel and their squared ratios \(A,C\)
exceed one.  At each of the other \(d-1\) positive-definite places, strict
Cauchy--Schwarz puts both ratios in \((0,1)\).  Pairings and wedges are
nonzero field tensors, so no endpoint is lost after embedding.

The pair cycles \(4A,4C\) belong to the returned order.  Thus
\(4r=4(A-1)\) and \(4s=4(C-1)\) are algebraic integers, positive at the
distinguished place and in \((-4,0)\) at every definite place.  Their
nonzero integer norms give

\[
                         r,s>4^{-d}\ge4^{-6}={1\over4096}.
\tag{1.1}
\]

The power \(4^{-d}\), rather than the quadratic \(1/16\), is the exact
all-place norm constant.

## 2. Determinant audit

After real unit normalization at the distinguished place, write adjacent
magnitudes \(a,b,c,d_0\in[0,1)\) and opposite magnitudes \(U,V>1\).  Direct
Leibniz expansion gives the single determinant expansion

\[
0=(U^2-1)(V^2-1)+K-2PU-2QV-2RUV,
\]

where

\[
\begin{aligned}
P&=ab+cd_0,&Q&=ad_0+bc,&R&=ac+bd_0,\\
K&=-a^2-b^2-c^2-d_0^2+a^2c^2+b^2d_0^2-2abcd_0.
\end{aligned}
\]

The four-normal nonzero graph contains cycles, so the signs in this formula
matter; the replay compares it with the full \(4!\)-term determinant rather
than a square-only shortcut.  Since \(P,Q,R<2\) and \(|K|<8\),

\[
 (U^2-1)(V^2-1)<4U+4V+4UV+8.
\tag{2.1}
\]

No Coxeter-angle list is used.

## 3. Uniform diagonal audit

Put \(s=V^2-1\).  From (1.1),
\(V>\sqrt{4097}/64\).  Dividing (2.1) by \(s\) gives

\[
U^2<
{4\over V-1}U+{4V+8\over V^2-1}+1.
\]

Both coefficients decrease for \(V>1\).  At the lower endpoint this becomes

\[
U^2<
(16384+256\sqrt{4097})U+(32769+256\sqrt{4097}).
\]

The exact trial certificate is

\[
\begin{aligned}
f(32772)&=537034767-8389888\sqrt{4097},\\
537034767^2-4097\cdot8389888^2
&=17,606,953,271,521>0.
\end{aligned}
\]

The trial point lies beyond the quadratic vertex, so the positive root is
smaller.  Rotation gives

\[
                              U,V<32772.
\tag{3.1}
\]

For \(d=2\), repeating the same calculation with \(r,s>1/16\) gives the
owner's sharper \(U,V<132\).

## 4. Ground-field generation audit

Pair products alone need not generate a field of degree greater than two.
The general proof therefore uses the bounded simple cyclic packet, not an
unstated pair-generation claim.

Reflection-word trace induction makes every cyclic Cartan coordinate an
algebraic integer.  Closed walks reduce to pairs and simple cycles; on four
facets these have length at most four.  At a definite place their magnitude
is at most \(2^\ell\).  At the distinguished place, adjacent correlations
are below one and opposite correlations are below \(32772\).  Thus every
simple cyclic generator has every conjugate bounded by

\[
H=16\cdot32772^4
 =18,455,752,922,365,956,096.
\]

The exact carrier hypothesis says these coordinates generate \(k\).  A
minimal generating subpacket has at most two elements: three strict
adjunctions would force degree at least eight.  For two generators, each
unordered pair of the at most six embeddings excludes at most one
coefficient \(c\) in \(\theta_1+c\theta_2\).  Among \(0,\ldots,15\), one
choice is primitive.  Hence every conjugate of a primitive integral
\(\theta\) is bounded by \(16H\), and Vandermonde gives

\[
 |\operatorname {Disc}(k)|
 \le(32H)^{30}
 =\bigl(590,584,093,515,710,595,072\bigr)^{30}.
\tag{4.1}
\]

Hermite--Minkowski turns this uniform discriminant bound into a finite field
set; bounded degree alone is not being substituted for field finiteness.

This proves a finite field set, not merely bounded degree.

## 5. Quadratic specialization and scope

When \(d=2\), either opposite pair cycle is automatically nonrational
because its two values lie in disjoint intervals \((4,69696)\) and
\((0,4)\).  It generates \(k\), giving the sharper discriminant bound
\(4,857,950,597\).

The theorem does not cover noncompact quadrilaterals, gratuitous operator
extensions larger than the cyclic ground field, or arbitrary \(n=2\)
incidence.  It neither constructs a K3 carrier nor derives a general
correspondence height from maximality.  No angle classification or
reflective-lattice classification enters.
