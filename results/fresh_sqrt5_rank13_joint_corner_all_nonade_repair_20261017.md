# Complete selected non-ADE triangle batch obstructs the local payment model

The [successor source](../scripts/fresh_sqrt5_rank13_joint_corner_all_nonade_repair_20261017.py) incorporates all 173,434 occurrences from the [complete 363-target non-ADE triangle catalogue](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_all_selected_nonade_triangles_20261017.md). It retains every predecessor constraint and adds complete 78-pair vertex budgets for all 892 newly encountered whole forests. The resulting domain has 3,214 forests, 111,190 free nonnegative corner-orbit payments, 180,954 literal rows, and 21,051 distinct face constraints.

The [exact saved certificates](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_joint_corner_all_nonade_repair_20261017.json) include a feasible common budget **152351905522/9999999999** and a dual lower bound **109332738192/9110360713 > 12**. The numerical optimum is approximately 15.2351905512; neither that decimal nor the recovered feasible budget is claimed as an exact optimum. Generation and a fresh saved reconstruction both passed. Replay rebuilds all literal corner mappings and budgets and checks the rational primal and dual, without rerunning the optimizer.

The saved minimized dual uses 280 triangle inequalities and 74 whole-forest budgets: 238 rows have all-selected non-ADE provenance and 42 have double-H4 provenance. No quadrilateral multiplier is needed. Thus independent nonnegative payments for every unordered root-pair orbit of an entire finite forest already fail to reach the dimension-13 target 12 on genuine local triangle configurations. The [small support reoptimization](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_triangle_dual_core_20261017.md) and [stronger active-triangle recovery](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_active_triangle_dual_20261017.md) preserve more informative exact bounds.

This is a method obstruction, not a dimension bound or an ambient realization theorem. The local stars need not coexist in one compact arithmetic polytope or in one quadratic form. The model includes only triangles with the stated selected endpoint types and the inherited partial quadrilateral domain; 3,575 of the 6,789 finite rank-13 forests are absent. These omissions cannot invalidate the displayed lower bound for a rule required to handle the retained configurations, but global compatibility restrictions could remove some configurations. The proposed dotted-B1 successor was therefore left unrun: adding further face constraints cannot repair this infeasibility.
