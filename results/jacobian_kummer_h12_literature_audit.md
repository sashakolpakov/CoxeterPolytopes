# Bounded literature and corpus audit for the 172-facet Jacobian--Kummer H12 chamber

## Conclusion

No exact published match was found for either

\[
L=U(2)\oplus D_5(-1)^{\oplus 2}\oplus\langle-4\rangle
\]

equipped with the certified mixed (2/4/8)-reflection chamber, or for the
172-facet finite-volume noncompact Coxeter polytope in \(\mathbb H^{12}\).

This is a bounded negative search result, **not** a proof of worldwide
priority.  The defensible claim is that the chamber is absent from the exact
primary sources and the official CoxIter graph corpus enumerated below.  A
publication should therefore say "apparently new" until a broader expert and
bibliographic audit has been completed.

## Exact primary-source comparison

The construction starts from Dolgachev--Shimada,
*15-nodal quartic surfaces. Part II: The automorphism group*:

- arXiv: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.05390>
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1007/s12215-019-00464-7>
- authors' PDF: <https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/idolga-new/wp-content/uploads/sites/1467/2024/08/15nodalquartics2.pdf>
- official computational data:
  <https://www.math.sci.hiroshima-u.ac.jp/shimada/preprints/15NodalQuart/15nodal/compdata/15nodalcompdata.txt>

The paper and its full TeX/source data publish the general genus-2 Jacobian
Kummer K3 surface (Y_{16}), its Neron--Severi lattice (S_{16}), the induced
chamber (D_{16}), its 316 walls, and its finite chamber stabilizer.  They do
not state or tabulate the trace-nine involution's rank-13 fixed lattice, the
180 folded wall orbits, the 172 irredundant facets, their Coxeter graph, or the
finite-volume conclusion proved here.

The principal predecessor papers checked were:

- Shigeyuki Kondo, *The automorphism group of a generic Jacobian Kummer
  surface*, J. Algebraic Geometry 7 (1998), 589--609:
  <https://pure.mpg.de/pubman/faces/ViewItemOverviewPage.jsp?itemId=item_3125692>
- Jonghae Keum, *Automorphisms of Jacobian Kummer surfaces*, Compositio
  Mathematica 107 (1997), 269--288:
  <https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1000148907120>
- Hisanori Ohashi, *Enriques surfaces covered by Jacobian Kummer surfaces*:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.5352>,
  <https://doi.org/10.1017/S0027763000009764>

These concern geometric automorphisms or free involutions.  No exact occurrence
of the fixed lattice or 172-facet chamber was located in them.  The chamber
construction does not require the trace-nine lattice isometry to be induced by
an automorphism of the surface.

## Exact lattice identity

The replayable certificate is
[`jacobian_kummer_h12_fixed_lattice_certificate.json`](../data/enumerations/jacobian_kummer_h12_fixed_lattice_certificate.json),
generated by
[`certify_jacobian_kummer_h12_fixed_lattice.py`](../scripts/certify_jacobian_kummer_h12_fixed_lattice.py).
It proves that the saturated fixed lattice has

\[
\operatorname{sig}(L)=(1,12),\qquad |\det L|=256,
\]

Smith invariants (1^8,2^2,4^3), and discriminant form

\[
q_L\cong u(2)\oplus\langle3/4\rangle^{\oplus2}
       \oplus\langle7/4\rangle.
\]

The same exact invariants and discriminant form are exhibited for
(U(2)\oplus D_5(-1)^2\oplus\langle-4\rangle).  Nikulin's indefinite
even-lattice uniqueness criterion applies because

\[
13\geq \ell(A_L)+2=7.
\]

Reference: V. V. Nikulin, *Integral symmetric bilinear forms and some of their
applications*, Math. USSR-Izv. 14 (1980), 103--167:
<https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/im1677>,
<https://doi.org/10.1070/IM1980v014n01ABEH001060>.

The same certificate proves integrality of all 172 mirror reflections.  Their
primitive ((r^2,\operatorname{div}(r))) types are

| primitive mirror type | count |
|---|---:|
| ((-2,1)) | 16 |
| ((-4,2)) | 36 |
| ((-4,4)) | 96 |
| ((-8,4)) | 24 |

Consequently, norm-two-reflective lattice lists alone neither contain nor rule
out this mixed-root reflection group.

## Rational arithmetic lineage

The replayable exact certificate
[`higher_dimensional_rational_lineage_certificate.json`](../data/enumerations/higher_dimensional_rational_lineage_certificate.json),
generated by
[`certify_higher_dimensional_rational_lineage.py`](../scripts/certify_higher_dimensional_rational_lineage.py),
diagonalizes the rational quadratic spaces and compares determinant square
classes and every local Hasse invariant (including the real place).  It proves
that this rank-13 space is rationally similar both to the odd-unimodular
Lorentzian space underlying Vinberg's classical `H12` example and to the space
underlying McLeod's `f_3^12` example.  Those two reference spaces are also
similar in rank 13.

Therefore the 172-wall chamber cannot define a new arithmetic
commensurability class.  Its remaining possible novelty is strictly finer:
the integral lattice, its mixed-root reflection subgroup, the chamber, or the
particular divisor-wall extraction.  No equality or finite-index relation of
reflection groups follows merely from rational similarity.

The Scharlau--Kirschmer census does not settle this case: its stated scope is
strongly squarefree reflective Lorentzian lattices, whereas the present
discriminant group has exponent four and determinant 256.

## Official CoxIter corpus boundary

The official repository is <https://github.com/rgugliel/CoxIter>; CoxIter is
described in R. Guglielmetti, *CoxIter -- Computing invariants of hyperbolic
Coxeter groups*, <https://doi.org/10.1112/S1461157015000273>.

The audited repository state was commit
[`b800be48d8240fbabd2d1c9542e61054584ad7fe`](https://github.com/rgugliel/CoxIter/commit/b800be48d8240fbabd2d1c9542e61054584ad7fe),
dated 2025-07-19.  Its 1,214 files with suffix `.coxiter` have global maximum
facet count 50.  The dimension-12 files have only 13, 14, 16, or 18 facets.
Thus the 172-facet diagram is absent from this precisely delimited corpus for
the exact and immediately checkable reason (172>50).

That corpus statement does not imply absence from all articles, theses,
private computations, or differently encoded databases.
