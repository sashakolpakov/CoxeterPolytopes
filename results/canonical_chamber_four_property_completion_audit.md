## 3. Why a lattice-only property 4 is false

\[

\[
 \text{mirror lattice + canonical vector + maximality}

The precise missing gate is P-native simultaneous scheme-theoretic

\[

## 5. Field finiteness after an exact admissible finite-volume full-trace/order-module K3 return

The K3 rank inequality bounds \(d=[k:\mathbf Q]\), but bounded degree alone
does not give finitely many fields.  The frozen rank-four K3 eigen-nef family
in hyperbolic dimension one has fixed polarization square and infinitely many
quadratic fields; its escaping scalar is

\[
                         H\cdot\tau_t(H)=32a-10.           \tag{5.1}
\]

Hyperbolic dimension one is the exact exception.  For every exact admissible
finite-volume full restriction-of-scalars trace/order-module complex-K3
carrier in dimension \(n\ge2\), field finiteness is now automatic, with no
imposed mixed correspondence height.

### 5.1 Noncompact chambers

At a cusp, the incident wall normals span the orthogonal quotient of the
null line: otherwise the compact horosphere section would have a nonzero
recession direction.  Their common fixed space is therefore exactly that
\(k\)-rational null line.  After any nonidentity real embedding of a
totally-real admissible form, the form is definite and has no null line.
Base change of the common kernel gives a contradiction.  Hence

\[
 P\text{ exact admissible, finite-volume and noncompact}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad k=\mathbf Q.                   \tag{5.2}
\]

The proof handles nonsimple cusps, finite projective order modules and
nonprimitive normals.  With the full K3 trace it also gives \(n\le19\), but
does not replace the rational weak-log ceiling \(n\le25\).

### 5.2 Compact chambers

Put \(m=n+1\ge3\).  Choose a facet of minimal visual aperture.  The elementary
narrow-facet calculation bounds every absolute normalized pairing inside its
star by \(14\).  Compactness and the simple finite vertex link make the star
span the whole Lorentz space.  Choose an actual \(k\)-basis of wall normals
from this star.  Its Gram support has exactly one connected Lorentz block.

Let \(k_I\) be the field generated by the cyclic Cartan products of that
block.  If \(k_I\subsetneq k\), a second real embedding of \(k\) fixing
\(k_I\) makes all edge ratios a multiplicative coboundary.  The conjugate
Cartan block is then diagonally similar to the distinguished Lorentz block,
while admissibility requires it to be positive definite.  This contradiction
proves \(k_I=k\); no separate minimal-field assumption is needed.

Reflection-word traces make all block cycles integral.  They use at most
\(m\) basis nodes and every conjugate has absolute value below \(14^m\).
The full-trace inequality \(dm\le20\) gives, for \(d>1\),

\[
                         d\le6,\qquad m\le10.             \tag{5.3}
\]

A minimal generating packet has at most two elements.  The primitive grid
\(x+qy\), \(0\le q\le15\), therefore supplies an integral primitive element
whose conjugate differences are bounded by \(32\cdot14^{10}\).  Consequently

\[
 |\operatorname {Disc}k|
 \le\bigl(32\cdot14^{10}\bigr)^{30}.                    \tag{5.4}
\]

Combining (5.2) and (5.4) proves a literal finite field set for **every**
finite-volume exact admissible primitive full-trace complex-K3 carrier in
hyperbolic dimension at least two whose facet reflections are \(k\)-linear
and preserve the returned finite projective order-module.  The earlier
nondotted-core, simplex, polygon, quadrilateral, pentagon and hexagon
theorems remain sharper calibrations or fallbacks for partial packets, but
they are no longer the arithmetic frontier once the exact carrier is
supplied.

This closes the field-set implication, not Property 4.  It does not construct
the K3 carrier from raw \(P\), prove projected facet effectivity, or show that
the returned eigen-nef section is exhaustive.

## 7. Exact remaining theorem

The ordinary rational lane is closed, several adapted/genus-one branches
have sharp AG endpoints, and the exact admissible finite-volume full
restriction-of-scalars trace/order-module complex-K3 field implication is now
closed.  `RLogComp` and `K3Comp` make two large inner species finite
iff/`EMPTY` problems.  They do not repair the outer theorem: the exact
three-branch disjunction itself is false.  A revised raw-\(P\) theorem must
enlarge the carrier category while retaining

- reduced rational twice-primitive boundaries versus primitive genus-one
  mirrors;
- higher-genus rational facets with their contraction and Picard data;
- non-rational K3 eigen-nef packages with a full restriction-of-scalars
  trace/order module, admissibility, and exact chamber return.  Literal
  physical in-trace normalization is additionally
  required only when invoking the existing finite-native-host theorem.  The
  bounded spanning-root theorem is automatic only after literal finite-sided
  finite-volume full-Picard/full-nef K3 return; it does not promote a
  projected eigenslice to that scope.  For a pinned compact full-Picard
  eigenslice, PHYS/EMB/ROOT decide the same-vector K3 return finitely, but may
  certify `EMPTY`; and
- mixed divisor/action data, or another surface/variety species, broad enough
  to survive **X3** and the absolutely maximal rank-$22$ Borcherds chamber.
  The projected \(\Delta(2,4,6)\) packet remains a positive scoped
  calibration with \(n\le19\), not a universal branch.  Finite projected
  ambient hosts are not required for abstract maximal-chamber counting once
  finite native pairs and reflection maximality are available.

The correct conclusion is therefore:

> AG already turns an exact carrier into the desired dimension bounds and,
> in every exact admissible finite-volume full restriction-of-scalars
> trace/order-module complex-K3 branch of dimension at least two, a finite
> field set.  The weak-log rational and compact full-Picard K3 inner
> searches are finite iff/`EMPTY` compilers, not universal producers.
> Moreover, \(\Delta(2,4,6)\) proves that the advertised exact-return
> direct/weak-log/minimal-trace disjunction cannot be exhaustive for all raw
> maximal arithmetic chambers.  Every finite-sided finite-volume literal
> full-Picard/full-nef K3 return of rank \(3\) through \(20\) still supplies a
> bounded spanning basis of physical square-\((-2)\) facets, finitely many
> Picard hosts, and one complete-root Weyl orbit per oriented host.  The next
> global debt is not to prove the false three-way statement or universal
> projected-root K3.  The projected \(\Delta(2,4,6)\) packet is a positive
> one-input calibration with \(n\le19\), while **X3** and the absolutely
> maximal rank-$22$ Borcherds chamber show that a class-wide compiler must add
> mixed divisor/action data or another carrier category.  If such a compiler
> supplies finitely many native pairs, Nikulin's fixed-data theorem and
> maximality finish the abstract chamber count without finite projected

> global ABSW from AG alone or nonexistence of every possible surface or
> variety carrier.
