# A triangle-only local payment obstruction above fourteen

The [fresh recovery source](../scripts/fresh_sqrt5_rank13_active_triangle_dual_20261017.py) uses the saved exact primal of the [full selected non-ADE model](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_joint_corner_all_nonade_repair_20261017.md) to choose a smaller support. It retains only triangle inequalities whose payment and incident vertex budgets lie within 10^-6 of their respective active values. This numerical motivation affects selection only: validity of the resulting rational dual does not assume complementary slackness or numerical accuracy.

The reduced domain has 5,886 triangle rows, 560 complete whole-forest budgets, and 18,627 nonnegative corner-orbit variables. The [saved certificates](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_active_triangle_dual_20261017.json) give a feasible budget **304649179568/19999999999** and the exact dual lower bound **36557903013600/2400001927753 > 14**, approximately 15.23245. The numerical optimum is 15.2324589775 and is not asserted to be an exact optimum. Generation and a fresh saved reconstruction verify all selected literal indices, exact face payments, 78-pair vertex budgets, and rational dual column inequalities; replay does not rerun the optimizer.

The conclusion concerns nonnegative corner payments determined by an entire finite vertex forest and its unordered marked root pair. Such a rule cannot pay every retained triangle by two while keeping every retained rank-13 vertex budget at most twelve. The stronger bound also motivates a separate exact orthogonal-A1 padding check against the rank-14 target fourteen. Neither interpretation is a dimension theorem: simultaneous incidence and compatibility with one ambient arithmetic quadratic form remain additional global requirements. Discriminant compatibility is being investigated separately, and no dotted-B1 LP was run after this obstruction.
