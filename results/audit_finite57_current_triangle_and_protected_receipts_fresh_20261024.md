## 2. Complete triangle-to-current-table comparison

An actual triangle has three distinct black side roots. Every principal
diagram after deletion of one black belongs to a PSD actual corner. Therefore
every minimal indefinite core contains all three blacks. A core with at most
six roots has a Steiner tree through them and at least two distinct pair
paths avoiding the third mark, each of length at most five. This argument
retains the unbounded low-order native labels.

complete for this domain. It does not treat three pairs in one unchanged
diagram as three actual corners.
