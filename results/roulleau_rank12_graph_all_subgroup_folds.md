# Complete subgroup folds of Roulleau's two graph-only rank-12 K3 stocks

## Result

The complete weighted subgroup denominator is closed for Roulleau's 14-wall
`U+E8(-1)+A1(-1)^2` and 19-wall `U+D8(-1)+A1(-1)^2` nef chambers.  Their
weighted wall groups are respectively `C2` and `D12 = S3 x C2`; all 2 and 10
subgroup conjugacy classes were enumerated.  Exactly three proper classes pass
the fixed-rank, genuine-facet, integral-reflection, exact Coxeter-angle, and
Vinberg finite-volume gates:

| source and class | result | Vinberg audit | fixed/reflection lattice | lineage |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| `U+E8+A1^2`, `C2` | `H10`, 12 facets | 17 finite vertices, 2 cusps, 99 elliptic edges; zero bad edges | fixed determinant 4; unique index-2 odd-unimodular stable maximum | the known 12-wall `I_(1,10)` full chamber, index 1 |
| `U+D8+A1^2`, first `C2` class | `H10`, 15 facets | 42 finite vertices, 10 cusps, 294 elliptic edges; zero bad edges | fixed determinant 16; three incomparable index-4 odd-unimodular stable maxima | index 1054 in each known `I_(1,10)` full reflection group; literal diagram absent from both pinned CoxIter corpora |
| `U+D8+A1^2`, second `C2` class | `H10`, 16 facets | 70 finite vertices, 6 cusps, 426 elliptic edges; zero bad edges | fixed determinant 16 but a different discriminant quadratic form; unique index-4 odd-unimodular stable maximum | index 992 in the known `I_(1,10)` full reflection group; literal diagram absent from both pinned CoxIter corpora |

Thus all three are genuine finite-volume noncompact hyperbolic Coxeter
polytopes.  The first is an old abstract chamber.  The 15- and 16-facet
subgroup chambers are newly extracted here and are bounded-corpus nonmatches;
their worldwide literal-diagram priority remains open.  They do not give a new
arithmetic commensurability class.

The maintained project ledger has one other `H10/15` catch, the rank-14
`U+E8+A1^4` fold.  An exact complete edge-coloured relation-graph comparison
proves that it is not the 15-facet chamber here.  There is no prior `H10/16`
row in the project ledger.

The two determinant-16 fixed lattices have identical Smith invariants but are
not being conflated: their discriminant quadratic-form histograms differ.  It
is only after passing to their reflection-stable odd-unimodular overclosures
that both enter the same classical maximal arithmetic group.

## Exact construction and volume gate

For each published complete wall graph, the first twelve curves form an
integral basis.  The reconstruction has the stated Lorentzian signature and
determinant, recovers the whole wall Gram exactly, and gives integral
coordinates for every extra curve.  Roulleau's printed decompositions of the
degree-two divisor are checked against those coordinates, independently
pinning the extra-wall transcription.

Every survivor has only negative orbit normals, every source-wall orbit is
pairwise disjoint, and the resulting reflections act integrally on the
saturated fixed lattice.  The cone certificate proves every orbit inequality
is an irredundant facet.  Vinberg's criterion is then applied in its complete
form: every elliptic rank-nine edge has exactly two finite or ideal endpoints,
and every full affine zero-neighbourhood is audited.  The three bad-edge and
bad-parabolic counts are all zero; the nonempty ideal-vertex lists prove
noncompactness.

## Lineage and surface-action caveat

All maximal facet-reflection-stable integral overclosures are exhausted through
the discriminant modules.  They are odd unimodular of signature `(1,10)`, hence
the known `I_(1,10)` lattice whose Scharlau--Kirschmer chamber has 12 walls, 17
finite vertices, and two cusps.  Solomon Euler-characteristic ratios give the
exact subgroup indices `1`, `1054`, and `992`.

Roulleau labels both general K3 families `t`: their nontrivial surface
automorphism acts trivially on `NS(X)`.  The diagram involutions used here act
nontrivially on `NS`, so none is asserted to be an automorphism of the general
surface.  The algebraic input is the complete divisor/nef wall stock; a surface
automorphism is not needed for the fixed-cone construction.

For the 16-wall class, the proposed special-period Nikulin lift is now also
ruled out: the K3 complement has maximal totally singular mod-two dimension
six, so it cannot contain the required primitive `E7(-2)`.  Any surface lift
preserving the archived fixed rank must have order at least four; see the
[`H10/16` obstruction and completion criterion](r12_h10_16_nikulin_obstruction.md).
