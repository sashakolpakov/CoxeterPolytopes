## 7. Exact strict contradiction at dimension20

Write f_i for the face numbers of P and C for the sum of all its
angle charges. Section3 and double counting angles give

    C>=sum_(two-faces Q)(5-number_of_sides(Q))
      =5f_2-binomial(20,2)f_0=5f_2-190f_0.              (2)

The last incidence equality uses simplicity: precisely binomial(20,2)
two-faces pass through each finite vertex. Compactness supplies the
ordinary simple convex polytope and its simplicial dual; there are no
cusp correction terms here.

Let h_0,...,h_20 be the nonnegative symmetric h-vector of that dual.
Then h_0=h_20=1,

    f_0=sum_i h_i,
    f_2=sum_i binomial(20-i,2)h_i.

Using h_i=h_(20-i) to symmetrize the coefficient gives exactly

    5f_2-190f_0
      =sum_i [35+(5/2)(i-10)^2]h_i
      >=35f_0+500.                                    (3)

The number500 is not a rounded or strict numerical margin. Each
of h_0 and h_20 contributes250 beyond35f_0; all other squared
terms are nonnegative. The center coefficient is exactly35.
On the other hand, summing (V) over the actual vertices gives
C<=35f_0. This contradicts (2)--(3). Thus no compact arithmetic
Coxeter20-polytope has actual ground field K7, under the stated
source-bound charge theorems and inherited inputs.
