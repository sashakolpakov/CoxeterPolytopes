# Nine finite-volume `H9` polytopes from two K3 doubling cubes

## Outcome

The two divisor-lattice seeds `T1` and `T2` each have exactly four mutually
orthogonal doubling walls:

```text
T1: 4, 7, 12, 14
T2: 1, 5, 9, 14.
```

For every nonempty subset `S`, glue the `2^|S|` seed chambers in the commuting
reflection subgroup generated by those walls.  Exact boundary enumeration of
all 15 subsets per seed gives

```text
T1: 5 edge-labelled isomorphism types,
T2: 4 edge-labelled isomorphism types.
```

The resulting nine types are pairwise nonisomorphic.  They are hyperbolic
Coxeter polytopes in `H9`, have 15 through 18 facets, and are all
finite-volume and noncompact.

The frozen certificate is
[`alexeev_h9_doubling_cubes.json`](../data/enumerations/alexeev_h9_doubling_cubes.json),
generated by
[`certify_alexeev_h9_doubling_cubes.py`](../scripts/certify_alexeev_h9_doubling_cubes.py).

## Why the construction is exact

A doubling wall has no odd Coxeter label.  Reflection across it therefore
glues two copies of the chamber to another Coxeter chamber.  Here the four
selected walls are pairwise perpendicular, so their integral reflections
commute.  Every subset generates an elementary abelian group of exact order
`2^|S|`; iterated doubling removes the selected walls and takes the complete
orbit of every remaining wall.  Direct exact checks establish that

- every boundary normal is primitive and its reflection preserves the same
  saturated rank-ten lattice;
- the orbit-sum interior is timelike and strict on every boundary facet;
- the boundary Gram matrix is acute and every normalized product is a
  Coxeter or parallel product; and
- the nine selected relation diagrams are pairwise nonisomorphic.

The chamber is a union of exactly `2^|S|` copies with disjoint interiors, so
its hyperbolic volume is `2^|S|` times the seed volume.

## Independent finite-volume gates

The gluing theorem already preserves finite volume.  The certificate also
recomputes it independently for every type.  GMP rational double description
enumerates all extreme rays; every ray pairs positively with the chosen
timelike interior, every Lorentz square is positive or zero, and every row
has at least one isotropic ray.  Thus all nine chambers have finite volume,
are noncompact, and have zero hyperideal rays.

A literal Vinberg replay independently enumerates every elliptic rank-eight
edge and every connected affine component.  Every edge has exactly two
finite/ideal endpoints, every affine zero-neighbourhood is a full certified
cusp, and both bad-audit counts are zero in all nine cases.

## Algebro-geometric meaning

All boundary mirrors are obtained by integral isometries from the T1 or T2
seed mirrors.  Consequently the complete doubling cube stays on the same
saturated rank-ten fixed/divisor lattice embedded in the full K3 Picard
lattice and inherits a weak full-Picard divisor carrier.  This is a useful
positive compiler: one algebraic carrier produces several different
finite-volume Coxeter polytopes.

No doubled chamber is asserted to be a nef cone, and the lattice reflections
are not asserted to be surface automorphisms.  Any strong promotion still
requires a geometric action, effective source-curve orbits, and exclusion of
hidden nef walls.  In particular, although the T2 seed now has a strong
invariant-nef realization, doubling crosses its nef walls; that promotion
does not pass to the enlarged chambers.

## Lineage boundary

At pinned CoxIter commit
[`b800be48`](https://github.com/rgugliel/CoxIter/commit/b800be48d8240fbabd2d1c9542e61054584ad7fe),
the 1,214 ordinary files and 2,959 doubling files contain exactly eight
`H9/15` records and no `H9/16`, `H9/17`, or `H9/18` record.  Neither 15-facet
candidate even shares a global relation histogram with the eight recorded
diagrams.  Thus all nine are new to the project and absent from those pinned
corpora.

This is bounded evidence, not a worldwide priority theorem.  The seed
diagrams T1 and T2 are themselves only apparently new after bounded audits,
and no complete classification covers noncompact nonsimple polytopes with
these facet counts.

The construction method is classical: this is an exact application of
[Allcock's doubling-wall lemma](https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.0138), not a new
doubling theorem.  Each row is an index-`2^k` reflection-subgroup chamber in
its T1 or T2 seed group, so the nine chambers add no maximal reflection group,
carrier lattice, or commensurability class beyond the seeds.  Their special
feature is that four doubling reflections commute, turning the finite orbit
into a hypercube and making the complete subset census possible.

The named few-facet classifications do not provide a worldwide denominator
here.  [Tumarkin](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0301133) treats finite-volume
`n+2` facets, [Tumarkin's compact result](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0406226)
treats compact `n+3`, and
[Roberts](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.08451) treats a restricted noncompact
`n+3` class.  These chambers are noncompact and nonsimple with `n+6` through
`n+9` facets.  They also lie outside
[Bredon's ADEG class](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05153), because every row has
parallel facet pairs.  Thus the exact priority statement is: nine
pairwise-nonisomorphic new-to-project chambers and bounded literal-diagram
candidates, with no claim of a new construction, new arithmetic class,
first dimension, or worldwide novelty.
