# Narrow-facet incidence and new geometric caps

The new results are scoped geometric and incidence restrictions, two exact
H9 caps, and a stronger H20 chain obstruction. **No new finite-volume
polytope or construction dimension has been found.** The internally reviewed
all-arithmetic bound-21 and unrestricted finite-volume bound-163 candidates
are unchanged. Compact arithmetic dimension 21 over Q(sqrt5) remains open;
the unrestricted compact ceiling remains 29. External mathematical
validation and priority of the proposed improved bounds remain unresolved.

The [preceding checkpoint](compact21_single_field_and_h9_symmetries_2026_10_16.md)
and its frozen archive are preserved. It contains the field reduction to
Q(sqrt5) at compact dimension 21 and the two latest full-lattice
nonreflectivity proofs. Neither excluded arithmetic host needs a longer
root enumeration. The new geometric caps instead allow nonarithmetic
coefficient extensions.

## Two H9 caps and a compact tetrahedral face

The first [new cap](fresh_nonarithmetic_wall3_cap_20261016.md), with
[independent review](fresh_nonarithmetic_wall3_cap_20261016_review.md), takes
the old 33-wall chamber to 34 actual facets, 95 finite vertices and 257
finite edges. It cuts the old escape and creates three finite vertices.
A replacement strict escape still proves infinite volume.

The second [cap](fresh_nonarithmetic_escape34_20261016.md), independently
[reviewed](fresh_nonarithmetic_escape34_20261016_review.md), gives **35 actual
facets, 98 finite vertices and 263 finite edges**. It closes three old
finite-edge germs and a complete compact tetrahedral three-face. Positive
Gram-dual vertex rays show that all other walls are redundant on this face;
no intrinsic Coxeter-angle claim about that face is needed. There are still
356 open finite-corner germs, and an exact strict spacelike escape remains.

Its projection onto the three-face lies strictly inside the tetrahedron.
An exact positive-ray decomposition consequently proves that no coherent
acute cap perpendicular to all six white support roots can cut this escape.
The proof allows the original chosen center to be removed; it uses a common
interior point of the proposed extended chamber. Nonright contacts to this
support and changed retained walls remain open.

A separate [all-real obstruction](fresh_h9_right31_all_real_escape_20261016.md)
excludes escape-cutting caps on the literal right-to-T-plus-31 support.
The proof uses a continuous norm inequality and the gap before the first
nonright Coxeter angle; there is no field, coefficient-size or label cutoff.
It is not an exclusion of arbitrary caps or of noncutting compatible walls.

edge parameter gives Lorentz signature, positive-definite conjugate and
actual finite endpoint rays. Their local port flows preserve the old
restricted-model obstruction above 40. They are local infinite-volume
chambers, not globally compatible finite-volume polytopes.

Two such fixed long edges at a `D5+2E8` vertex cannot close into a finite
quadrilateral. The [free-pair test](fresh_sqrt5_free_pair_quadrilateral_20261016.md)
and [independent review](fresh_sqrt5_free_pair_quadrilateral_20261016_review.md)
cover all 210 port pairs and every real possible finite new-new pairing.
The Schur determinant remains positive throughout the full interval
`[-2,0]`, forcing two negative directions. The negative rank-closure branch
is dotted and preserves the neighboring finite sectors. This illustrates
the value of coupling edges, but only for the specified edge parameter;
it does not exclude all short faces at that vertex type.

## A genuine narrow-facet frequency restriction, and its present limit

For the two central E8 ports of `D5+2E8`, the
[new narrow-edge argument](fresh_sqrt5_narrow_e8_ports_20261016.md), with
[independent review](fresh_sqrt5_narrow_e8_ports_20261016_review.md), covers
all four same-type reattachments. Their common A1 gives integrality of the
framing product by an actual cyclic product, rather than by normalization
alone. Conjugate definiteness and minimality 14 leave only one scalar
candidate, which is strictly between minus one and zero and hence is not a
Coxeter product. All central same-type narrow returns are excluded.

The common support has 29 possible finite one-root completion types.
Among the 28 other types, at most four deletion ports match the support.
On a selected narrow facet, write `Dc` for `D5+2E8` vertices whose marked
facet root is central E8, `Do` for the other such vertices, and `O` for
all remaining vertices. Counting distinguished edges gives

`Dc+2Do <= sum_U m(U)V_U <= 4O`.

Thus at most **four fifths** of that facet's vertices have type `D5+2E8`.
This is a selected-facet restriction; it is not a whole-polytope frequency
bound and does not force a chosen small two-face through a central port.

The [marked baseline diagnostic](fresh_sqrt5_marked_facet_barrier_20261016.md)
shows why this restriction alone does not finish the argument. The facet
dimension is 20, so the required strict average threshold is **35**, whereas
the old baseline has maximum **38**. Its maximizing marked types are
`A1+D20` with the isolated A1 marked and `D21` with the far long-arm endpoint
marked. The E8 inequality is inactive at both.

The [marked transition census](fresh_sqrt5_marked_maximum_transitions_20261016.md)
tests all 54,040 attachment subsets across their 40 ports. Five D21 ports
lose every return to these maxima, but `A1+D20` still has a narrow same-port
return at every one of its 20 ports. An
[independent review](fresh_sqrt5_marked_flow_20261016_review.md) reconstructs
the baseline maximum and verifies the 20 selected local edges, their 40
future finite endpoint rays and their balanced port flow. It does not repeat
the full attachment census or the five D21 exclusions. The surviving flow
still has cost 38, so this combination of local restrictions cannot force
35. Whole-face compatibility or a changed weight rule is required.

## The H20 chain requires multiple dotted contacts

On the separate 133-wall H20 branch, the
[uniform-contact theorem](fresh_h20_uniform_chain_cap_gate_20261016.md) and
[independent review](fresh_h20_uniform_chain_cap_gate_20261016_review.md)
exclude an added wall meeting all 26 chain walls at one common finite angle.
All 54,744 possible small support profiles are independently reconstructed;
an analytic reduction covers larger labels and nonfinite support contacts.
Both remaining continuous axes have strictly positive products with retained
blocking walls throughout the required parameter interval. The same closed
interval covers the uniform parallel endpoint.

The stronger [nonabelian rotation theorem](fresh_h20_chain_abelian_gate_20261016.md)
now has a completed [independent review](fresh_h20_chain_abelian_gate_20261016_review.md).
The exact chain parameter `C` satisfies a degree-50 polynomial over a
degree-64 field. Independent finite-field calculations give irreducibility
at a degree-one prime over 2017 and irreducible factors of degrees 13 and 37
at one over 359. Simple tower roots, unit denominators, unit leading
coefficients and squarefree reductions justify the number-field deductions.
The unequal residue degrees show that the parameter extension is not Galois,
and hence `C` does not lie in the maximal abelian extension of the rationals.

Five consecutive finite or parallel chain contacts would lie in that
abelian extension. The contact recurrence would either express `C` in it,
or force all chain contacts to be equal, which the uniform theorem excludes.
Therefore **every five consecutive chain walls contain a dotted contact**,
and every coherent added wall has at least five dotted chain contacts.
This applies to arbitrary real new normals retaining this chamber, without
assuming arithmeticity or restricting finite labels. The chamber still has
infinite volume; unequal contacts with sufficiently many dotted relations
remain a construction route.
