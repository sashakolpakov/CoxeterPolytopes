# Complete high-rank fishing denominator for Alexeev's 18 matrices

## Exact scope

Alexeev's ancillary source contains 18 explicit complete mixed-root Coxeter
matrices.  The fixed-section fishing denominator above `H9` is now closed for
all 18, but two logically different closure statements must be kept separate.

- For 17 matrices, the complete weighted wall-diagram group and every
  subgroup conjugacy class have been enumerated: 469 subgroup classes in
  total, of which exactly 11 proper classes have fixed rank at least 11.
- For the exceptional 210-wall `(22,2,0)` matrix, all 28 element classes
  (hence every cyclic fixed section) are enumerated, and a separate exact
  Reynolds/closure argument proves the complete high-rank noncyclic filter.
  It leaves one noncyclic possibility, the certified `V4` class with fusion
  `2a+2c+2c`.  No enumeration of every subgroup conjugacy class of the
  order-241,920 wall group is claimed.

Thus the theorem is:

> Every cyclic or noncyclic subgroup capable of producing a proper fixed
> section in `H10` or above is accounted for in all 18 explicit matrices.
> All subgroup conjugacy classes are exhausted for 17 matrices, not 18.

## The 17 all-subgroup stocks

The 17 matrices split into four independently archived blocks.

| block | stocks | subgroup classes | proper fixed-rank `>=11` classes | outcome |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| ten small stocks | 10 | 49 | 0 | closed negatively above `H9` |
| `(20,2,1)`, `(15,9,1)` | 2 | 42 | 3 | three finite-volume noncompact chambers |
| `(18,4,0)`, `(15,11,1)`, `(15,7,1)` | 3 | 266 | 6 | six finite-volume noncompact chambers |
| dual `(16,6,1)`, `(16,12,1)` | 2 | 112 | 2 | the two casts give one abstract finite-volume noncompact chamber |
| **total** | **17** | **469** | **11** | all subgroup classes exhausted |

The ten negative stocks are

```text
(11,11,1) (12,10,1) (13,9,1) (13,11,1) (14,8,0)
(14,8,1) (14,10,1) (14,12,1) (15,13,1) (16,14,1)
```

Their weighted groups have 49 subgroup classes in total.  The largest
proper fixed rank is 10, so none can produce a fixed polytope above `H9`.

## Exceptional `(22,2,0)` stock

The exceptional wall group has order 241,920.  Its complete 28-class element
census leaves exactly four nonidentity cyclic high-rank classes:
`2d/H10/47`, `3b/H11/22`, `2a/H13/50`, and `2c/H14/98`.  The complete
high-rank noncyclic filter leaves only `V4/H10/34`.  All five casts have
already passed the literal Vinberg finite-volume test and are noncompact.

This closes the high-rank fishing question without silently replacing it by
the much stronger, unproved assertion that every subgroup conjugacy class of
the exceptional group was listed.

## Historical ceiling on priority claims

Allcock's 2006 theorem, [*Infinitely many hyperbolic Coxeter groups through
dimension 19*](https://doi.org/10.2140/gt.2006.10.737), proves infinitely many
isometry classes of finite-volume Coxeter polyhedra in every `H^n` for
`n <= 19`.  Consequently none of the `H10`, `H11`, or `H13` catches here can
be a first example in its dimension.  Absence of a literal normalized Gram
from the pinned Alexeev, CoxIter, and prior-project corpora is only a bounded
diagram comparison; it cannot establish worldwide priority.  The five
unmatched six-section diagrams and the dual 29-wall diagram should therefore
be described only as newly extracted here and unmatched in the stated
bounded corpus.

[certify_alexeev_18_stock_denominator.py](../scripts/certify_alexeev_18_stock_denominator.py).

[alexeev_ten_small_zero_high_denominator.json](../data/enumerations/alexeev_ten_small_zero_high_denominator.json),
