## Why these are genuine finite-volume polytopes

Roulleau's pinned TeX source prints the complete weighted intersection graph
at lines 9669--9827.  The transcription has rank 14 and inertia `(1,13,6)`;
the leading curves `A1,...,A14` form an integral Néron--Severi basis of
determinant `-16`, and all 20 wall rows reconstruct integrally in that basis.

For every one of the 13 high subgroup classes, the certificate computes the
saturated fixed lattice, not merely the rational fixed space.  Every wall
orbit consists of pairwise disjoint `(-2)`-curves, its orbit sum has norm
`-2|orbit|`, and reflection in that sum is an integral involutive isometry of
the saturated fixed lattice.  Exact cone enumeration then proves that every
retained inequality is a genuine irredundant facet and that there is a strict
timelike interior point.

Finally, Vinberg's literal edge-endpoint test is applied to each of the five
distinct diagrams.  It finds zero bad elliptic edges, finite or ideal
endpoints for every edge, and validates every full affine zero-neighbourhood.
The numbers of parabolic-neighbourhood audits are respectively
`39,18,16,7,7`.  Thus none of the five volume assertions is inferred merely
from Lorentzian signature, rational polyhedrality, or a cusp count.

The discriminant-module calculation is exhaustive: it forms every
reflection-invariant totally isotropic subgroup and closes the resulting
subgroups under sums.  All maximal-overlattice bases, Grams, roots, and
reflection matrices are frozen in the lineage JSON.  In `TYPE_3` the two
even maxima and the odd maximum have the same cyclic order-three bilinear
value but are separated by parity.  In `TYPE_5` the nonzero discriminant
elements have bilinear value `1/3`, distinguishing SK entry 4 from the other
odd determinant-three genus, whose value is `2/3`.

For `TYPE_1`, the three maximal overclosures are different sublattices of the
same rational space but are all isometric to `I_(1,12)` by uniqueness of the
odd indefinite unimodular lattice.  The candidate Euler characteristic is
`21421/1471492915200`; the full `P12` characteristic is
`691/382588157952000`, so the index is the same `8060` for each maximum.
For `TYPE_4`, the ratio

`(-31/5748019200) / (-1/183936614400) = 992`.

Both `H11` types have zero Coxeter Euler characteristic, as do their
containing full groups.  Their finite containment is proved, but an Euler
ratio cannot recover the numerical index.  `TYPE_5` has exact normalized
diagram equality with its complete full chamber, so its index is one.

## Algebraic status

The carrier is genuinely algebro-geometric: the ambient 20 inequalities are
the complete `(-2)`-curve nef walls of Roulleau's K3 surface, and the fixed
lattices and orbit mirrors are constructed inside its divisor lattice.
However, the general surface has `Aut(X)=C2 x C2`, while the complete weighted
wall diagram has the much larger group `C2 x S5`.  This certificate therefore
calls the 13 actions diagram/Néron--Severi isometries and does not assert that
they all lift to automorphisms of a K3 surface.  Surface-action realization,
abstract-diagram priority, and maximal-reflection lineage are deliberately
separate columns of the ledger.
