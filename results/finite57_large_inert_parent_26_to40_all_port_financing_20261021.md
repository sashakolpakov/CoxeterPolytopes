## 1. Unique recipient, with all parent ports included

or two A1 and a B/D piece. Here D3=A3 and B1=A1; no connected
piece of rank17 becomes A17. Exceptional affine ranks are at most8.

Consequently the p>=26 parent supplies NO A17, and the other parents
have total rank M<=14 and cannot supply one either. Thus every actual

seal. Likewise the source has exactly one whole D16: no other inert
factor can have rank16. These statements hold at every deletion port,
