# Q(√2) dimension bound and a discriminant-31 obstruction

The new research result is a proof candidate that **compact arithmetic
hyperbolic Coxeter polytopes with ground field Q(√2) have dimension at most 20**.
The full classification and its independent review are linked below. Together
with the frozen cubic-seven candidate, this would leave **Q(√5) as the only
ground field in dimensions 22–29**. The overall arithmetic ceiling remains 29.
Internal exact verification is distinct from external mathematical validation
and establishing whether the result is new in the literature.

A separate new rank-ten arithmetic lattice was tested constructively and
proved nonreflective by an exact infinite-order chamber symmetry. No new
finite-volume polytope or new example dimension was obtained.

## The dimension-bound argument

- The [Q(√2) Lannér catalogue](fresh_sqrt2_lanner_catalogue_20261015.md)
  gives the complete finite alphabet 2,3,4,6,8, nine triangle types, and the
  one-4 cycles of orders 4 and 5. Its all-label reduction accompanies its
  exact finite enumeration.
- The [complete reduction of quadrilaterals with no distance-two marked corner](fresh_sqrt2_quad_exhaustion_20261015.md)
  includes shared white vertices and every cross contact on two minimal
  opposite indefinite pieces. It starts with 51 marked pieces and 6,010
  identifications, producing 115 labelled supports in 52 marked classes.
  Forty classes force two negative directions; one fails the arithmetic
  cycle field. Nine finite-label and two dotted families remain.
- All 44 one-extra-white finite-label profiles surviving the finite-corner
  and short-path tests are checked exactly.
  Seven singular cores admit none. The two nonsingular cores can complete
  only to those sealed cores. The proof treats external components of
  arbitrary size, including components joining two core vertices.
- The [pendant-tree lemma](fresh_sqrt2_dotted_tail_20261015.md), with a
  [separate independent review](fresh_sqrt2_dotted_tail_20261015_review.md),
  classifies the two dotted families after the general attachment reduction.
  Rank and arithmetic integrality leave three local stars. None is asserted
  to give a finite-volume ambient chamber.
- The [independent weight calculation](fresh_sqrt2_weight_bound_20261015.md)
  and its [review](fresh_sqrt2_weight_bound_20261015_review.md) assign weights
  only to entire finite components. Every triangle receives at least 2 and
  every quadrilateral at least 1; each vertex receives at most 2n−2 for n≥9.
  At n=21 the strict h-vector lower bound exceeds 40, contradicting the
  upper bound 40. The contradiction persists in every higher dimension.

## Arithmetic scope and algorithm choice

Every arithmetic lattice generated by hyperbolic reflections is of the
simplest type, so the arithmetic reflection problem already lies in the
quadratic-form setting. A finite-volume arithmetic example with nonrational
ground field is compact. Over Q, the noncompact arithmetic bound is 21,
with dimension 20 excluded and 21 attained. Compactness and arithmeticity
must therefore be kept separate when applying the Borcherds dimension-21
record. [Belolipetsky, §§1–2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.03111).

Vinberg's enumeration is applicable to admissible number-field lattices,
as used in the construction below. Allcock's published edgewalking algorithm
specifically works over Z; its geometric idea does not by itself supply a
complete algorithm for every totally real field or for an unrestricted
nonarithmetic search. [Allcock, p. 2](https://web.ma.utexas.edu/users/allcock/research/edgewalking3.pdf).

The current arithmetic dimension programme is therefore:

| Case | Current conclusion |
|---|---|
| Noncompact arithmetic | Published n≤21, n≠20; 21 attained |
| Compact arithmetic, ground field Q(cos(2π/7)) | Frozen internally reviewed candidate n≤20 |
| Compact arithmetic, ground field Q(√2) | New internally reviewed candidate n≤20 |
| Compact arithmetic, all ground fields | Overall upper bound still 29; Q(√5) remains in 22–29 |
| Compact Coxeter, without arithmeticity | Published upper bound still 29 |
| General finite-volume Coxeter | Frozen internally reviewed candidate n≤166; unchanged here |

Vinberg's published high-dimensional field restriction is used only in
dimensions at least 22. Thus excluding Q(√2) and the cubic field does not
by itself classify every possible ground field in dimension 21.

## A complete obstruction for the new construction host

The [height-20 pilot](fresh_frontier_construction_20261015.md) used

    L = E8 ⊥ [[−2√2,−1], [−1,2]] over Z[√2].

Its determinant ideal has norm 31, and its complete primitive reflector
norm alphabet is 2 and 2(9+5√2). The first prefix had 13 walls, three finite
vertices, and a strict infinite-volume escape. An order-two symmetry gave
a fourteenth actual wall but did not establish nonreflectivity.

The [height-100 continuation](fresh_norm31_continuation_20261015.md) enumerated
32 exact control levels and 11,882 recursion nodes, producing 28 walls.
The complete finite-corner census has 11 vertices and ten finite edges.
An exact integral Lorentz map between two complete outward corner sectors
has determinant −1 and trace 9+5√2>10. It preserves the full reflection
chamber and has infinite order, proving the literal lattice nonreflective.

The [independent review](fresh_norm31_continuation_20261015_review.md) uses
separate field arithmetic to check all wall and facet witnesses, a different
finite-clique enumeration, the matrix and its integral inverse, and both
corner-persistence decompositions. The complete Vinberg search also passes
saved replay. This obstruction concerns the literal lattice; it is neither
a dimension bound nor a classification of all commensurable lattices.
