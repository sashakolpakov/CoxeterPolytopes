## Mathematical statement supplied by a successful batch

Fix an **actual ambient dimension 58** corner or face lying in a named source
domain. Assume the source's geometric argument correctly identifies its
whole marked finite/affine factors, physical corner incidence, and native
profile or equality model. For each selected positive terminal in that batch,
the conservative current literal weight is at least its chosen lower. For
each explicitly recomputed receipt, its listed corners are distinct and their
conservative current weights sum to at least one.

This is a statement about current terminal coefficients at declared geometric
and finite-output premises. It does not prove the completeness of the original
geometric domain reduction, its inertia exclusions, all extra-white sealing,
the full G4 inventory, all-rank geometry, or the finite57 theorem. An inherited
`PASS` string is a packet schema check, not a new validation of that packet.

Finite whole-component rank is at most 58. At an ideal corner the rank of an
affine factor is at most 57, hence its order at most 58. The earlier dimension
59--65 rules and bounded higher-tail stages are outside this binding. These
ambient facts must precede use of the finite loops below; they are not inferred
from a successful loop.

## Exact domain and soundness of the current evaluation

Native physical graphs are reconstructed with norm-two diagonal and exact
unit, square-root-two or square-root-three products, or read from the frozen
exact Gram packet. Rationally scaled physical roots remain allowed: the
engine derives native labels from normalized squared products. It exhausts
all full edge-and-nonedge isomorphisms to its finite/affine targets and requires
one invariant current marked value. Unsupported labels or ambiguous types
fail; there is no floating-point label inference or pruning.

The controller source was compared with the named generators' affine
numberings, deletion sets, path attachments, marks and supporting-white
sets. The inherited outputs are inputs, not claimed re-enumerations. In
particular, D10's mixed whole-affine E8 factor has order nine; B12's immediate
roles are read in their stored local node numbering; the C12 proper cases
reuse the identical B11 certificate rather than constructing a new corner.

The engine successor's small-affine graph recipes were separately source
read: E6/E7/E8 stars have arm lengths (2,2,2), (1,3,3), (1,2,5); F4 has
path labels 3,3,4,3; G2 has 3,6; affine A1 has native parallel label 99.
No such source reading is reported as execution.

No unmarked component is credited, even if it happens to appear completely
in a displayed Gram. The controller supplies neither `extra`, `complete`
nor `sealed_witnesses`. Positive Boolean/context bonuses are therefore
ignored. For a potentially guarded finite D13 long-end/short-leaf pair, the
engine caps the incomplete-context lower at one half. It does not subtract
the ranks of displayed unsealed components to rule out future guards. Actual
whole marked-factor proofs remain essential; the engine cannot infer them
from a displayed finite subdiagram.

The small-type cross check includes A1--A5, B2--B5, D4/D5, F4, H3/H4, and
one symbolic I2 descriptor. The last means **all I2(m), m >= 5**, because the
printed cross weight is independent of m. A2 and B2 already cover m=3,4.
It is not an enumeration of finitely many angle labels in that symbolic
cross statement. Unsupported labels in a physical fixed Gram still fail.

## Termination, resource boundaries and reproducibility

All model lists are finite and exact-key duplicates are rejected. Physical
matrix order is at most 64, each source read is at most 4 MiB, at most 100
manifest entries are accepted, and each output collection is capped at
10,000 rows/keys. Engine search shares one counter with a one-million-step
refusal: exhaustion fails the batch, never truncates a purported complete
enumeration. Exact scalar syntax and radical representations have their
engine bounds. Positive outputs are all evaluated without float pruning.

The native corridor finite loops have bounded rank, finite BFS and finite
marked pairs; the unbounded I2 parameter has been eliminated by the literal
coefficient identity, not a bounded sample. These establish halting or
explicit refusal; they do not promise that every batch fits the outer
59-second admission window.

Before printing, the complete JSON is serialized and its UTF-8 byte count,
including the final newline, must be at most 500 KiB, below the outer
512-KiB stdout limit. An oversize packet refuses with no partial JSON or
silently omitted provider keys. The four rank shards remain unchanged;
whether any needs smaller resource batches is an execution question, not
a claimed failure or permission to truncate a complete key domain.

Root must run each selector through the existing one-process guarded research
runner with the shared exclusive lock, the unchanged admission/floor limits,
and `--parent-lock`. This script checks that the shared lock is held, refuses
optimized execution, verifies selected input pins before use and again after
the batch, and rechecks its own bytes and manifest. It does not replace the
process-tree monitor or provide an independent heap/time gate.

A successful run reports its precise read inputs, current per-corner values,
chosen lowers, distinct-corner totals, remaining premises and outgoing links.
No run has occurred as of this source freeze. A future receipt must retain
