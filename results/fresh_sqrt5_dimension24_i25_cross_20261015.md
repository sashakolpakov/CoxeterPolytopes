## The rule

Let `a` be the number of entire A1 components in the finite vertex diagram, `b` the number of entire I2(5) components, and `h` the truth value of `b > 0`. Preserve the frozen q2/H4 baseline. Every additional coefficient is nonnegative.

- A connected marked-pair bonus depends on its entire component and marked-pair orbit, `a`, and `h`.
- An A1-to-component-root pair receives `u(a,h)/a`. Its destination can be any non-A1 component, including I2(5).
- An I2(5)-to-component-root pair receives `v(a)/(2b)`. Its destination excludes A1 and I2(5), so this never duplicates the preceding family.
- Other disconnected pairs receive zero.

Missing variables have value zero. The full variable order and rational coefficients are in the certificate. There are 11,636 variables and 4,305 nonzero bonuses: 3,897 connected bonuses, 277 A1 cross bonuses, and 131 I2(5) cross bonuses. The latter use 107 candidate rooted-component orbits. The coefficients were rounded upward to a 1/10,000 grid and then rechecked exactly; no numerical optimality claim is made.

## Complete spectator and vertex counts

There are 2,516 input face rows, including every dimension-feasible singular stopping state of the completed finite queue and the earlier conservative families. For a core with residual spectator rank `s`, the verifier enumerates every common spectator pair `(e,f)` of A1 and I2(5) counts satisfying

`e >= 0`, `f >= 0`, and `s-e-2f = 0` or `s-e-2f >= 2`.

The remaining rank can be filled by non-A1, non-I2(5) finite components: A2 and A3 already realize every integer rank at least two. This is a conservative enumeration and does not assume that every such spectator choice is arithmetic. The same `(e,f)` is added at all four corners. The resulting 65,300 face cases coalesce into 31,446 distinct rational inequalities. All pass.

At a vertex with fixed `(a,b)`, summing the A1 cross angles contributes `u(a,h)` once per destination root. Summing all I2(5) cross angles contributes `v(a)` once per eligible destination root, because there are exactly `2b` source roots. Thus the total remains additive over components after fixing `(a,b)`. An exact unbounded rank dynamic program over the remaining finite types checks all 157 feasible contexts at rank 24; A1 and I2(5) components themselves are counted explicitly.

The maximum 53.5054 occurs at
