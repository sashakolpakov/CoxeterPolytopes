## A source inconsistency that must not be hidden

The coordinate/equivalence lane in Roulleau's source determines the divisor
configuration exactly: the `A1,...,A19` graph, the statement that
`A1,...,A14` is an Néron--Severi basis, the coordinates of
`A20,A22,A24,A26`, and the four relations
`A20+A21 = A22+A23 = A24+A25 = A26+A27 = 2D2` reconstruct all 27 classes.
The resulting Gram has rank 14, inertia `(1,13,13)`, and diagonal `-2`.

The final printed cross-incidence TikZ picture is not compatible with these
data.  Under literal last-draw semantics its Gram has rank 24 and inertia
`(6,18,3)`.  Giving the thick `A24--A25` segment precedence over its duplicate
thin segment still gives rank 22 and inertia `(5,17,5)`.  The literal picture
differs from the coordinate Gram in 25 lower-triangular entries.  There is
also a prose-index typo in the `D132` intersections: the printed lists overlap
at `A18` and omit `A14`, whereas the displayed coordinates force the intended
values on `A14,...,A17`.

Accordingly, the coordinate/equivalence reconstruction is authoritative.
The regression test deliberately freezes both impossible printed ranks, so a
future transcription cannot silently switch back to the picture.

All wall-orbit sums come from pairwise disjoint `(-2)`-curves.  Their
reflections are integral involutive isometries of the saturated fixed

The two even-dimensional indices are exact Solomon characteristic ratios.
Both characteristics vanish in dimension 11, so the two `H11` containments
are proved finite but their numerical indices are not recovered by this
method.  The original determinant-12 `C3` section has the same complete
finite-special-subset counts and seven cusps as SK List11 entry 12; this is a
strong census collision, not yet an asserted explicit chamber conjugacy.

None of the four normalized relation matrices matches the pinned current
CoxIter ordinary corpus or its separate index-two-doubling archive.  This is
a bounded literal-diagram nonmatch only: worldwide priority remains open.
All four lie in known arithmetic lattices, so the construction creates no new
arithmetic commensurability class.

## What is genuinely algebro-geometric

The ambient object is not an arbitrary matrix: it is the complete divisor nef
cone of Roulleau's K3 surface, and every folded mirror is formed inside its
Néron--Severi lattice from an orbit of actual curve classes.  This is the
promised divisor-lattice carrier for the four hyperbolic polytopes.

It is nevertheless essential not to upgrade a graph symmetry into a surface
automorphism without proof.  Roulleau's `t` tag says that the hyperelliptic
involution of the general surface acts trivially on `NS(X)`; the general
surface has automorphism group `C2`.  Therefore none of the selected
nonidentity diagram subgroups is claimed to act on the general K3 surface.
A special-period lift has not been assessed, and no such lift is required for
the divisor-cone section or the hyperbolic-volume proof.
