## The theorem-status split

The hostile artifact keeps three logically different claims separate.

1. Six explicit descendants are independently reconstructed from the seed
   reflection actions. Their boundary root sets agree exactly with the
   primary archive, and their cones receive fresh GMP and Vinberg replays.
2. The finite `C2^3` claim is a complete seven-subset census. It does not
   require an asymptotic argument.
3. The three infinite-family claims use closed orbit proofs. Each universal
   formula has a separate group-injectivity, wall-stabilizer, collision, facet
   count, and convex-doubling gate.

## Infinite-dihedral normal forms

For every parallel pair `(a,b)`, exact arithmetic gives

\[
a^2=b^2=-2,
\qquad (a,b)=2,
\qquad \delta=a+b,
\qquad \delta^2=0,
\]

with primitive nonzero `delta`. Let `s_a,s_b` be the two reflections,
`T=s_a s_b`, and `K=T-I`. In every family,

\[
\operatorname{rk}K=2,
\qquad \operatorname{rk}K^2=1,
\qquad K^3=0.
\]

Consequently the binomial identity

\[
T^z=I+zK+\frac{z(z-1)}2K^2
\]

holds for every integer `z`, including negative `z`. Moreover,

\[
T^z(a)=a+2z\delta.
\]

Since `a` and `delta` are independent, all translation powers are distinct.
The determinant separates `T^z` from `T^z s_a`, giving the injective normal
form

```text
T^z  or  T^z*s_a,  z in Z.
```

This proves each selected parallel pair generates `D_infinity`; it is not a
finite word search.

For the two path-times-cube families, every cube reflection fixes `a` and
`b`, the cube masks are pairwise distinct, and the path and cube actions
commute. If a product element is the identity, its path factor fixes `a,b`
and hence is trivial; the cube mask is then trivial. This proves the factors
are exactly `D_infinity x C2` and `D_infinity x C2^2`.

## The two one-parameter facet formulas

The audit solves globally, for every nonselected seed wall `r,s`, every
equation

\[
T^z s_a^\epsilon c(r)=\pm s,
\qquad z\in\mathbb Z,
\]

where `c` ranges over all cube masks. Pairing with `a` and `delta` solves `z`
exactly; no range cutoff occurs. There are no cross-seed-wall identifications
and no opposite-sign identifications. The complete stabilizer of each wall
is precisely the subgroup generated by selected reflections orthogonal to
that wall. Path endpoint orbits also have zero collision with every
nonselected orbit.

For an `N`-chamber path beginning across `a`, the internal gallery has

```text
floor(N/2) edges labelled a,
floor((N-1)/2) edges labelled b.
```

A wall occurrence joins across exactly those internal edges whose reflection
fixes it. After the orthogonal cube factor is included, the exact component
sums are:

| Seed | nonendpoint coefficient | `a` joins | `b` joins | two endpoints | result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `A14_8_1_C02` | 15 | 11 | 11 | 2 | `2+15N-11 floor(N/2)-11 floor((N-1)/2)` |
| `A14_10_1_C02` | 15 | 12 | 12 | 2 | `2+15N-12 floor(N/2)-12 floor((N-1)/2)` |

Using

\[
\left\lfloor\frac N2\right\rfloor+

gives `4N+13` and `3N+14` for every positive `N`. Because the two join

## The rectangular formula for all `m,n`

Write the two parallel pairs as `(a,b)=(1,14)` and `(c,d)=(8,15)`. All four
cross products vanish, the two translations commute, and

\[
a+b=c+d=\delta.
\]

The exact rank check on `a,c,delta` and the dihedral normal forms prove that
the generated group is faithfully `D_infinity x D_infinity`, rather than
only a quotient of that abstract product.

Seven nonselected walls, numbered `3,4,5,6,7,10,11`, are fixed by all four
generators. The remaining four corner bases are walls `2,9,12,13`. For
`T_x=s_a s_b` and `T_y=s_c s_d`, their pairings after arbitrary integer
translations are:

| parity | wall | grid coordinate | pairings with `(a,b,c,d)` |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `AC` | 2 | `(2k+1,2l+1)` | `(-4k, 4k+2, -4l, 4l+2)` |
| `AD` | 9 | `(2k+1,2l)` | `(-4k, 4k+2, 2-4l, 4l)` |
| `BC` | 12 | `(2k,2l+1)` | `(4-8k, 8k, -8l, 8l+4)` |
| `BD` | 13 | `(2k,2l)` | `(4-8k, 8k, 4-8l, 8l)` |

These are symbolic matrix identities in `k,l`. Norm `-4` versus `-8`
recovers the first parity; the `c`-pairing modulo 4 or 8 recovers the second;
the displayed linear values then recover `k,l` uniquely. Every corner has
positive pairing 2 or 4 with `delta`, excluding equality with the negative
of another corner. Thus there are no additional identifications anywhere in
the infinite grid.

For an `m`-cell path, the two alternating corner classes have component
counts

\[
m-\left\lfloor\frac m2\right\rfloor,
\qquad
m-\left\lfloor\frac{m-1}2\right\rfloor,
\]

whose sum is `m+1`; the second direction similarly contributes `n+1`.
Therefore the four corner classes give exactly `(m+1)(n+1)` walls. The four
endpoint mirrors remain distinct for all translation exponents and cannot
collide with a fixed or corner wall. Hence

\[
F(m,n)=4+7+(m+1)(n+1)=mn+m+n+12
\]

for every `m,n>=1`.

## Convexity, volume, and noncompactness

Every selected seed wall passes the exact even-angle doubling-wall gate.
Orthogonal factors stay fixed while doubling proceeds in another direction.
Induction with the classical doubling lemma therefore makes each finite
gallery or box a convex Coxeter polytope. The normal-form injectivity proves
that its seed-chamber interiors are distinct, so its volume is exactly the
displayed number of copies. The resulting reflection subgroup has finite
index in the nonuniform seed group and is therefore still nonuniform.

This is the classical Allcock doubling/redoubling mechanism. The project
contribution is the exact Alexeev/K3 seed carrier, the product decomposition,
and the certified formulas—not a new general gluing theorem.

## Bounded lineage and scope

The six diagrams have zero exact encoded labelled-graph matches in the
pinned ordinary and index-two CoxIter snapshots. Five facet headers are
absent entirely; only the 15-facet cube row has same-header records, namely
the eight pinned index-two `H9/15` records, and it has neither a relation-
histogram nor an exact graph collision with them.

This is a bounded corpus statement. It is not a worldwide priority theorem,
especially where a corpus record leaves a dotted distance unspecified. The
audit also makes no nef-cone or surface-automorphism claim.
