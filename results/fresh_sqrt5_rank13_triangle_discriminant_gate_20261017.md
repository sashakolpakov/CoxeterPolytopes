# A shared discriminant does not remove the rank-thirteen local payment obstruction

The [fresh exact check](../scripts/fresh_sqrt5_rank13_triangle_discriminant_gate_20261017.py) reconstructs one literal triangle for each of the 3,649 positive rows in the [triangle-only dual above fourteen](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_active_triangle_dual_20261017.md). It independently matches all three whole-forest corner codes and their multiplicities to the saved payment row. Of these stars, **651 have only even-sized connected components** and determine **57 distinct squareclasses** of ambient determinant. The remaining **2,998 stars contain odd-sized components** and retain scaling freedom.

The [saved exact results](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_triangle_discriminant_gate_20261017.json) restrict the original dual separately to every represented determinant class, retaining the flexible stars in each case. They also test an arbitrary unrepresented class. Every one of these 58 restricted duals still has lower bound above the rank-13 target twelve. Their minimum is **28068746415840/2302464560863**, approximately 12.1907398242; the maximum is approximately 12.9771691895. The flexible stars alone already give **28068746269440/2302464541063 > 12**. None of these restricted versions of this particular dual exceeds fourteen.

This is a precise negative result for the proposed refinement: requiring one common determinant squareclass is insufficient to restore a corner-local budget of twelve on the witnessed triangle domain. It is not a global dimension bound, does not prove that these stars share a complete quadratic form, and does not show an optimal bound for any class-restricted model. Hasse invariants and simultaneous compact incidence remain additional conditions.

The gauge argument is as follows. Let G and H be two K-valued nonsingular Gram matrices for the same full normalized acute diagram, with totally positive diagonal entries. Set c_i=H_ii/G_ii. Every nonzero edge gives c_i c_j=(H_ij/G_ij)^2, so the c_i have one squareclass on each connected component. Choose a representative c from one c_i, hence totally positive, and write c_i=c s_i^2 with the physical signs s_i>0. The negative off-diagonal signs then give H=c D G D on that component, where D is diagonal with entries s_i. Consequently its determinant changes by c^m times a square, where m is the component rank. Even m fixes the determinant squareclass. For odd m, an arbitrary totally positive component rescaling remains possible without changing normalized contacts or reflection Cartan coefficients. This proof received a separate narrow mathematical read from the construction agent; that read was not an arithmetic replay.

Each triangle has fourteen independent roots in a fourteen-dimensional K form, so its determinant is an ambient determinant rather than that of a proper subspace. If an odd component is present, a desired determinant with the same physical/conjugate signs differs by a totally positive factor, which that component can absorb. Such a star cannot be rejected by determinant alone. The rank-14 A1-padding certificate is separate: its ambient form has dimension fifteen and is not assigned a fixed determinant class by this calculation.

For the even-component stars, the check constructs an explicit K gauge. Ordinary 3/5 bonds use norm-two roots and products -1 and -phi. A framing 4/6/10 bond is a bridge; multiply root norms on its new-root side by z=2, 3, or 2+phi respectively, and give the bridge product -z. All scale factors are totally positive. Exact finite-component inverses and a last Schur pivot verify physical inertia (13,1) and conjugate positive definiteness and compute the determinant. Two determinant classes are equal exactly when their ratio has a square root in Q(sqrt5); the verifier solves that question using rational square tests in both possible norm branches, with no numerical classification.

Only one saved literal representative of each merged payment row is used. Its presence supplies a valid witnessed inequality in the corresponding determinant class. Absence of that representative does not establish absence of every other realization of the same row. Therefore the restricted dual lower bounds are valid, but this package supplies neither complete class-specific face coverage nor a class-specific feasible weighting theorem. Generation and fresh saved reconstruction check the same exact literal mappings, determinants, squareclasses, and restricted dual column bounds.
