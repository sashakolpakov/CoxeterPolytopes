The matrix normalization also differs from the producer. A separately chosen spanning forest assigns each vertex the formal product of the original norm-two edge magnitudes along its tree path. ## Payments and the unpaid qualification

All **84** initial H4-paid classes have the asserted actual finite corner component and marked endpoint pair. This payment persists under arbitrarily many external whites: a spherical connected diagram containing an induced H4 is already H4, since the only finite connected diagrams with a 5-bond are I2(5), H3 and H4. The same proof applies to all 62 contact profiles that create such a payment. Arithmetic feasibility of a paid profile need not be established for this sufficient weight conclusion.

The two initial affine exclusions, the 24 unresolved dotted contact domains and all 286 finite contact domains are reproduced. The remaining finite classes divide as follows:

- **112 singular cores:** no nonempty accepted contact remains in an **unpaid** quadrilateral.
- **44 nonsingular cores:** the same unpaid sealing property holds.
- **130 nonsingular cores:** 458 unpaid profiles remain collectively.

The first-contact argument is valid with this qualification. If an actual full quadrilateral remains unpaid, each individual external white node must be orthogonal to every core node for a sealed class. A chain through initially orthogonal whites cannot evade the argument, because it would require a first edge from the core. Arbitrary components entirely orthogonal to the core remain possible.

For the 44 nonsingular cores, such an orthogonal extension is incompatible with a complete quadrilateral star: the external white block is positive definite, so the full block-diagonal Gram would remain nonsingular, while |W|+4 normals lie in ambient dimension |W|+3. They are therefore excluded **only in the unpaid case**. A paid extension is not claimed impossible. The 112 singular cores are not excluded by this rank argument and still need a suitable weight or further obstruction.

The 369 dotted rows are structurally admissible unresolved rows, not certificates of arithmetic realization. Multiple external whites, their mutual contacts, the full-star rank and the dotted parameter remain open for the surviving cases. The review does not promote a complete one-node calculation into a complete star classification.
