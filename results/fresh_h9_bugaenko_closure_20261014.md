## Complete case-0 closure

Case 0 previously had 864 complete-table necessary partial labelings. Literal permutations within its twin blocks give a verified group of order 288. The 864 labelings split into six orbits of 144. For every orbit representative, testing the least nonright label at the remaining meeting pairs forces enough of those pairs to be right that two disjoint minimal nonfaces become orthogonal.

In the first four orbits these negative blocks are the Lannér diagrams on `{0,1,2,3}` and `{9,10,11,12,13}`. In the other two they are the first of these and the dotted pair `{4,5}`. Thus every labeling has at least two independent negative directions, impossible in the Lorentz space of H9. All six proof trees close at their first obstruction; no numerical distance fitting is involved.

## Early exact gates and completed family

The new search applies three necessary tests before expanding the next missing face:

* Unknown meeting pairs are temporarily right for a spherical-diagram relaxation. If assigning label 3 already makes an actual vertex nonspherical, the pair is forced right in every completion. This follows from monotonicity of the nonnegative cosine matrix.
* Two disjoint minimal nonfaces with every cross pairing forced or assigned right require two negative directions. Their internal labels need not yet be assigned to use this obstruction.
* A fully fixed 11-by-11 Gram minor with nonzero determinant modulo 241 proves rank at least eleven. Every such minor avoids dotted distances and the variable category of labels at least seven.

The modular map sends the exact cosine ring generated by `sqrt(2), sqrt(3), sqrt(5)` and inverses of two into the finite field of order 241. Consequently a nonzero residue proves a nonzero characteristic-zero determinant. It is not a floating-point rank test.

Finite labels at least seven share one necessary category: they can only occur in isolated rank-two spherical components, and the triangle Lannér condition is constant throughout that category. Parallel pairs are separate; for this compact incidence problem a missing pair is strictly dotted. These gates impose no metric-field or dotted-distance cutoff.

The [DFS](../scripts/fresh_h9_bugaenko_closure_20261014_search.py) caches explicit partial-state relabelings within literal twin blocks. Each permitted permutation fixes the missing-face constraints. The cache key is the full relabeled state, with unknown, right, and dotted entries distinguished, rather than a graph digest. The [largest-first variant](../scripts/fresh_h9_bugaenko_closure_20261014_large_first.py) changes only constraint order. It closes case 20 in 126 evaluated states after the earlier ordering reached 10,000 states.

For the central carrier, the complete operation census contains 15,790 ordered recipe orbits. A missing face of order greater than five excludes 15,582 immediately.
