# Incidence profiles, a new H22 chamber, and a field obstruction

The incidence graph supplies **564 exact new walls**: four replace the preceding central pair, then a coherent family of 560 cuts the saved escape. All retained wall families remain compatible. A different exact strict escape proves that the resulting 5,398,110-facet chamber still has infinite volume.

The more consequential result is a restriction on further completion. One retained deficient cusp cannot acquire any incident wall whose projective normal is defined over a real field not containing √11, in the fixed physical coordinates below. Thus finite additions of rational directions cannot complete this chamber. This is a scoped obstruction, not nonexistence of finite-volume Coxeter polytopes in H22.

## Four walls from the incidence graph

Use the known Borcherds H21 source with 210 normals W and form Q of signature (21,1). The 42 norm-two normals split into two sets P,L of size 21. Their bipartite incidence matrix A satisfies

`A Aᵀ = 4I + J`, with five 1s in each row.

Write a=sum(P), b=sum(L), and define

`v=(2a+5b)/21`, `v′=(5a+2b)/21`, `h=v+v′=(a+b)/3`.

The coordinates are integral. Exact contractions give `v²=v′²=−2`, `(v,v′)=−5`, `h²=−14`. The products of v with old short walls are −1 on P and zero on L, with the roles reversed for v′. Both pair −4 with every old long wall.

In the physical form `diag(Q,1)`, add `(v,±2)` and `(v′,±2)`. Their norms are two. The 846 changed pairs with the old210 and each other consist of 84 right, 86 π/3 and 676 dotted relations. The center `(h,0)` is strict timelike. As for every construction here, nonpositive distinct pairings allow projection of that center onto each wall, proving each inequality is an actual facet.

The old m=3 center normals `(h,±4)` are sums of the two same-sign new normals and are redundant. They are removed from the defining set; each has positive pairing with its new summands. The other previous central heights are also incompatible with the new walls. All other retained families are compatible, including some π/4 contacts with the old cusp caps.

## A binary angle-profile family

Prescribe products −u_i with the P normals, where u is a binary vector. For products −w_j with the L normals, the singular short-wall Gram matrix imposes

`Aᵀu + 2w = k·1`.

The solution has old projection

`x=(k v′−sum(u_i P_i))/2`, with `x²=(sum(u_i²)−k²)/2`.

This is a metric consequence of the graph, not a claim that graph branching alone solves the geometry. The parity equations have a 12-dimensional binary kernel. Among its 4,096 vectors, exactly 280 have twelve 1s and even line sums at most four. Set k=4. All 280 resulting integral projections satisfy `x²=−2`, pair −4 with both v and v′, and pass every old210 angle gate.

Add both `(x,+2)` and `(x,−2)`. All 560 normals have square two and strict center product −8. Their 156,520 mutual pairs are:

Their products with the four incidence walls are 1,120 right and 1,120 dotted pairs. Their products with the old210 are also checked exactly.

The 280 projections form one verified orbit under the same integral generators used by the retained banks. The complete signed U, cusp, R and repair families are invariant under those generators; in particular the lower repair heights depend only on invariant component sizes and norms. An exact representative-to-full-bank gate therefore verifies every new-to-retained pair. Internal retained pairs use their existing certificates, with the lower certificate, frame arrays and highest-root arrays explicitly hash bound.

## The volume test and the retained cusp

A [separate exact witness](fresh_h22_incidence_binary_20261013_escape.json), checked by its [replay](../scripts/fresh_h22_incidence_binary_20261013_escape.py), has square

`238943525343/10^12 > 0`.

It is future oriented and strictly inside all 5,398,110 halfspaces. Thus the chamber has infinite volume. Floating-point constraint generation only proposed this point; the inequalities and its norm are exact.

There is also a structural explanation of one remaining difficulty. The previously certified null ray δ₄ is the sum of retained parallel normals `old26+c4`. In physical coordinates it is `(d,2√11)`, with rational d and `d²=−44`. All 564 new normals are strict there: their cusp pairing is at most `−25+4√11<0`. The complete inherited link therefore still has 18 incident facets, normal rank 18 and quotient rank 17 in a 21-dimensional horosphere. An explicit positive-square recession vector remains perpendicular to all its incident normals.

## A field obstruction to repairing this cusp

The [exact field certificate](fresh_h22_incidence_field_obstruction_20261013.json) proves the positive identity

`d = (1/10000) sum(all210 W_i) + sum(22 positive rational multiples of W_i)`.

The 22 selected normals span the whole old space. Consequently a vector y acute to all old normals can satisfy `(d,y)=0` only if y=0.

Let F be any real field with √11 not in F, and suppose a nonzero prospective normal has a projective representative `(y,t)∈F²³`. Orthogonality to δ₄ would give

`(d,y)+2√11 t=0`.

Linear independence of 1 and √11 over F forces `t=0` and `(d,y)=0`, hence y=0: a contradiction. No such normal can be incident to δ₄.

Retaining **both** old26 and c4 matters. Their sum is δ₄, so any new wall acute to them has nonpositive cusp pairing and cannot remove the cusp. For the fields just considered its pairing must therefore be strictly negative. Any finite collection leaves the deficient link unchanged. This excludes finite completion by rational directions, and by mixtures of projective fields none of which contains √11, while retaining this chamber. It leaves directions over fields containing √11 and replacements of retained walls open. The condition refers to these fixed physical coordinates and is not presented as a coordinate-free restriction on all reflection groups.

The [field checker](../scripts/fresh_h22_incidence_field_obstruction_20261013.py) uses only integers and Fractions. It rechecks the positive identity, spanning rank, retained parallel pair, local rank and recession vector; completeness of the inherited incidence census is explicitly inherited from the source-bound prior certificate.

The [separate source and proof review](fresh_h22_incidence_field_obstruction_20261013_review.md) and fresh exact replay also pass, including the necessity of retaining the parallel pair and the restriction to finite additions.
