## A complete high-domain theorem for deep finite pairs

The [full A39 theorem](fresh_full_a39_corridor_high_domain_20261017.md)
handles **all 26,812 corners of all 6,703 frozen high-overlap contexts**.
Suppose the actual whole finite A/B/D component contains the marked pair
at distance 8, with both marks at distance at least 17 from every endpoint,
branch and endpoint of a nonsimple bond. Then the full actual supporting
star has an opposite-black path of length at most 7. Consequently the deep
corridor is excluded in the branch with no such full-star short path.

This covers the proposed deep-pair case of all 251 formerly unresolved
contexts, and also all earlier structural outputs. It does not resolve
those contexts for arbitrary pair shapes. The general supporting frontier
still has 6,452 structural certificates and 251 unresolved contexts in
its original sense.

The proof uses a forced positive definite A39 path with the marks at
positions 15 and 23. It does not require an old Lorentz principal core.
This removes the former gauge, radical and signature exclusions and handles
both connected and disconnected displayed marked components. All old
path embeddings are enumerated; persistent branches, heavy bonds and cycles
are treated explicitly. Exact edge projections and the no-short-path
hypothesis restrict each omitted black root to three possible white slots.
Every completed contact profile either violates strict actual-edge
positivity or gives a Gram matrix with two negative directions.

There are 379 distinct contact templates and 4,983 exact joint profile
checks. Restoring their 13,644 orientation multiplicities gives 57,582
edge failures, 50,137 Lorentz-index failures and 8,337 orientations already
excluded by fixed contacts. The other corners have an incompatible displayed
feature or fixed marked distance, or admit no distance-8 joining orientation.
No depth17 corridor case in the inherited high domain remains untreated.
The [A43/depth20 predecessor](fresh_full_a43_corridor_high_domain_20261017.md)
is also complete and frozen. The stronger depth17 statement has its own
generation, fresh replay and independent saved arithmetic check.

A separate [endpoint theorem](fresh_large_finite_corridor_endpoint_obstruction_20261017.md)
was completed first by the earlier residual-matrix method. It treats all
116 eligible endpoint corners and all 1,428 contact profiles, while explicitly
leaving 72 gauge/signature cases outside that method. The full corridor theorems
now handle their deep-corridor case too. Both methods and their
frozen certificates are preserved.

## Why this has not yet lowered 65

The candidate change halves selected distance-8 pairs deep inside large
finite components. At rank 65 its hypothetical same-component costs are
A/B/D = 474.5/475/476.5 instead of 486/486/487 when the feature depth is 17.
The producer and independent checker count 23/22/21 eligible pairs in the
actual rank-65 diagrams. These are conditional component costs, not a
verified replacement finite-forest budget.

In particular, a short7 alternative does not automatically supply a full
unit under the current ideal-five weights. Affine cycles can have unpaid
distance-6 or distance-7 pairs. The payment-interface audit finds that the
cycle replacements needed for those short alternatives retain a nearby
whole-component leaf and cannot be deep. The new
[cycle interface theorem](fresh_deep_finite8_cycle_interface_20261017.md)
also settles the affected universal-cycle case: an 8-edge arc,
a complementary arc of at least 18, and an endpoint3 replacement. A deep
new mark forces an actual spectator neighbor. A fixed-side, unequal-height
Cauchy bound gives a strict determinant gap 29/144 at the endpoint Q=18;
concavity and reciprocal-Q monotonicity cover the entire tail. The separate
depth20 version has gap 73/336 at Q=21. All 288 exact endpoint values pass
an independent rational checker.

The [remaining payment obligations](fresh_deep_finite8_payment_interface_next_step_20261017.md)
are threshold7 low/disjoint coverage, complete composition with the actual
short-path extraction and later finite-short8 branches, and a simultaneous
typed finite-forest budget. The old low/disjoint results assume absence of
paths of length at most8 and cannot simply be reused at threshold7.
The complete high-overlap and cycle theorems do not supply those missing
hypotheses. Consequently there is no new global bound yet.

The earlier dimension-65 necessity

    7*v_L >= v_S + 5280 + (445/23)*v_i

is unchanged. It still requires at least 755 finite vertices containing a
component of rank at least 18, or 758 if an ideal vertex exists. The subclass
with all finite component ranks at most 17 still has ceiling 64.
