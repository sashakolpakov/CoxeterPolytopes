# Unrestricted dimension156 and dimension29 outside small cusp factors

This checkpoint completes an internally checked computer-assisted proof
of **dimension at most156 for arbitrary finite-volume real hyperbolic
Coxeter polytopes**, improving the preceding163 bound. It also proves
**dimension at most29 when every irreducible affine cusp factor has
rank at least9**. These are separate statements: the second restricts
the cusp types and does not give an unconditional29 bound.

The compact-arithmetic ceiling remains21. No new finite-volume example
or construction dimension has been established. The proofs have internal
mathematical reviews and exact computational checks; external refereeing,
proof-assistant formalization and publication priority are unestablished.

## The unrestricted bound

The [dimension156 proof](fresh_distance14_unrestricted_dimension156_20261017.md)
removes the worst case of the previous distance15 corner rule. Its two
minimal indefinite cores would have to join at their unique radius7
nodes. One core type is excluded by an exact inverse-diagonal budget.
For the other, actual corner positivity forces a simple bridge, and the
resulting literal Gram has two negative directions. Hyperbolic geometry
allows only one. This proves a universal distance14 payment, including
nonsimple ideal vertices and every Coxeter label.

At a cusp with factor ranks r_i and V=product(r_i+1), the normalized
cost is at most sum_i min(14,r_i/2). A new convex-order argument
concentrates every rank partition, without changing that cost, to
copies of28 and at most one smaller and one larger part. Exact checks
of all924 resulting profiles in link dimensions156 through162 show
that the shape curvature exceeds the cost. The inherited163 theorem
covers all larger dimensions. Thus every ambient n>=157 is excluded.

The [separate review](fresh_distance14_unrestricted_dimension156_20261017_review.md)
records the mathematical argument and independent rational computations
of the physical Gram obstructions and all reduced profiles. The
smallest exact normalized margin is1476616/20511149. A valid local
link profile in dimension156 instead has negative margin, so this
particular payment comparison gives no155 bound. That local profile
is not an ambient finite-volume example.

is asserted. The rank9 dimension proof has no ambient enumeration
