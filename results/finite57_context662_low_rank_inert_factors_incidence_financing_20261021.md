# Context662 with arbitrary inert factors of rank at most ten

21 October 2026. New manual mathematical supporting theorem, pending
full root and outside-author review. No scientific, symbolic, numerical,
enumeration, Lean or TeX job ran. No manuscript, current rule, accepted
source or dimension bound was changed. This is one supporting proof for
the existing finite57 paper family.

**Theorem.** In a hypothetical finite-volume Coxeter57-polytope, consider
actual context662 quadrilaterals whose supporting-white short-path escape
is absent. Suppose their ideal source has whole varying affine D16 and
EVERY other whole affine cusp factor has rank at most10. After deleting
the finite A17 endpoint half, their missing half-payments can all be
allocated to their opposite finite vertices, with effective charge LESS
THAN362 at every recipient. No ideal curvature surplus is spent.

This covers all inert factor types, multiplicities and ports under the
rank cutoff, rather than only the earlier four-D10 source profile. It
does not cover the entire context662 family, every A17 producer, a
whole-entry deletion or a finite maximum364. No ceiling56 follows.

## 3. Actual-face injection and global half-debt allocation

The source family has a unique A17 at q, since all other components
have rank<=10. Releasing its unordered endpoint pair and intersecting
the OTHER55 incident facets defines a unique actual two-face through
the simple finite vertex q. Therefore Q->q is injective, just as in
21aab, without any assertion that every such vertex has an inverse.
The source ideal corner is unique: its other three corner Grams are
positive definite. This counts actual faces, not core embeddings.

Let w-minus delete the A17 endpoint half and leave ideal weights
unchanged. Each source face retains its two D17 quarters, hence half
its required unit. Restore a half only on its actual ideal incidence,
and allocate that debt to q. Injectivity gives at most one debt there,
while deletion removed its unique endpoint half. Consequently

    effective finite charge <=C-minus(q)+1/2=C0(q)<362.

Summing over faces gives exactly the same global allocation identity
as21aab: the total added ideal-incidence half-charge equals the total
assigned finite half-debt. No ideal-envelope surplus is spent or
multiplied. Other finite vertices and the ideal-side GLOBAL charge
keep their old budgets. Validity on other affected faces is separate.
