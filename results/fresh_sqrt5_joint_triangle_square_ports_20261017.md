# A joint three-port capacity for simplex and quadrilateral aggregates

Let v be a vertex of a compact simple acute Coxeter polytope and U a set
of three incident edge directions, called ports. Let s_U count the maximal
simplex faces whose port blocks meet U in at least two ports. Let t_U
indicate that one simplex block contains all three ports. Let q_U count
the distinct canonical quadrilateral aggregates occupying at least one
of the three port pairs in U. Then

\[
 \boxed{\qquad 2s_U+t_U+q_U\le3.\qquad}             \tag{1}
\]

The quadrilateral aggregates are the groups with the same complete
incident-wall star. A group counts once in q_U even when it occupies two
port pairs. On the current dotted catalogue these groups are the unique
maximal compact prisms. The statement does not assume uniqueness of an
arbitrary maximal product face.

## Geometric proof

By the [simplex-port theorem](fresh_simplex_disjoint_ports_20261017.md),
triangular adjacency is a disjoint union of cliques. Therefore s_U is zero
or one. If s_U is zero, each of the three port pairs specifies at most one
quadrilateral, so at most three distinct groups can occur.

If all three pairs are triangular, s_U=t_U=1 and q_U=0, giving equality.
Otherwise t_U=0 and exactly
one pair is triangular. The two remaining pairs are either quadrilateral
or have more sides. At most one square gives q_U<=1. If both are squares,
the [triangle-plus-two-squares completion](fresh_prism_simplex_shared_capacity_20261017.md)
makes the three-face generated by U a triangular prism. Those two square
faces have the same complete n+2-wall star: both meet each of the five
facets of the three-prism, in addition to its n−3 supporting walls.
The [canonical aggregation theorem](fresh_quad_product_aggregation_20261017.md)
therefore places them in the same group. Again q_U=1, proving(1).

This proof concerns compact faces. It is not extended to arbitrary
finite-volume chambers merely because v is finite: triangles with ideal
neighbors need not satisfy the triangle-port transitivity used here.
The construction_audit agent independently checked this scope, the
common-full-star step, and the additional full-triangle term t_U. That is
a conceptual review, not a numerical replay.

## Information beyond separate pair and simplex packing budgets

On three abstract ports a,b,c, assign mass1/3 to each two-port simplex
block and mass2/3 to each single-pair quadrilateral footprint. Every pair
load is one. The simplex blocks pairwise intersect, and their total mass
is one, so the complete disjoint-simplex-packing relaxation also passes.
All earlier [mixed wedge indicators](fresh_mixed_face_port_incidence_20261017.md)
vanish: no one aggregate occupies two of the three pair positions.
Yet the left side of(1) is4, exceeding3.

This is an exact fractional relaxation witness, not a claim that those
footprints coexist in an arithmetic chamber or satisfy every global flag
constraint. It proves that(1) contains information absent from the stated
pair and separate simplex-packing constraints.
