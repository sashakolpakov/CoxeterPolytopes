## Exhaustive all-integer collision proof

The critical step is not a bounded experiment in the chain length.  Put
`K=T−I`.  Since `K³=0`, for every integer `z`, including negative `z`,

```text
T^z = I + z K + z(z-1)/2 K^2.
```

For both branches `T^z` and `T^z S36`, both root signs, and every ordered pair
of the 170 nonendpoint seed facets, the audit solves the resulting vector
linear/quadratic equation in `z` exactly.  A nonzero coordinate supplies all
possible integer roots, after which every coordinate is verified.

The exhaustive result is:

- 156 finite solutions `T^0 r_i=r_i`;
- 32 finite solutions `T^0 S36 r_i=r_i`;
- 32 finite solutions `T^-1 S36 r_i=r_i`;
- 28 all-`z` identities: the same 14 common orthogonals in each branch;
- no negative-root collisions, no cross-facet collisions, and no
  endpoint-to-side-orbit collisions.

The separate outer-end equations find no solution to
`T^z(W36)=±W31`; the only solution to `T^z S36(W31)=±W31` is the irrelevant
backward value `z=−1` with negative sign.  Therefore the two outer facets are
distinct for every finite forward path.

The 170 side facets split as follows:

| class | count |
|---|---:|
| orthogonal to both W31 and W36 | 14 |
| orthogonal only to W31 | 32 |
| orthogonal only to W36 | 32 |
| orthogonal to neither | 92 |

For a path of `N` seed chambers, the exact collision classification gives

```text
side facets

```

Adding the two outer endpoint facets proves the universal formula

```text
f(N)=124N+48,  for every integer N>=1.
```

As a redundant finite check, direct boundary reconstruction for `N=1,...,12`

The boundary of every path contains all seed roots except W36; that

## Coxeter and volume consequences

The geometric step is the classical doubling lemma: Daniel Allcock,
[*Infinitely many hyperbolic Coxeter groups through dimension 19*](https://web.ma.utexas.edu/users/allcock/research/doubling.pdf),
Lemma 1.3.  Alternating across the two parallel doubling walls shows that
every finite path is a Coxeter polytope.  Its volume is exactly `N` times the
finite seed volume, and it remains noncompact.  Consequently the family is
pairwise nonisometric, independently witnessed by both its strictly increasing
facet counts and its distinct volume multiples.
