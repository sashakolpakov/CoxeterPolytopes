# An explicit order-four Weierstrass carrier for Vinberg's `H14/17` chamber

## Positive theorem

**TOOT.** Let `i^2=-1`.  For `(b,c)` in the nonempty Zariski open set
specified below, let `X_(b,c)` be the minimal resolution of

```text
y^2 = x^3 + t^2(t^2-3)x + t^3(2+b t^2+c t^4),
```

and let

```text
sigma(x,y,t) = (-x, i y, -t).
```

For a very general parameter pair,

```text
NS(X_(b,c)) = U + E8(-1) + D6(-1).
```

The map `sigma` is a primitive non-symplectic automorphism of order four.  It
is the identity on `U+E8(-1)` and exchanges the two spinor nodes of
`D6(-1)`.  Consequently its Picard action is integrally conjugate to
Kondō's `tau_2` and to Brandhorst--Hofmann row `0.4.2.2`.  Its fixed section
of the complete nef chamber is therefore the certified finite-volume,
noncompact Coxeter polytope

```text
R16-UE8D6-C2-H14-17 = Vinberg P14, with 17 facets in H^14.
```

This supplies the previously missing equation-level algebraic carrier for
the rank-16 order-four theorem.  It is a new explicit realization in this
project, not a claim that Vinberg's abstract polytope or this normalized
Weierstrass equation is new worldwide.

## The discriminant and the exact parameter open

Put `u=t^2`.  For


```text
A = t^2(t^2-3),       B = t^3(2+b t^2+c t^4),
```

exact expansion gives


```text
4 A^3 + 27 B^2 = t^8 Q_(b,c)(t^2),
```

where


```text
Q_(b,c)(u) = 27 c^2 u^3 + (4+54bc)u^2
             + (27b^2+108c-36)u + 108(b+1).
```

The two useful factorizations are


```text
disc_u(Q) = 432(3b+9c+2)^3
            (27b^2c-54bc+b-432c^2-9c-2),
Q(3)      = 9(3b+9c+2)^2.
```

Thus it is enough to require


```text
c(b+1)(3b+9c+2)
 (27b^2c-54bc+b-432c^2-9c-2) != 0.                 (2.1)
```

This open is nonempty: `(b,c)=(0,1)` gives


```text
Q(u)=27u^3+4u^2+72u+108,
disc(Q)=-254721456,       Q(3)=1089.
```

The witness only proves that the parameter open is nonempty; no claim is
made here that this particular number-field member has Picard rank exactly
16.

In homogeneous base coordinates `[T:S]`, the coefficients are


```text
A_8  = T^2(T^2-3S^2)S^4,
B_12 = T^3(2S^4+bT^2S^2+cT^4)S^5.
```

They define a global elliptic K3 Weierstrass model.  Kodaira's valuation
table and (2.1) give

| place | `(ord A, ord B, ord Delta)` | fiber | Euler number | root lattice |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| `t=0` | `(2,3,8)` | `I2*` | 8 | `D6(-1)` |
| six roots of `Q(t^2)` | `ord Delta=1` | six `I1` | 6 | none |
| `t=infinity` | `(4,5,10)` | `II*` | 10 | `E8(-1)` |

The Euler numbers sum to 24.  The zero section, fiber, and nonidentity
components of the two reducible fibers therefore give


```text
U + E8(-1) + D6(-1)  subset  NS(X_(b,c)),
```

of rank 16 and determinant `-4`.

The equation is preserved by `sigma`: `A` is even, `B` is odd, and both
sides change sign.  Moreover


```text
sigma^2(x,y,t)=(x,-y,t),
sigma^*(dt wedge dx/y)=-i(dt wedge dx/y).
```

Hence `sigma` has order four and is primitive non-symplectic.

## Why the very general Picard lattice is exact

There are two independent moduli.  The distinct `I2*` and `II*` fibers
intrinsically distinguish zero and infinity.  A fibration-preserving base
isomorphism is consequently `t -> lambda*t`.  After the accompanying
Weierstrass scaling, preservation of the normalized coefficient
`t^2(t^2-3)` forces `lambda^2=1`; it does not remove either parameter `b`
or `c`.  Forgetting the marked fibration has discrete fibers, because
elliptic fibrations and sections on a fixed K3 surface are represented by
integral Néron--Severi classes.  Thus the family of K3/automorphism pairs is
two-dimensional.

For a primitive order-four automorphism, the transcendental lattice has
only `i` and `-i` eigenvalues: a rational `+1` or `-1` eigenspace would be
orthogonal to the period and hence algebraic.  Therefore `rank(T)` and the
Picard rank are even.  The displayed divisors give `rho>=16`.  If the very
general rank were larger, it would be at least 18, so `rank(T)<=4` and the
primitive-eigenvalue period space would have dimension at most one.  This
contradicts the two-dimensional family.  Hence the very general Picard rank
is 16.

There is no same-rank saturation ambiguity.  A proper even overlattice of
the displayed determinant-four lattice would have index two and determinant
one.  It would be even unimodular of signature `(1,15)`, impossible because
`1-15` is not divisible by eight.  Thus the displayed sublattice is the
whole Néron--Severi lattice.

## Why the `D6` action is the outer swap

This point is essential: the fiber symbols alone do not determine the
Picard action.

The automorphism fixes the fiber and zero-section classes.  The `II*`
component graph is `affine E8` and has no nontrivial symmetry.  At the
`I2*` fiber, the zero section is invariant, so the affine component that it
meets is fixed.  The stabilizer of that affine node in the `affine D6`
component graph has exactly two elements: the identity and the involution
exchanging the two opposite spinor components.

The identity is impossible.  If `sigma` acted identically on the whole
rank-16 Picard lattice, its whole-`H^2` `+1,-1` eigenspace ranks would be


```text
(r,l)=(16,0),
```

because the rank-six transcendental action has characteristic polynomial
`(lambda^2+1)^3`.  But Proposition 1 of
[Artebani--Sarti](https://doi.org/10.2969/jmsj/06720503) gives, for a purely
non-symplectic order-four K3 automorphism,


```text
r-l-2 = 4 alpha.
```

The left side would be 14.  Therefore the `D6` component action is not the
identity and must be the spinor-node swap.  It follows that


```text
char(sigma|NS) = (lambda-1)^15(lambda+1).
```

There is also a direct local check of the same choice.  Near `t=0`, the two
tangent branches of the cubic through the double root have expansions

```text
x=t+kappa*t^2+...,       3*kappa^2=-(b+1).
```

The base/fiber involution `(t,x)->(-t,-x)` sends `kappa` to `-kappa`.
Resolving the `I2*` singularity turns these two branches into the two spinor
fork ends, so they are exchanged.  Thus the component calculation and the
Lefschetz congruence independently select the same nontrivial `D6` action.

This also recovers the Brandhorst--Hofmann fixed-locus fingerprint.  Here
`(r,l)=(15,1)`, so `alpha=3` and the generator fixes three rational curves
and ten isolated points.  Its square has Nikulin invariants `(16,2,1)` and
fixes seven rational curves and one genus-two curve.  Their Euler
characteristics, 16 and 12, agree with the exact `H^2` traces.

## Integral action, discriminant, and glue

Use simple roots in which the two spinor roots are `alpha_5,alpha_6`.  The
action fixes the first four `D6` roots and swaps these last two.  Its fixed
and coinvariant pieces are


```text
rank(NS^sigma)=15, signature=(1,14), determinant=4, Smith=1^14 4,
(NS^sigma)^perp = <alpha_5-alpha_6> = <-4>.
```

The coinvariant is rootless.  The fixed discriminant-form histogram is
`{0:1, 1:1, 3/4:2}`, exactly the one in the pinned order-four theorem.
On `A_D6=(Z/2)^2`, the action fixes the vector class and exchanges the two
spinor classes.  Under the explicit class relabeling in the certificate,
its permutation is conjugate to the archived Kondō permutation
`(0,1,2,3)->(0,2,1,3)`.

The primitive glue is also literal, not inferred from a genus.  Put


```text
s=alpha_5+alpha_6,       d=alpha_5-alpha_6.
```

Then the fixed-plus-coinvariant lattice has index two in `NS`, and adjoining


```text
alpha_5=(s+d)/2
```

recovers `NS`.  In Smith coordinates this is the nonzero glue pair `(2,2)`
between the order-two classes of the fixed `Z/4` and coinvariant `Z/4`
groups.  It is exactly the pair in the archived Kondō action and in
Brandhorst--Hofmann row `0.4.2.2`.  The stable lifting argument in the
[order-four theorem](kondo_rho16_h14_bh_order4_lift.md) then gives an
integral conjugacy of the full Picard actions, not merely equality of
characters.

The same theorem identifies this action with Kondō's `tau_2`.  A separate
compatible database realization, full-group row `1.4.2.1`, supplies a
commuting symplectic involution and an abstract `C4 x C2` enrichment.  The
present equation certifies the cyclic row `0.4.2.2`; it does not assert that
every very general `X_(b,c)` carries the extra involution or give a coordinate
formula for one.

## Nef chamber and Vinberg gate

The displayed regular automorphism preserves a Kähler class.  Transport an
invariant Kähler chamber by the Weyl group to the archived complete
19-wall nef chamber.  Since the integral Picard action is the one just
identified, its wall action is the unique graph symmetry with fixed rank
15, namely Kondō's `tau_2`.  The literal fixed section is therefore
`R16-UE8D6-C2-H14-17`.

Its hyperbolic status comes from the complete Vinberg audit already pinned

## Lineage

The ambient construction is not ad hoc.  Example 6.3 of
[Artebani--Sarti, *Symmetries of order four on K3 surfaces*](https://doi.org/10.2969/jmsj/06720503)
uses precisely


```text
y^2=x^3+a(t)x+b(t),       a even, b odd,
(x,y,t)->(-x,iy,-t).
```

The present family is a concrete two-parameter simultaneous
Noether--Lefschetz enhancement at the two fixed base points: `I2*` at zero
and `II*` at infinity.  The lattice/action identification is supplied by
[Brandhorst--Hofmann](https://doi.org/10.1017/fms.2023.50), and the chamber
lineage by [Kondō](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0027763000001653), Vinberg, and
the existing exact `P14` comparison.
