## 1. The independent finite lane

Consider twelve norm-`-2` proposed Coble--Mukai walls.  Their ordinary
support graph is required to be

1. connected on twelve vertices;
2. of maximum ordinary degree at most three;
3. to have exactly thirteen nonzero pairs;
4. to have pairings only in `{0,1,2}`, with exactly two or three pairing-2
   pairs; and
5. to have root Gram inertia `(1,9,2)` and saturated radical quotient of
   determinant `-4`.

The final condition says that the root span has index two in any unimodular
`E10` extension.  It is a genuine extra restriction: this lane does not
contain determinant `-1,-16,-36,...` root presentations from the same graph
scout.

This is also not all of `D_1_split^nodot`.  In particular Type II has fifteen
simple pairs and no pairing-2 pair, so it is outside the lane from the
outset.  No non-singleton or all-nodot conclusion can be drawn from the
theorem below.

\[
