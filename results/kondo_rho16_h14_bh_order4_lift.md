# An order-four K3 realization of Kondo's rank-16 H14 polytope

## Positive theorem

**TOOT.** There is a complex projective K3 surface with generic

`NS(X) = U + E8(-1) + D6(-1)`

and an effective `C4 x C2` automorphism group.  For suitable generators `s`
(symplectic, order two) and `g` (order four), the purely non-symplectic
element

`sigma = s g`

has order four but acts on `NS(X)` as Kondo's graph involution `tau_2`.
After marking the invariant nef chamber, its fixed section is the known
finite-volume noncompact Coxeter polytope

`R16-UE8D6-C2-H14-17` (`Vinberg P14`, `14-vinb85.coxiter`).

Thus this is a genuine algebro-geometric realization of a known hyperbolic
Coxeter polytope, and also a new project-level full-group enrichment.  It is
not a claim of a new abstract Coxeter diagram, dimension, or arithmetic
commensurability class.

## Why the earlier negative theorem does not apply

The old result excluded an **order-two** K3 automorphism inducing `tau_2` on
this Picard lattice.  The new automorphism has order four:

- `sigma|NS = tau_2` has order two;
- `sigma^2|NS = 1`; and
- `sigma^2|T = -1` on the rank-six transcendental lattice.

So the order-two obstruction remains true.  What is closed positively is the
previously open higher-order-period escape: the same divisor-lattice
involution is induced by an actual order-four automorphism.

For both rows the exact reconstruction gives

  `(x-1)^15(x+1)`.

For the full row, `s` and `g` commute and generate all eight elements.  Their
Picard image is `C2 x C2`, with kernel `<g^2>=C2`.  The common fixed lattice
has rank 8, signature `(1,7)`, and determinant `-256`; the symplectic
coinvariant is negative definite, rank 8, determinant 256, and rootless.

The selected cyclic subgroup of `1.4.2.1` is not merely similar to the pure
row.  Checking the complete 79-row pure order-four table by the fixed-lattice

## Integral action identification

An equality of Picard genera would not be enough.  The replay compares the
archived Kondo action and each database action as integral lattice actions:

- the fixed lattice has rank 15, signature `(1,14)`, determinant 4, Smith
  invariants `1^14,4`, and discriminant group `Z/4`;
- the coinvariant lattice is exactly `<-4>`, with action `-1`, hence is
  rootless;
- the identity maps preserve both discriminant quadratic forms; and
- they carry both elements of the index-two primitive glue graph.

Nikulin's stable lifting condition applies because fixed rank 15 is at least
discriminant length 1 plus 2.  The fixed and coinvariant maps therefore glue
to an integral conjugacy of the full Picard actions.  This rules out a
genus-only false positive.

There is also a chamber check.  Effectivity supplies an invariant Kähler
chamber.  Move it to the archived complete 19-wall chamber by the Weyl group.
The induced action is then a symmetry of the complete weighted wall graph.
That graph has exactly four automorphisms and three nonidentity involutions,
whose fixed ranks are 15, 8, and 9.  Consequently the rank-15 action is
uniquely the archived `tau_2` graph-symmetry class.

## Vinberg finite-volume gate

The hyperbolic claim comes from the complete Vinberg audit, not from the
Lorentzian signature alone.  The fixed section has:

| invariant | value |
|---|---:|
| hyperbolic dimension | 14 |
| facets | 17 |
| finite vertices | 89 |
| ideal vertices (cusps) | 5 |
| hyperideal vertices | 0 |
| elliptic edges audited | 704 |
| bad edges | 0 |
| full parabolic-neighborhood audits | 8 |

Thus every elliptic edge has the required finite or ideal endpoints and all
maximal parabolic neighborhoods are complete.  The conclusion is
**finite-volume noncompact**, not merely “no detected obstruction.”

## Search boundary and reusable criterion

The companion exact scout checked 587 pure rows and 3,561 full rows.  The
pure rank-16 target has the unique exact hit `0.4.2.2`.  Of the 2,140 full
order-two rows, two have the source Picard lattice but neither has the target
action.  Among the remaining 1,421 full rows there is one source-lattice hit,
the exact positive row `1.4.2.1`.  No exact rank-15 `C2` or `C3` target action
was found in either lane.

This yields a useful positive criterion for future fishing:

1. start with an effective algebraic automorphism class, not an arbitrary
   Lorentzian lattice;
2. reconstruct its generic `NS` as the orthogonal complement of the
   cyclotomic transcendental lattice;
3. match the proposed divisor action through fixed lattice, coinvariant
   lattice, discriminant forms, and primitive glue;
4. identify the invariant nef-chamber symmetry class; and
5. invoke a complete Vinberg edge and parabolic-neighborhood audit.

The new example shows why the search must allow automorphism order larger
than its order on the divisor lattice.  Searching only involutions would
have proved the correct negative theorem and missed the positive geometry.
