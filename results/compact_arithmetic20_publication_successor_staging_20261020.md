## 3. Proposed domain theorem: finite seeds and exact dotted boxes

This text belongs BEFORE the queue counts and before the exact evaluator.
It expands the current Proposition final touching-star coverage.

**Definitions.** An actual quadrilateral has four cyclic side roots
b0,b1,b2,b3, called black, and n-2 common supporting roots W, called white.
A contact state records all four marked black roles, entered white roots,
every native contact label, every fixed dotted parameter, and its exact
Gram data up to the explicitly checked positive gauge congruence. Its
four adjacent-black corners are positive definite. An unpaid state has
not already earned a persistent baseline unit by a proved local rule.

**Seed/domain theorem.** Every unpaid actual golden quadrilateral has an
initial representative among the complete opposite-pair union domains.
Every subsequent touching white root lies in a finite, explicitly
defined one-white domain. Dotted seed parameters lie in finite exact
integer boxes, valid in EVERY larger extension of the seed.

**Proof: initial supports.** Each opposite extension W+{b0,b2} and
W+{b1,b3} is indefinite: a positive principal set would define an actual
face by positive acute projection, making opposite sides meet. A minimal
indefinite subset contains both black roots, since deleting either is
inside a finite corner. An affine subset gives an actual feasible ideal
point, forbidden compactly. Thus each minimal subset is Lanner of order
at most5. There are finitely many such arithmetic pieces. Enumerate all
permitted white identifications between the two pieces and every remaining
contact between roots of their union. Those unfixed pairs belong to an
actual finite corner, so their labels belong to the golden native finite
alphabet2,3,4,5,6,10; they are not sampled dotted distances. Require all
four corner diagrams finite and retain exact maps to the marked union.
The published finite/affine classification and the complete finite
Lanner catalogue are explicit inputs to this finite enumeration.

**Proof: dotted parameters.** In each of the24 remaining minimal dotted
families, delete its dotted endpoints0,2. The common block B is a
nonsingular Lorentz matrix. Let the dotted Gram entry be -x, endpoint
squares d0,d2, and write the two-dimensional Schur residual as

    [[a,-x-c],[-x-c,b]].

In any extension with one negative direction, this residual is positive
semidefinite: B has already used the negative direction. Hence
(x+c)^2<=ab, and the two-cycle invariant satisfies

    t=4x^2/(d0*d2)<=8(ab+c^2)/(d0*d2).

This bound is inherited by every larger principal extension. Exact ceiling
gives an integer U; the24 concrete U values lie between9 and76. Disjoint
walls give t>4. Arithmetic cyclic integrality and conjugate positivity give

    t=A+Bphi in Z[phi], 4<t<=U, 0<=t'<=4.

Consequently B=(t-t')/sqrt5 is positive and at most U. Also
A=(phi*t'-phi'*t)/sqrt5 is nonnegative and at most U: use U>=4 and
sqrt5-|phi'|=phi. Thus the literal integer rectangle
0<=A<=U,0<B<=U is complete; the independent reviewer deliberately scans
the larger symmetric rectangle[-U,U]^2. There is no distance cutoff.
Each candidate is tested for the exact cyclic field/gauge, all cycle
integralities, physical inertia and conjugate positivity. The elementary

The complete domain consists of ALL such label tuples for which all four
enlarged corners are finite, with at least one nonright contact to the

## 4. Proposed queue theorem: termination AND final actual coverage

**Finite queue theorem.** Under the preceding seed/domain theorem, the
contact algorithm terminates and covers every unpaid actual quadrilateral
in compact dimension n<=29. Every retained singular state is a stopping
obligation EVEN IF it has accepted children. At dimension21 the final
contact representative has whole marked components and only common
orthogonal spectators outside it.

Suggested pseudocode, with mathematical rather than implementation-only
stopping criteria:

```text
Insert every accepted marked seed, keeping each literal seed map.
While an unprocessed representative remains:
    If it is singular, record its zero-further-contact obligation.
    At 31 roots: retain a singular obligation; reject nonsingular rank.
    Otherwise generate its COMPLETE one-white domain.
    For each tuple:
        record any proved persistent basic payment;
        otherwise test exact field, relation and both-embedding inertia;
        reject only a certified necessary-condition failure;
        map an accepted child by an explicit marked full-label bijection
            and checked positive gauge congruence;
        queue a new representative and retain the literal child map.
    A conditionally sealed unpaid state permits only orthogonal spectators.
```

**Termination proof.** A contact core on N roots contains a positive corner
of order N-2 and a negative direction. Its nullity is therefore at most1.
The full star in dimension n has n+2 roots and rank n+1. The inherited
published compact ceiling29 therefore permits at most31 star roots.
A nonsingular31-root core cannot fit in Lorentz rank<=30, while a singular
31-root core remains an obligation. Node count, native alphabet and the
finite seed parameter lists together bound the total number of labeled
states. Quotienting by only verified marked maps cannot remove a possible
actual state. Each representative is processed once, so the queue is
finite; merely observing an empty queue is not the termination proof.

**Actual coverage proof.** Start with an actual minimal union. Whenever
an unentered actual white component touches the current state, enter a
first touching root and then follow its white edges. Each such root is
inside the complete one-white domain. An actual chamber cannot fail a
necessary arithmetic, signature or inherited-relation test. A persistent
payment already pays the face independently of adjustable bonuses; in
the other case follow its verified literal child map. At an unsealed
singular state, continue if any actual touching root remains. Select the
FINAL touching state, not the first singular subcore. At a conditionally
sealed unpaid stop, the sealing theorem says an additional touching root
would supply persistent payment. Thus every remaining supporting component
of an unpaid face is orthogonal to all entered white and black roots.

The orthogonal spectator block is positive definite, so it cannot create
the missing null relation of a nonsingular final contact core. A possible
full star must therefore stop at one of the retained singular obligations.
All marked finite corner components are now entire components of the
actual corner; external components are the same common orthogonal
spectators at all four corners. This final-state choice is indispensable
for same-hub denominators. Payment computed at an embedded subcore is not
a replacement for the actual final angle. D5/H4 modifications preserve
the persistent-payment implication by their simultaneous consumer theorems,
