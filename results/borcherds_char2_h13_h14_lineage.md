## Result

The two fixed sections are genuine finite-volume, noncompact hyperbolic
Coxeter polytopes.  They do **not**, however, have the same novelty status.

## Certified lineage theorem

Let `P_50` and `P_98` denote the two restricted chambers produced from the
complete 210-wall Borcherds--Dolgachev--Kondō chamber.

1. `P_50` is a finite-volume noncompact Coxeter polytope in `H13`.  Its
   reflection lattice is the unique indefinite even 2-elementary lattice
   with Nikulin invariants `(r,a,delta)=(14,10,0)`.  The reflection group
   generated by its 50 walls has finite index in the full reflection group of
   this lattice.  The latter has a 16-wall finite-volume chamber published by
   [Alexeev](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.09110).
2. `P_98` is a finite-volume noncompact Coxeter polytope in `H14`, but its
   entire weighted Gram matrix, after the doubled-dual exchange of short and
   long roots, is permutation-equivalent to Alexeev's published 98-wall
   `(15,9,1)` chamber.  Consequently `P_98` is the same abstract Coxeter
   polytope, not merely a chamber in the same rational commensurability class.

The theorem uses complete Gram matrices and the literal Vinberg
edge/parabolic test.  It is stronger than an identification by determinant,
facet count, edge histogram, or rational quadratic space alone.

## `H13/50`: what is old and what may still be new

### The divisor lattice is the known `(14,10,0)` lattice

The saturated fixed divisor lattice `L_13` has

```text
rank                 14
signature            (1,13)
determinant           -2^10
Smith invariants      1^4 2^10
parity                even
coparity              delta=0
```

The last assertion is checked directly: `2 L_13^*` is integral and every
diagonal entry of `L_13^{-1}` is integral.  By Nikulin's uniqueness theorem
for indefinite even 2-elementary lattices, these data identify `L_13` with
`(14,10,0)`.

Its doubled dual `L_13^dagger=L_13^*(2)` is even of determinant `-16`, with
Smith invariants `1^10 2^4`.  Its discriminant quadratic-form census is

```text
q=0 : 6 classes
q=1 : 10 classes.
```

This agrees with Scharlau--Kirschmer `List13/Gram13` entry 10,
`II_{13,1}(2^{-4})`.  That table is the doubled-dual convention: duality
interchanges `(-2)` and divisibility-two `(-4)` roots without changing the
Coxeter chamber.

The doubled dual now has a complete geometric handoff rather than only a
census label.  An explicit determinant-minus-one isometry identifies it
with the actual complex-K3 Picard lattice `U+D8(-1)+D4(-1)`.  Exact positive
halfspace transport carries all 50 facets; their primitive target split is
`40*(-2,div1)+10*(-4,div2)`, and they generate the entire Picard lattice.
Every reflection is integral.  This gives a new characteristic-zero mixed
divisor realization of the known chamber, not a same-form overclosure or a
full-nef claim; see the
[scaled-dual carrier theorem](ss2_2a_h13_50_ud8d4_divisor_carrier.md).

The target K3 is now explicit as well.  A normalized six-parameter
short-Weierstrass family has singular fibers `I4*+I0*+8I1`, trivial
Mordell--Weil group, and exact very-general Picard lattice
`U+D8(-1)+D4(-1)`.  A determinant-minus-one marking from its literal
fiber-divisor basis to the archived K3 basis transports all fifty mirrors
and their integral reflections; the mirrors still generate the full Picard
lattice.  This standard fibration and its six-dimensional `P'_14` moduli
space are classified by Clingher--Malmendier.  The new project-level content
is the exact equation-to-fifty-wall bridge; see the
[equation-level carrier theorem](ss2_2a_h13_50_ud8d4_weierstrass_carrier.md).

### The 16-wall full chamber is recovered directly

Alexeev's ancillary matrix for `(15,11,1)` has 66 walls.  Taking zero-based
wall 21 in the size-36 divisibility-two `(-2)` orbit, its orthogonal walls are
exactly

```text
1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 16, 18, 22.
```

These 16 walls have twelve roots of square `-4` and four of square `-2`.
Their Coxeter-edge census is

```text
m=2 : 104
m=3 :  14
m=4 :   2.
```

Complete exact enumeration gives the finite-special-subset vector

```text
(16,120,560,1820,4364,7965,11232,
 12272,10300,6488,2920,842,111)
```

and six maximal parabolic neighborhoods.  These are exactly the face and
cusp entries in Scharlau--Kirschmer `List13` entry 10.  This supplies a
machine-readable derivation of the `(14,10,0)` chamber even though the
ancillary file does not contain a standalone matrix assignment with that
name.

### The precise group relation

Every one of the 50 production walls is an integral root of `L_13`, so its
reflection lies in the full root-reflection group `W_r(L_13)`.  Both the
50-wall chamber and the 16-wall full chamber have finite hyperbolic volume.
Therefore

```text
W_50 < W_r(L_13)
```

has finite index, and the 50-wall chamber is tiled by finitely many copies of
the 16-wall chamber.

The value of the index is still open.  The successful H10 strategy—taking a
ratio of Coxeter-group Euler characteristics—does not work here because
hyperbolic dimension 13 is odd and both Euler characteristics vanish.  A
completion now needs one of the following concrete steps:

1. compute both hyperbolic covolumes in a common normalization and divide;
2. construct the explicit chamber tiling/coset action inside the 16-wall
   group; or
3. identify a finite quotient in which the 50 reflections generate a
   subgroup of provably equal index and prove the congruence kernel adds
   nothing.

The bounded CoxIter audit finds no `H13`/50-facet header in either the pinned
1,214-file ordinary snapshot or the separate 2,959-file reflection-doubling
snapshot.  This supports only the statement “not found in those two
snapshots.”  It does not establish worldwide novelty.

A second bounded comparison exhausts the two directly relevant tabular
primary sources.  Alexeev's pinned ancillary file has 18 explicit Coxeter
matrix assignments; its only 50-wall assignment is the rank-20 `(20,2,1)`
case in `H19`, not an `H13` chamber.  Scharlau--Kirschmer `List13` has 17
full-chamber entries and none has 50 walls.  This rules out a literal table
duplicate in those sources, while still leaving papers and unlisted
finite-index subgroup chambers outside the quantified denominator.

The algebro-geometric content is real: class `2a` lies in the geometric
Dolgachev--Kondō subgroup, and the hyperbolic lattice is obtained as a
saturated fixed part of the Néron--Severi lattice.  Turning this into a named
quotient surface and identifying its divisor cone intrinsically, rather than
through the ambient 210-wall chamber, remains a separate geometric task.

## `H14/98`: exact rediscovery, not a new polytope

### The unique reflection-stable closure

The fixed divisor lattice `L_14` is even of rank 15, signature `(1,14)`,
determinant `256`, and Smith type `1^8 2^6 4`.  It is not itself
2-elementary.

There are 127 nonzero order-two discriminant classes that define integral
index-two overlattices.  Testing all 98 reflection matrices leaves exactly
one invariant class:

```text
v = (0,1,1,0,...,0) mod 2,
x = (e2+e3)/2,
x^2 = -1.
```

Adjoining `x` gives the unique `W_98`-stable closure `M`.  It is odd, has
determinant `64`, signature `(1,14)`, and Smith type `1^9 2^6`.  These are the
data of Scharlau--Kirschmer `List14/Gram14` entry 7,
`I_{14,1}(2^6)`.

### Entry-by-entry doubled-dual equality

Alexeev's `(15,9,1)` lattice has 98 simple roots in orbits of sizes
`14,14,14,56`.  The candidate has the same orbit sizes, but that observation
alone would not be a lineage proof.

The certificate gives an explicit permutation of all 98 walls.  Under this
permutation:

- the 84 candidate roots of square `-4` correspond to Alexeev roots of
  square `-2`;
- the 14 candidate roots of square `-2` correspond to Alexeev divisibility-two
  roots of square `-4`.

For Alexeev's lattice `S`, form `S^dagger=S^*(2)`.  Keep a `(-2)` root
unchanged, replace a divisibility-two `(-4)` root by half of itself, and
double the bilinear form.  After the archived permutation, the resulting
98-by-98 Gram matrix equals the candidate Gram matrix **entry by entry**.
The common candidate Gram hash is

```text
257c0444e7774232672520096ef5639074d83ef2e1a58d625ab2435007f2f330
```

This proves exact chamber equality after doubled duality.  It is stronger
than equality of the edge census

```text
m=2 1547, m=3 49, m=4 70, parallel 735, ultraparallel 2352.
```

It also explains the corrected codimension-two count:

```text
1547 + 49 + 70 = 1666,
```

not 1656.  The permanent Vinberg artifact now contains the correct complete
finite-special-subset vector

```text
(98,1666,14616,83559,342888,1056573,2490674,
 4501714,6177500,6299076,4598006,2249968,646786,75898),
```

with 2,114 cusps, exactly Scharlau--Kirschmer entry 7.

Consequently the abstract chamber index relative to Alexeev's doubled-dual
full chamber is one.  The `H14/98` construction cannot be advertised as a new
hyperbolic Coxeter polytope.  Its remaining lineage interest is the specific
index-two fixed sublattice inside the odd doubled-dual lattice and the way the
Frobenius wall symmetry recovers the chamber.  The `2c` symmetry is outside
the geometric K3 subgroup, so this is not a divisor cone produced by a K3
automorphism.

There is nevertheless now a complete positive divisor-lattice bridge.  Let
`M` be the unique stable odd index-two closure above.  Its doubled dual
`N=M^vee(2)` is even of determinant `512`, and an explicit determinant-one
integral isometry identifies it with Alexeev's actual
`Pic(Hilb^2(X))=(15,9,1)`.  Primitive covector transport preserves all 98
oriented inequalities with positive factors.  The target mirrors split as
`14*(-2,div1)+70*(-2,div2)+14*(-4,div2)`, all reflections are integral, and
the 98 primitive normals generate the full target Picard lattice.  This is
stronger than abstract Gram equality: it joins the characteristic-two fixed
lattice construction to a characteristic-zero hyperkähler divisor lattice
facet by facet.  Since Alexeev already published the same 98-wall chamber on
that lattice, it is recorded as a positive enrichment rather than a new
geometric-chamber slot.  No MBM, prime-exceptional, Kähler/movable-cone, or
chamber-preserving automorphism claim is made; see the
[Hilbert-square scaled-dual carrier theorem](ss2_2c_h14_98_hilb2_scaled_dual_carrier.md).

## Why the bounded corpus alone would have misled us

Neither the ordinary nor the reflection-doubling CoxIter snapshot contains
an `H14`/98-facet header.  Nevertheless the chamber is exactly Alexeev's
published diagram.  The Alexeev source is not represented by that corpus
entry.

This is the useful warning for the fishing program: corpus absence is a
filter, not a priority theorem.  Full weighted-diagram comparison against
primary-source matrices is mandatory whenever those matrices exist.
