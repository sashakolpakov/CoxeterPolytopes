# A nondotted field-generating core forces a finite K3 field set

**Status (2026-08-11): proved conditional finite-field theorem.**  Let an
exact full-trace complex-K3 carrier have field-module rank \(m\ge3\), field
degree \(d\), and

\[
                              md\le20.                    \tag{0.1}
\]

Assume that its minimal Vinberg field is generated by integral cyclic
Cartan coordinates attached to pair cycles and chordless simple cycles, and
that every perimeter pair used by this generating packet is finite or
parabolic at the distinguished real place.  Equivalently, for every such
perimeter edge,

\[
 p_{ij}=a_{ij}a_{ji}
 =\frac{4h(e_i,e_j)^2}{h(e_i,e_i)h(e_j,e_j)}\le4.         \tag{0.2}
\]

Then the occurring fields form a finite set.  A uniform safe bound is

\[
                     \boxed{|\operatorname {Disc}k|\le2^{300}}.          \tag{0.3}
\]

The hypothesis concerns only a field-generating core.  The chamber may have
arbitrarily many facets and may have dotted or ultraparallel pairs outside
that core.

## 1. Exact carrier hypotheses

Let \(k\) be totally real, let \(\mathcal O\subset k\) be the returned
order, and let \(M\) be a finite projective rank-\(m\)
\(\mathcal O\)-module.  Let \(h\) be an admissible symmetric \(k\)-form:
it has signature \((m-1,1)\) at the distinguished embedding \(\sigma _0\)
and is positive definite at every other real embedding.  Suppose:

1. every returned facet reflection preserves \(M\);
2. a complex projective K3 surface has Neron--Severi lattice equal to the
   primitive full trace of \(M\), so \(md=\rho(X)\le20\); and
3. the minimal Vinberg field \(k\) is generated by a collection of pair
   cycles and chordless simple cycles in the nonzero Cartan graph, every
   perimeter pair of which satisfies (0.2).

Here a pair-cycle generator is itself required to satisfy (0.2).  No bound
is imposed on a pair that does not occur in the chosen generating packet.
When \(d=1\), the conclusion is immediate, so the proof below assumes
\(d>1\).

## 2. Integral cycles and the rank bound

For a facet normal \(e_i\), put

\[
 a_{ij}=\frac{2h(e_i,e_j)}{h(e_i,e_i)},\qquad P_i=1-s_i.
\tag{2.1}
\]

For a directed cycle \(C=(i_1,\ldots,i_\ell)\), rank-one composition on
the finite projective module gives

\[
 c(C)=a_{i_1i_2}\cdots a_{i_\ell i_1}
     =\operatorname {Tr}_{\mathcal O}(P_{i_1}\cdots P_{i_\ell})
     \in\mathcal O.                                      \tag{2.2}
\]

Let \(C\) be chordless in the nonzero Cartan graph.  At a
non-distinguished embedding, the principal Gram matrix on its \(\ell\)
vertices is positive semidefinite, cyclic tridiagonal, and has every
perimeter off-diagonal nonzero.  Rows \(2,\ldots,\ell-1\) and columns
\(3,\ldots,\ell\) give a triangular \((\ell-2)\)-minor with nonzero
diagonal.  Hence

\[
       \ell-2\le\operatorname {rank}G_C\le m,
       \qquad \ell\le m+2.                               \tag{2.3}
\]

This is the only global graph input.  It neither bounds the number of
facets nor asserts that every graph edge is nondotted.

## 3. Every generating coordinate is bounded at every place

Symmetry of \(h\) makes a directed cycle equal to its reversal.  Therefore

\[
                        c(C)^2=\prod_{(i,j)\in C}p_{ij}.  \tag{3.1}
\]

At \(\sigma _0\), hypothesis (0.2) bounds every factor by \(4\).  At every
other embedding, positive definiteness and Cauchy--Schwarz give the strict
bound \(0\le\sigma(p_{ij})<4\).  Equations (2.3) and (3.1) imply, for every
embedding \(\sigma:k\hookrightarrow\mathbf R\),

\[
                          |\sigma(c(C))|
                          \le2^\ell\le2^{m+2}.            \tag{3.2}
\]

Pair cycles obey the same final bound directly.  Thus every coordinate in
the assumed field-generating packet is an algebraic integer with uniformly
bounded conjugates.

## 4. Primitive element and discriminant

Put \(D=\lfloor20/m\rfloor\).  For \(m\ge3\) and \(d>1\), one has
\(d\le D\le6\).  Choose a minimal subcollection of the bounded cyclic
coordinates that generates \(k\).  Every strict adjunction multiplies the
degree by at least two, so at most two coordinates are needed.

If two are needed, call them \(x,y\).  Among

\[
                         x+qy,\qquad 0\le q\le15,         \tag{4.1}
\]

one is primitive.  Indeed, each of the at most
\(\binom62=15\) unordered pairs of embeddings forbids at most one value of
\(q\), and joint field generation prevents a pair of embeddings from
agreeing on both \(x\) and \(y\).  The selected primitive algebraic integer
\(\theta\) satisfies

\[
 |\sigma(\theta)|\le16\,2^{m+2}=2^{m+6},\qquad
 |\sigma(\theta)-\tau(\theta)|\le2^{m+7}.                \tag{4.2}
\]

The field discriminant divides the discriminant of \(\theta\), hence

\[
 |\operatorname {Disc}k|
 \le2^{(m+7)d(d-1)}.                                     \tag{4.3}
\]

Exact maximization subject to \(m\ge3\), \(d\ge2\), and \(md\le20\)
gives

\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrr}
m&3&4&5&6&7&8&9&10\\ \hline
D=\lfloor20/m\rfloor&6&5&4&3&2&2&2&2\\
(m+7)D(D-1)&300&220&144&78&28&30&32&34.
\end{array}                                               \tag{4.4}
\]

The maximum is \(300\), at \((m,d)=(3,6)\), proving (0.3).
Hermite--Minkowski then gives a finite field set.

The exponent \(300\) is the sharp maximum of the displayed uniform binary
primitive-grid estimate.  It is not asserted to be the optimal possible
numerical discriminant constant.

## 5. Relation to the simplex theorem and nonclaims

For an ordinary finite-volume Coxeter simplex, every facet pair is finite
or parabolic and every simple cycle has length at most the facet count
\(m\).  The simplex theorem therefore replaces \(2^{m+2}\) in (3.2) by
\(2^m\) and obtains \(2^{240}\).

The present theorem allows a higher-facet chamber and dotted pairs outside
the generating core.  Its price is the rank-only chordless bound
\(\ell\le m+2\), which changes the safe binary exponent from \(240\) to
\(300\).  It does **not**:

- construct a K3 carrier or prove POLYTOPE-to-SURFACE essential
  surjectivity;
- prove that every minimal Vinberg field admits such a nondotted generating
  core;
- control a field-generating pair cycle with \(p_{ij}>4\), or a generating
  chordless cycle containing an ultraparallel perimeter edge;
- bound the canonical mixed intersection
  \(H\cdot\tau_\theta(H)\);
- prove finiteness of chambers, integral hosts, or complete Gram packets; or
- use reflection maximality, finite volume, or a classification of Coxeter
  diagrams.
