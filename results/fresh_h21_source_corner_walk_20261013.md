## Exact endpoint construction

Let the inequalities be `(x,w_i) <= 0`, with strict timelike center `h`, and let `v` represent a finite corner. Its 21 incident normals have positive-definite Gram matrix `A`. Releasing incident wall `j`, let `u_j` be the dual vector in their span. The edge is

`x(t) = v - t u_j`,

and its next endpoint occurs at the least positive ratio `(v,w_i)/(u_j,w_i)` among walls with `(u_j,w_i) < 0`. Every one of the 210 wall inequalities is checked exactly at the resulting primitive rational ray. Its entire active set is recomputed, rather than inferred from the first colliding wall.

At an ideal corner `delta`, the active diagram is a disjoint union of irreducible affine components of total rank 20. The certificate checks each component's corank-one Gram matrix, strictly positive kernel, and positive null-vector identity. Deleting one wall from each component gives every outgoing edge support `E`, a positive-definite set of 20 normals. Let `p` be the orthogonal projection of `h` to `E` perpendicular. The edge is

`x(t) = p + t delta`,

and, moving from the cusp as `t` decreases, the other endpoint is at

`t = max_i (-(p,w_i)/(delta,w_i))`, over `(delta,w_i) < 0`.

All signs, endpoint norms, active sets, and positive-definite edge supports are checked over the rationals. In particular, ideal endpoints are actual ideal vertices with complete affine links; they are not classified as unresolved rays.

## Why the census is complete

The [six source symmetries](../data/enumerations/fresh_h20_global_interior_sections_20261013.json) preserve the ambient form, the center, and all 210 normals exactly. The [group archive](../data/enumerations/fresh_h21_source_corner_walk_20261013_group.npz) stores all 241,920 distinct wall permutations with predecessor words. Both implementations check those words and closure under every generator.

Each vertex orbit is identified by the lexicographically least image of its complete active-wall set under this group. Its stabilizer is computed from the entire group. Finite-wall releases and ideal-component deletions are partitioned into full stabilizer orbits, with exact coverage checks. Every representative endpoint returns to a recorded corner orbit, and every recorded corner has been processed.

The projective closure of the known finite-volume source chamber is a convex polytope with connected corner graph. A nonempty vertex set closed under every outgoing edge therefore contains the entire graph. This is the completeness argument; the agreement with an external cusp classification is a separate check.

The three cusp types absent from the earlier sample are `D4^5`, `A1^3 + A17`, and `D7 + A11`, represented by corner records 58, 388, and 390. The independently generated norm-two root types agree with all 18 published complement types; see the [separate lattice and literature audit](fresh_h22_extension_feasibility_20261013.md).

## Independent replay and scope

The [separate checker](../scripts/fresh_h21_source_corner_walk_20261013_independent.py) imports no producer projection or endpoint code. It independently verifies the group, all canonical active sets and stabilizers, all rational corner inequalities, affine kernels, and the exhaustive deletion-orbit partitions.

For each edge it uses a different certificate: two distinct feasible future causal corners have exactly the same 20 positive-definite incident walls. These walls define a hyperbolic line. The connecting segment is feasible, and every other inequality is strict in its relative interior, so no wall intervenes. This verifies the endpoints without reproducing the producer's inverse-matrix formulas. The saved [independent audit](fresh_h21_source_corner_walk_20261013_independent.json) records the full replay.

Replay the frozen certificate with:

```sh
PYTHONPATH=src:. python scripts/fresh_h21_source_corner_walk_20261013_independent.py --verify
```

To reconstruct the walk from its inputs:

```sh
PYTHONPATH=src:. python scripts/fresh_h21_source_corner_walk_20261013.py --max-vertices 1000 --max-seconds 1200
```

The producer also supports `--resume` for an explicitly incomplete checkpoint. The final authoritative artifact has an empty queue and contains no live-job status.

These 18 representatives organize all inherited H21 cusps, but an H22 cap collection that breaks the source symmetry must still be checked against the relevant images. Checking only the 18 representatives would not establish complete cusp coverage. Additional directions in the raised dimension and the full finite-volume criterion remain separate obligations.
