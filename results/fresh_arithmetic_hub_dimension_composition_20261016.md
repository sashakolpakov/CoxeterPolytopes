# All-field arithmetic bound with hub-count weights

The internally reviewed proof candidate now gives **dimension at most 22**
for finite-volume arithmetic hyperbolic reflection groups. Generation and
saved replay of the [composition checker](../scripts/fresh_arithmetic_hub_dimension_composition_20261016.py)
and [bound-22 certificate](fresh_arithmetic_hub_dimension_composition_20261016_bound22.json)
pass. This is a computer-assisted research result awaiting external
mathematical validation; no literature-priority claim is made.

Suppose a compact arithmetic chamber has dimension greater than 22.
Vinberg's compact theorem gives dimension at most 29. His field restriction
in dimensions at least 22 leaves only Q(√2), Q(√5), and Q(cos(2π/7)). The
complete earlier Q(√2) and cubic-seven candidates give dimension at most 20.
The [new complete Q(√5) argument](fresh_sqrt5_hub_count_coverage_20261016.md)
gives dimension at most 22. Thus each of the twenty-one field/dimension
branches for dimensions 23–29 is excluded.
[Vinberg, Theorems 1–2](https://www.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=mmo&option_lang=rus&paperid=444&what=fullt).

The noncompact arithmetic case already has the sharp published upper bound
21, with dimension 20 excluded. It is unaffected by these compact weights.
Every arithmetic lattice generated by reflections is of simplest type, so
this composition already includes arithmetic first-type reflection groups.
It says nothing about dimensions of general first-type lattices that are
not virtually generated by reflections.
[Esselmann](https://doi.org/10.1006/jnth.1996.0141),
[Belolipetsky, Lemma 2.1 and §§1,7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.03111).

The checker requires complete field certificates and validates their
recursive manifests. It inherits the actual arithmetic and geometric
proofs, rather than treating a source hash as a proof. The unrestricted
compact bound 29 and the separate unrestricted finite-volume candidate 163
are unchanged. No new finite-volume example is supplied by this result.

```sh
python3 scripts/fresh_arithmetic_hub_dimension_composition_20261016.py --bound 22
python3 scripts/fresh_arithmetic_hub_dimension_composition_20261016.py --bound 22 --verify
```
