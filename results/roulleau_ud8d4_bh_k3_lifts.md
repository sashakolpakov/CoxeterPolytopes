# Two genuine K3-automorphism realizations of the `U+D8+D4` folds

## Positive result

Two folds that were previously certified only as wall-diagram and
Néron--Severi isometries are induced by genuine automorphisms on suitable
special complex K3 surfaces:

| Brandhorst--Hofmann row | automorphism order | order on `NS` | archived action | hyperbolic polytope | Vinberg result |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---|
| `0.8.1.3` | 8 | 2 | class 6, `TYPE_2` | `H11 / 16` | finite-volume, noncompact; 112 finite vertices, 9 cusps, 750 elliptic edges |
| `0.16.0.3` | 16 | 4 | class 14, `TYPE_4` | `H10 / 14` | finite-volume, noncompact; 44 finite vertices, 3 cusps, 254 elliptic edges |

Thus these are positive algebro-geometric realizations of two actual
hyperbolic Coxeter polytopes.  They do **not** assert that either abstract
diagram is new worldwide.  The new statement in this project is the surface
realization: the earlier certificate deliberately stopped at diagram/lattice
isometries because the general Roulleau surface has too small an automorphism
group.

The exact certificate is
[`roulleau_ud8d4_bh_k3_lifts.json`](../data/enumerations/roulleau_ud8d4_bh_k3_lifts.json),
generated by
[`certify_roulleau_ud8d4_bh_k3_lifts.py`](../scripts/certify_roulleau_ud8d4_bh_k3_lifts.py).

## Database-to-divisor calculation

The source rows are from Brandhorst--Hofmann's complete classification of
finite K3 automorphism groups ([article](https://doi.org/10.1017/fms.2023.50),
[database v2.1](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10410278)).  For each pinned
22-dimensional K3-lattice row, the replay:

1. verifies the even unimodular K3 lattice of signature `(3,19)` and the
   exact order of the distinguished isometry;
2. takes the saturated kernel of the relevant cyclotomic polynomial to get
   the generic transcendental lattice;
3. takes its saturated orthogonal complement to get the generic Picard
   lattice; and
4. restricts the action to that Picard lattice.

Both rows give

`NS = U + D8(-1) + D4(-1)`,

the even 2-elementary lattice with invariants `(r,a,delta)=(14,4,0)`,
determinant `-16`, and signature `(1,13)`.  In both cases the transcendental
lattice has rank 8, signature `(2,6)`, and determinant 16.  Its characteristic
polynomial is `(x^4+1)^2` in the order-eight case and `x^8+1` in the
order-sixteen case.

## Why the archived actions are exactly the database actions

Matching a characteristic polynomial is not enough.  The certificate splits
each Picard action primitively into its fixed lattice `F`, coinvariant lattice
`C`, and the order-four glue graph defining `NS` as an overlattice of
`F + C`.

For `0.8.1.3` and class 6:

- `F` has signature `(1,11)`, determinant `-16`, and Smith invariants
  `1^10 4^2`;
- both fixed discriminant quadratic forms have histogram
  `{0:2, 1/4:4, 1:2, 5/4:4, 3/2:4}`;
- the fixed discriminant isometry on `Z/4 + Z/4` is
  `[[1,1],[1,0]]`;
- the archived coinvariant is `diag(-4,-4)`, while the database basis gives
  `[[-8,4],[4,-4]]`; the integral intertwiner is
  `[[1,-1],[0,1]]`, and both actions are `-1`; and
- that fixed map and the induced coinvariant discriminant map carry all four
  elements of the primitive glue graph exactly.

For `0.16.0.3` and class 14:

- `F` has signature `(1,10)`, determinant `8`, and Smith invariants
  `1^10 8`;
- both fixed discriminant quadratic forms have histogram
  `{0:2, 3/8:2, 11/8:2, 3/2:2}`;
- the fixed discriminant isometry on `Z/8` is multiplication by `7`;
- the archived and database coinvariants are two bases of `A3(-2)`, with
  Grams
  `[[-4,-2,-2],[-2,-4,-2],[-2,-2,-4]]` and
  `[[-4,0,2],[0,-4,2],[2,2,-4]]`; the certificate gives an explicit
  unimodular isometry that also intertwines the order-four actions; and
- again, all four primitive glue elements are carried exactly.

The fixed lattices are indefinite and their ranks 12 and 11 are far above
their discriminant lengths 2 and 1.  Nikulin's uniqueness/surjectivity
criterion ([Theorem 1.14.2](https://doi.org/10.1070/IM1980v014n01ABEH001060))
therefore lifts the displayed discriminant isometries to integral fixed-lattice
isometries.  Together with the explicit coinvariant intertwiners and compatible
glue, they extend to conjugacies of the **full** Picard-lattice actions.

## Kähler and Torelli handoff

In both cases one half of the coinvariant Gram is an even negative-definite
integral lattice.  Every nonzero coinvariant norm is therefore a negative
multiple of four, so the algebraic coinvariant contains no `(-2)` roots.
This is the required Kähler-type nondegeneracy, not merely a signature check.

The Brandhorst--Hofmann rows already represent genuine purely
non-symplectic K3 automorphisms.  Transport their Hodge actions through the
Picard conjugacies, retain their periods, and choose the marked Kähler chamber
to be the corresponding action-stable complete Roulleau chamber.  The refined
period map and Strong Torelli then realize the transported actions by K3
automorphisms.  The automorphism orders are larger than their divisor-action
orders because their remaining order acts on the transcendental lattice.

## Hyperbolic finite-volume handoff

The volume conclusions still come from the complete Vinberg audits, not from
the existence of a K3 surface.  For `H11/16`, all 750 elliptic edges have two
finite/ideal endpoints and 18 full parabolic neighbourhoods pass.  For
`H10/14`, all 254 elliptic edges pass and seven full parabolic neighbourhoods
pass.  Both have cusps and hence are noncompact; both have zero bad edges and
finite volume.  The second reflection group has exact index 992 in the known
full `U+E8(-1)+A1(-1)` reflection group.  The first has proven finite
containment in the odd unimodular full group, while its numerical index remains
unrecovered because both odd-dimensional Coxeter Euler characteristics vanish.
