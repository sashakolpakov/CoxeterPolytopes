exactly **19** finite-automorphism K3 Néron--Severi stocks of rank at least

## Ancillary-code audit

The source tar contains two Magma files, but not nineteen frozen wall
matrices.

- `FiniteAutAlgoMagma.txt` supplies `SmoothRationalCurves` (line 26), its
  incremental variant (line 102), the ADE-lattice Gram constructor (lines
  300 and 347), and `MatrixOfIntersections` (line 378).
- `ExamplesMagma.txt` gives executable rank-11/90-wall code at lines 76--142
  and rank-13/39-wall code at lines 145--214.  Its `LL;` and matrix-printing
  commands do not include their output, so it is a recomputation route, not
  a frozen matrix source.

For the 90-, 59-, and 39-wall cases, the paper explains at lines 729--820
that the degree search alone was too heavy and that exact completeness was
instead obtained by induced chambers in (II_{1,17}).  This distinction is
recorded per case in the JSON certificate.

## Hyperbolic status

All nineteen full nef chambers are already positive finite-volume,
noncompact hyperbolic Coxeter polytopes.  The reason is structural:
Roulleau's surfaces have finite automorphism group, so the K3 Weyl group is
finite index in the arithmetic orthogonal group.  Every lattice in the table
contains (U), hence has an isotropic boundary ray and the chamber has a
cusp.  This establishes finite volume without confusing rational
polyhedrality with a standalone Vinberg test.

These are published examples.  The useful fishing opportunity is their
finite wall-stock symmetry denominator.  Roulleau's `t`, `n`, and `tn`
labels describe how hyperelliptic involutions act on (NS(X)); they are not
the full weighted-wall automorphism groups and will not be substituted for
an exact graph computation.

## Closed denominator and next fishing frontier

The source-scout and transcription stages are complete.  All fourteen
rank-11--14 stocks are exact machine stocks.  The two smaller rank-12 graph
stocks exhaust `2+10` subgroup classes and yield three cofinite `H10` folds.
The 59-wall formula stock has weighted group `C2^2 x S6` of order `2880`;
GAP exhausts all `914` subgroup conjugacy classes.  Exactly two proper classes
fix rank at least 11.  Their strict fixed-nef sections are:

- `H10/38`: 526 finite vertices, 141 cusps, 4,518 elliptic edges;
- `H10/45`: 1,048 finite vertices, 93 cusps, 7,332 elliptic edges.

Both have zero bad Vinberg edges and zero bad affine neighborhoods.  Their
saturated fixed lattices are even of determinant 64 and have Smith invariants
`1^6 2^4 4`; respectively 15 and 3 maximal reflection-stable index-8
overclosures are odd unimodular `I_(1,10)`.  The exact indices in Vinberg's
`P10` group are `2,276,640` and `2,023,680`.  Neither selected action comes
from an automorphism of the general K3: the first double-plane deck action has
fixed rank 4, the second deck action is trivial on `NS`, and the rank-11
central `p67` action is a diagram/lattice symmetry only.  Special-period
realization and worldwide literal priority remain open.

The 17-wall stock exhausts its four `S3` subgroup classes and gives known full
reflection chambers in `H11` and `H10`.
The 39-wall stock exhausts all 57 classes of `S5 x C2`: two high classes have
isotropic orbit normals, while the other three give a bounded-priority
`H11/31` chamber and one `H10/23` chamber represented by both `C3` and `S3`.
The 15- and 27-wall rank-14 stocks exhaust respectively `4` and `33` subgroup classes.
The 15-wall stock gives two known full chambers; the 27-wall stock gives four
cofinite diagrams whose bounded literal priority remains open.  The two
rank-11 direct graphs give published `H10/12` and `H10/14` full nef chambers,
but their complete weighted subgroup denominator has no proper fixed section
above `H9`.  The 90-wall stock has exact weighted group `S8 x C2`; its faithful
rank-11 action proves at once that every nontrivial subgroup fixes rank at
most 10, so it also has no fold above `H9`.  The Roulleau rank-`>=11`
fishing denominator is therefore closed; the next catches must come from a
different stock family or from lowering the theorem boundary.
