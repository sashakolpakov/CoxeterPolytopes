# Whole-simplex charging substantially reduces, but does not remove, the local obstruction

The [fresh partial model](../scripts/fresh_sqrt5_rank13_simplex_aggregate_lp_20261017.py) replaces independent triangle demands by aggregate demands over the [uniquely determined maximal simplex faces](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_triangle_simplex_flags_20261017.md). It reconstructs all triangular corners of every retained simplex, including newly encountered corners and whole forests. A triangle requires two units, a tetrahedron eight, and a four-dimensional simplex twenty. The model has 3,649 literal simplex stars, 1,408 distinct aggregate rows, 605 whole rank-13 forests, and 19,425 free nonnegative corner-orbit variables; every represented vertex retains its complete 78-pair budget.

The [saved exact certificates](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_simplex_aggregate_lp_20261017.json) give a feasible budget **251118144400/19999999999** and a lower bound **3013418485632/240000283351 > 12**. The numerical optimum is approximately 12.5559072196, substantially below the previous independent-triangle optimum near 15.23. Exact recovery supplies bounds, not an exact optimum. Generation and fresh saved reconstruction check the full aggregate mappings, all vertex budgets, and every rational dual column inequality.

The new dual uses 336 aggregate rows: 52 triangle stars, 69 tetrahedra, and 215 four-dimensional simplex stars. Its largest whole-forest multipliers occur at `A1+A5+H3+H4`, `A4+H3+H4+I2(5)`, and `A6+H3+H4`. Thus respecting this genuine higher-face incidence removes much of the earlier obstruction, but this retained domain still cannot reach the rank-13 target twelve under a single unrestricted whole-forest payment rule.

This remains a partial method diagnostic, not a dimension bound. Only simplex stars extending the earlier positive-dual triangle representatives are included. General maximal-simplex families, quadrilaterals, and absent finite vertex forests remain uncovered. A later common-discriminant restriction may interact with simplex aggregation, but its feasibility and complete class-specific coverage are not asserted here.
