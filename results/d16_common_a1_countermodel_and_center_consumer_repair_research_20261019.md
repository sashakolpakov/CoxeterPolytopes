## 2. The forced parallel cusp can itself be completed locally

One might try to exclude the preceding model because d1,d2 are parallel.
Their future null sum is

    v=d1+d2=-omega15_1-omega15_2+2sqrt2*h.

Its old incident finite factors are A15+A15+A1+D24: in each D16 remove
alpha15, leaving the A15 chain alpha0 through14. For these four finite

are `theta_i=alpha0_i+...+alpha14_i=e1_i-e16_i`; the A1 root is a; the
D24 highest root is its standard `e1+e2`. Each theta_J has square2 and
is orthogonal to v. Add

    n_J=v-theta_J.

All four have square2 and are mutually orthogonal. Their pairings with
old roots are native and nonpositive. Each n_i completes its old A15
factor to affine A15; n_a completes the old A1 to affine A1; and n_D
completes D24 to affine D24. Each has product -1 with the missing leaf
of the OTHER D16. Its product with its own missing leaf is zero for
n_i; both n_a and n_D have product -1 with both missing leaves.
The only double bond in these old-root pairings is `n_a.a=-2`.

The products with c_i,d_i are particularly simple:

    n_J.d_i=0 for every J,i;
    n_i.c_i=0, n_i.c_(3-i)=-1;
    n_a.c_i=0, n_D.c_i=-1.

Thus all added-wall products are zero, -1 or -2. They are strict at h:
`n_J.h=-2sqrt2`. The old vertex remains an actual finite vertex of the

    affine A1(d1,d2), affine A15, affine A15, affine A1, affine D24.

bounded product of alcoves, not an unfinished finite-factor cone.

The new walls do not cut either old prism. On the i-th supporting
section, use the existing prism coordinates

`-4,-5/2,-10/7,-2,-1,-10/7`, so is strictly negative everywhere

This strengthens only a **local compatibility** conclusion. It does not
construct a finite-volume57-polytope or prove that the full halfspace
