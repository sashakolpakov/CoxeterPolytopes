## Why change the angle

The [extension feasibility audit](fresh_h22_extension_feasibility_20261013.md) proves a conditional restriction for completions retaining the old 210-wall reflection subgroup. Its unipotents force the admissible base field of any pseudo-arithmetic completion to be Q. Its trace field must then be multiquadratic over Q. A retained π/7 pair contributes a cubic subfield, so such a completion would have to be non-pseudo-arithmetic. This does not prove that the π/7 construction cannot be completed.

The new construction avoids that particular restriction. With `T=2cos(π/6)=sqrt(3)`, each original cap recipe has the form

```
r = -(q*T/2) u + epsilon*sqrt(A+C*T²) v,
A=q/v², C=-q²*u²/(4*v²), (u,v)=0.
```

Here u,v and A,C are rational, and A>0, C≥0. Its norm identity and every prescribed finite contact are checked as rational identities in the free parameter T. Previously prescribed π/7 contacts therefore become π/6 contacts. Every remaining pair is checked afresh; earlier dotted inequalities are not assumed to survive the substitution.

One type fails: original type 12, candidate 190, against parent wall 40 has normalized cosine squared `48/49`. If the angle were π/m for an integer m, then `2cos(2π/m)=94/49` would be a rational algebraic integer. It is not an integer, so no Coxeter label can repair this pair. This type is omitted. The other 22 original types pass.
