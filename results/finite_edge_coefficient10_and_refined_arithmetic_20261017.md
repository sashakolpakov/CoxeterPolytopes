## Seven-simplex faces and finite-edge triangle payments

The [171-case calculation](fresh_finite_edge_seven_simplex_20261017.md)
and [four remaining cases](fresh_seven_simplex_affine_and_d8_20261017.md)
complete all175 black/internal-white choices in the order-eight through
order-ten quasi-Lannér census. Arbitrary additional supporting whites
are handled by affine-kernel orthogonality and nonnegative Schur
corrections, including unbounded indirect white tails. No rank cutoff
on those tails is used.

The [coefficient10 theorem](fresh_finite_edge_cusp_tradeoff10_20261017.md)
independently reconstructs the last four cases with separate SymPy
arithmetic and composes the exact partition. Every actual seven-simplex
face has at most three ideal vertices, or all eight vertices are ideal.
For blocks with at least two finite vertices, the all-ideal alternative
is irrelevant. Together with the previously proved bounds for eight-
and nine-simplex faces, this yields

\[
 2(T_0+T_1)\le10E_{ff}=5n v_f-5E_{fi}\le5n v_f.
\]

Here T_j counts triangular faces with j ideal vertices. The argument uses
actual original-wall simplex stars; it does not assume their induced
angles form a Coxeter simplex. The unresolved formal extremizer is now
a six-simplex with two finite and five ideal vertices. Triangles with
two or three ideal vertices and quadrilateral demand remain separate,
so this inequality alone gives no ambient dimension bound.

The [residual incidence budget](fresh_finite_edge_cusp_tradeoff10_20261017.md)
keeps these obligations explicit:

\[
 2(T_2+T_3)+Q\ge(b_n-5n)v_f+5E_{fi}
                  +\alpha_n\sum_v E(L_v).
\]

The coefficients and cusp-link notation retain their definitions in the
frozen truncation and tradeoff proofs. Generation and fresh saved replays
passed for all three new seven-simplex packages. These are complete
internally reviewed lemmas, not externally refereed dimension theorems.
