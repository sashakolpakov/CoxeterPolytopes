# All-field arithmetic dimension composition

**Generation and saved replay pass.** The
[composition checker](../scripts/fresh_arithmetic_dimension_composition_20261015.py)
and [bound-23 certificate](fresh_arithmetic_dimension_composition_20261015_bound23.json)
bind complete field certificates and explicitly check every excluded dimension.

The internally reviewed research proof candidate states that a finite-volume arithmetic hyperbolic
reflection group has dimension at most 23. The compact case follows from
the three field-specific arguments and Vinberg's published high-dimensional
field restriction; the noncompact case already has the stronger sharp
bound 21. This concerns reflection lattices and their Coxeter chambers,
not arbitrary arithmetic lattices or infinite-volume reflection groups.

## Logical composition

Assume there is a compact arithmetic reflection chamber of dimension n>23.
Vinberg's compact theorem first gives n≤29. Since n≥24≥22, his field
restriction leaves precisely three possible arithmetic ground fields:
Q(√2), Q(√5), and Q(cos(2π/7)).
[Vinberg, Theorems 1–2](https://www.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=mmo&option_lang=rus&paperid=444&what=fullt).

- For Q(√2), the [complete all-size quadrilateral review](fresh_sqrt2_quad_exhaustion_20261015_review.md)
  supplies the coverage premise of the separate
  [weight review](fresh_sqrt2_weight_bound_20261015_review.md), giving n≤20.
- For Q(cos(2π/7)), the [complete independent field proof](fresh_compact_global_bound_20261015_review.md)
  gives n≤20.
- For Q(√5), the [new complete coverage composition](fresh_sqrt5_dimension23_coverage_20261015.md)
  excludes all of 24,25,26,27,28,29 and states n≤23.

Thus all eighteen field/dimension branches in n=24,…,29 are excluded. This
uses complete field proofs, not merely their conditional local weight
calculations. The checker validates their recursive source hashes and
records each branch explicitly. It is a logical and dependency binder;
the independent arithmetic and geometric reviews remain named inputs.

If the chamber is noncompact and arithmetic, Esselmann's theorem gives
n≤21 with n≠20; Borcherds' example attains 21. Hence the compact bound 23 also applies to all finite-volume arithmetic
reflection groups in this proof candidate.
[Esselmann](https://doi.org/10.1006/jnth.1996.0141),
[the dimension statement and example in Belolipetsky, §§1 and 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.03111).

Every arithmetic hyperbolic lattice generated by reflections is of the
simplest type. There is no further arithmetic reflection type outside this
composition. This does not bound dimensions of general simplest-type
lattices whose reflection subgroup has infinite index.
[Vinberg's lemma, restated as Belolipetsky Lemma 2.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.03111).

The result remains a computer-assisted research proof candidate. Internal
exact checks and independent implementations do not establish external
validation, formal proof-assistant verification or priority in the literature.
The unrestricted compact bound and the separate nonarithmetic finite-volume
programme are outside this composition.
