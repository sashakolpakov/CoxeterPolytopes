## What the primary source does and does not supply

Kondō's [classification paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.00591) supplies the
following ingredients directly.

- Theorem 1.1/Table 2 gives the characteristic-five, one-boundary Type-VII
  isomorphism class.
- Proposition 3.1 identifies its Coble--Mukai lattice with `E10`.
- The general negative-curve statement says that the only negative curves
  are boundary, `(-2)`, and `(-1)` curves.
- Proposition 3.4 identifies the Coble--Mukai chamber with the nef slice.
- Proposition 3.7, applied in Example 4.15, makes the displayed twenty
  effective roots the complete effective irreducible-root list.
- Proposition 3.8 gives the Halphen description of every genus-one
  fibration, and Lemma 3.11 identifies those fibrations with primitive
  isotropic vectors of the Coble--Mukai chamber.
- Example 4.15 constructs the characteristic-five Type-VII surface and says
  that the twenty curves have the Type-VII graph; Proposition 4.16 gives
  `Aut(S)=S5` and its `R`-invariant.

The primary paper does **not** state “51 `(-1)` curves,” “72 full-nef
walls,” the `H10` face lattice, or the finite-volume endpoint census.  Those
are derived and computed here.  The abstract number 51 is nevertheless not
new; a separate characteristic-two Type-VII source is recorded below.  The
new content here is the characteristic-five one-boundary lift of those
cusps to all 51 `(-1)` curves and the resulting full `H10` chamber.

## The integral `Gamma_VII -> 20C` bridge

An equality of unweighted graphs would not justify importing Shimada's
cusps.  The bridge is stronger and can be proved from the exact twenty-root
Gram alone.

Let `L` be the rank-ten lattice generated by the twenty Type-VII roots.  An
HNF basis computed from their entrywise Gram has

```text
det(L)                    -4
Smith invariants          1^8, 2, 2
A_L                       (Z/2)^2.
```

The four two-torsion discriminant classes have representatives whose exact
squares are

```text
0, -5/2, -2, -7/2  modulo 2Z.
```

Hence there is exactly one nonzero even/isotropic order-two class.  It
defines the unique even index-two overlattice of `L`; that overlattice is
unimodular of signature `(1,9)`, hence is `E10`.  Therefore the entrywise
Gram isometry between Kondō's labelled `Gamma_VII` roots and Shimada's
`20C` roots extends to an integral `E10` isometry.  This proves the existence
of the marking used by the certificate.  It does not assert uniqueness of a
marking after composing with lattice automorphisms.

This discriminant-form calculation closes a real lineage gap: neither the
name `Type VII` nor the `R`-invariant alone was used as a substitute for an
integral coordinate identification.

## Why there are exactly 72 full-nef walls

Write the unique boundary as

\[
                 B\in |-2K_S|,\qquad b=B/2=-K_S.
\]

Then `b^2=-1`, `b^perp=CM(S)=E10`, and the rational-surface lattice splits
integrally as

\[
          \operatorname{NS}(S)=E_{10}\perp\langle-1\rangle
                               \cong I_{1,10}.
\]

Every primitive cusp `f` of the complete twenty-root chamber gives a
genus-one fibration.  One boundary rules out the index-one Halphen case,
which blows singularities on two fibres.  In the index-two case, one
boundary forces the blown reduced fibre to be `I1` or `II`; its node or cusp
has one exceptional `(-1)` curve `E`, and

\[
                         B+2E=2f,\qquad E=f-b.       \tag{1}
\]

Conversely, adjunction gives `B.E=2` for every `(-1)` curve.  The divisor
`B+2E` is nef and isotropic: its intersections with `B`, `E`, and every
other irreducible curve are nonnegative.  The one-boundary Halphen
description makes `f=E+b` the primitive half-fibre.  Thus (1) is a
bijection between primitive Coble--Mukai cusps and all `(-1)` curves, not
merely a way to produce some of them.

The exact `20C` chamber has 51 primitive ideal rays.  Kondō supplies exactly
twenty `(-2)` curves, and the boundary is the only `(-4)` curve.  His
negative-curve trichotomy therefore gives the complete wall list

\[
 \{b\}\ \cup\ \{R_1,\ldots,R_{20}\}\ \cup\
 \{f-b:f\text{ is one of the 51 primitive cusps}\},
\]

of size `1+20+51=72`.  The exact double description separately proves every
one of these 72 inequalities irredundant.


Every wall has a rank-ten incident face, so all 72 walls are genuine.  The

```text
order 10:  24,466,316 compatible masks ->  18,761 elliptic masks.
```

### All parabolic ends, including disconnected cusps

Every connected affine diagram is generated from a positive-definite
principal deletion and certified by an exact zero determinant.  The search

each component its complete zero-neighbourhood is an exact ideal active
set.  Conversely, each of the 1,731 ideal active sets decomposes into these
affine components with total Coxeter rank nine; cusps with up to six
connected components occur and are explicitly retained.

This is the missing distinction between a noncompact infinite-volume
chamber and a finite-covolume chamber with Euclidean cusp links.  Both the
elliptic edge-extension and full parabolic-neighbourhood tests pass with

## Arithmetic and index lineage

All 72 primitive normal reflections are integral involutive isometries, all
pairs have Coxeter/parallel/ultraparallel relations, and their Smith span is
the whole `I_(1,10)`.  The group is therefore an arithmetic reflection
subgroup of the classical `P10` group.  Solomon's exact calculation gives

\[
 \chi(W_{72})=-\frac{527}{7560},\qquad
 \chi(W(P10))=-\frac1{11147673600},
\]

and hence

\[
                    [W(P10):W_{72}]=777093120.
\]

That integer is not novelty evidence.  Shimada's official Type-VII table
already lists `777093120` for the `20C` `H9` induced chamber; see the
[primary slides, table on page 20](https://www.math.sci.hiroshima-u.ac.jp/shimada/slides/20190904Sendai/2019SepSendai.pdf).
The same preservation of the seed index occurs for the Type-I and Type-II
one-boundary lifts.  It is evidence for a general cusp-extension/index
theorem, not evidence that the present arithmetic class is new.

The Scharlau--Kirschmer
[reflective Lorentzian archive](https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Markus.Kirschmer/lorentz/index.html)
catalogues full reflection chambers of lattices, not all finite-index
reflection-subgroup chambers.  It identifies the ambient lattice/full-group
lineage but cannot decide whether this 72-wall subgroup chamber was printed
elsewhere.

## Bounded catalogue and literature audit

At CoxIter commit `b800be48d8240fbabd2d1c9542e61054584ad7fe`, the complete
local scan covers 1,214 ordinary `.coxiter` records and 2,959 records in the
separate doubling archive.  Neither lane contains an `H10/72` header.  This
is grade `B1` evidence only: there is no same-header record on which an exact
weighted-Gram comparison can be run.  CoxIter is not a worldwide census of
finite-index chambers.

Broad exact-phrase searches for `H10/72`, “72 facets” with hyperbolic
Coxeter terminology, the exact Euler characteristic, and the exact index
found the known Shimada `20C` seed but no source for the full 72-wall
chamber.  Search silence is discovery evidence, not a mathematical absence
theorem.

There is an important positive lineage hit for the **51-cusp seed**.
Mukai's report *Automorphism of K3 surfaces and decomposition groups of
rational sextics* in
[Oberwolfach Report 33/2018](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46758?nt=1)
explicitly gives 51 genus-one fibrations on the characteristic-two
Type-VII Coble surface `R_-10`, split as

```text
A8: 20; A7+A1: 15; A4+A4: 6; A5+A2+A1: 10.
```

Katsura--Kondō's
[characteristic-two classification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.14537)
places Type VII there with two or ten boundary components; Mukai's
`R_-10` is the ten-boundary carrier, not the present characteristic-five
one-boundary surface.  This is an exact seed match, not merely a coincident
total: decomposing the 51 archived `20C` ideal active sets by their
nonorthogonal components gives

```text
(9):20, (8,2):15, (5,5):6, (6,3,2):10,
```

which is precisely Mukai's four affine types.  Thus the 51 ideal
directions/fibration census was known independently of this computation.
That source does not give the one-boundary formula `E=f-b`, 51 resulting
`(-1)` walls, or the full `H10/72` cone.

Dimension ten was populated long before this construction.  In particular,
Allcock's [infinitely-many theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.0138) gives
infinitely many finite-covolume Coxeter groups in every dimension at most
19.  Thus no first-in-dimension claim is available.

The defensible novelty boundary is:

> A new exact positive full-divisor realization in this project, and a
> plausible literal-subgroup-chamber priority candidate; not a new
> dimension, lattice, maximal reflection group, or arithmetic
> commensurability class, and not a proved worldwide-new abstract polytope.
