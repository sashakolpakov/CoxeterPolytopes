## Exact statement verified on printed page 6

Bugaenko fixes

\[
 \varphi=(\sqrt5+1)/2,\qquad
 f_n=-\varphi x_0^2+x_1^2+\cdots+x_n^2
\]

over the ring `Z[phi]`. The integral automorphism group is defined for this
literal unimodular form. Its reflection subgroup `Gamma_n` is generated by all
hyperplane reflections belonging to that integral group. The text introduces
its fundamental polyhedron `P_n` and explicitly states these two conclusions:

* `P_n` is bounded for `n <= 7`.
* `P_n` has infinite hyperbolic volume for `n >= 8`.

These are paraphrases, not a transcription of the article. The first-page
scan makes the two inequality directions and the endpoint eight unambiguous.
The form has coefficient `-phi`, not `-2*phi`. The scope is the complete
integral reflection subgroup, not a selected finite list of its roots, an
unrelated reflection subgroup, or all quadratic forms over the same field. Passing to the time-component-preserving index-two automorphism
group does not remove any hyperplane reflection in question.

The article also explains there why boundedness and finite volume coincide in
this arithmetic setting. For the project's application it is already enough
that the displayed infinite-volume conclusion rules out a compact chamber.
No extrapolation from results in dimensions at most seven is being used.
