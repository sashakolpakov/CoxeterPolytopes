# Silver unit `N(A15 D9)` through height 50

## Outcome

The exact Vinberg search of

\[
  \langle-(1+\sqrt2)\rangle\oplus N(A_{15}D_9)
  \quad\text{in }\mathbf H^{24}
\]

does **not** yet produce a compact or finite-volume example.  The complete
primitive chronology through rational height `50` has 208 accepted
positive-height walls.  The cyclic index-eight Niemeier glue is already
substantial: 9 of 15 walls in the second nonempty packet and 140 of 191
walls in the third lie outside the root lattice.  Nevertheless, the exact
strict hyperideal ray displayed below survives every wall in this prefix.

This is a bounded statement.  A later wall may cut the witness, so it is not
a global nonreflectivity theorem.

## Why this coefficient

The coefficient `epsilon=1+sqrt(2)` represents the unique admissible unit
square-class: multiplying it by a totally positive unit square merely rescales
the time coordinate.  It is therefore the canonical determinant-one first
screen for this new spatial host.  Its unit ideal support also gives the
shortest complete mirror-norm list and exposes the Niemeier glue at the second
nonempty packet.  Prime-supported coefficient classes, such as the separate
prime-seven class, are not excluded by this calculation.

## Exact index-eight glue

The root lattice has discriminant

\[
  \det(A_{15}\oplus D_9)=16\cdot4=64.
\]

Use the `D9` diagram with chain nodes `1,...,7` and branch nodes `8,9`.
The Niemeier extension is generated by

\[
  g=\omega_2(A_{15})+\omega_9(D_9),\qquad
  g^2=\frac74+\frac94=4.
\]

Its discriminant class is `(2,1)` in
`(Z/16) + (Z/4)` and has order eight.  For Dynkin weights define

\[
 \rho_A=\sum_{i=1}^{15} i z_{A,i}\pmod {16},
\]

\[
 \rho_D=2(z_{D,1}+z_{D,3}+z_{D,5}+z_{D,7})
       +3z_{D,8}+z_{D,9}\pmod4.
\]

Membership is equivalent to

\[
  \rho_A\equiv0\pmod2,
  \qquad \rho_D\equiv\rho_A/2\pmod4,
\]

separately for the rational and `sqrt(2)` coefficients.  No root-lattice
proxy is used.

An explicit basis consisting of the first fourteen `A15` simple roots, all
nine `D9` simple roots, and `g` has integral even Gram matrix of determinant
one.  The minima in the seven nonzero glue cosets are

\[
  4,4,6,4,6,4,4.
\]

Consequently no glue coset adds norm-two roots.  The height-zero root system
is exactly `A15+D9`, with `240+144=384` roots, two dominant root orbits, and
24 simple walls.

## Complete mirror norms and controls

For a primitive mirror `r=(x,y)`, reflection integrality gives

\[
 q(r)\mid2I(r),\qquad I(r)=(\epsilon x,(y,N)).
\]

Even unimodularity makes the pairing ideal a unit away from the ramified
prime `(sqrt(2))`.  At that prime, primitivity and evenness leave valuations
zero or two.  Every totally positive unit is a square.  Thus the complete
canonical primitive norms are

\[
  1,\quad2.
\]

full index-eight glue lattice, eliminating imprimitive copies of norm-one

## Packet geometry and glue action

The first packet consists of the two factorwise affine highest-root caps.
Its section is the product of the affine `A15` and `D9` alcoves.  Its exact
ray census is

\[
  16\cdot10=160=2\text{ finite}+0\text{ ideal}+158\text{ hyperideal}.
\]

The second packet has 15 walls:

- 6 root-lattice walls;
- 1 pure order-two `A15` glue wall;
- 8 walls genuinely coupling `A15` and `D9`.

The third packet has 191 accepted walls: 51 root-lattice walls, 28
non-root-lattice walls with trivial `D9` projection, and 112 genuinely
cross-component glue walls.  Thus this screen does reach the discriminant
coupling that motivated the host; its failure is geometric, not an omitted
glue class.

The exact `A15` chain enumerator uses

\[
 z^TC_{A_d}^{-1}z=T-\frac{W^2}{d+1},
\]

with integer pairs for the two coefficients after multiplication by 16.
At the second norm-two packet it visits 10,259 states and reaches only 222
leaves.  At the third it combines 160 exact capped `D9` weights with 161
exact-energy `A15` weights; the full residue join gives 197 candidates.

## Strict hyperideal witness

Let `z=(1,w)` have zero `A15` Dynkin weights and only the fifth `D9` Dynkin
weight nonzero:

\[
  w_{D,5}=\frac7{10}.
\]

The relevant inverse-Cartan diagonal is
`(C_D9^-1)_(5,5)=5`, so

\[
 z^2=5\left(\frac7{10}\right)^2-(1+\sqrt2)
    =\frac{29}{20}-\sqrt2>0.
\]

All height-zero inequalities hold because `w` is dominant.  Exact evaluation
on all 208 positive-height walls is strict.  The closest pairing occurs in
the second packet and equals

\[
  \frac{1-\sqrt2}{5}<0.
\]

The largest third-packet pairing is still

\[
  \frac3{10}-\frac12\sqrt2<0.
\]

Hence the height-50 prefix has infinite volume and is not compact.
