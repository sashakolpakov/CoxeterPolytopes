# Silver unit `N(A17 E7)` through two nonempty packets

## Outcome

The exact search of

\[
  \langle-(1+\sqrt2)\rangle\oplus N(A_{17}E_7)
  \quad\text{in }\mathbf H^{24}
\]

does not produce a compact or finite-volume prefix.  The cyclic index-six
glue acts at the second nonempty Vinberg packet: six of its twelve walls lie
outside the root lattice, and four genuinely couple the `A17` and `E7`
factors.  Nevertheless the exact hyperideal ray displayed below is strict
against all fourteen positive-height walls.

This is a complete primitive chronological enumeration through rational
height 15, containing the first two nonempty packets.  Later walls may cut
the witness, so this is not a global nonreflectivity result.

## Exact index-six glue

The root lattice has discriminant

\[
  \det(A_{17}\oplus E_7)=18\cdot2=36.
\]

In the Dynkin numbering used by the backend, the Niemeier extension is
generated by

\[
  g=\omega_3(A_{17})+\omega_6(E_7),\qquad
  g^2=\frac52+\frac32=4.
\]

This class has order six.  Its discriminant subgroup is

\[
  \langle(3,1)\rangle\subset(\mathbf Z/18)\oplus(\mathbf Z/2).
\]

For Dynkin weights `z`, the residue functionals are

\[
  \rho_A(z)=\sum_{i=1}^{17} i z_i\pmod {18},\qquad
  \rho_E(z)=z_4+z_6+z_7\pmod2.
\]

The glue condition is imposed separately on the rational and `sqrt(2)`
coefficients:

\[
  \rho_A\equiv0\pmod3,qquad
  \rho_E\equiv\rho_A/3\pmod2.
\]

An explicit basis consisting of `alpha_1,...,alpha_16`, all seven E7 simple
roots, and `g` has integral even Gram matrix of determinant one.  The exact
height-zero dominant census contains two norm-two orbits and no glue root,
hence precisely the `A17+E7` root system with 432 roots.

## Complete mirror norms

Put `pi=sqrt(2)` and `epsilon=1+pi`.  For a primitive root `r=(x,y)`,
reflection integrality gives

\[
  q(r)\mid 2I(r),\qquad I(r)=(\epsilon x,(y,N)).
\]

The spatial block is even unimodular.  Away from `(pi)`, local primitivity
makes `I(r)` a unit.  At `(pi)`, a unit control gives norm valuation zero;
otherwise evenness supplies valuation at least two and divisibility bounds
it by two.  Every totally-positive unit of `Z[sqrt(2)]` is a square.
Therefore the complete canonical primitive mirror norms are

\[
  1,\quad2.
\]

For norm two, division by `sqrt(2)` is tested in the full index-six glue
lattice, so imprimitive copies of norm-one mirrors are removed.

The first packet consists of the two factorwise affine highest-root caps.

  18\cdot8=144=2\text{ finite}+0\text{ ideal}+142\text{ hyperideal}.

| A17 residue | E7 residue | walls | interpretation |

Together with the first packet, all 14 positive-height walls have the

## Rank-17 exact enumerator

A generic 17-level field-valued Schur recursion is unnecessarily expensive.
The backend instead uses the A-chain identity

\[
  z^TC_{A_d}^{-1}z=T-\frac{W^2}{d+1},
\]

for each prefix, where

\[
  W=\sum_{i=1}^{d} i z_i,
  \qquad
  T=\sum_{j=1}^{d}\left(\sum_{i=j}^{d}z_i\right)^2.
\]

This is exactly the Schur lower bound after eliminating the unfilled A17
coordinates.  Multiplying target energies by 18 makes the entire recursion
integer-pair arithmetic in `Z[sqrt(2)]`.  At the second norm-two control it
visits 11,992 states, prunes 22,824 branches by the affine cap and 8,622 by
the two-embedding energy bound, and checks only 205 leaves.  It returns 12
exact A17 weights, which join the 18 capped E7 weights to the twelve walls
above.

## Strict hyperideal witness

Let `z=(1,w)` have zero E7 Dynkin weights and A17 Dynkin weights

\[
  w=(0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,9/10,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0).
\]

The middle inverse-Cartan diagonal is

\[
  (C_{A_{17}}^{-1})_{9,9}=\frac92.
\]

Consequently

\[
  z^2=\frac92\left(\frac9{10}\right)^2-(1+\sqrt2)
     =\frac{529}{200}-\sqrt2>0.
\]

All height-zero Dynkin inequalities are nonnegative.  Exact evaluation on
the fourteen positive walls gives strictly negative values; the closest is

\[
  -\frac25-\frac3{10}\sqrt2<0.
\]

Thus the ray lies strictly in the positive-height halfspaces and is
hyperideal.  The complete two-packet prefix has infinite volume and is not a
compact Coxeter polytope.
