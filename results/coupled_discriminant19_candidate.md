# A Discriminant-19 K3 Carrier for the Rank-10 Search

Status: exact field-form, trace-lattice, primitive K3-embedding, explicit
elliptic-model, and bounded norm-two Vinberg certificates.  The distinguished
eigenspace has a positive vector, but the fixed (I_{19})-fibration nef gate
is not marked explicitly.  No claim of reflectivity, nonreflectivity, finite
covolume, or compactness is made.

## Field form

Let (F=\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)), let
(\phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2), and consider the rank-10
(\mathbb Z[\phi])-lattice

\[
H_{19}=
\begin{pmatrix}
-2\phi&\phi\\
\phi&2
\end{pmatrix}
\oplus E_8.
\]

The determinant of its coupled block, and hence of (H_{19}), is

\[
(-2\phi)2-\phi^2=-1-5\phi,
\qquad
N_{F/\mathbb Q}(-1-5\phi)=-19.
\]

At the identity embedding the coupled block has negative determinant and
signature ((1,1)).  At the conjugate embedding its first diagonal entry is
(-2\phi'=2(\phi-1)>0) and its determinant is
(5\phi-6>0), so it is positive definite.  Adding (E_8) gives exact
signatures

\[
(9,1)\quad\hbox{and}\quad(10,0).
\]

These are the required two-place signatures for an arithmetic hyperbolic
rank-10 carrier.  They do not imply that its reflection group has finite
covolume.

## Trace lattice

Use the totally negative codifferent element

\[
\alpha=-\frac{\phi}{\sqrt5}
=-\frac25-\frac15\phi.
\]

In the interleaved basis
((e_0,\phi e_0,e_1,\phi e_1)), the trace form of the coupled block is

\[
B=
\begin{pmatrix}
 2& 4&-1&-2\\
 4& 6&-2&-3\\
-1&-2&-2&-2\\
-2&-3&-2&-4
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The following four column vectors form a determinant-one matrix:

\[
u=(-1,1,-1,0),\quad
v=(-2,2,-2,1),\quad
w_1=(2,-2,3,-1),\quad
w_2=(7,-6,4,-1).
\]

Direct multiplication gives

\[
[u\ v\ w_1\ w_2]^{\mathsf T}B[u\ v\ w_1\ w_2]
=U\oplus\left(-\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&10\end{pmatrix}\right).
\]

For each (E_8) coordinate, replacing
((e_i,\phi e_i)) by ((e_i,\phi e_i-e_i)), and then grouping like
coordinates, identifies its trace factor with
(E_8(-1)\oplus E_8(-1)).  The complete rank-20 change of basis also has
determinant (+1).  Therefore

\[
N_{19}:=\operatorname{Tr}_{F/\mathbb Q}(\alpha H_{19})
\simeq
U\oplus T_{19}(-1)\oplus E_8(-1)^{\oplus2},
\qquad
T_{19}=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&10\end{pmatrix}.
\]

In particular, (N_{19}) is even, has signature ((1,19)), determinant
(-19), and discriminant group (\mathbb Z/19\mathbb Z).  Multiplication by
(\phi) is recorded as an integral self-adjoint operator satisfying
(M_\phi^2=M_\phi+I).

## Explicit primitive K3 embedding

Write the K3 lattice as

\[
\Lambda_{K3}=U_1\oplus U_2\oplus U_3
\oplus E_8(-1)^{\oplus2},
\]

with bases ((e_i,f_i)) for the hyperbolic planes.  Map the displayed
(U) identically to (U_1), map the two generators of (T_{19}(-1)) to

\[
z_1=e_2-f_2,
\qquad
z_2=-f_2+e_3-5f_3,
\]

and map both negative (E_8) summands identically.  Then
((z_i,z_j)=-T_{19}).  Its orthogonal complement is generated by

\[
q_1=e_2+f_2+f_3,
\qquad
q_2=e_3+5f_3,
\]

and has Gram matrix (T_{19}).  Appending (f_2,f_3) to the 20 embedding
columns produces a (22\times22) matrix of determinant (-1).  This proves
primitivity directly, rather than only through a discriminant-form criterion.

## Identification with Shioda's (I_{19}) surface

Shioda proves that the complex elliptic K3 surface with a section and an
(I_{19}) fiber is unique.  He also records

\[
T(S_{19})=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&10\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\operatorname{NS}(S_{19})\simeq U\oplus A_{18}(-1).
\]

Thus the transcendental lattice is exactly the complement in the preceding
primitive embedding, and the trace lattice gives a marked Néron--Severi
lattice of this singular K3 surface.  This is not merely a match by numerical
discriminant.

An explicit model over (\mathbb Q), in Shioda's notation, is

\[
y^2=x^3-3f(t)x-2g(t),
\]

where

\[
\begin{aligned}
f={}&t^8+6t^7+21t^6+50t^5+86t^4+114t^3+109t^2+74t+28,\\
2g={}&2t^{12}+18t^{11}+90t^{10}+312t^9+816t^8+1692t^7
       +2832t^6\\
&+3864t^5+4272t^4+3746t^3+2517t^2+1167t+299.
\end{aligned}
\]

The repository verifies the exact Davenport--Stothers identity

\[
f^3-g^2=-\frac{27}{4}
(4t^5+15t^4+38t^3+61t^2+62t+59).
\]

For the standard Weierstrass discriminant convention this gives

\[
\Delta=-11664(4t^5+15t^4+38t^3+61t^2+62t+59).
\]

After the local change (s=1/t), the orders at (s=0) are

\[
\operatorname{ord}_0(c_4)=0,
\qquad
\operatorname{ord}_0(\Delta)=19,
\]

so the fiber at infinity is (I_{19}).  The quintic is squarefree and
coprime to (c_4), giving five (I_1) fibers.  Their Euler numbers sum to
(19+5=24), and Shioda--Tate with the published Picard number 20 gives
Mordell--Weil rank zero.

Primary source: T. Shioda, *The elliptic K3 surfaces with a maximal singular
fibre*, C. R. Math. 337 (2003), 461--466,
[doi:10.1016/j.crma.2003.07.007](https://www.numdam.org/articles/10.1016/j.crma.2003.07.007/).

## Integral control vector and height-zero roots

There is a particularly useful integral timelike vector

\[
p=(2,-\phi,0,\ldots,0),\qquad D=1+5\phi.
\]

For $r=(x,y,z)\in H_{19}$, put

\[
t=\phi x+2y,\qquad w=E_8z.
\]

Exact multiplication in $\mathbb Z[\phi]$ gives

\[
(p,p)=-2D,\qquad (p,r)=-Dx,
\]

and diagonalizes the norm equation as

\[
2(r,r)=t^2-Dx^2+2w^{\mathsf T}E_8^{-1}w.
\]

Thus norm-two roots of positive height and fixed $x$ are precisely the
integral solutions

\[
t^2+2w^{\mathsf T}E_8^{-1}w=4+Dx^2,
\quad t\equiv\phi x\pmod {2\mathbb Z[\phi]},
\quad t,w_i\geq0,
\]

after imposing the preceding-wall inequalities.  Their Vinberg height is

\[
\delta(r)=\frac{Dx^2}{4}.
\]

At the conjugate embedding the positive-definite equation implies the exact
bound

\[
(x')^2\leq\frac4{5\phi-6}
=\frac{4(1+5\phi)}{19}<2.
\]

The height-zero equation is

\[
2y^2+E_8(z)=2.
\]

Here $y^2$ and $E_8(z)/2$ are totally nonnegative algebraic integers
summing to one.  If both were nonzero, their two real values would lie
strictly between zero and one, contradicting integrality of their nonzero
algebraic norms.  Hence either $y=\pm1,z=0$, or $y=0$ and
$E_8(z)=2$.  Writing $z=a+b\phi$, coefficient comparison gives

\[
E_8(a)+E_8(b)=2,
\qquad 2(a,b)_{E_8}+E_8(b)=0,
\]

so positivity and evenness force $b=0$ and $a$ to be one of the 240
ordinary $E_8$ roots.  The complete height-zero system is therefore
$A_1\oplus E_8$, with 242 roots and nine simple roots.

## Exact bounded Vinberg prefix

The conjugate bound makes the scan finite at every identity bound.  Through
$0<x\leq5$, the exhaustive control coordinates and numbers of newly
accepted simple roots are

| $x$ | $1$ | $\phi$ | $1+\phi$ | $2\phi$ | $2+\phi$ | $1+2\phi$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| new roots | 2 | 1 | 2 | 1 | 0 | 7 |

Together with the nine height-zero simple roots this is an exact 22-wall
prefix.  The vector

\[
c=p+\frac1{10}(0,1,E_8^{-1}{\bf1})
\]

has square $211/50-10\phi<0$ and pairs strictly negatively with all 22
walls, certifying that their oriented cone has nonempty hyperbolic interior.

The following nine wall indices determine an exact extreme ray of that cone:

\[
(0,1,2,3,4,5,8,9,13).
\]

In the standard $H_{19}$ basis its canonical representative is

\[
\begin{aligned}
v={}&(1,-\tfrac12\phi,-3+4\phi,-6+8\phi,-9+12\phi,
       -\tfrac{15}{2}+10\phi,\\
    &\hspace{2.7em}-6+8\phi,-\tfrac92+6\phi,
       2+\tfrac12\phi,-\tfrac92+6\phi).
\end{aligned}
\]

It satisfies every prefix inequality $(v,r_i)\leq0$, with equality exactly
at those nine independent walls, and

\[
(v,v)=\frac{115-71\phi}{2}>0.
\]

Consequently this **bounded prefix** is neither compact nor finite-volume,
so Vinberg's stopping criterion does not apply at $x\leq5$.  This is a
scoped obstruction only: later roots can cut the spacelike ray, so it proves
neither nonreflectivity nor failure of eventual finite volume.

The full enumeration counters and roots are archived in
`data/candidates/h19_vinberg_prefix.json`; the ray is recomputed independently
by `scripts/diagnose_h19_vinberg_prefix.py`.

## Positive eigenspace versus the fixed nef marking

At the identity embedding, multiplying the field square
$(p,p)=-2-10\phi$ by the negative trace scalar gives

\[
\alpha(p,p)=\frac{14+32\phi}{5}>0.
\]

Thus the distinguished real eigenspace has a nonempty positive cone.  The
corresponding integral restriction-of-scalars vector has total trace square
$12$; this latter number includes both embeddings and is recorded only as
an arithmetic consistency check.  The control vector itself is orthogonal to
the complete height-zero $A_1+E_8$ system, so it is not an ample witness.

The primitive K3 embedding and the equality of transcendental lattices
identify the abstract singular K3 surface, but they do not provide an
oriented integral isometry from the repository's trace basis and
$\mathbb Z[\phi]$-action to Shioda's fiber, section, and 18 effective fiber
components.  Without that effective marking, the intersection of the fixed
identity eigenspace with the $I_{19}$-fibration nef chamber cannot be tested
honestly.  A Weyl-conjugate marking can place a generic positive eigenvector
in an ample chamber existentially, but no explicit Weyl word or fixed-fibration
claim is recorded here.

## What remains open

This construction supplies a rigid algebraic-geometric carrier for the
dimension-9 search, together with a distinguished elliptic fibration and a
self-adjoint $\mathbb Z[\phi]$-action.  It does **not** yet show:

1. whether the fixed $I_{19}$-fibration marking has an eigen-nef point;
2. whether the exact norm-two search eventually terminates;
3. whether its final chamber is finite-volume or compact; or
4. whether the abstract field lattice is reflective or nonreflective.

The correct status is therefore
`singular_k3_carrier_and_eigen_positive_cone_constructed_fixed_eigen_nef_and_reflectivity_unresolved`.
