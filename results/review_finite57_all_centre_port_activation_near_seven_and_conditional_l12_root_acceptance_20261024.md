## Root checks of the activation argument

At a fixed simple heavy vertex different centres belong to different
WHOLE D components. Each operation's proved actual receiving edge
releases an old root in that component. Two such ports cannot have
the same receiving endpoint: the segment or ray from the finite
vertex is unique, and two different edges have different tangent
rays. Thus activating more source ports does not multiply the
already complete incoming actual-edge counts at a fixed recipient.
This is an actual-edge argument, not an assumption about private
neighbours or about abstract diagram completions.

Every intrinsic source refund is a literal held pair(h,yi) inside
that same component. Different component centres give different pairs.
The old H/J/X/E/BF/PZ/M/K/Q/L assignments are ledger moves, not
unrecorded deletions of these pairs. Existing local operations retain
their port tags and are not repeated. Zero-FAR7/J is tagged only when
the actual receiving edge is the same; PZ remains its separately
funded older load and is not retagged as an ideal-centre token.

## Root checks of the aggregate argument

Put t=Qn+u and g=v-b_F, including the newly covered positive NEAR7
ports. The graph itself has not changed. Then

    t+g=H+c+1-b_F.

Let t0 be the tokens already consumed by K/Q/L1/L, keeping their
original heavy-source debits inside this FULL component. They are
different resources from the g activated/tagged local ports. Ignoring
other favourable old savings, the total heavy charge is at most

    372H-8g-8t0.

When b_F<=c+1, choose s=max(0,H-g-t0). There are at least s unused
tokens, since t+g>=H and therefore t-t0>=H-g-t0 whenever needed.
Issue their old-amount8 credits once, retaining all receiving bounds.
The resulting heavy total is at most

    372H-8(g+t0+s)<=364H.

Direct signed zero-sum bookkeeping among the H heavy vertices then
sets their individual charges to the common average<=364. Every
external credit remains present. Superseded residual-T partial debits
are removed only WITH their matched same-face recipient credits;
this decreases rather than increases those old receiver loads.
Other components retain their original residual union.

Full components have disjoint heavy vertices and token resources.
Their external recipients may coincide, but all caps above count the
full global actual-port union independently of which components are
selected. This proves simultaneous conditional financing.
