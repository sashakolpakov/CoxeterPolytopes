## Necessary forests in dimension 23

Use the SAME actual farthest vector, with norm squared `<1`. A dominant
component means A rank at least7 or B/D rank at least9. Ordinary rates
and the single-defect accounting force a dominant component; two would
already cost more than one. All its spectators therefore have the
accepted small-component rates, except at most one isolated short A1
of type I, or instead one isolated rank-two7 defect of energy `>9/100`.

Here is the derivation. The A half bound gives spectator rank at most7,
so `r>=16`. A17/A18 are excluded by their `>5/7` bound and spectator
rank6/5 even after either permitted debit. A19 with four ordinary ranks
is excluded; so is A20 with three ordinary ranks using `>11/14`.
The transferred A cap excludes rank21 and above.

For B/D, the `>9/14` bound leaves spectator rank at most5, hence
`r>=18`. Prefix bounds remove ranks18–20 after the single debit.
At rank21, the actual floors are B21 `>.9401975`, D21 `>.9240775`.
Two ordinary spectator ranks or a rank-two defect already contradict
the norm. If one spectator is I, the other is an ordinary A1, whose

Thus the coarser `rank/14` rounding must not leave a spurious D21 case.
Rank22 has only one A1 spectator, which must be I; B23 is capped.

Type J does not survive any listed A alternative: its two A1 ranks

## What would and would not be a material next step

Extending the `>5/7` theorem to lower A ranks alone does not remove
A19/A20 with their permitted debit, and cannot affect whole D23.
Strengthening rank-four ordinary rates only removes some spectator
patterns; it cannot touch B22+I, D22+I or whole D23. These are poor
standalone routes to a substantial new ceiling.

One concrete uniform subproblem is an **actual centered-prefix theorem
for A_r**, analogous to the accepted B/D prefix method. Put `N=r+1`,
`S_i=sum_(j<=i) x_j` in sum-zero A coordinates, and set

`f_i=sqrt(i(N-i)/N)-61/(100 sqrt(2))`, for `5<=i<=N-5`.

The proposed target is to derive the concave-reference energy from
actual walls for every relevant `r>=13`, allowing only explicitly
controlled isolated omissions with compensating actual core pressure.
No displayed prefix floor is presently proved by this note. The new
geometric work is the complete interior-node own/foreign inventory,
positive feasible-point pressures, and the weak-endpoint alternatives;
an edge-only Gram relaxation would not suffice.

If all these floors were strong, their positive-dual reference gives

`e_A >= 2*f_5^2/5 + sum_(i=5)^(N-6) (f_(i+1)-f_i)^2`.

An isolated interior omission loses exactly half the square of the
local second difference. Endpoint omissions need their own actual-foot
compensation; they cannot be discarded by this formula. This makes the
subproblem precise without assuming the desired ambient bound.

The scale is promising, rather than merely a change of rank cutoff.
Already the two five-coordinate end blocks would give A19
`e_A>.9036036` and A20 `e_A>.9217296`: use
`.61/sqrt(2)<54/125`, `sqrt(15/4)>387/200`, and
`sqrt(80/21)>39/20`. These are conditional scalar consequences of the
unproved floors. They would remove A19 plus I plus an ordinary A1,
and A20 plus I plus an ordinary A1, respectively. The full centered
profile supplies further energy, potentially usable with the existing
isolated-wall height coupling rather than another per-rank A paper.

This is an A-side route toward a dimension20 programme, not such a
programme's completion. In particular **whole D23 remains a critical
independent obstruction**. Its arbitrary-spectator rank cap does not
exclude the spectator-free case. Any later uniform classical-tail
argument must handle that case explicitly, and should use its actual
wall/endpoint restrictions rather than recycle the permissive component
relaxation. No new uniform ceiling follows from this triage.
