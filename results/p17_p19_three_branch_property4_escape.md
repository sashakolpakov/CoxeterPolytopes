# The classical `P17`--`P19` packets escape all three current AG transfer branches

**Status (2026-08-11): proved, exact scoped obstruction.** For the pinned
standard integral realizations of Vinberg's `P17` and the
Vinberg--Kaplinskaja `P18`, `P19` chambers, the three carrier branches in the
current canonical-chamber-rigidity transfer theorem are all empty. This is
not a no-surface theorem. It proves that completeness relative to the
declared integral reflection host does not make the present three-way
compiler exhaustive; another carrier species or a strictly broader
geometric return is necessary.

## 1. The fixed inputs

For \(n=17,18,19\), let \(P_n\) be the classical finite-volume chamber in

\[
                         L_n=I_{1,n}.                       \tag{1.1}
\]

The displayed primitive wall vectors span \(L_n\), every wall has square
\(-1\) or \(-2\), and the chamber is the complete reflection chamber of its
declared host. The exact packets have respectively \(22,37,50\) facets and
\(2,15,5\) primitive square-\((-1)\) facets.

The theorem concerns these **pinned integral realizations**, including their
primitive wall modules. Complete reflection return is relative to the
declared host \(I_{1,n}\). We do not assert absolute maximality among every
commensurable or arithmetic reflection overgroup, and we do not identify all
integral realizations of the underlying unlabelled Coxeter diagrams.

## 2. Direct rational return is empty

The marking-independent facet-contraction MMP has already exhausted every
canonical marking of a hypothetical smooth projective rational full-nef
carrier. The numbers of markings are

\[
                         8,\quad 24,\quad 120.             \tag{2.1}
\]

In every marking, sequential contraction recovers the same free cluster on
\(\mathbf P^2\). The genus-one cubic facet and the required quartic then give

\[
        A|_C=11p+r,\qquad
        \mathcal O_C(A)=\mathcal O_C(12p),\qquad r\ne p.  \tag{2.2}
\]

Injectivity of the degree-one Abel map on the smooth locus of an integral
plane cubic forces \(r=p\), a contradiction. Therefore

\[
 P_n\text{ has no direct complex rational full-nef carrier},
 \qquad n=17,18,19.                                      \tag{2.3}
\]

This is the frozen rational \(P_n\) MMP/Abel theorem, not a new inference
from the Coxeter classification.

## 3. The certified weak-log branch is also empty

The weak-log branch might try to replace a primitive square-\((-1)\) facet
curve by a reduced rational boundary curve whose class is twice the primitive
facet vector. We avoid any MMP assumption here: every possible physical
colouring is tested directly against adjunction and the exact wall relations.

### Lemma 3.1 (complete physical alphabet on a facet ray)

Let \(S\) be a smooth projective rational surface with the exact full-nef
chamber \(P_n\). A negative irreducible curve exposes a facet of the nef cone.
Indeed, if \(B^2<0\) and \(A\) is ample, then

\[
        H=A-\frac{A\mathbin{\cdot}B}{B^2}B               \tag{3.1}
\]

satisfies \(H\mathbin{\cdot}B=0\), while it is positive on every distinct
irreducible curve because intersections of distinct irreducible curves are
nonnegative.

Let \(r\) be the primitive integral generator of a displayed negative facet
ray. In the certified weak-log alphabet, exactly the following cases can
occur:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
r^2 & \text{physical curve on the ray} & K_S\mathbin{\cdot}r\\ \hline
-1 & r\text{ is a rational }(-1)\text{-curve} & -1\\
-1 & B=2r\text{ is a rational boundary }(-4)\text{-curve} & +1\\
-2 & r\text{ is a rational }(-2)\text{-curve} & 0.
\end{array}                                               \tag{3.2}
\]

The values follow from adjunction. Primitivity makes the list exhaustive:
an integral class on the ray is \(qr\) with \(q\in\mathbf Z_{>0}\), and
\((qr)^2=-4\) has the sole boundary solution \(r^2=-1,q=2\). In particular,
there is no boundary scaling on a primitive square-\((-2)\) ray.

### Lemma 3.2 (the adjunction system has no solution)

The facets span \(L_n\), so a proposed canonical class defines one linear
functional \(K\mathbin{\cdot}(-)\). By (3.2), each primitive norm-one facet
independently receives the target \(-1\) or \(+1\), while every norm-two
facet receives \(0\). Thus the complete numbers of possible physical
colourings are

\[
             2^2=4,\qquad 2^{15}=32768,\qquad 2^5=32.    \tag{3.3}
\]

For each colouring, the certificate solves for \(K\) from the first
\(\rho=n+1\) independent facet equations over \(\mathbf Q\), then tests every
remaining facet relation exactly. No colouring survives: all four `P17`
assignments first fail at facet 19, all 32768 `P18` assignments first fail at
facet 20, and all 32 `P19` assignments first fail at facet 21. Hence

\[
 \boxed{
 P_n\text{ has no certified complex weak-log rational carrier},
 \quad n=17,18,19.}                                      \tag{3.4}
\]

This obstruction occurs at the canonical-adjunction linear system, before
MMP, nefness, semiampleness, or section-algebra gates. It therefore does not
reuse the direct rational carrier census circularly.

## 4. The exact-return minimalizable trace branch reduces to K3 and fails

An exact full-nef return has
\(\rho(S)=n+1\in\{18,19,20\}\), equal to the rank of the trace block.  A
nontrivial contraction would lower the Picard rank and therefore could not
contain the descended full-rank block.  Thus the finite-orbit MMP alternative
is already minimal.  Canonical orthogonality of a full-rank block forces
\(K_S\equiv0\).  The classification of minimal complex surfaces of Kodaira
dimension zero now leaves only K3: an Enriques surface has numerical Picard
rank ten, while abelian and bielliptic surfaces have Picard rank at most four.
Consequently it is enough—and necessary—to test the literal K3 normalization
below.

A complex K3 Picard lattice is even. The declared host \(I_{1,n}\) is odd
and contains both primitive norm classes \(1\) and \(2\), so it cannot itself
be the full Picard lattice with the displayed form.

Even one common rational similarity does not repair the physical facet
condition. Pull a putative K3 intersection form back to the raw rational wall
module and write it as \(c(\ ,\ )\). If a norm-one wall line and a norm-two
wall line contained rational representatives \(\lambda_1r_1\) and
\(\lambda_2r_2\), both of K3 square \(-2\), then

\[
       c\lambda_1^2=2,\qquad c\lambda_2^2=1,\qquad
       \left(\frac{\lambda_1}{\lambda_2}\right)^2=2.     \tag{4.1}
\]

This is impossible over \(\mathbf Q\). Hence no common rational trace
normalization can put actual square-\((-2)\) K3 curves on every displayed
facet line. The literal K3 branch is empty already at its `PHYS` gate, before
primitive embedding, periods, or hidden-root tests.

This uses the same-module/same-line physical-root contract. It does not
exclude a projected-wall construction in a larger Picard lattice, which is
a different and strictly weaker carrier notion.

## 5. Branch-exhaustion theorem

### Theorem 5.1

For each pinned standard-host chamber \(P_n\), \(n=17,18,19\), the following
three carrier spaces are empty:

1. direct smooth complex rational full-nef carriers;
2. certified smooth complex weak-log rational full-nef carriers;
3. exact-return minimalizable full-trace carriers, which at these ranks reduce
   to complex K3 carriers with rational same-line physical square-\((-2)\)
   facet roots.

Therefore declared-host reflection completeness does not imply nonemptiness
of the three current carrier branches:

\[
 \boxed{
  \text{finite-volume complete chamber in the pinned host}
  \not\Longrightarrow
  \text{nonemptiness of the current three carrier branches}.} \tag{5.1}
\]

The missing outer Property-4 theorem cannot be obtained merely by improving
the downstream dimension, field, host, or chamber bounds. At least one of the
following must happen upstream:

- add a genuinely new surface or variety carrier species;
- prove a broader projected or invariant-cone return with its own boundedness
  theorem; or
- weaken the four-property contract, forfeiting the present direct AG
  implication.

Absolute reflection maximality is a separate input. Thus this theorem is a
branch-exhaustion obstruction for the pinned hosts, not by itself a strict
counterexample to a statement quantified only over absolutely maximal
arithmetic reflection groups.

## 6. Exact scope

The theorem does **not** claim:

- nonexistence of the classical \(P_{17},P_{18},P_{19}\) chambers;
- absolute or maximal-arithmetic reflection maximality of the pinned hosts;
- nonexistence of every carrier on a nonrational surface or a
  higher-dimensional variety;
- a positive-characteristic obstruction;
- a no-go for an unlabelled diagram with a different arithmetic realization;
- or failure after replacing same-vector physical walls by uncontrolled
  projections from a larger lattice.

It proves a precise negative statement: on three explicit declared-host
packets, every output species of the present three-way compiler is empty.
