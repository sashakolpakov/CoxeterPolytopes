and the new face masses, and checked against the full-model certificate.

is compared with the sum of all its actual vertex-budget coefficients.
All14 two-owner columns retain both costs. No column is assigned to one
owner or reinterpreted as a zero-cost transfer.

The illustrative repair holds alpha fixed and only increases nonnegative
vertex allowances. It therefore preserves every already certified column
inequality, including signed face columns with positive costs and every

it is neither a new optimized value nor target12 primal feasibility.
