# The corrected genus-one facet is rank-blind; the exact `P_n` family stops at `n=16` only by global incidence geometry

**Status (2026-08-11): proved two-scope theorem.**  This note extends the
anticanonical-facet analysis to the full-nef rational carriers `P12` through
`P16` and the marking-independent no-go chambers `P17` through `P19`.

The result has two deliberately separate parts.

1. The local Neron--Severi/canonical packet of the unique genus-one facet is
   **rank-blind**.  Reflection integrality and adjunction do not give a
   dimension bound once that facet is not literally `-K`.
2. For the exact marked `P_n` facet systems with `12<=n<=19`, a global
   algebro-geometric theorem gives a complex rational full-nef carrier if
   and only if `n<=16`.  The bound comes from the oriented contraction and
   proximity design plus a `Pic^1` Abel obstruction, not from bare
   reflection maximality.

## 1. The same corrected facet in every rank

On `I_(1,n)` use the standard rational marking

\[
 K=-3H+E_1+\cdots+E_n.                                    \tag{1.1}
\]

For every `n>=12`, put

\[
 C=3H-E_1-\cdots-E_{11}.                                  \tag{1.2}
\]

This is the unique arithmetic-genus-one facet in each of the displayed
`P12`--`P19` wall packets.  It satisfies, independently of `n`,

\[
 C^2=-2,\qquad K.C=2,\qquad
 p_a(C)=1+\frac{C^2+K.C}{2}=1.                             \tag{1.3}
\]

Because `C` is primitive of square `-2`, reflection in `C^perp` is integral
for every `n`.

The correction from the anticanonical class is

\[
 C=-K+Z_n,
 \qquad
 Z_n=E_{12}+\cdots+E_n.                                   \tag{1.4}
\]

Directly,

\[
 C.Z_n=0,qquad Z_n^2=11-n.                               \tag{1.5}
\]

Thus all rank dependence has moved into the negative correction `Z_n`; the
mirror norm, canonical degree, genus, and reflection integrality of `C`
remain fixed.

### Proposition 1.1 (local rank blindness)

No dimension bound can follow from only

\[
             C^2=-2,\quad K.C=2,\quad p_a(C)=1,
             \quad s_C\in O(\operatorname{NS}).             \tag{1.6}
\]

Indeed (1.1)--(1.2) give those identities on `I_(1,n)` for every `n>=12`.
The `P12`--`P16` surfaces are five literal full-nef controls in which `C` is
an actual irreducible smooth elliptic facet, so the correction is not merely
a formal lattice trick.

This pinpoints why the anticanonical theorem does not extend.  When `C=-K`,
reflection integrality quantizes `K^2`.  When `C=-K+Z`, it quantizes only
`C^2`; the unrestricted negative class `Z` absorbs `K^2=9-n`.

## 2. The positive `P12`--`P16` carriers

Start with eleven successive free points along a smooth flex cubic.  The
strict cubic has class (1.2) and square `-2`.  Subsequent centres leave the
cubic, so its class and square no longer change.

The complete visible curves are built in three stages.

- For `n=12,13`, use the exceptional free chain, the flex line, and `C`.
- For `n=14,15`, add an irreducible nodal quartic `Q` with the certified
  contact-eleven branch.
- For `n=16`, add the irreducible sextic `S` following the prescribed branch
  through the sixteenth centre.

Exact proximity and Bezout inequalities put every possible irreducible
plane curve class in a finite rational cone.  Every extreme ray of that cone
has an exact nonnegative decomposition in the visible curves.  Hence these
are equalities of complete Mori cones, not searches for short curves:

\[
                  \overline{\operatorname{NE}}(X_n)
                  =\operatorname{Cone}(\text{displayed facets}),
                  \qquad12\le n\le16.                       \tag{2.1}
\]

Dualizing gives literal full-nef returns

\[
                  \mathbf P(\operatorname{Nef}X_n)=P_n.     \tag{2.2}
\]

The exact positive data are

| `n` | facets | genus-one class | status |
|---:|---:|---|---|
| 12 | 14 | `C=-K+E12` | full-nef carrier |
| 13 | 15 | `C=-K+E12+E13` | full-nef carrier |
| 14 | 17 | `C=-K+E12+E13+E14` | full-nef carrier |
| 15 | 18 | `C=-K+E12+...+E15` | full-nef carrier |
| 16 | 20 | `C=-K+E12+...+E16` | full-nef carrier |

Each chamber is finite-volume and reflection-saturated in its declared
class, but those global Coxeter facts do not change (1.3).  They are not the
source of the stopping bound below.

## 3. Why the exact `P_n` facet systems stop at `n=16`

Beginning at `P17`, the full wall packets require the further quartic
classes

\[
 A_n=4H-E_1-\cdots-E_{a_n},\qquad
 a_n=\min(n,18).                                           \tag{3.1}
\]

Thus `A_17` follows seventeen centres, whereas `A_18` and `A_19` follow
eighteen (the `P19` class has zero `E19` coefficient).

Suppose a complex smooth rational surface had the complete `P17`, `P18`, or
`P19` Mori/nef facet system.  The physical rational chain facets admit
successive Castelnuovo contraction.  Exact facet-contraction MMP
enumeration shows that every possible marking is conjugate by a weighted
diagram isometry to the standard plane blowup marking: there are respectively
`8`, `24`, and `120` canonical markings, exactly the full diagram orbits.

The contraction order therefore reconstructs the same free cluster.  The
image of `C` is an integral plane cubic, and the unit multiplicity at the
first centre makes it smooth at `p`.  The flex line gives

\[
                         \mathcal O_C(1)=\mathcal O_C(3p).   \tag{3.2}
\]

The `Q` facet forces the chain to leave `C` after the eleventh centre.  A
putative curve in class (3.1), following that required chain, would restrict
as

\[
                              A|_C=11p+r,                    \tag{3.3}
\]

where `r` is a degree-one point different from `p`.  But its degree also
gives

\[
             \mathcal O_C(A)=\mathcal O_C(4)
                            =\mathcal O_C(12p),               \tag{3.4}
\]

so `O_C(r)=O_C(p)`.  Injectivity of the degree-one Abel map on the smooth
locus of an integral plane cubic forces `r=p`, contradicting the prescribed
departure from `C`.  This includes the nodal and cuspidal cases; global
smoothness of the putative cubic is not assumed.

The literal quartic class changes between `P17` and `P18`, but the same
degree-four restriction obstruction persists in all three packets.
Consequently:

### Theorem 3.1 (scoped exact `P_n` carrier boundary)

Within the exact marked facet systems `P_n`, `12<=n<=19`,

\[
 \boxed{
 P_n\text{ has a complex smooth rational full-nef carrier}
 \iff n\le16.}                                             \tag{3.5}
\]

The positive implication is (2.1)--(2.2).  The negative implication is the
marking-independent contraction/Abel argument.  Both are already proved in
[`rational_i1n_inverse_design.md`](rational_i1n_inverse_design.md); the new
certificate extracts and checks the common genus-one correction and the
logical source of the bound, and pins the dedicated `P17` and `P18/P19`
surface-MMP archives.

## 4. The geometric property encoded beyond the lattice

The decisive object is not another canonical vector.  It is the ordered
geometric contraction stack carried by the facets:

\[
 P
 \longrightarrow
 \text{Castelnuovo contraction order}
 \longrightarrow
 \text{proximity cluster}
 \longrightarrow
 \text{restriction data in }\operatorname{Pic}(C).          \tag{4.1}
\]

Numerically, (1.4) merely says that `C+K` is a negative correction.
Geometrically, the facet chain says which exceptional classes comprise that
correction, in what order they contract, and where the associated centres
must lie.  The line, cubic, quartic, and new-wall incidences then impose
global line-bundle identities on `C`.  Those identities are either realized
by a nonempty cluster/Hilbert branch (`n<=16`) or fail by the Abel map
(`n>=17` in this family).

This is precisely the nontrivial property that a polytope-to-surface functor
must return:

> **P-native contraction/incidence compatibility:** the oriented wall data
> must compile to a simultaneous contraction/proximity design whose required
> curve sections exist and whose restrictions in `Pic` of every positive-
> genus facet are compatible.

Bare Neron--Severi intersection data records the proposed degrees.  It does
not prove that the associated restriction divisors or simultaneous plane
curves exist.

## 5. Scope

The two conclusions must not be conflated.

- Proposition 1.1 is an infinite rank-blindness statement about the local
  canonical/genus-one packet.
- Theorem 3.1 is only about the exact finite family of marked `P12`--`P19`
  facet systems.

This note does not prove a universal `n<=16` theorem for every
reflection-maximal chamber with a genus-one facet.  Nor does it classify
abstract `P_n` chambers, deny existence of `P17`--`P19` as hyperbolic
polytopes, or give a positive-characteristic no-go.  The arbitrary
unique-genus-one maximal-chamber problem remains open.
