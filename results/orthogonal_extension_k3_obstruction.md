# No Orthogonal Rank-One Extension Passes the K3 Trace Gate

This note strengthens the obstruction for
\(L_9^{(0)}=L_8\oplus A_1\) to every orthogonal rank-one extension of the
recorded Bugaenko form.

## Setup

Let \(F=\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\), \(\mathcal O_F=\mathbb Z[\phi]\), and

\[
\alpha=-\phi/\sqrt5.
\]

The audited Bugaenko trace lattice is

\[
N_8\simeq\langle2\rangle\oplus\langle-2\rangle
\oplus E_8(-1)^{\oplus2}.
\]

Its 2-primary discriminant group is \((\mathbb Z/2)^2\).

Consider any nondegenerate orthogonal extension

\[
L(c)=L_8\oplus\langle c\rangle,
\qquad c\in F,
\]

for which the trace pairing is integral and even.  Put
\(\beta=\alpha c=A+B\phi\), and define

\[
t=\operatorname{Tr}(\beta),
\qquad s=\operatorname{Tr}(\beta\phi).
\]

Because \(\phi^2=\phi+1\), the rank-two trace block in the basis
\(e,\phi e\) is

\[
T_c=
\begin{pmatrix}
t&s\\
s&t+s
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

## Lemma

If \(T_c\) is even, then every entry of \(T_c\) is divisible by 2.

Indeed, evenness gives \(t\equiv0\pmod2\) and
\(t+s\equiv0\pmod2\), hence \(s\equiv0\pmod2\).

If \(T_c\) is nondegenerate, its 2-primary discriminant group therefore needs
at least two generators: reduction of the cokernel modulo 2 has quotient
\((\mathbb Z/2)^2\).  Since the trace lattice is the orthogonal sum
\(N_8\oplus T_c\), its 2-primary discriminant group needs at least four
generators.

## K3 obstruction

A rank-20 primitive sublattice of the unimodular rank-22 K3 lattice has a
rank-2 orthogonal complement with the same discriminant group.  The
discriminant group of a rank-2 integral lattice is generated by at most two
elements.  It cannot match a 2-primary group of generator length at least 4.

Therefore no even trace lattice arising from an orthogonal rank-one extension
\(L_8\oplus\langle c\rangle\) embeds primitively as a Picard-rank-20 K3
lattice.

The result is scoped to the fixed trace scalar and an orthogonal direct-sum
extension.  It points to the next viable constructions: nonorthogonal
rank-10 forms, integral neighbours, or overlattices whose gluing reduces the
2-primary discriminant length.
