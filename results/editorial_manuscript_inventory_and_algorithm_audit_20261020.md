## Minimal organization by technique

Keep the six papers. Give a short common orientation: actual wall/
face semantics, a local cost, an exhaustive reduction, then a global
budget. Declare the technique boundary immediately:57 and arithmetic20
use angle payments and curvature; compact23 uses one actual vector's
orthogonal energy; RA sharp uses descent/codes; volume papers use
facet cuts and equality. Shared terminology does not justify merging
all six into a sprawling manuscript or making a seventh framework paper.

Within each paper, show the global contradiction early, then an explicit
exhaustive case split. Mark where geometry ends and a finite algorithm
begins. Give each algorithm its domain, halting proof, soundness and
no-false-negative/completeness proof, followed by the actual recorded
result. Group lengthy local proofs by mathematical family in appendices;
put hashes, commands and historical atlases in the versioned supplement.
For compact23, the current companion-input boundary is honest, but
ordinary/Q/cap proofs still need publication integration or a clearly
defined mathematical supplement. A hash list cannot replace them.
