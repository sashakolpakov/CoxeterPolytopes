# Rational-surface inverse design for the odd-unimodular Vinberg chambers

## Result

There is a genuine positive algebro-geometric result in dimensions above
nine.

> **Theorem.** For every $12\le n\le16$, there is an explicit smooth
> projective rational surface $X_n$, obtained by blowing up a free
> infinitely-near chain over a plane cubic flex, such that
> 
> \[
> \overline{\operatorname{NE}}(X_n)
> \]
> 
> is generated exactly by the irreducible curves listed below.  Every
> generator has square $-1$ or $-2$, and the projectivized nef cone is
> exactly Vinberg's finite-volume, noncompact odd-unimodular chamber
> $P_n\subset\mathbb H^n$.

Thus $P_{12},P_{13},P_{14},P_{15},P_{16}$ have complete
divisor-lattice realizations as rational-surface nef chambers.  The
**abstract polytopes are classical**, so these are not new hyperbolic
Coxeter polytopes.  Worldwide priority for these particular complete
rational-surface realizations has not been established.

There are now also marking-independent stopping theorems at
$n=17,18,19$:

> **Rational-carrier no-go.** No smooth projective rational surface has
> complete Mori cone generated by the facet classes of $P_{17}$,
> $P_{18}$, or $P_{19}$.

An exact surface-MMP enumeration shows that every possible rational-surface
marking is one of respectively 8, 24, or 120 weighted-diagram images of the
standard free-chain marking.  Each therefore reduces to the same
cubic-restriction contradiction.  This is a theorem about
**algebro-geometric carriers**; the abstract finite-volume Vinberg and
Vinberg--Kaplinskaja polytopes are classical and certainly exist.

## 1. The explicit surfaces

Use homogeneous coordinates $[X:Y:Z]$, and put

\[
 p=[0:1:0],\qquad L=Z,
\]

\[
 C=Y^2Z-X^3-Z^3,
\]

\[
 Q=Z^4+(X+Z)C,
 \qquad
 S=C^2+Z^2Q.
\tag{1.1}
\]

The cubic $C$ is smooth, $p$ is a flex, and $L\cap C=3p$.  At $p$,
the quartic $Q$ is an ordinary node with tangent cone $z(x+z)$ in the
chart $Y=1$, while

\[
 Q|_C=Z^4.
\]

Consequently its two local branches meet $C$ with multiplicities $1$
and $11$.  Select the order-eleven branch.  Blow up $p_1=p$, follow
$C$ through $p_2,\ldots,p_{11}$, and then follow the selected $Q$
branch through $p_{12},\ldots,p_{15}$.  For $X_n$, truncate after
$p_n$ when $12\le n\le15$.

For $X_{16}$, the selected branches of $Q$ and $S$ share the first
fifteen centres and then separate; take $p_{16}$ on $S$.  Along the
selected $Q$ branch, the divisorial values are

\[
v_k(C^2)=2k\quad(k\le11),\qquad v_k(C^2)=22\quad(k>11),
\]

\[
v_k(Z^2Q)=k+7\quad(k\ge3).
\]

Their successive minima give multiplicity $2$ at
$p_1,\ldots,p_7$ and multiplicity $1$ at
$p_8,\ldots,p_{15}$; after separation $S$ also passes through
$p_{16}$.

Singular 4.4.1's `absfact.lib::absFactorize` returns exactly one
nonconstant absolute factor, with exponent one, for each of $C,Q,S$.
The replay is
[certify_rational_i1n_curves.sing](../scripts/certify_rational_i1n_curves.sing).

Let $H$ be the pullback of a line and $E_i$ the total-transform
exceptional basis.  The visible irreducible curves are

\[
R_i=E_i-E_{i+1}\ (1\le i<n),\qquad E_n,
\tag{1.2}
\]

and, when their squares are negative,

\[
\begin{aligned}
L&=H-E_1-E_2-E_3,\\
C&=3H-E_1-\cdots-E_{11},\\
Q_n&=4H-2E_1-E_2-\cdots-E_n &&(14\le n\le15),\\
Q_{16}&=4H-2E_1-E_2-\cdots-E_{15},\\
S_{16}&=6H-2E_1-\cdots-2E_7-E_8-\cdots-E_{16}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1.3}
\]

Here $R_i,L,C,Q_{15},Q_{16}$ have square $-2$, while $E_n,Q_{14}$,
and $S_{16}$ have square $-1$.  For $n=12,13$, the truncated quartic
has square $1,0$, respectively, so it is not a nef-cone facet.

## 2. Why these are the complete Mori cones

Write an irreducible plane curve distinct from the visible curves as

\[
D=dH-\sum_{i=1}^n m_iE_i,
\qquad
A_j=\sum_{i=1}^j m_i.
\]

Because the cluster is free, proximity gives

\[
m_1\ge m_2\ge\cdots\ge m_n\ge0.
\tag{2.1}
\]

Intersection with the visible irreducible plane curves gives exact Bézout
inequalities.  Every exceptional irreducible curve is already one of the
curves in (1.2).

### 2.1 Dimensions 12 and 13

Intersection with $L$ and $C$ gives

\[
A_3\le d,\qquad A_{11}\le3d.
\tag{2.2}
\]

Set

\[
y=\max\left(0,{A_n-3d\over2}\right),\qquad x=d-3y.
\tag{2.3}
\]

For

\[
B_j=\sum_{i\le j}
\bigl(x\mathbf1_{i\le3}+y\mathbf1_{i\le11}\bigr),
\]

put $z_j=B_j-A_j$.  Since the tail length $n-11$ is at most two,

\[
0\le y\le m_{11}\le d/3,
\]

so $x\ge0$.  The remaining inequalities $z_j\ge0$ follow in four
blocks:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
j\le3:& A_j\le j(d-2m_{11})\le j(d-2y)=B_j,\\
4\le j\le8:& A_j+(9-j)y\le d+6m_4\le3d,\\
9\le j\le11:& A_j\le A_{11}\le3d\le B_j,\\
j\ge12:& A_j\le A_n\le3d+2y=B_j.
\end{array}
\]

Hence

\[
D=xL+yC+\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}z_jR_j+z_nE_n
\tag{2.4}
\]

with every coefficient nonnegative.  This proves

\[
\overline{\operatorname{NE}}(X_n)
=\operatorname{Cone}(R_i,E_n,L,C),\qquad n=12,13.
\tag{2.5}
\]

### 2.2 Dimensions 14 and 15

Bézout with $Q$ adds

\[
A_n+m_1\le4d.
\tag{2.6}
\]

Let $K_n$ be the rational polyhedral cone defined by (2.1), (2.2), and
(2.6).  Exact rank/nullspace enumeration, independently reproduced with
GMP cddlib, gives

\[
\#\operatorname{Ray}(K_{14})=94,
\qquad
\#\operatorname{Ray}(K_{15})=145.
\tag{2.7}
\]

For every primitive ray, the certificate solves exactly for
$x,y,w\ge0$ and $z_j\ge0$ in

\[
D=xL+yC+wQ+\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}z_jR_j+z_nE_n.
\tag{2.8}
\]

There are zero failed rays.  The primitive-ray SHA-256 values are

```text
P14  a81a8babd5fe2028bfd173882cb80eee974a6b680e131f053416afe3760e623f
P15  edb9d4dfea27168e5f39bdabc1f2e93e7004b13dc37bcdcdbaa0044eb8f82751
```

Thus the cone generated by the visible curves contains every possible
irreducible-curve class and is contained in the Mori cone, proving equality.

### 2.3 Dimension 16

The two additional inequalities are

\[
A_{15}+m_1\le4d,
\qquad
A_{16}+A_7\le6d,
\tag{2.9}
\]

from $Q$ and $S$, respectively.  The resulting exact cone has 325
primitive extreme rays.  Every one has an exact nonnegative decomposition

\[
D=xL+yC+wQ+vS+\sum_{j=1}^{15}z_jR_j+z_{16}E_{16}.
\tag{2.10}
\]

Again there are zero failed rays.  The primitive-ray SHA-256 is

```text
c6733607bfa5c0653cd1fadc9752d9ddf8bf366a7937bc166e4980f60d3d98be
```

This proves the complete Mori and nef cones of $X_{16}$.

## 4. The sharp rational-surface obstruction from 17 through 19

For every $n\ge17$, the published simple-root set requires the classes

\[
L=H-E_1-E_2-E_3,
\]

\[
C=3H-E_1-\cdots-E_{11},
\]

\[
Q=4H-2E_1-E_2-\cdots-E_{15},
\]

and the dimension-dependent quartic

\[
A_n=4H-E_1-\cdots-E_{\min(n,18)}.
\]

Thus the class follows 17 centres for $P_{17}$ and 18 centres for
$P_{18}$ and $P_{19}$.  Suppose these are irreducible curves in the
standard free-chain marking.

Since $L\cdot C=0$, Bézout forces

\[
L|_C=3p,
\qquad
\mathcal O_C(1)=\mathcal O_C(3p).
\tag{4.1}
\]

The common multiplicities of $C$ and $Q$ through $p_{11}$ already
contribute

\[
2+10=12=\deg(C)\deg(Q).
\]

Thus $C\cdot Q=0$ forces the selected $Q$ branch, and hence $p_{12}$,
away from $C$.  The smooth-at-$p$ quartic $A_n$ follows the chain, so it
shares exactly $p_1,\ldots,p_{11}$ with $C$.  Therefore

\[
I_p(A_n,C)=11,
\qquad
A_n|_C=11p+r,
\tag{4.2}
\]

where $r$ is an effective degree-one Cartier divisor away from $p$.
But (4.1) gives

\[
\mathcal O_C(A_n)=\mathcal O_C(4)=\mathcal O_C(12p),
\]

and hence

\[
\mathcal O_C(r)=\mathcal O_C(p).
\tag{4.3}
\]

The degree-one Abel map of an integral plane cubic is injective on its
smooth locus; a degree-one Cartier point is smooth.  Equation (4.3) forces
$r=p$, contradicting (4.2).  The proof works for smooth, nodal, or cuspidal
integral $C$, since $p$ is smooth.

There is a useful intrinsic extension.  If a putative nef realization on a
smooth rational surface has the $B_n$ facet chain represented by smooth
rational curves, sequentially contract

\[
E_n,R_{n-1},\ldots,R_1.
\]

Each next curve becomes a $(-1)$-curve, and the final smooth rational
surface has Picard rank one, hence is $\mathbb P^2$.  Reversing the
contractions recovers the standard free chain and the obstruction above.

That conditional contraction argument by itself does not cover arbitrary
facet curves: a negative curve of square $-1$ or $-2$ can have positive
genus.  The missing marking classification is supplied by an exact MMP on
the **complete facet cone**.

Suppose a smooth projective rational surface $X$ had

\[
\overline{\operatorname{NE}}(X)
=\operatorname{Cone}(D_1,\ldots,D_m),
\tag{4.4}
\]

with the $P_n$ facet intersection matrix for $n=17,18$, or $19$.  On a
nonminimal rational surface, a $K_X$-negative extremal ray is generated by
a smooth rational $(-1)$-curve.  Since (4.4) is the complete curve cone,
that ray contains a current nonzero image of some $D_i$.  It occurs with
multiplicity one: if a distinct irreducible image were numerically $q e$,
its intersection with $e$ would be $-q<0$, impossible.  After contracting
$e$, every image is represented in $e^\perp$ by

\[
f^*f_*D=D+(D\mathbin{\cdot}e)e.
\tag{4.5}
\]

Thus every possible smooth rational MMP is a finite calculation using only
the facet lattice vectors.  The numbers of distinct image-cone states in
descending ranks are

```text
P17: 1,2,4,4,8,8,8,8,16,8,8,8,8,8,8,8,16,8
P18: 1,15,6,12,12,24,24,24,24,48,24,24,24,24,24,24,24,48,24
P19: 1,5,75,30,60,60,120,120,120,120,240,120,120,120,120,120,120,120,240,120
```

At rank two the exact endpoint census is

| chamber | $\mathbb F_1$ states | minimal $\mathbb F_2$ states | $\mathbb P^2$ endpoints | canonical classes | diagram-group order |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $P_{17}$ | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 |
| $P_{18}$ | 24 | 24 | 24 | 24 | 24 |
| $P_{19}$ | 120 | 120 | 120 | 120 | 120 |

The $\mathbb F_1$ states continue to the displayed $\mathbb P^2$
endpoints.  Reconstructing the canonical class by

\[
K_X=-3H+\sum e_j
\]

at a $\mathbb P^2$ endpoint, or by

\[
K_X=-2s-4f+\sum e_j
\]

at a minimal $\mathbb F_2$ endpoint, gives the same class set.  In each
dimension it is exactly the orbit of $(-3,1^n)$ under the full weighted
$P_n$ diagram automorphism group.  Every diagram automorphism lifts to an
integral unimodular isometry of $I_{1,n}$ and permutes the complete facet
set.

Conjugate any putative marking so that its canonical class is standard.
Adjunction then makes the conjugated $B_n$ facet chain a chain of smooth
rational curves.  It contracts to $\mathbb P^2$, recovers the standard free
chain, and the Abel contradiction (4.1)--(4.3) applies.  This proves the
marking-independent rational-carrier no-go for all three chambers.

For $P_{17}$ there is also an independent exact dual-cone check.  Its facet cone has 807
primitive extreme nef rays.  Under all facet-adjunction inequalities, only
72 branches $K\mathbin{\cdot}N\le-1$ are feasible; 64 have the exact bound
$a\le123$, while the eight unbounded branches form the diagram orbit of the
isotropic cusp $H-E_1$.  On that cusp, write

\[
K=(a,1-2c_1,\ldots,1-2c_{17}).
\]

Negativity and parity give $c_1\ge(a+3)/2$, whereas adjunction with $L$
gives $c_1+c_2+c_3\le(a+3)/2$.  Monotonicity forces
$c_1=(a+3)/2$ and $c_2=\cdots=c_{17}=0$; then $K^2=-8$ forces $a=-3$.
The other seven cusps follow by diagram symmetry.  The dedicated exact
$P_{18}$/$P_{19}$ MMP certificate and full proof are in
[`rational_p18_p19_mmp.md`](rational_p18_p19_mmp.md).

## 5. Lineage and what is actually new

- [Vinberg's 1972 paper](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/sm3032) computes the
  odd-unimodular reflection chambers through $n=17$.  In particular,
  $P_{12},\ldots,P_{16}$ and their finite-volume status are old.
- [Vinberg--Kaplinskaja 1978](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/dan41538) treats
  $O_{18,1}(\mathbb Z)$ and $O_{19,1}(\mathbb Z)$.  The exact reconstruction
  here recovers all 37 and 50 simple roots in those dimensions, but produces
  no new abstract chamber.
- [Guglielmetti's CoxIter](https://doi.org/10.1112/S1461157015000273)
  supplies the pinned relation graphs and the independent finite-volume,
  cusp, $f$-vector, and volume replay.
- Galindo--Monserrat--Moreno-Ávila--Pérez-Callejo's
  [2026 pencil-at-infinity theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15703)
  gives a different complete rational-surface cone factory, but the
  square-$-1/-2$ part of that factory stops at $\mathbb H^{11}$.  The
  off-cubic quartic and sextic branches above genuinely leave its stated
  construction.

The new mathematical content established by this project is therefore the
complete effective/nef-cone proof for the explicit $P_{12}$ through
$P_{16}$ rational surfaces, plus the unconditional smooth-rational-carrier
no-go for $P_{17}$ through $P_{19}$.  The safe priority label is:

> **new project theorem and positive algebro-geometric realization;
> abstract chamber classical; worldwide carrier priority open.**
