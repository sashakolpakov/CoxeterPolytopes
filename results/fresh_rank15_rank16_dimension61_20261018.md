## Global contradiction and the higher-dimensional tail

Let D5 be the sum of 5 minus the number of sides over all two-faces.
The local payments give

    D5 <= 434*v_f + sum_(ideal v) C_v.

The inherited shape-curvature theorem gives its lower bound with the
two-extrema correction. Every coefficient of the relevant simplex or
product-of-simplices H-polynomial is at least 1. At d=61 each of the two
extreme vertices therefore has

    E-G >= sum_(2j>61) (2j-61) = 961.

Multiplication by 5/2 at each extreme gives the single global correction
4805. This is the same correction already present in the ambient62
frontier, not a second copy of a vertex payment. Thus

    D5 >= 434*v_f + sum_(ideal v) kappa(L_v) + 4805.

gives the impossible inequality at the start. This excludes dimension
62, including the case v_i=0. The
[completed bound 62](fresh_sparse_coupon_order11_13_dimension62_20261018.md)
already excludes every dimension at least 63. The combined conclusion
