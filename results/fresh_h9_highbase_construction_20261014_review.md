The category `7` represents every **finite integer label at least 7**, not parallel or dotted pairs. For a triangle containing such a label, other labels `(2,2)` have reciprocal sum `1+1/m>1` for every finite `m`. Every other pair has reciprocal sum at most `1/2+1/3+1/7=41/42<1`. Replacing every large finite label by the category therefore preserves exactly whether the triangle is Lannér. The separate checker enumerates all 175 ordered hyperbolic triangle categories using rational reciprocals.

The same compression preserves all finite-vertex tests. A connected finite diagram containing a large edge cannot contain a third node: choose a third node adjacent to one endpoint. The large edge has squared cosine strictly above `3/4`, the additional edge at least `1/4`, and the three-node determinant is at most `1−a²−b²<0`; a further edge only decreases it. Thus every such finite component is exactly `I2(m)`. The wrapper in the producer correctly accepts arbitrary two-node finite components before calling its higher-rank finite-diagram classifier. Shared category labels may merge distinct large integers, which only enlarges the allowed states and is safe for an exclusion. There is no truncation of the finite label range hidden in this step.

A failed relaxed vertex test therefore cannot discard an actual compact Coxeter labeling.
