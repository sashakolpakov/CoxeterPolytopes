## Why a simple D16 long-leaf capacity refund fails

The current D16 long-leaf roles are(0,14),(0,15), each half ordinarily and
unit in the presence of a separate whole A1. Both can have their genuine
selected-core obligations at the same vertex. This can be shown directly,
without a census, by a compact triangular-prism3-face.

Let alpha0,...,alpha15 be norm-two D16 simple roots, with alpha0 the long
endpoint and alpha14,alpha15 its two short leaves. In the standard Euclidean
realization alpha_j=e_(j+1)-e_(j+2) for j<=14 and alpha15=e15+e16. Let omega_j
be their dual basis. Then

    omega1=e1+e2,
    omega14=(e1+...+e15-e16)/2,
    omega15=(e1+...+e15+e16)/2.

In particular omega1^2=2, omega14^2=omega15^2=4,
omega1·omega14=omega1·omega15=1 and omega14·omega15=7/2.
Take a separate norm-two root a and a unit timelike vector h orthogonal
to the entire vertex root span. Define

    c=-omega1-a/2+h/sqrt(2),
    d=-omega15+sqrt(2)*h.

Both have square2. The only nonzero products with vertex roots are
c·alpha1=c·a=d·alpha15=-1, and c·d=0. This is exactly the saved core used by
1644 and1664 in the [A1-guarded D16 theorem](fresh_d16_a1_long_leaf_d17_zero_ambient58_frontier_20261018.md).
An explicit identification with its physical labels is

    (alpha0,...,alpha15)
      =(1,0,4,5,6,7,8,9,18,17,16,15,14,10,11,12),
    a=3, c=2, d=13.

To see actual faces, impose the supporting walls a and all alpha_j except
alpha0,alpha14,alpha15. In the Klein slice write

    x=h-(U*omega0+V*omega14+W*omega15)/sqrt(2).

The remaining five halfspaces are exactly

    U,V,W>=0,
    U+V+W<=1,
    U/2+7V/2+4W<=2.

Their six vertices are

    (0,0,0), (1,0,0), (0,4/7,0), (0,0,1/2),
    (1/2,1/2,0), (4/7,0,3/7).

The squared Euclidean parts of x at these points are respectively
0,1/2,32/49,1/2,3/4,32/49. All are strictly below1, so this entire bounded
prism lies inside hyperbolic space. Its V=0 and W=0 faces are the two
quadrilaterals at the long-leaf marked pairs. Thus they genuinely coexist;
one cannot replace their two units by a single unit merely by asserting
that opposite-leaf reverse walls are incompatible. They share the same d.

This persists for the entire formal vertex A1+2D16+D24. Use the same a,h
and add c_i,d_i as above for each orthogonal D16 factor. Cross-factor
products are

    c1·c2=0, c1·d2=d1·c2=-1, d1·d2=-2.

All are allowed acute Coxeter products. The D24 roots are orthogonal to
the four added walls. Each prism is an actual supporting3-face of this
halfspace intersection: on its supporting section the other factor's
c,d walls have strictly negative product and impose no cut. This supplies
compatible local walls and actual compact prism faces at the specified
vertex. It does not construct a complete finite-volume57-polytope.

If one instead insists on two different d walls attaching to the two
leaves of one D16, their product is7/2-2=3/2>0 and they are incompatible.
That true pairwise exclusion does not give the desired capacity bound,
because the single-wall prism above already realizes both obligations.
