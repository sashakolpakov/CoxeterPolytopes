## Closing the quadrilateral proof

The frozen mixed rule costs at most3n at every vertex and already pays every
triangle at least two. Its affine-cycle quadrilateral branch was also complete.
Two new ingredients finish the exhaustive quadrilateral case split:

* The [classical affine-tree proof](fresh_simple_cusp_affine_tree_completion_20261017.md)
  covers every main-path, doubled-terminal and fork-leaf position in every
  dimension n>=18. The proof uses the weighted-tree Green formula, complete
  finite/affine replacement lists, and the two exceptional E8 calculations.
  Its finite exact checks confirm the formulas; they do not replace the
  all-rank argument. A separate [terminal/E8 audit](fresh_simple_cusp_affine_tree_terminal_audit_20261017.md)
  reconstructs the physical matrices and exceptional determinants.
* The [four-finite-corner transfer](fresh_simple_finite_quad_vinberg_transfer_20261017.md)
  checks the compactness-sensitive hypotheses of Vinberg's original local
  proof. Actual diagrams orthogonal to an indefinite diagram are positive
  definite; the remaining affine exclusions fit in carriers smaller than n.
  The full quadrilateral dependence supplies the nonzero determinant
  coefficient. A separate [source review](fresh_simple_finite_quad_vinberg_transfer_20261017_review.md)
  accepts this transfer with the published finite classifications and local
  determinant tables retained as explicit inputs.

Every quadrilateral therefore receives at least one unit. Nonnegative weights
pay the required 5-k on every k-gon with k>=5. The exact composer combines
these facts with the strict simple-polytope h-vector inequality. For n>=30,
the total payment would be both at most3n f0 and strictly greater than3n f0.
This proves n<=29. The [root mathematical review](fresh_all_simple_dimension29_20261017_root_review.md)
separately checks the case partition, parity formulas and strict threshold.

## A construction topology excluded for all labels

The [central-truncation gate](fresh_nonarithmetic_central_truncation_domain_20261017.md)
tests a distinct pair of central walls, with degrees three and five. The
first center needs only three consecutive finite ring corners; its fourth
corner would be removed by the second center. The earlier four-corner gate
did not cover this arrangement.

For all twenty literal rings and all four omitted corners, the enlarged
domain is now excluded for every finite Coxeter label and every real field.
The proof inherits35,840 exact low-contact norm exclusions, checks1,408
one-high-contact branches, and rules out six two-high-contact real rectangles
by a strict norm margin. No required first center exists in this domain.

This is an obstruction for the specified rings, not for altered ring
parameters or every patch topology. The control chamber remains64walls,
198finite vertices and15target-face open ends, with certified infinite
volume. There is no new finite-volume example.
