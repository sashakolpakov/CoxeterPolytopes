# Gram--Belyi carrier: effective bounds and the exact obstruction

**Status (2026-08-10): theorem-quality strategic boundary.**  The current
Gram--Belyi carrier gives an unconditional, effective dimension and field
bound relative to an explicit marked Belyi-packet certificate.  A uniform
bound for a class follows conditionally from a uniform absolute
Gram--Belyi/polarization bound.  The present carrier does not itself produce
that uniform bound: its unmarked SNC surface has constant canonical and
Hodge invariants, while exact return is stored in the marked finite map and
its polarization.  Stable/log boundedness, Picard/Hodge restrictions, and
Hurwitz/Galois rigidity therefore do not close the global bridge without a
new hypothesis.

This memo distinguishes:

- **unconditional** implications proved from a supplied exact packet;
- **conditional** routes whose missing hypotheses are stated explicitly; and
- statements **impossible from the current listed data**, as witnessed by an
  exact Coxeter counterfamily.

No ABSW theorem, Borel--Prasad finiteness theorem, Prasad volume formula, or
Mostow--Prasad rigidity theorem is used below.

## 1. Unconditional certificate-relative theorem

Let `P` be an arithmetic finite-volume Coxeter chamber with `f` ordered
facets, let

\[
 M=\binom f2,
 \qquad
 u_{ij}=\frac{(r_i,r_j)^2}{r_i^2r_j^2},
 \qquad
 \lambda_{ij}=\frac{u_{ij}}{1+u_{ij}}\qquad(u_{ij}>0).
\]

For every nonorthogonal pair, suppose an exact certificate supplies a
normalized Belyi polynomial `B_ij` of degree `e_ij` such that
`B_ij(lambda_ij)` is `0` or `1`.  Give every orthogonal pair the standard
degree-two bit-packet and put

\[
 E=2\#\{(i,j):u_{ij}=0\}
   +\sum_{u_{ij}>0}e_{ij}.
\]

All hypotheses are checkable from the exact Gram of `P`: one verifies the
marked value and that all finite critical values of each polynomial lie in
`{0,1}`.

> **Certified-packet bound.**  Under these hypotheses,
> \[
> \beta_{\rm GB}(P)\le E,
> \qquad
> n\le
> \left\lfloor\frac{\sqrt{1+4E}-1}{2}\right\rfloor,
> \]
> and
> \[
> [k_{\rm Vin}(P):\mathbf Q]
> \le
> 2^M\prod_{u_{ij}>0}(2e_{ij})^4(e_{ij}!)^2.
> \tag{1.1}
> \]
> The marked packets, normalized Grams, chambers, and Vinberg fields with
> `E` bounded are effectively enumerable.

**Proof.**  Minimality gives
`h_Bel(lambda_ij)<=e_ij`.  Every pair has minimal packet degree at least two,
so

\[
 f(f-1)\le\beta_{\rm GB}(P)\le E.
\]

The facet normals span a Lorentzian space of dimension `n+1`, hence
`n<=f-1`, proving the dimension bound.

A degree-`e` Belyi cover has an ordered monodromy pair in
`S_e x S_e`, giving at most `(e!)^2` pairs before quotienting.  The three
nontrivial ordered source marks lie over `{0,1}`, a set of size at most
`2e`; the harmless fourth power bounds all source-frame markings.  Thus the
Galois orbit of one marked coordinate packet is at most
`(2e)^4(e!)^2`.  Multiplying over leaves bounds the field generated by all
`u_ij`.  Adjoining the distinguished square roots needed for a unit-normal
Gram costs at most `2^M`, and the Vinberg cyclic-product field is a
subfield.  This proves (1.1).  Finite permutation enumeration and exact
algebraic elimination give effectivity.  \(\square\)

This is the certificate version of equations (1.5)--(1.6), Theorem 3.1, and
equations (3.3)--(3.4) in
[`gram_belyi_coxeter_dessin_theorem.md`](gram_belyi_coxeter_dessin_theorem.md)
(lines 78--100 and 219--304).  Its field-counting argument was independently
audited in
[`gram_belyi_coxeter_dessin_theorem_hostile_audit.md`](gram_belyi_coxeter_dessin_theorem_hostile_audit.md)
(lines 136--168).

### Polarized form and a sharpened dimension inequality

For the canonical minimal carrier,

\[
 A_P^2=2\bigl(M+\beta_{\rm GB}(P)\bigr).
 \tag{1.2}
\]

Since `beta_GB>=2M`,

\[
 A_P^2\ge6M=3f(f-1)\ge3n(n+1).
 \tag{1.3}
\]

Consequently a uniform `A_P^2<=V` implies

\[
 n\le
 \left\lfloor\frac{\sqrt{1+4V/3}-1}{2}\right\rfloor.
 \tag{1.4}
\]

It also gives the frozen coarse field bound by taking
`B=floor(V/2)` in equation (3.4) of the cited theorem.  Identity (1.2) and
the exact-return marking are proved in that report at lines 126--217.

These are noncircular implications: neither a field list nor a dimension
catalogue is an input.  They are not yet a uniform theorem for all maximal
chambers, because no uniform `E` or `V` is currently derived from
maximality.

## 2. Unconditional constant-invariants obstruction

The carrier curve `C_P` consists of one rational backbone and `M` rational
leaves, with one node attaching each leaf to the backbone.  Its dual graph
has `M+1` vertices and `M` edges and is a tree.  Hence

\[
 p_a(C_P)=0,
 \qquad
 \chi(\mathcal O_{C_P})=(M+1)-M=1.
\]

Put `S_P=C_P x P1`.  Kunneth then gives

\[
 \chi(\mathcal O_{S_P})=1,
 \qquad
 h^1(\mathcal O_{S_P})=h^2(\mathcal O_{S_P})=0.
 \tag{2.1}
\]

The nodal curve is Gorenstein, and so is its product.  On the fundamental
cycle of the SNC surface,

\[
 \omega_{S_P}
 =p_1^*\omega_{C_P}\otimes p_2^*\omega_{\mathbf P^1},
\]

and therefore

\[
 K_{S_P}^2
 =2\deg(\omega_{C_P})\deg(\omega_{\mathbf P^1})
 =2(-2)(-2)=8.
 \tag{2.2}
\]

Thus `K^2`, `chi(O)`, `q`, and `p_g` are constant for every Gram--Belyi
carrier, independently of the dimension, facet number, Belyi degree, or
Vinberg field.

The reduced topological Euler characteristic and the number of irreducible
components are not constant.  Additivity at the `M` nodes gives

\[
 e(C_P)=2(M+1)-M=M+2,
 \qquad
 e(S_P)=2M+4=f(f-1)+4.
\tag{2.3}
\]

This yields one honest surface-native positive statement:

> **Euler--facet theorem.**  The unmarked carrier surface determines the
> facet number through (2.3), and
> \[
> n(n+1)\le e(S_P)-4.
> \]
> Consequently a class-uniform, independently derived bound
> `e(S_P)<=E_0` would give
> \[
> n\le\left\lfloor
> \frac{\sqrt{4E_0-15}-1}{2}
> \right\rfloor.
> \]

This invariant remembers the deliberately inserted facet-pair count, but it
has no intrinsic uniform upper bound in the present construction.  At fixed
`M` it does not see the leaf-map degrees, `beta_GB`, or the marked fields.
The obstruction below concerns the stated canonical/Hodge tuple, not every
conceivable invariant of the reducible scheme.

The surface is not stable.  A leaf component `E` meets the rest of `C_P` in
one node, so

\[
 \omega_{C_P}|_E=\omega_E(\text{node})=\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^1}(-1),
\]

and

\[
 \omega_{S_P}|_{E\times\mathbf P^1}=\mathcal O(-1,-2).
 \tag{2.4}
\]

In particular `K_S` is not ample.  The hostile audit explicitly describes
the output only as a projective SNC surface and warns that it is not a
claimed stable surface; see lines 92--112 of
[`gram_belyi_coxeter_dessin_theorem_hostile_audit.md`](gram_belyi_coxeter_dessin_theorem_hostile_audit.md).

> **Constant-invariants blindness theorem.**  The tuple
> `(K_S^2,chi(O_S),q(S),p_g(S))=(8,1,0,0)` supplies no varying quantity from
> which to derive `beta_GB`, the facet number, or a dimension-sensitive
> estimate for the current carrier.  Any formula
> `n<=F(K^2,chi,q,p_g)` would merely insert the external constant
> `F(8,1,0,0)`; these surface calculations do not determine it.  On the
> actual redoubling subfamily in Section 5, the same tuple accompanies
> unbounded `beta_GB` and facet number, so no finite bound for those two
> quantities can depend only on this tuple.
>
> More generally, forgetting the finite map, polarization,
> normalization/conductor labels, cross-ratios, and real markings destroys
> exact Gram return.  Retaining the canonical polarization restores exact
> return but exposes exactly the unbounded quantity
> `A_P^2=2(M+beta_GB)`.

This is an exact information-theoretic blindness statement; it does not
assert that hyperbolic dimension has no universal bound from some independent
theorem.  The return mechanism really uses the marked
four-point cross-ratios, distinguished real place, acute convention, and
time sheet; see lines 191--217 of
[`gram_belyi_coxeter_dessin_theorem.md`](gram_belyi_coxeter_dessin_theorem.md).

## 3. Stable and log surfaces: conditional only

There is presently no canonical stable/log pair attached to the carrier.
Any repair has two branches.

1. If it contracts or forgets the finite map/polarization and arithmetic
   markings, it falls under the obstruction theorem and no longer returns
   the Gram.
2. If it retains them as a stable map or marked polarized pair, its discrete
   degree/Hilbert datum contains `M+beta_GB`, equivalently `A_P^2/2`.

This matches the standard boundedness theorems rather than evading their
hypotheses.  Alexeev proves boundedness for semi-log-canonical surface pairs
with ample `K_X+B`, coefficients in a DCC set, and **fixed log volume**
`(K_X+B)^2=C`; the theorem does not derive a uniform `C` from stability
alone: [Alexeev, *Boundedness and K^2 for log surfaces*](https://arxiv.org/abs/alg-geom/9402007).
Likewise, stable-map compactness is degree-by-degree: Abramovich--Vistoli's
primary theorem explicitly fixes the degree:
[*Compactifying the space of stable maps*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9908167).

Fixed log volume also does **not** by itself give a finite field list.  The
failure has an explicit stable-pair model.  For an odd prime `p`, let

\[
 \lambda_p=\frac{1+\cos(\pi/p)}2
\]

and on `X=P1 x P1` take the ordered boundary

\[
 \Delta_p=V_0+V_1+V_\infty+V_{\lambda_p}
            +H_0+H_1+H_\infty,
\]

where `V` and `H` denote vertical and horizontal fibres.  This is an SNC log
canonical pair, and

\[
 K_X+\Delta_p=2V+H,
 \qquad
 (K_X+\Delta_p)^2=4.
\]

Thus it is a stable log surface with fixed coefficient set, fixed boundary
combinatorics, and fixed log volume.  The ordered triples `0,1,infinity`
kill both `PGL2` factors, while the remaining cross-ratio recovers
`lambda_p`; hence the pair has trivial automorphisms and field of moduli
`Q(lambda_p)`, of degree `(p-1)/2`.  These degrees are unbounded.  This is
the concrete distinction between **bounded finite type** and a
zero-dimensional finite Galois set.  It also shows why the
zero-dimensional Gram-image hypothesis cannot be dropped from the existing
dessin-native field theorem.

A log-BMY inequality has the direction

\[
 (K_X+\Delta)^2\le3e_{\rm orb}(X,\Delta)
\]

under its hypotheses; see Langer,
[*Logarithmic orbifold Euler numbers of surfaces with applications*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0012180).
It gives an absolute upper bound only after the orbifold Euler term is
bounded independently.  In the present carrier, the branch, conductor, and
marked-component complexity grows with `M+beta_GB`; no independent uniform
Euler bound is known.

There is nevertheless a rigorous conditional bridge.

> **Conditional log bridge.**  Suppose a class of chambers has a canonical
> exact-return log model `(X_P,Delta_P)` for which log BMY applies, and there
> are class-uniform constants `c,E_0` satisfying
> \[
> A_P^2\le c(K_{X_P}+\Delta_P)^2,
> \qquad
> e_{\rm orb}(X_P,\Delta_P)\le E_0.
> \]
> Then `A_P^2<=3cE_0`, and equations (1.1)--(1.4) give effective dimension
> and Vinberg-field bounds.

This would be noncircular only if `c,E_0` were derived independently from a
`P`-intrinsic condition such as reflection maximality.  Neither inequality
is currently available.

## 4. Picard and Hodge restrictions: a different carrier theorem

There is a genuine surface-native inequality when the entire torsion-free
`O_k`-lattice of rank `n+1` embeds additively in `NS(S)`:

\[
 [k:\mathbf Q]\,(n+1)\le\rho(S).
 \tag{4.1}
\]

This can give a dimension and field-*degree* bound when a carrier class has
a uniform Picard ceiling.  For example, a complex K3 carrier has
`rho<=20`.  It does not by itself give a finite field list: bounded number-
field degree without discriminant, ramification, height, or a finite Gram
image still allows infinitely many fields.

Crucially, (4.1) does **not** apply to the Gram--Belyi carrier.  Its quadratic
root system is external marked data, not an embedded Neron--Severi lattice.
Treating it as an NS embedding would manufacture a false dimension theorem.
This exact distinction is recorded at lines 222--239 of
[`dessin_native_field_effectivity_theorem.md`](dessin_native_field_effectivity_theorem.md)
and lines 113--134 of
[`dessin_native_field_effectivity_hostile_audit.md`](dessin_native_field_effectivity_hostile_audit.md).

Nor does moving to the universal weak rational-surface eigencarrier give a
ceiling: it uses up to `4[k:Q](n+1)-3` blowups and explicitly has no K3 rank
bound; see lines 30--44 of
[`rational_surface_eigencarrier_universality.md`](rational_surface_eigencarrier_universality.md).

## 5. Hurwitz and Galois rigidity: one-way only

Bounded Belyi degree gives finitely many permutation triples and therefore a
finite Galois set.  The converse fails.

There is a precise moduli formulation.  Let the unbounded marked-packet
stack be the disjoint union over all degrees, and send a packet to its ordered
four-point cross-ratio `lambda`.  The normalized genus-zero marked-point
Belyi theorem says that every algebraic
`lambda notin {0,1}` has at least one packet.  Hence on geometric points the
unbounded stack maps onto

\[
 M_{0,4}(\overline{\mathbf Q})
   =\overline{\mathbf Q}\setminus\{0,1\}.
\]

> **Unbounded-degree vacuity theorem.**  Existence of some Gram--Belyi leaf,
> with no degree bound, cuts out no proper algebraic locus in the four-point
> moduli line.  Its image is the full positive-dimensional `M_0,4`.  The
> finite-dessin mechanism enters only after imposing an absolute degree
> cutoff.

Thus unbounded dessins do not replace the algebraic-number classification;
they re-encode all of it.  Global Gram-rank, Coxeter, arithmeticity, and
finite-volume equations can of course cut the product of these moduli lines,
but Belyi existence itself contributes no additional boundedness.

- The branch locus `{0,1,infinity}` does not bound degree: `x^d` is a
  normalized Belyi polynomial over `Q` for every `d`.
- On a rational degree-`d` leaf, Riemann--Hurwitz reads
  \[
  2d-2=\sum_x(e_x-1),
  \]
  and permits arbitrary `d`; the ramification sum grows with it.
- A trivial Galois orbit does not bound degree, as the maps `x^d` already
  show.

Even a proposed translation of reflection maximality into **indecomposability
of the dessin map** would be insufficient.  For every odd prime `p`, put

\[
 B_p(x)=\frac{1+T_p(2x-1)}2,
 \qquad
 \lambda_p=\frac{1+\cos(\pi/p)}2,
\tag{5.1}
\]

where `T_p` is the Chebyshev polynomial.  Then `B_p` is a normalized Belyi
polynomial over `Q`: its finite critical values are `0,1`, it sends `0` to
`0` and `1` to `1`, and `B_p(lambda_p)=0`.  Its prime degree makes it
composition-indecomposable.  Nevertheless its degrees are unbounded, while
the marked fields `Q(lambda_p)` have degrees `(p-1)/2`.  In particular the
minimal Belyi heights of the `lambda_p` are also unbounded: otherwise
finiteness of normalized marked Belyi packets in bounded degree would put
the infinitely many distinct `lambda_p` in one finite set.  Thus fixed branch
locus, rational field of definition of the map, and dessin indecomposability
still do not provide the missing bound.

Equivalently, three-point rigidity makes each fixed-degree passport locus
zero-dimensional, but the disjoint union over all degrees is not finite type.
Pointwise rigidity without one bounded finite-type envelope is therefore not
the zero-dimensional-image theorem needed for field effectivity.

Minimality of the packet does not repair the converse.  The frozen exact
redoubling family supplies finite-volume chambers
`P_{2(p+1),A}` in fixed `H10`, over `Q`, in one fixed Picard lattice and with
fixed root-norm/divisibility and finite-angle alphabets, but for every odd
prime `p`,

\[
 \beta_{\rm GB}(P_{2(p+1),A})\ge p.
\tag{5.2}
\]

The exact Gram and valuation proof is in lines 306--356 of
[`gram_belyi_coxeter_dessin_theorem.md`](gram_belyi_coxeter_dessin_theorem.md),
with producer
[`certify_gram_belyi_h10_lower_bound.py`](../scripts/certify_gram_belyi_h10_lower_bound.py)
and archive
[`gram_belyi_h10_lower_bound.json`](../data/enumerations/gram_belyi_h10_lower_bound.json).

> **Descendant obstruction theorem.**  On any class containing this
> finite-index redoubling tower, there is no function of
> \[
> n,\ [k:\mathbf Q],\ \text{the fixed Picard lattice},\
> \text{root norm/divisibility alphabet},\
> \text{finite-angle alphabet},\ K_S^2,\chi,q,p_g
> \]
> that bounds `beta_GB` or `A_P^2`.

This is the precise impossible-from-current-data claim.  It does not assert
that no stronger invariant could exist.

## 6. Maximality caveat and surviving routes

The redoubling chambers are finite-index descendants.  Therefore the
obstruction theorem does **not** disprove a uniform theorem for
reflection-maximal chambers.  It proves that maximality must do genuine
work: generic stable/log/Hodge/Hurwitz facts that also hold for descendants
cannot supply the missing bound unless maximality is translated into a new
algebraic condition.

Exactly three noncircular routes remain.

1. **Maximality-to-polarization.**  Prove
   \[
   P\text{ reflection-maximal}\Longrightarrow A_P^2\le V
   \]
   from intrinsic marked geometry.  This is the missing bridge already
   isolated at lines 240--266 of
   [`coxeter_borcherds_dessin_route.md`](coxeter_borcherds_dessin_route.md).
2. **Intrinsic divisor carrier.**  Prove an exhaustive carrier theorem in
   which the full arithmetic lattice genuinely embeds in `NS`, with uniform
   Picard and absolute facet-degree bounds.  Equation (4.1) then applies.
3. **Finite Gram image.**  Build one bounded finite-type, `P`-only carrier
   grammar and prove its unit-normal Gram image zero-dimensional.  A finite
   image directly enumerates the geometric Gram and Vinberg fields even if
   the surface fibres move.  Bounded Hilbert polynomials alone give only
   finite type, not zero dimension; see lines 179--220 of
   [`dessin_native_field_effectivity_theorem.md`](dessin_native_field_effectivity_theorem.md).

## 7. Final logical ledger

### Unconditional

- An explicit marked packet of total degree `E` gives equations (1.1) and
  the effective dimension bound.
- A bound on `A_P^2` gives the sharper equation (1.4).
- The unmarked carrier invariants are exactly `(K^2,chi,q,p_g)=(8,1,0,0)`.
- The exact redoubling family has unbounded minimal Gram--Belyi degree with
  fixed dimension, rational field, Picard lattice, and root alphabets.

### Conditional

- A class-uniform `E`, `A^2`, or independent log-BMY bridge gives a uniform
  dimension and finite field list.
- A genuine NS embedding plus a Picard ceiling gives a dimension and field-
  degree bound, but not a finite field list by itself.
- A bounded finite-type carrier grammar with zero-dimensional Gram image
  gives a finite effective field list.

### Impossible from the current listed data

- Ordinary `K^2`, `chi`, `q`, and `p_g` of `S_P` cannot bound the chamber.
- Fixed dimension, field degree, the displayed Picard lattice, and the
  displayed root alphabets cannot bound `beta_GB` on unrestricted
  descendants.
- Stability, fixed branch locus, Riemann--Hurwitz, or Galois rigidity alone
  cannot reverse “bounded Belyi degree implies finite fields.”

Thus the current carrier has completed the implication

```text
bounded absolute marked Gram--Belyi degree
  => effective dimension, chamber, and Vinberg-field bounds,
```

but a global ABSW/Prasad-free theorem now requires a genuinely new
maximality-to-marked-volume lemma, an exhaustive intrinsic divisor carrier,
or a bounded zero-dimensional Gram-image theorem.  Further fixed-degree
passport elimination does not change this strategic boundary.
