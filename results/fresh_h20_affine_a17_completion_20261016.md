# An exact five-wall cusp completion in H20

Five explicitly defined algebraic walls complete the persistent null ray
of the [135-wall chamber](fresh_h20_complementary_cap_20261016.md) to a
proper cusp with link of type affine G2 + affine A17. The resulting
chamber has **140 actual facets**. The old strict escape E1 still survives,
so this is a local cusp completion, not a finite-volume construction.

The [source](../scripts/fresh_h20_affine_a17_completion_20261016.py) and
[certificate](fresh_h20_affine_a17_completion_20261016.json) define the new
vectors by exact projections and positive square roots. Rational outward
interval arithmetic checks strict signs. Generation and fresh saved
replay both pass. No frozen predecessor is edited. An independent review
of this five-wall completion is not included in this primary package.

## The retained cusp data

## Exact construction of the five roots

Let W be the ordered list of the 13 path roots and let G be its rational
Gram matrix. Prescribe five contact rows B_i, each with value -1 at the
following two endpoints and zero at every other old incident wall:

| New wall | Endpoints |
|---|---|
|135|4,6|
|136|10,12|
|137|15,17|
|138|17,31|
|139|31,3|

The old-span projection is p_i = B_i G^-1 W. If the new roots are to be
mutually perpendicular and have square 2, their free remainders must
have Gram matrix `S = 2I - B G^-1 B^t`. Direct rational calculation gives

```
S = [ 1/2  -1/6    0     0   -1/3 ]
    [-1/6  11/30 -1/5    0     0  ]
    [  0   -1/5   7/10 -1/2    0  ]
    [  0     0   -1/2    1   -1/2 ]
    [-1/3    0     0   -1/2   5/6 ].
```

This is a weighted cycle Laplacian with positive weights
`1/3,1/6,1/5,1/2,1/2`. It has rank 4 and kernel generated by the all-ones
vector. The first four principal coordinates have exact LDL pivots
`1/2,14/45,4/7,9/16`.

For reproducibility, the certificate chooses a specific positive
orthonormal frame in the four free directions. First project H off the
positive 15-dimensional span U of W and roots0,1. Put

```
hbar = H - proj_U(H),
k = hbar/(-nu.H),
eta = k + (k^2/2)*nu.
```

Then `eta.nu=-1`, `eta^2=0`, and both vectors are orthogonal to U. Project
the ambient coordinate vectors off U and then send v to
`v+(v.eta)*nu+(v.nu)*eta`. Ordered Gram–Schmidt selects coordinates
`[0,3,6,12]`; all four pivots are rigorously positive. The recorded LDL
factor realizes S in this positive four-space: use its first four rows
and take the fifth remainder to be minus their sum. These equations,
rather than numerical zero tests, establish the exact prescribed Gram
matrix and all orthogonality identities.

Let u_i=p_i+z_i denote the resulting unshifted roots. Adding any real
multiple of nu preserves their norms, mutual pairings and old incident
contacts. For an old nonincident root n_j, `nu.n_j<0`; therefore a
sufficiently large positive shift makes the new contact strictly dotted.
The chosen exact roots are

```
n_(135+i) = u_i + lambda_i*nu,
(lambda_0,...,lambda_4) = (4,4,3,4,4).
```

Every one of the 595 new-to-old-nonincident contacts is strictly less
than -2. The other 80 new-to-old contacts are the stated right or pi/3
contacts. The ten new-to-new contacts are exactly zero. Thus all 685
new pair gates are accounted for without an angle cutoff.

## A bounded full-rank cusp link

The 18-cycle is

```
3,4,135,6,7,8,9,10,136,12,13,14,15,137,17,138,31,139.
```

Its Gram matrix is precisely affine A17. The positive null relation is
also literal in the ambient space. For an Ar path, the sum of the two
endpoint rows of its inverse Gram matrix is the all-ones row (counting
both endpoints when r=1). Consequently the five old-span projections
sum to minus the sum of all 13 old white roots. The five free remainders
sum to zero. After the shifts, the sum of the 18 cycle roots is therefore
`19*nu`.

Together with the retained affine G2 relation, this gives two affine
components of Euclidean ranks 2 and 17, with the same future null ray.
Their alcoves form a bounded product in the 19-dimensional horosphere.
The complete incidence at nu consists of these 21 roots: the 16 old
incident walls and the five new ones. Every other retained wall is
strictly negative at nu. Hence nu is now a proper bounded cusp.

## Coherence, actual facets and finite vertices

All five new roots pair strictly negatively with H. The intersection is
therefore full-dimensional. Acuteness gives an explicit interior point
of each wall: for a square-2 root n, use `H-(H.n)n/2`. Its norm is
negative and it is strict for every other wall. All 140 walls are actual
facets.

The old finite vertices persist under an acute addition that retains H.
Indeed, at an old finite vertex v with positive-definite incident Gram
matrix G_v, write `H=a*v+sum b_i*n_i`, with a>0 and
`b=G_v^-1(H.n_i)<=0`. For a new acute wall r with H.r<0, this implies
`H.r>=a*(v.r)`, so v.r<0. This retains at least the 170 already certified
finite vertices; the old 168-vertex list was partial and is not promoted
to a complete ambient census.

There are **no new finite vertices**, completely. A finite vertex of an
acute H20 chamber has a positive-definite support of 20 incident walls.
Any such support containing a new root cannot contain an old
nonincident wall, since their pair is dotted. It must lie in the
21-node affine G2 + affine A17 star. Deleting at most one of its nodes
leaves one entire affine component, so its Gram matrix cannot be
positive definite. The three previously certified proper cusps are also
strict for all five new walls and persist. No full new exterior-ray or
ideal-point census is asserted.

## The remaining escape and the height limitation

The exact old escape

```
E1=x-e/100-(49/50)*f-omega17
   -(1/100000)*sum_{j!=17}(omega_j)
```

has square `1842000049/4000000000>0` and remains strict for the five new
walls. Their pairings lie near
`-21.5181,-22.0723,-16.5901,-21.3978,-22.0241`.
Thus the 140-wall chamber still has infinite volume.

For this particular free-frame choice, the all-dotted lower height
thresholds are approximately `2.2224,2.5248,1.6864,2,2.5664`, whereas
cutting E1 would require heights below approximately
`0.09145,-0.00922,-0.01343,0.11331,-0.00046`. This diagnoses why these
five canonical connectors do not cut E1. It is not an obstruction for
other orientations, other contact patterns or all walls through nu.
