The certificate has no global maximum, reachability, or attainment field. `transition` supplies a successor and its upper inequality for every stored predecessor and every allowed item within the cutoff. Consequently list induction derives both storage and an upper bound for every actual item list. `tight` applies to every stored positive finite value. Its rank equation and `rank_pos` make the predecessor strictly smaller, so strong rank induction produces an attaining list. This excludes spurious stored states without assuming their reachability. The two inductions are independent and combine into `memo_exact` and its converse.

Both unreachability directions are covered. A `none` at an attainable rank contradicts `list_upper`; an allegedly unreachable stored `some` contradicts `memo_attained`. At a positive rank, transition and tightness also prove the no-stored-predecessor characterization. Zero is correctly excluded from that latter theorem and is fixed separately to `some 0`. There is no appeal to table values beyond the cutoff. A tight predecessor's own capacity follows from its rank equation; a missing separate predecessor-bound field is therefore harmless.

Lists are a sound representation of unrestricted residual multisets: every multiset ordering is included in the universal list upper bound, an attaining list gives a multiset attainer, and `totals_perm` proves both additive totals permutation-invariant. The source does not construct a separate quotient `Multiset` type, nor does this imply anything about omitted alphabet types or remaining pair interactions.

Arbitrary negative integer weights cause no problem: every item rank is positive, and every fixed-rank witness has length at most its rank. `Option.none` is never confused with zero or a negative sentinel. The empty alphabet is permitted; then tightness forbids every positive stored value within the cutoff. At cutoff zero the empty list supplies the sole relevant base, while all positive entries remain outside scope. Items whose ranks exceed the cutoff simply have no usable transitions. Duplicate item ranks/types are also permitted; every alphabet index is still checked.
