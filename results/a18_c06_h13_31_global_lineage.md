# The `A18-C2c-H13-31` chamber: strengthened exact lineage

## Outcome

`A18-C2c-H13-31` is presently the strongest worldwide-priority-open
**literal chamber** candidate in this project.  It is an exact
finite-volume, noncompact Coxeter polytope in `H13` with 31 facets.  The new
lineage audit does not prove that the abstract chamber is unpublished, but it
substantially narrows the open gap:

- its maximal 15-wall reflection denominator is now realized explicitly by
  Tumarkin's first `H13`, `n+2`-facet pyramid;
- all pure doubles through 31 facets of the encoded Allcock, Vinberg,
  McLeod, and Tumarkin `H13` seeds are exact nonmatches;
- every arbitrary-subtree gluing obtained from any pairwise-disjoint set of
  doubling walls in Allcock's published `H13` chambers `F` and `Q` is an
  exact nonmatch; and
- all 31 target reflections are given as explicit conjugates of the 15
  Tumarkin simple reflections, leaving a replayable finite-index problem.

The certificate is
[`a18_c06_h13_31_global_lineage.json`](../data/enumerations/a18_c06_h13_31_global_lineage.json),
generated by
[`certify_a18_c06_h13_31_global_lineage.py`](../scripts/certify_a18_c06_h13_31_global_lineage.py).

This is **not** a worldwide priority theorem.  Arbitrary reflection
subgroups of the denominator, non-doubling constructions, and literature
without a recoverable diagram remain outside the proved denominator.

Independently of priority, the chamber now has a strong algebraic

[nef/effectivity theorem](a18_c06_ue8d4_nef_effectivity.md).  This strengthens
the geometric lineage but does not settle worldwide abstract priority.

After primitive normalization in this maximal lattice, the target mirrors

The new identification starts with Tumarkin's diagram

Deleting node 8 therefore gives an integral basis of the Lorentzian
quotient.  The quotient has determinant `-4` and Smith invariants


## Allcock's complete published `H13` subtree lane

[Allcock's 2006 paper](https://doi.org/10.2140/gt.2006.10.737) uses the
21-wall `D6D6` face `F`.  Since `F` has no three pairwise-disjoint doubling
walls, he doubles it across `14|203` to obtain a 25-wall chamber `Q`, then
uses three disjoint doubling walls of `Q` and arbitrary finite subtrees of
their Cayley tree.

For a chamber with `m` facets and a tree made from `I` copies, let `E_i` be
the number of internal tree edges of colour `i`.  If `c_i` is two plus the
number of other facets orthogonal to the selected wall, direct facet
propagation gives

```text
facets = m I - sum_i c_i E_i.
```

The certificate reconstructs Figure 3 exactly, including the heavy `H-F`
and `I-G` bonds omitted from the previous project transcript, computes every
doubling wall, and enumerates every maximal pairwise-disjoint set—not only
Allcock's named triple.

For `Q`, the gluing costs are at most 20.  Hence every tree with `I >= 3`
has at least

```text
25 I - 20(I-1) = 5I+20 >= 35
```

facets.  At `I=2`, two previously overlooked doubling walls have cost 19
and therefore do give 31-facet chambers.  Both have exact histogram

```text
infinity: 110,  m=2: 321,  m=3: 20,  m=4: 14,
```

so neither is the target.

For `F`, every genuine two-generator Cayley tree is a path with alternating
edge colours.  Every admissible two-wall pair has both gluing costs equal to
17, so its exact facet formula is

```text
21I-17(I-1)=4I+17.
```

It is never 31.  The two old four-copy 31-facet records were artifacts of the
omitted `H-F` and `I-G` bonds and are withdrawn.  This closes the whole
arbitrary-subtree construction based on the published `H13` chambers `F`
and `Q`.  It is stronger than the initial pure-iterated-doubling audit.

## Exact subgroup-index handoff

The integral denominator isometry permits a direct group presentation.
The certificate first conjugates the target chamber into the standard
Tumarkin chamber, then reduces each primitive target root to a simple root.
All 31 target reflections are stored as

```text
w s_i w^-1
```

in the 15-generator Tumarkin Coxeter group, with exact reconstruction checks.
The conjugating-word length histogram is

```text

34:1, 35:2, 36:1, 43:1, 44:2, 45:1, 52:1, 53:1.
```

A bounded GAP/ACE run with the `hard` strategy and 200 million workspace
words overflowed after defining 13,333,332 temporary cosets, with 12,951,331
active at overflow.  This is deliberately recorded as **inconclusive**:
temporary cosets may later collapse, so it is neither a lower bound for the
index nor evidence of infinite index.  Finite index follows geometrically
from the containment of finite-volume chambers; only its numerical value is
open.  The stored 31 words are the correct input for a future coset-action,
low-index, or arithmetic fundamental-domain computation.

## Algebraic scope

The source `(18,4,0)` lattice is K3-admissible, and the construction is an
exact involution-fixed section of Alexeev's published divisor-wall chamber.
That is the algebraic reservoir which produced the chamber.

The geometric lifting gap for this particular diagram involution is now
closed by the [order-four K3 lift theorem](a18_c06_h13_31_k3_order4_lift.md):
an explicit discriminant anti-isometry and period realize the `C06` action
on `NS(X)` by a genuine non-symplectic order-four K3 automorphism.  This gives
a surface-automorphism-fixed divisor/reflection-chamber section.  It still
makes no claim about a quotient surface or quotient nef cone, because the
48-wall source is a mixed-root reflection chamber rather than an asserted
literal nef cone.  Hyperbolicity, integrality, Coxeter angles, finite volume,
and the reflection-group lineage remain logically independent of the lift.

## Current claim boundary

What is proved is an exact new extraction in this project, a complete
finite-volume certificate, a primary identification of its known maximal
denominator, and several substantial named-lineage exclusions.  What remains
open is the decisive historical question: whether the literal 31-wall
reflection subgroup/chamber already occurs somewhere outside the audited
corpora and constructions.

The shortest plausible completion paths are now:

1. finish the exact index/coset-action computation and search the literature
   by the resulting subgroup invariant;
2. compare the frozen 31-generator presentation against additional
   machine-readable reflection-subgroup archives;
3. prove a structural classification of finite-index reflection subgroups of
   the `II_(13,1)(2^-2)` denominator under a usable index/facet bound; or
4. locate an older explicit diagram and give a facet permutation, thereby
   closing the candidate negatively.

Until one of these succeeds, the correct label is **strongest current
worldwide-open literal-chamber candidate**, not “new hyperbolic polytope
theorem.”
