## Universal counting lemma

At a simple finite vertex let its spherical Coxeter diagram have k>=2
irreducible components of one fixed type H, with rank r. There are exactly

  r^2 binom(k,2)

unordered pairs of distinct marked facets lying in two different H
components. Assign every such planar angle the nonnegative weight

  u_H(c)/(r^2 binom(k,2)),

where c=(a,min(b,2),min(h,1),min(d,2)) records the same hub context as the
current rule: counts of A1,I2(5),H3,A4. The total new charge at this vertex
is exactly u_H(c). If k<2 there are no such angles and the total is zero.
Every individual angle and the vertex itself use their actual complete
finite components. No embedded proper subdiagram is treated as a hub.

For the three new types the per-angle weights are

| H | Multiplicity | Per-angle addition | Total vertex addition |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A1 | a>=2 |u_A1(c)/binom(a,2)|u_A1(c)|
| I2(5) | b>=2 |u_I25(c)/(2b(b-1))|u_I25(c)|
| H3 | h>=2 |2u_H3(c)/(9h(h-1))|u_H3(c)|

All additions are nonnegative, so they preserve any triangle, quadrilateral,
or long-face inequality already proved for the old weights. They are
available new payment routes when old bonuses are subsequently changed.
The lemma uses exact angle incidences and holds in every dimension and
ground field where the finite components occur. No arithmetic normal
