**Verdict: PASS.**  A second exact implementation reproduces the complete
nonorthogonal three-node packet tranche over `Q(sqrt(5))`, including both
stream hashes.

The audit imports the previously certified length-two alphabet as its sole
input, but does not import the owner triangle module.  It independently
implements arithmetic in `Z[phi]`, rational-prime factorization, inert and

## Squareclass and square-root algebra

For an identity-positive golden integer, principal-ideal valuation parities
do not quite determine its element squareclass: the remaining unit class is
generated by `phi`, and is detected by the conjugate sign.  This is complete
because `Z[phi]` has class number one, its units are `+/-phi^n`, and the
totally positive units are exactly the squares `phi^(2n)`.

The independent factorization gives the same 3,661 buckets from 6,874
nonzero edge values, with maximum bucket size 109.  As an additional local
check, every value was multiplied by the representative of its bucket and
the product was explicitly reconstructed as a golden integer square.

For `A+B*phi=(m+n*phi)^2`, the replay independently solves

`5(n^2)^2-(4A+2B)n^2+B^2=0`,

reconstructs `m`, multiplies every candidate back, and requires the unique
identity-positive root.  All 4,768,141 streamed squareclass-compatible
products pass that exact check.

## Enumeration multiplicity

The second implementation finds 27,088 compatible squareclass multisets
`i<=j<=k` with XOR zero.  For every one separately, it compares the actual
iterator length with the appropriate closed formula:

- `C(s+2,3)` when all three buckets coincide;
- `C(s+1,2)t` when exactly the first two coincide;
- `s C(t+1,2)` when exactly the last two coincide;
- `stu` when all are distinct.

The actual and formula totals both equal **4,768,141**, ruling out a hidden
permutation multiplicity or omitted equal-bucket case.  The raw pre-gate
count remains `C(6876,3)=54,158,527,500` unordered nonzero edge multisets.

## Determinants and degeneracy

With diagonal entries two, edge products `c12,c23,c31`, and acute directed
cycle `b=-sqrt(c12*c23*c31)`, direct determinant expansion gives

`D=8-2(c12+c23+c31)+2b`.

At the conjugate place, the strict length-two Cauchy inequalities make all
three `2x2` principal minors positive.  Hence `D'>0` is exactly the remaining
condition for positive definiteness of the conjugate triple.  The replay
finds 2,633,885 such packets and 2,134,256 conjugate-nonpositive rejections.
Every retained packet has negative selected determinant; none has positive
or zero selected determinant.

Raw and retained degeneracy must be distinguished.  An early frozen JSON
omitted the new raw count after that schema split; it was regenerated before
this audit was frozen.  The final exact counts are:

- raw selected-degenerate packets: **1**;
- retained selected-degenerate packets: **0**.

The sole raw zero is explicitly

`edges=(1,1,1), cycle=-1, determinant=0`,

the finite affine `(3,3,3)` triangle.  Its conjugate determinant is also
zero, so conjugate frame independence rejects it before any compactness
filter.  Thus the computation does not conflate a universally counted raw
degenerate packet with a retained compact candidate.

## Scope

This PASS closes only the local complete-triangle tranche.  It does not say
that a connected rank-23--30 frame must contain a complete nonorthogonal
triangle, and it does not check compatibility of overlapping triangles,
longer cycles, ideal gauges, orders, conductor lattices, or Vinberg hosts.
The `Q(sqrt(5))`, dimensions 22--29 nonexistence question therefore remains
open.
