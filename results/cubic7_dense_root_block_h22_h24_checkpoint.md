# Cubic-seven dense root-block checkpoint: H22 and H24

This checkpoint replaces diagonal high-rank triage by the Bugaenko-shaped
families

\[
  \langle-a\rangle\oplus(E_8\oplus E_8\oplus E_6)\quad(H^{22}),
  \qquad
  \langle-a\rangle\oplus E_8^3\quad(H^{24}),
\]

over \(O_K=\mathbf Z[\theta]\), \(\theta=2\cos(2\pi/7)\), for
\(a=\theta,2\theta,\theta(2-\theta)\).  Every field sign, lattice point,
root equation, reflection-divisibility test, height comparison, and reported
cone ray is exact.

## Complete primitive mirror norms

For a primitive root \(r=(x,y)\), put \(q=(r,r)\) and
\(I(r)=(a x,G_Ry)\).  Reflection integrality is exactly
\(q\mid 2I(r)\).

For \(E_8^3\), the positive block is unimodular.  Away from primes dividing
\(2a\), primitivity makes \(I(r)=O_K\).  At a squarefree prime dividing
\(a\), either the pairing ideal is still a unit or all spatial coordinates
lie in that prime and \(x\) is a unit; the norm congruence then has valuation
exactly one.  The same calculation at the inert prime \((2)\) for
\(a=2\theta\) is
\(q=-2\theta x^2\pmod {4}\).  Thus the complete H24 lists are

| timelike coefficient | canonical norm elements |
|---|---|
| \(\theta\) | \(1,2\) |
| \(2\theta\) | \(1,2\) |
| \(\theta(2-\theta)\) | \(1,2,2-\theta,2(2-\theta)\) |

For \(E_8^2\oplus E_6\), the only additional prime is the inert prime
\((3)\), because \(\det E_6=3\).  Modulo 3 the E6 radical is generated by

\[
  (1,2,0,1,2,0),\qquad v^2=12\equiv12\pmod {18}.
\]

Consequently every primitive local radical vector has 3-adic norm valuation
exactly one.  The complete H22 lists are therefore

| timelike coefficient | canonical norm elements |
|---|---|
| \(\theta\) | \(1,2,3,6\) |
| \(2\theta\) | \(1,2,3,6\) |
| \(\theta(2-\theta)\) | \(1,2,3,6,(2-\theta),2(2-\theta),3(2-\theta),6(2-\theta)\) |

These are element representatives, not only ideal classes.  The Minkowski
bound \(14/9<2\) gives class number one, while the signs of
\(-1,\theta,\theta^2-2\) span all three sign bits.  Hence the narrow class
number is one and every totally positive unit is a square.

## Exact height-zero audit

The dominant-weight recursion exhausts all canonical norm classes at height
zero.  In every H22 seed it returns precisely three norm-two dominant orbits,
one in each finite block, proving that the full system is
\(E_8+E_8+E_6\) (552 roots).  In every H24 seed it likewise returns precisely
three norm-two dominant orbits, proving that the full system is \(E_8^3\)
(720 roots).  Thus the displayed finite Weyl chambers used for positive
levels are complete rather than assumed.

## Exact H24 cone status

In Dynkin-weight coordinates \(z_b=E_8y_b\), the 24 height-zero inequalities
are \(z_{b,i}\ge0\).  Each new wall is

\[
  h_{E_8}\mathbin\cdot z_b\le2t.
\]

At \(t=1\), each block is therefore an 8-simplex with vertices zero and
\((2/h_i)e_i\).  The full section is the product of three such simplices, so
its projective cone rays are exactly its \(9^3=729\) vertices.  Exact norm
classification gives

- 4 finite vertices;
- 0 cusps;
- 725 hyperideal rays.

All 27 walls are irredundant.  Hence this H10 prefix is not finite-volume and
not compact.  This is not a nonreflectivity theorem: later exact Vinberg
walls can remove hyperideal rays.
