## Finding

No unconditional assertion that a saved sample list proves universal
geometric completeness was found in these main manuscripts. Both already
separate those obligations explicitly. There are nevertheless three
different meanings of "reconstruct" that the shared presentation should
keep distinct: enumerate a domain from its definition; recompute values
on an inherited complete domain; and bind an earlier theorem/certificate.
The last two do not become the first merely through hashes or a PASS.

## Finite57: what is reconstructed and what is imported

**The final endpoint really re-enumerates the reduced ideal domain.**
`high_parts()` and `check_ideal()` in the dimension57 checker generate
every nondecreasing partition of57 whose parts are at least8, optionally
preceded by one low part1..7. The function compares the complete unique
generated domain with all2552 saved rows, and multiplies every factor
polynomial afresh. It also generates all28 low-pair transfers and checks
their exact stop-loss values. Thus the terminal-domain/polynomial claim
in Lemma `low8` is a genuine domain reconstruction.

Its extension to ALL cusps still requires the written concentration
theorem: low transfers terminate (sum of squared ranks strictly rises),
preserve normalized cost, lower normalized margin and V, and end at a
positive terminal margin. The actual factor-fiber envelope remains a
separate geometric theorem. Pointers: finite57 manuscript Lemma `low8`;
`docs/finite_volume57_lemma_map.md`, obligations15--17; and
`fresh_cusp_factor_payment_framework_20261017.md`.

**The final endpoint does not regenerate the finite forest cover.**
It binds the inherited3298-class/748-fiber result, checks the exact8+6
finite closure and derives the constants377,4205. This is stated correctly
in its docstring, exported verification scope and the manuscript's
reproducibility section. `representative_cover()` in the coupled frontier
checker recomputes type/pair domination and compares the inherited3298
expanded alternatives with the inherited39 actual classes; it does not
independently enumerate that older global representative domain.

For the1549 saved residual tables, `complement()` DOES verify every
allowed last-item inequality, all reachable/unreachable ranks and an
attaining predecessor. The mathematical certificate theorem needs:

* a complete residual alphabet and positive item ranks;
* full-target context parameters, so removing a residual component does
  not change its item cost or activate an unrecorded guard;
* vanishing residual pair interactions, or an explicit state carrying
  every remaining interaction;
* every fixed-type fiber and context branch covered, with upper
  relaxations distinguished from exact attainable maxima.

The actual specialization fixes all A1/A2 multiplicities, splits the
D14/A3 guard into no-A3 or one reserved A3, and charges that presence
bonus once even if additional A3 are allowed. The quarter-family A4
exclusion and the two separately bounded increasing families are also
part of completeness. A Bellman proof alone does not establish these
context facts or the arbitrary-label I2 domination.
Pointers: manuscript Proposition `oldcount`;
`fresh_d17_a16_coupled_ambient58_frontier_20261018.md`, sections
"Coverage", "Complete bounds" and "Fresh exact actual refinement";
`docs/finite_volume57_lemma_map.md`, obligations3--4,14.

**The new local checker reconstructs maps over an inherited atlas.**
Its `support(over)` starts from saved `over['isolation']['cases']`,
filters the target and recomputes all D13 embeddings, giving801/862 and
33/76. It does not reconstruct the universal isolation cases themselves.
The same applies to the216 literal chosen-output configurations and later
corners: their algebra is recomputed on named inherited domains.
The manuscript explicitly says native completeness and universal routing
are written inherited theorems. Preserve that qualification whenever
calling this a "complete target domain" reconstruction.
Pointers: manuscript `Selected cases and arbitrary additional whites`;
`docs/finite_volume57_lemma_map.md`, obligations3,7--13.

## Compact20: complete arithmetic evaluator versus geometric search

**The independent saved-primal checker streams the native rows.**
Its `native_faces()` reads `BASE.faces` from
`fresh_sqrt5_parameter_weight_stress_20261015.json` and singular
`QUEUE.records` from `fresh_sqrt5_nonsingular_rank29_fast_20261015.json`,
retaining node count<=23. It does not regenerate their initial supports,
finite/dotted contact boxes, graph rejection tests or continuation queues.
It independently recomputes the angle coefficients, exact spectator
tuples, all new denominators/pools and rational payment on those rows.
The shared older reader/finite-diagram primitives are explicit imports;
the new pool engine, producer and Bellman builder are not imported.

Thus "checks every enlarged face" means every row in the mathematically
proved complete native atlas. It must not be shortened to "reconstructs
all possible geometric faces". The manuscript correctly states in
Proposition `coverage` and its verification section that the complete
contact domains/maps are inherited mathematical premises and large queues
are not regenerated by this final check.

The corresponding completeness theorem must state:

* every actual opposite-pair minimal union has an initial representative;
* the one-white arithmetic domains are finite and exhaustive, with proved
  physical/conjugate/integral bounds rather than a sampled cutoff;
* literal continuation maps preserve the actual marked-role/payment
  meaning, and every rejection pays a persistent basic contribution;
* a final touching state is used, with every singular stopping state
  retained even when expandable; its marked factors are whole;
* the stated geometric root bound and finite canonical state/parameter
  sets justify queue termination; actual white-entry induction and queue
  implementation are separate statements;
* the BASE domain retains the dotted stopping obligations, while the
  added finite queue completes the later finite attachment frontier.

## Required wording boundary for the shared contract

State the mathematical domain and its coverage/termination theorem first;
then state the exact evaluator/certificate theorem on that domain; finally
state the pinned run result. For these two papers the outer geometric
coverage is inherited, finite forest induction is independently checkable,
and the57 reduced ideal leaves are freshly enumerated. Source hashes bind
those layers and are not a proof that an omitted case cannot exist.

No new false mathematical assertion or failed exact inequality is alleged
by this limited audit. It identifies the precise quantifiers that must
remain attached to the papers' existing reconstruction claims.
