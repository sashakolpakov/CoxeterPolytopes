## Why membership is a separate necessary condition

The ambient arithmetic lane assumes ground field **K=Q(sqrt(5))**. It does not merely assume that K is contained in a potentially larger ground field. For square-two normals b,b', the length-two Vinberg cycle is `(b.b')^2`. It must be an algebraic integer **and** belong to K. Membership follows from the definition of the ground field as the field generated by all cyclic products; integrality is a separate part of the arithmeticity criterion. See [Nikulin, §2, printed p.5, equation(3)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0708.3991). Reversing the Lorentz sign convention does not change this square.

No claim is made that every normalized Gram entry itself belongs to K. In particular a native product may involve sqrt(tau). This is precisely why the test is applied to its square.

For H,C,tau in K with tau positive,

` (H+C sqrt(tau))^2 = H^2+C^2 tau+2HC sqrt(tau). `

It belongs to K exactly when sqrt(tau) belongs to K or HC=0. If sqrt(tau) is outside K and HC is nonzero, membership of the square would recover sqrt(tau) by subtracting the constant term and dividing by2HC, a contradiction. The independent field embeddings agree with this criterion on every tested value.
