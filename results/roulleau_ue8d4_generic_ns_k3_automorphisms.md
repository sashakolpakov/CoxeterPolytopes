# Two genuine K3-automorphism realizations of the `U+E8+D4` folds

## Positive theorem

Two fixed sections that were previously certified only as symmetries of a
divisor lattice are induced by genuine automorphisms of complex projective K3
surfaces:

| Brandhorst--Hofmann row | automorphism order | order on `NS` | Roulleau action | Coxeter polytope | Vinberg conclusion |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| `0.6.3.1` | 6 | 3 | `R14_UE8D4_C3_S3` | `H11 / 13` | finite-volume, noncompact; 27 finite vertices, 1 cusp, 162 elliptic edges |
| `0.16.0.1` | 16 | 2 | `R14_UE8D4_C2` | `H12 / 14` | finite-volume, noncompact; 35 finite vertices, 2 cusps, 234 elliptic edges |

Thus both known abstract hyperbolic Coxeter polytopes now have an explicit
algebro-geometric realization in this project: each is the automorphism-fixed
section of a complete K3 nef chamber.  This is a positive realization result,
not a claim that either abstract Coxeter diagram is new worldwide.

The order-six case has since been made equation-level explicit.  The very
general minimal resolution of
`y^2=x^3+t^3(t-1)(t-a)(t-b)(t-c)`, with
`(x,y,t)->(omega*x,-y,t)`, has exactly the required
`U+E8(-1)+D4(-1)` Picard lattice and `D4` triality.  See the
[Weierstrass-family theorem](ue8d4_order6_weierstrass_family.md).

The symmetric subfamily has a cyclic order-twelve enhancement.  In
particular the named rational surface
`y^2=x^3+t^3(t^2-1)(t^2-4)` has geometric Picard lattice exactly
`U+E8(-1)+D4(-1)` by an independent `p=7` Frobenius certificate, and its
order-six and order-twelve elements induce the same triality.  See the
[named `C12` theorem](ue8d4_named_c12_member.md).

The exact certificate is
[`roulleau_ue8d4_generic_ns_k3_automorphisms.json`](../data/enumerations/roulleau_ue8d4_generic_ns_k3_automorphisms.json),
generated by
[`certify_roulleau_ue8d4_generic_ns_k3_automorphisms.py`](../scripts/certify_roulleau_ue8d4_generic_ns_k3_automorphisms.py).

## Why these are genuine surfaces, not lattice guesses

The source rows belong to Brandhorst--Hofmann's classification of finite K3
automorphism groups ([article](https://doi.org/10.1017/fms.2023.50),
[database v2.1](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10410278)).  The database README
states that every line represents a moduli space of pairs `(X,G)` and an
effective character with respect to an effective subgroup.  The certificate
pins that README, the complete ZIP, both member files, their row counts, the
two row numbers, and the individual row hashes.

For each 22-dimensional row, the external replay:

1. reconstructs an even unimodular K3 lattice of signature `(3,19)`;
2. verifies the exact order of the distinguished isometry;
3. takes the saturated kernel of the relevant cyclotomic polynomial to obtain
   the generic transcendental lattice `T`;
4. takes `T`'s saturated orthogonal complement to obtain the generic
   Néron--Severi lattice; and
5. restricts the action to that lattice.

In both rows,

```text
rank(T) = 8,  signature(T) = (2,6),  det(T) = 4,
NS = U + E8(-1) + D4(-1),
rank(NS) = 14,  signature(NS) = (1,13),  det(NS) = -4.
```

The Smith invariants of both `T` and the nontrivial part of `NS` end in
`2,2`.  The transcendental characteristic polynomial is
`Phi_6(x)^4` for `0.6.3.1` and `Phi_16(x)` for `0.16.0.1`.  Hence these are
exact generic-`NS` calculations on the Brandhorst--Hofmann automorphism loci,
not Picard-rank lower bounds.

## Exact match with the two archived divisor actions

A characteristic polynomial alone would not identify a Picard action.  Here
the match can be made directly through the distinguished `D4` summand.

For Roulleau's chamber, the four roots `A13,A12,A14,A15`, ordered with `A12`
central, have Gram matrix

```text
[-2  1  0  0]
[ 1 -2  1  1]
[ 0  1 -2  0]
[ 0  1  0 -2].
```

Their primitive orthogonal complement is even unimodular of signature
`(1,9)`, and the combined basis has determinant one.  Thus the source lattice
splits integrally as

```text
II_(1,9) + D4(-1) = U + E8(-1) + D4(-1).
```

The complete source `S3` fixes the `II_(1,9)` summand and permutes the three
outer `D4` roots.  For each Brandhorst--Hofmann matrix, the certificate gives
another determinant-one block basis with literal `U`, an even negative
unimodular rank-eight block, and the same `D4` Gram.  The Picard action is the
identity on `U+E8(-1)` and is exactly the corresponding diagram permutation
on `D4(-1)`.  Uniqueness of the even unimodular lattice of signature `(1,9)`
then gives an integral equivariant isometry of the full Picard actions.

There is an independent discriminant-form check.  In both source and database
bases,

```text
A_NS = (Z/2)^2,       q = {0:1, 1:3}.
```

The order-three action cycles all three nonzero classes; the involution fixes
one and swaps the other two.  Explicit `2 x 2` changes of discriminant basis
intertwine the archived and database actions.

The fixed and coinvariant lattices are:

| action on `NS` | fixed lattice | algebraic coinvariant |
|---|---|---|
| order 3 | rank 12, determinant `-3`, Smith `1^11 3` | `A2(-2)`, in the database basis `[[-12,6],[6,-4]]` |
| order 2 | rank 13, determinant `4`, Smith `1^12 4` | `<-4>` |

Both coinvariants are rootless.  In canonical bases their Grams are
`[[-4,2],[2,-4]]` and `[-4]`; every nonzero norm is a negative multiple of
four, so neither contains a `(-2)` vector.

## Kähler chamber and Torelli handoff

Effectiveness is part of the database row, so each distinguished Hodge
isometry already comes from a genuine K3 automorphism and preserves its Kähler
chamber.  What remains is to identify that chamber with the particular
Roulleau chamber used by the polytope certificate.

All `(-2)`-Weyl chambers in a chosen positive-cone component are conjugate by
the Weyl group.  Transport the database Kähler chamber to Roulleau's complete
15-wall chamber by such a Weyl element.  A root reflection acts trivially on
the discriminant group.  Meanwhile the Roulleau chamber stabilizer is `S3`,
and its action on the three nonzero discriminant classes is faithful: the
displayed transposition and 3-cycle generate all of `GL(2,2)`.  Consequently
the transported action lies in the correct chamber conjugacy class, and one
final chamber symmetry makes it exactly the archived generator.

This proves that suitable marked K3 surfaces from the two database rows have
the Roulleau chamber as nef chamber and induce precisely the archived folds.
The order-sixteen automorphism restricts to an involution on `NS`; the
order-six automorphism restricts to order three.  Their remaining order is
transcendental.

This does not contradict Roulleau's statement about the general surface in
his original family.  We do not claim that its nontrivial diagram actions are
geometric.  We use different K3 surfaces on the pinned Brandhorst--Hofmann
automorphism loci, whose generic Picard lattice is nevertheless exactly the
same rank-14 lattice.

## Vinberg finite-volume conclusion

The existence of a K3 surface does not by itself prove finite volume.  That
part is inherited from the complete, pinned Vinberg audits:

- For `H11/13`, all 162 elliptic edge diagrams have exactly two finite/ideal
  endpoint extensions; there are 27 finite vertices, one ideal vertex, two
  full parabolic-neighbourhood audits, and zero bad edges.
- For `H12/14`, all 234 elliptic edge diagrams pass; there are 35 finite
  vertices, two ideal vertices, three full parabolic-neighbourhood audits, and
  zero bad edges.

The complete root list and all affine neighbourhood tests are part of those
audits.  Hence both polytopes have finite volume.  Their positive cusp counts
prove that they are noncompact.  Both diagrams are already known full
reflection chambers of their fixed lattices, with reflection index one.
