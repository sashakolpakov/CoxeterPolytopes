# A named rational `U+E8+D4` K3 with a cyclic order-12 action

## Positive theorem

Let `X/Q` be the minimal resolution of

```text
y^2 = x^3 + t^3 (t^2-1)(t^2-4).
```

Then

```text
NS(X_Qbar) = U + E8(-1) + D4(-1).
```

If `omega` is a primitive cube root of unity, `X_C` has the
commuting automorphisms

```text
zeta (x,y,t) = ( omega*x, -y,  t),   order 6,
iota (x,y,t) = (     -x, i*y, -t),   order 4,
h = zeta*iota = (-omega*x,-i*y,-t),  order 12.
```

They satisfy `iota^2=zeta^3`, and

```text
<zeta,iota> = <h> = C12,   zeta=h^10,   iota=h^3.
```

On the Néron--Severi lattice, `iota` is the identity, while `zeta` and `h`
are the literal outer triality on the `D4` summand.  The three cyclic actions
are the exact Brandhorst--Hofmann types `0.4.3.1`, `0.6.3.1`, and
`0.12.1.1`.  Thus one named rational surface gives equation-level
realizations of both the known full `H13/15` nef chamber and the known
finite-volume noncompact triality chamber `H11/13`.

This is a positive realization theorem, not a new-abstract-polytope claim.
Its new content in this project is the named rational member, the arithmetic
proof of its exact Picard lattice, and the simultaneous `C4/C6/C12`
equation-level realization.

## Geometry and the one-parameter `C12` family

The fiber configuration is

| place | fiber | root lattice | Euler number |
|---|---|---|---:|
| `0` | `I0*` | `D4(-1)` | 6 |
| `+/-1,+/-2` | four `II` | none | 8 |
| `infinity` | `II*` | `E8(-1)` | 10 |

The Euler numbers sum to 24.  The fiber, zero section, and reducible-fiber
components supply the rank-14 lattice

```text
U + E8(-1) + D4(-1)  subset NS(X_Qbar),   determinant -4.
```

The construction is not isolated.  It belongs to the symmetric subfamily

```text
y^2 = x^3 + t^3 (t^2-r^2)(t^2-s^2),
r*s*(r^2-s^2) != 0,
```

which is genuinely one-dimensional after base scaling, with parameter
`r/s` modulo a finite symmetry.  The same order-12 map `h` acts throughout.
Primitive order 12 makes the transcendental rank divisible by
`phi(12)=4`.  Starting from Picard rank at least 14, the next possible rank
is therefore 18; its primitive-eigenvalue period space has projective
dimension zero.  It cannot contain this nonisotrivial curve.  Hence the very
general symmetric member already has exact Picard rank 14.  The arithmetic
argument below proves it for the particular rational point `(r,s)=(1,2)`.

## Exact Picard rank of the named member

Reduce at `p=7`.  The four roots are `1,2,5,6`, pairwise distinct and
nonzero, so the fiber types remain `I0* + 4 II + II*` and the minimal regular
model has good K3 reduction.

At the `I0*` fiber the first blow-up, `x=tX`, `y=tY`, gives

```text
Y^2 = t (X^3+4+...),
```

so the three nonidentity arms are indexed by `X^3=-4=3`.  The element 3 is
not a cube in `F_7`, hence Frobenius cycles the arms; they split over
`F_(7^n)` exactly when `3|n`.  The raw cuspidal cubic has
`q+1` points.  Resolving it changes this to `2q+1` in the cyclic case and
`5q+1` in the split case, corrections `q` and `4q`.  The split `II*` fiber
has `9q+1` points.

An independent character-sum count gives:

| `n` | `q=7^n` | raw finite-fiber sum | `#X(F_q)` | known-NS normalized trace | quotient trace |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 7 | 56 | 127 | 11 | 0 |
| 2 | 49 | 2,422 | 2,913 | 11 | -28 |
| 3 | 343 | 117,992 | 122,452 | 14 | 0 |
| 4 | 2,401 | 5,779,942 | 5,803,953 | 11 | 12,740 |

Let `W` be the rank-eight quotient of `H^2` by the known divisor
lattice.  There is no determinant-sign guess.  Over `F_7`,
Frobenius commutes with `zeta`, and

```text
W = V_lambda + V_(lambda^-1),   dim V_lambda = 4.
```

Cup product makes the second Frobenius block (7^2(A^{-1})^t) if the first
is `A`, so the determinant on `W` is exactly `7^8`.  Newton identities
therefore reconstruct

```text
P_W(T) = T^8 + 14 T^6 - 3087 T^4 + 33614 T^2 + 7^8.
```

No root is `7` times a root of unity.  Indeed, after putting `T=7x`,
the primitive normalized equation is

```text
7 x^8 + 2 x^6 - 9 x^4 + 2 x^2 + 7 = 0.
```

For a root of unity `x`, the algebraic integer `u=x^2+x^(-2)` would
satisfy

```text
7 u^2 + 2 u - 23 = 0,
u = (-1 +/- 9 sqrt(2))/7.
```

Neither value lies in the ring of integers `Z[sqrt(2)]` of `Q(sqrt(2))`,
a contradiction.  The
certificate also checks every cyclotomic polynomial of degree at most eight.

The Tate conjecture for K3 surfaces in odd characteristic, proved in the
needed generality by
[Madapusi Pera](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.6326), now gives

```text
rho(X_F7bar) = 14.
```

Good-reduction specialization injects `NS(X_Qbar)` into this lattice, while
the displayed divisors already have rank 14.  Thus `rho(X_Qbar)=14`.
There is no same-rank saturation ambiguity: a proper enlargement would have
index two and be even unimodular of signature `(1,13)`, impossible because
`1-13` is not divisible by eight.  This proves the stated exact Néron--Severi
lattice.

## The three Picard actions

The holomorphic two-form is `dt wedge dx/y`.  Its multipliers under
`zeta`, `iota`, and `h` are respectively `-omega`, `-i`, and `i*omega`,
primitive roots of orders 6, 4, and 12.

On the `I0*` blow-up chart:

```text
zeta: X -> omega*X,             cycles the three outer arms;
iota: X -> X and Y -> -i*Y,    fixes the three arms;
h:    X -> omega*X,             has the same triality as zeta.
```

The affine `E8` diagram of the `II*` fiber is rigid, and the fiber and zero
section are fixed.  Consequently `iota` is the identity on all of `NS`,
whereas `zeta` and `h` act as identity on `U+E8(-1)` and triality on
`D4(-1)`.

For triality the exact integral data are

```text
fixed lattice:       U + E8(-1) + A2(-1), rank 12, determinant -3;
coinvariant lattice: A2(-2), Gram [[-4,2],[2,-4]];
[NS : fixed + coinvariant] = 3.
```

The quotient Smith diagonal is `1^13,3`.  The discriminant group of `NS` is
`(Z/2)^2`; triality cycles its three nonzero classes, while `iota` fixes them.
This records the glue, not only the rational eigenspaces.

This uniqueness statement is the result of scanning every row, not a search

index-three glue, and discriminant actions therefore agree exactly.

The classification article is
[Brandhorst--Hofmann, *Finite subgroups of automorphisms of K3 surfaces*](https://doi.org/10.1017/fms.2023.50).

## Coxeter consequence

The order-4 action fixes the full rank-14 Picard lattice and hence gives an
equation-level realization of Roulleau's complete 15-wall `H13` K3 nef
chamber.  The order-6 and order-12 actions have the common fixed lattice
`U+E8(-1)+A2(-1)` and give the already certified triality chamber:

```text
dimension 11, 13 facets,
27 finite vertices, 1 cusp,
162 elliptic edges,
0 bad edge audits.
```

The full Vinberg audit classifies this chamber as finite-volume and
noncompact.  Thus the surface produces actual hyperbolic Coxeter polytopes;
the algebraic symmetry is not being offered in place of the volume test.
