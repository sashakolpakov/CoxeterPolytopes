## 4. Full variable-cone no-return proof

    cot(alpha)=(coth(R)*S-C*cos(B))/sin(B)

    sqrt2*sqrt(d²r²-1)-d*r>r

the inequality strict. Consequently cot(alpha)>r and alpha<beta.
