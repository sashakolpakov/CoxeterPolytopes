# An explicit Inose-family carrier for the complete `H17/19` K3 chamber

## Positive theorem

**TOOT.** Let `A,B` satisfy

```text
A(A^3-(B+1)^2)(A^3-(B-1)^2) != 0.                 (1.1)
```

The minimal resolution of the classical Inose pencil

```text
X_(A,B): y^2 = x^3 - 3 A t^4 x + t^5(t^2-2Bt+1)  (1.2)
```

is an elliptic K3 surface.  For a very general parameter pair,

```text
NS(X_(A,B)) = U + E8(-1) + E8(-1).
```

Its nef cone is therefore a copy of the published complete 19-wall chamber
for this lattice.  The corresponding hyperbolic Coxeter polytope is the
finite-volume, noncompact chamber

```text
R18-UE8x2-NEF-H17-19: 19 facets in H^17.
```

This is a positive equation-level realization in the project ledger.  The
equation is the classical Inose pencil, and the abstract chamber is the
classical Vinberg/Kondō chamber; neither is claimed new worldwide.  What is
new here is the explicit, checked bridge

```text
equation -> exact very-general NS -> complete nef walls -> Vinberg volume.
```

## Singular fibers and the parameter open

Put

```text
a4 = -3 A t^4,
a6 = t^5(t^2-2Bt+1).
```

Then

```text
Delta = -432 t^10 R_(A,B)(t),
R_(A,B)(t) = (t^2-2Bt+1)^2 - 4A^3t^2,
disc_t(R) = 4096 A^6
            (A^3-(B+1)^2)(A^3-(B-1)^2).
```

Condition (1.1) makes the four roots of `R` simple and nonzero.  Since
`c4=144At^4`, they give four `I1` fibers.  At zero the valuations

```text
(ord a4, ord a6, ord c4, ord Delta)=(4,5,4,10)
```

give a `II*` fiber.  After `u=1/t` and the global Weierstrass scaling, the
same valuations occur at infinity, giving the second `II*`.  Thus the fiber
list and Euler sum are

| fibers | root contribution | Euler number |
|---|---|---:|
| `II* + II* + 4 I1` | `E8(-1)+E8(-1)` | `10+10+4=24` |

The open is visibly nonempty: `(A,B)=(1,3)` gives

```text
R(t)=(t^2-8t+1)(t^2-4t+1),   disc(R)=184320.
```

This witness is used only to prove that (1.1) is nonempty.  No Picard-rank
claim is made for that named member.

## Why the very general Néron--Severi group is exact

The fiber, zero section, and nonidentity components of the two `II*` fibers
give

```text
L=U+E8(-1)+E8(-1) subset NS(X).
```

This lattice has rank 18, signature `(1,17)`, and determinant `-1`.  In
particular it is unimodular: the embedding is primitive, there is no
same-rank saturation ambiguity, and once `rho=18`, Shioda--Tate also forces
Mordell--Weil rank and torsion to vanish.

It remains to prove the generic rank.  The residual quartic expands as

```text
R=t^4-4Bt^3+(4B^2+2-4A^3)t^2-4Bt+1.
```

Consequently its coefficients recover the two parameters, up to finite
ambiguity:

```text
B   = -[t^3]R/4,
A^3 = (4B^2+2-[t^2]R)/4.
```

More invariantly, an isomorphism preserving the ordered `II*` fibers has
base map `t -> lambda t`.  Comparing the monic palindromic quartics gives
`lambda^4=1` and, generically, `lambda^2=1`; swapping zero and infinity adds
only a further finite identification.  Thus the marked elliptic surfaces
have two-dimensional moduli.  Forgetting the fibration has
zero-dimensional fibers, since elliptic fibrations and sections on a fixed
K3 are integral divisor classes.

The `L`-polarized period domain has dimension `20-rank(L)=2`.  The locus
where an additional algebraic class appears is a countable union of proper
Noether--Lefschetz curves.  Hence a very general member has `rho=18`, and
unimodularity gives `NS=L` exactly.

## Complete chamber handoff

For any K3 surface with this Néron--Severi lattice, the nef cone is a Weyl
chamber in the positive cone.  Weyl transport identifies it with the
complete 19-curve configuration published by Vinberg and Kondō and recorded
by Roulleau.  Exact reconstruction of its intersection Gram matrix gives

```text
wall count = 19,
rank = 18,
inertia = (1,17,1),
determinant of a full-rank wall basis = -1.
```

The one-dimensional kernel is the expected linear dependence among the 19
walls; the divisor lattice itself is nondegenerate of signature `(1,17)`.

## Literal Vinberg volume gate

The claim is stronger than Lorentzian signature or local Coxeter
compatibility.  The complete wall system passes Vinberg's endpoint and
maximal-parabolic criterion:

| exact audit | value |
|---|---:|
| hyperbolic dimension | 17 |
| facets | 19 |
| elliptic rank-16 edge diagrams | 729 |
| finite vertices | 80 |
| ideal vertices | 2 |
| connected affine-neighborhood audits | 3 |
| bad edges | 0 |
| bad full parabolic neighborhoods | 0 |

The simple-root list is the published complete list, not a truncated root
search.  Hence every elliptic edge has exactly the required finite or ideal
endpoints, and every relevant affine diagram has a full parabolic zero
neighborhood.  Therefore the chamber is **finite-volume noncompact**.

## Lineage

The Weierstrass form is the standard Inose pencil; see Schütt,
[*Fields of definition of singular K3 surfaces*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0612396).
The complete curve configuration is recorded in Roulleau, Section 15.4, with
the classical lineage to Kondō, Section 4.2, Figure 2, and Vinberg.  Roulleau
also gives a double-plane model branched over a sextic with an `A17`
singularity.  The present theorem adds a second, particularly rigid
algebro-geometric carrier: two `II*` fibers make the desired divisor lattice
automatic and unimodular.
