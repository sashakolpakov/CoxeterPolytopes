# A changed prime-seven arithmetic host and targeted completion tests

No new finite-volume or compact polytope was found. This round did establish that the chosen H9 quadratic space differs from six previously searched cubic-nine spaces, construct a complete 23-wall bounded Coxeter prefix, and independently verify an exact spacelike escape. A local obstruction excludes every primitive reflection root in the escape's binary plane, at any height. Subsequent searches outside that plane allowed two or three A6 covectors, the entire binary block, and the scalar coordinate. They found no cutter through the stated height bound. The full arithmetic host remains open.

Write `T = 2 cos(pi/9)`, with `T^3 - 3T - 1 = 0`, `beta = 4 - T^2`, and `D = 2 + T - T^2`. The field has discriminant 81, ring of integers `Z[T]`, and class number one. Its unit signatures are generated by `T`, `D`, and `-1`, so every totally positive unit is a square. Set

```
B = [[2, -T], [-T, 2]],
L_alpha = <-alpha> + B + A6 + <2>,
M_alpha = <-alpha> + A6 + <2>,       alpha in {T, 2T}.
```

Here A6 has diagonal 2 and adjacent entries -1. The main H9 search used `alpha = 2T`. The H7 complement `M_alpha` was tested first: in a compact completion the `I2(18)` face would itself be a compact Coxeter polytope, and no spherical extension can attach to the bond labelled 18. This uses the face criterion documented in the [high-label face review](fresh_cubic9_high_label_faces_20261013.md); failure of a finite H7 prefix is not failure of the full H7 chamber.

The main determinant is `-28 T beta`, of field norm `-65856`. The prime 7 is inert, and its valuation in this determinant is odd. In contrast, it has valuation zero in the four prior `B^4 + <1>` spaces with coefficients `T`, `2T`, `T beta`, `2T beta`, and the two prior `B^2 + A4 + <2>` spaces with coefficients `T`, `2T`. In rank ten, similarity preserves the determinant squareclass; the norm ratios are nonsquares. Thus the new main space is not field-similar to any of those six spaces. This is a comparison with those repository cases, not a classification or worldwide novelty claim.

The preliminary A5 and D5 alternatives did not pass this distinction test: their determinant ratios to specified old hosts are respectively `3/beta^3 = (T/D)^2` and `4/beta^2 = (2/beta)^2`. Equality of determinant squareclasses alone proves neither isometry nor equality of full reflection groups. The [exact structural certificate](fresh_h9_changed_form_20261013_structure.json) records all six comparisons and these two narrower triage identities.

The complete *necessary* primitive norm pools, after scaling a normal by a unit, are:

These are exhaustive candidate pools; the report does not assert that every class occurs. The H9 dual exponent is `14 beta`, giving the initial divisibility bound `q | 28 beta`. Evenness removes odd norms for coefficient `2T`. For coefficient `T`, a norm divisible by 4 forces all coordinates even, hence an imprimitive normal. For the H7 complement, a norm with beta-valuation one would force every coordinate divisible by beta, which is impossible.

A further local test removes all classes divisible by 7 but not by beta. Reflection integrality makes the time, B, and scalar coordinates divisible by 7, while the A6 coordinates are congruent to `t(1,2,3,4,5,6)` modulo 7. Consequently `q/7 = -t^2` modulo 7. The residues `-1`, `-2`, and `-4` are nonsquares in `F343`; the corresponding residues multiplied by beta are squares and cannot be discarded. The structural certificate explicitly enumerates the required field residues and Euler symbols. In particular, the searches retain both `14 beta` and `28 beta`.

Complete zero-height enumeration gives `A6 x A1` in H7 and `I2(18) x A6 x A1` in H9: respectively 44 and 80 oriented roots, with two and four dominant representatives. There are no additional mixed zero-height mirrors. This census includes the full candidate norm pool, rather than assuming a direct-product chamber.

For H9 the surviving escape is

```
s = (2 + 4T - 2T^2)/7,
v = (1; 0,0; s,2s,3s,4s,5s,6s; 0),
v^2 = -72/7 - 26T + (120/7)T^2 > 0.
```

The natural binary slice is generated by the time axis and `d = 7 omega6 = (1,2,3,4,5,6)` in A6, with `d^2 = 42`. It is saturated: the time and first A6 coordinates give an identity coordinate minor. There is no primitive reflective normal in this entire binary slice, nor in its A6 reversal. Beta norm classes first force all supported coordinates divisible by beta. For the remaining safe classes `q = 2, 4, 14, 28`, the norm equation is `42t^2 - 2T x^2 = q`. The `q = 2, 14` cases reduce to `-x^2 = 1` modulo beta, impossible in `F3`. For `q = 4, 28`, reflection integrality forces `t` even, and reduction modulo 2 then forces `x` even. The [standalone structural replay](../scripts/fresh_h9_changed_form_20261013_structure.py) checks the finite residue tables and the escape's location in this plane. This is a bound-independent result only for that binary slice.

The constructive follow-up deliberately leaves this plane. In all runs, both B coordinates and the scalar are free; A6 covectors are integral lattice covectors with nonnegative distinguished embedding, and all six H9 candidate norms are retained. The exact height is `h = 2T x^2/q`. At the fixed bound `h <= 1000` there are 81 time/norm controls. The searches include:
