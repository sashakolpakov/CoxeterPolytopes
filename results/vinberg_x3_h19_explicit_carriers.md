# Two explicit algebraic carriers for Vinberg's 36-wall chamber in `H19`

## Positive theorem

**TOOT.** Vinberg's singular K3 surface `X3` has two independently certified
algebraic presentations carrying the complete discriminant-three Coxeter
system:

1. the extremal Inose fibration

   ```text
   y^2=x^3+t^5(t-1)^2,
   ```

   with fibers `II*+II*+IV`; and

2. the order-three abelian-quotient presentation, in constant-rescaled
   Weierstrass form

   ```text
   y^2=x^3+(t^3-1)^4,
   ```

   with three `IV*` fibers and `Z/3` Mordell--Weil torsion.

Both have

```text
NS(X3)=U+2E8(-1)+A2(-1),
```

and hence carry, as integral divisor classes, the 36 primitive mirrors of
Vinberg's finite-volume noncompact polytope in `H19`.  The exact chamber has
36 facets, 8,346 finite vertices, and 107 cusps.

These are explicit algebraic realizations of a known chamber.  Vinberg's
polytope, the surface `X3`, and the displayed elliptic models are not claimed
new worldwide.  The positive project contribution is the replayable bridge
from two equations and an abelian quotient through exact divisor lattices to
the complete mixed-root chamber.

## Carrier 1: the extremal Inose fibration

For

```text
y^2=x^3+t^5(t-1)^2,
```

one has

```text
Delta=-432t^10(t-1)^4.
```

The fibers at zero and infinity are `II*`; the fiber at one is `IV`.  Their
Euler numbers already sum to `10+10+4=24`, and their components together
with the fiber and zero section give

```text
U+E8(-1)^2+A2(-1).
```

This has rank 20, the maximum possible complex K3 Picard rank, and
determinant `-3`.  A proper same-rank overclosure would require a square
index dividing 3, so none exists.  Thus the displayed trivial lattice is the
whole Néron--Severi group and the Mordell--Weil group is zero.

## Carrier 2: an order-three abelian quotient

Let `E_omega` be `v^2=u^3-1` and let

```text
<alpha x alpha^2>,  alpha(v,u)=(v,omega*u),  omega^3=1,
```

act on `E_omega x E_omega`.  The quotient has nine `A2` rational double
points; its minimal resolution is `X3`.  A constant-rescaled elliptic model
is

```text
y^2=x^3+(t^3-1)^4.
```

It has three `IV*` fibers.  The visible sections

```text
(x,y)=(0,+/-(t^3-1)^2)
```

are the nonzero `3`-torsion sections.  The trivial lattice
`U+E6(-1)^3` has determinant `-27`; adjoining the diagonal order-three
fundamental-weight class gives an even integral index-three primitive
closure of determinant `-3`.  Its discriminant form and signature identify
it with `U+2E8(-1)+A2(-1)`.

This quotient model is genuinely useful for the algebraic-object-first
program: the finite-index glue in the divisor lattice is produced by a
visible torsion section rather than inferred abstractly.

## What the surface supplies, and what it does not

The 36 primitive integral normals split as

- 24 classes of square `-2` and divisibility one; and
- 12 classes of square `-6` and divisibility three.

The first are rational-curve walls in the Vinberg/OSCAR chamber.  The second
are integral divisor classes giving the additional short-root cuts; they are
not asserted to be irreducible curves.  Thus this is an intrinsic
**mixed-divisor reflection chamber contained in the nef cone**, not a claim
that all 36 facets are Mori walls of the full K3 nef cone.  This distinction
is exactly why attaching the explicit equations to the complete reflection
certificate matters.

Brandhorst--Zach compute the corresponding 36-wall chamber on this explicit
surface.  Vinberg's completed algorithm, as restated by Looijenga, proves
that the mixed-root list is complete.

## Literal Vinberg volume certificate

The 36-wall Gram matrix has rank 20, inertia `(1,19,16)`, and nonzero Smith
invariants `1^19,3`; hence it spans the full divisor lattice.  The exact
endpoint audit is:

| invariant | value |
|---|---:|
| elliptic rank-18 edges | 87,046 |
| two finite endpoints | 72,015 |
| one finite and one ideal endpoint | 14,544 |
| two ideal endpoints | 487 |
| bad edges | 0 |
| finite vertices | 8,346 |
| cusps | 107 |

Every elliptic edge has exactly two finite or ideal endpoints, and the full
parabolic neighborhoods are those in the complete Vinberg--Looijenga list.
Therefore the chamber has finite volume.  Its 107 cusps prove that it is
noncompact.

## Lineage

here, are catalogued by Brandhorst--Zach,
