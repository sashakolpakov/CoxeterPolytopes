# Complete saved selected-triangle data exposes the remaining partial-rule failures

The [fresh separation](../scripts/fresh_sqrt5_rank13_full_triangle_simplex_separation_20261017.py) expands all **24,523 ADE** and **173,434 non-ADE** saved selected-endpoint triangle occurrences, including the compressed ADE alternatives and rows outside the former positive-dual support. The 197,957 literal records reduce, by exact weighted graph isomorphisms, to **12,420 maximal-simplex stars** and **158 fixed determinant classes**. This adds 11,012 stars beyond the 1,408 previously checked stars. The simplex dimensions in this domain are two, three, and four, with 9,745, 1,927, and 748 unique stars respectively; no global simplex-dimension bound is inferred.

The [saved exact evaluation](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_full_triangle_simplex_separation_20261017.json) quantifies the failure of the [58 partial rules](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_discriminant_simplex_lp_20261017.md). Of the unique stars, **7,084 have all corner forests represented**; **4,277 of these are unpaid** in at least one applicable class. There are 125,565 failed class/star payments out of 341,446 exact tests. Another **5,336 stars require 1,362 absent whole forests**, so their payments are left undefined. Existing class rules are used in their classes; a newly encountered fixed class uses the saved unrepresented-class rule as a proposal, not as a previously certified complete rule.

The first exact zero-payment failure is especially simple: take an A13 path on roots 0 through 12 and add root 13 with a 5-bond to root 1. Its black roots are 1, 2, and 13. The three corner forests are `2A1+A11`, `A10+H3`, and `A13`; the complete star determinant is -8-12sqrt(5), a new fixed class. The saved generic rule gives all three corner variables zero, hence aggregate payment **zero instead of two**. A [separate explicit Gram-and-ray certificate](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_a13_triangle_failure_20261017.md) verifies its actual compact local geometry and arithmetic reflection module.

Generation and a fresh saved reconstruction check all literal diagrams, complete spherical-deletion sets, exact graph maps, new determinant classes, corner-orbit lookups, and rational payments. No optimizer is used. The earlier 58-class feasibility certificate remains valid on its declared smaller domain; this package demonstrates precisely why that certificate cannot yet support a dimension theorem.

The domain is complete for the two selected endpoint sets used by the saved ADE and non-ADE catalogues, not for all arithmetic triangles. Triangles with fewer than two selected endpoints, other simplex configurations, quadrilaterals, and absent finite vertex budgets remain outside. New stars and classes must be included before attempting a stronger interpretation of a repaired rule.
