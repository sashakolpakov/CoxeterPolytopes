# Complete cyclic denominator for the characteristic-two Borcherds chamber

Last updated: 2026-08-07.

## Bottom line

The full weighted-diagram group of the Borcherds 210-wall chamber has exactly
`28` element conjugacy classes.  Exhausting every order, not only the prime
classes, leaves exactly four nonidentity fixed spaces in hyperbolic dimension
at least ten:

| class | fixed rank | section | facets | geometric K3 class? |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `2a` | 14 | `H13` | 50 | yes |
| `2c` | 15 | `H14` | 98 | no, Frobenius diagram isometry |
| `2d` | 11 | `H10` | 47 | yes |
| `3b` | 12 | `H11` | 22 | yes |

Every composite-order class has fixed rank at most nine.  Thus there is no
unexamined cyclic `H10+` section hidden at order `4,5,6,7,8,10,12,14,15`, or
`21`.

All four high classes have strict genuine restricted facets, exact Coxeter
pairs, saturated integral fixed lattices, and integral primitive facet
reflections.  They replay the four separately pinned casts exactly; the full
element census creates no fifth cyclic cast.

## Exact reconstruction

The 42 square-minus-two walls form the point--line Levi graph of `PG(2,4)`.
The certificate constructs linear projective generators, field Frobenius,
and point--line duality on this graph.  Each of the 168 square-minus-four
walls has a unique pairing signature against the 42 short walls, so every
Levi action has at most one lift.  All five lifts preserve every entry of the
full weighted 210-wall Gram matrix.

GAP then certifies

- full group `PGammaL(3,4).2 = PSL(3,4).D12`, order `241,920`;
- Dolgachev--Kondo geometric subgroup `PGL(3,4).2`, order `120,960`;
- `28` full-group element conjugacy classes, whose sizes sum to `241,920`;
- `16` classes inside and `12` classes outside the geometric subgroup.

The 28 archive rows are sorted by unique invariant fingerprints rather than
GAP's internal class ordering, which is not stable across runs.

## Status of the four high casts

- `2a/H13/50` is finite-volume and noncompact by the literal Vinberg test.
  It is a geometric class on the characteristic-two K3.  No local ledger or
  pinned CoxIter record has the same dimension and facet count; global
  literature priority is being audited separately.
- `2c/H14/98` is finite-volume and noncompact, but its normalized Gram is the
  published Alexeev `(15,9,1)` 98-wall chamber.  Its Frobenius generator is
  non-geometric.
- `2d/H10/47` is the already certified finite-index chamber in the classical
  `f_2^10` arithmetic class.
- `3b/H11/22` is the already certified full chamber of
  `U+A2(-1)^5`, Scharlau--Kirschmer List11 entry 27.
