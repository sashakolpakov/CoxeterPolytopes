# Exact A1 padding carries the triangle payment obstruction to rank fourteen

The [fresh verifier](../scripts/fresh_sqrt5_rank14_triangle_padding_barrier_20261017.py) pads every rank-13 whole forest and every retained local triangle in the [triangle-only dual](fresh_sqrt5_rank13_active_triangle_dual_20261017.md) by one orthogonal, unmarked A1 root. It reconstructs all 91 unordered root pairs at each padded forest, rather than treating the former 78-pair multiplicities as unchanged.

The old marked-pair orbit map is injective: its code gains one unmarked A1 component, which can be removed to recover the old code. Permutations among the new and old A1 components can increase an old orbit's multiplicity, but cannot decrease it. All 18,627 old orbits are checked literally against 20,436 new orbits in 560 padded whole forests; every old multiplicity is at most its new multiplicity. Additional root-pair weights are nonnegative. Therefore a rank-14 payment rule with budget at most fourteen would restrict to a rank-13 payment rule with budget at most fourteen on these 5,886 triangles.

The [saved exact certificate](fresh_sqrt5_rank14_triangle_padding_barrier_20261017.json) directly verifies the padded dual and gives the slightly stronger lower bound **36557903013600/2399991247457 > 14**. It does not rerun an optimizer. Orthogonal A1 padding preserves each individual compact local triangle: append a positive one-dimensional K form, its orthogonal wall, and the same triangle in that wall. Physical and conjugate inertias become (14,1) and (15,0), respectively; reflection integrality is preserved by the orthogonal rank-one lattice summand. This does not assert that all the triangles coexist in one compact arithmetic chamber.

For completeness, the target is derived from the h-vector rather than borrowed from another dimension. For a simple n-polytope, f0 is the sum of h_i and f2 is the sum of binomial(n-i,2)h_i. Pair h_i with h_(n-i), using nonnegativity and symmetry. The minimum paired ratio is k(k-1)/2 when n=2k, and k^2/2 when n=2k+1. The inequality is strict because h0=hn=1 and the endpoint ratio is larger. Also, 2T+Q is at least 5f2-binomial(n,2)f0. Thus the target is twelve at n=13 and fourteen at n=14; the verifier records every paired coefficient in both dimensions.

This excludes only the corner-local nonnegative payment method required to cover the displayed local configurations. It is not a nonexistence theorem for compact arithmetic Coxeter polytopes in either dimension. Global incidence or arithmetic-form compatibility may rule out combinations of local configurations, and those conditions are outside this padding argument.
